Imagine a lake, surrounded with hills covered with a huge\nforest, with nobody but ourselves. So lovely, so quiet, so restful! I will leave this place in about ten days\nand go to the Thousand Island Park. I will meditate by the hour there and be\nall alone to myself. The very idea is ennobling.\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

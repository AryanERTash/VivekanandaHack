["\n", "II\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "BARANAGORE,\n", "22nd February, 1889.\n", "DEAR SIR, (Shri Pramadadas Mitra)\n", "I  had  intended to go to Varanasi, and I planned to reach there after\nvisiting the birthplace of my Master. But unluckily on the way to that\nvillage I had an attack of high fever followed by vomiting and purging as in\ncholera. There was again fever after three or four days \u2014 and as the body is\nnow so weak that I can barely walk even two steps, I have been compelled now\nto give up my previous intention. I do not know what is God's will, but my\nbody is quite unfit for treading on this path. Anyway, the body is not\neverything. Recovering my health after a few days here, I entertain the hope\nof visiting you there. The will of Vishweshwara, the Lord of the universe,\nwill prevail \u2014 whatever that may be. You also kindly bless me. My respects\nto you and brother Jnanananda.\n", "Your servant,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

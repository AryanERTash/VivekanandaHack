["\n", "INDIA'S MESSAGE TO THE WORLD\n", "\nThe following notes were discovered among Swami Vivekananda's papers. He\nintended to write a book and jotted down forty-two points as a syllabus for\nthe work, but only a few points were dealt with as an introduction by him\nand the work was left unfinished. We give the manuscript as found.\n", "\n", "Syllabus\n", "\n1. Bold has been my message to the people of the West. Bolder to those at\nhome.\n", "\n2. Four years of residence in the marvellous West has made India only the\nbetter understood. The shades are deeper and the lights brighter.\n", "\n3. The survey \u2014 it is not true that the Indians have degenerated.\n", "\n4.  The  problem  here  has  been as it has been everywhere else \u2014 the\nassimilation of various races, but nowhere has it been so vast as here.\n", "\n5. Community of language, government and, above all, religion has been the\npower of fusion.\n", "\n6.  In  other  lands  this has been attempted by \"force\", that is, the\nenforcement of the culture of one race only over the rest. The result being\nthe production of a short-lived vigorous national life; then, dissolution.\n", "\n7. In India, on the other hand, the attempts have been as gentle as the\nproblem vast, and from the earliest times, the customs, and especially the\nreligions, of the different elements tolerated.\n", "\n8. Where it was a small problem and force was sufficient to form a unity,\nthe effect really was the nipping in the bud of various healthy types in the\ngerm of all the elements except the dominant one. It was only one set of\nbrains using the vast majority for its own good, thus losing the major\nportion of the possible amount of development, and thus when the dominant\ntype had spent itself, the apparently impregnable building tottered to its\nruins, e.g., Greece, Rome, the Norman.\n", "\n9. A common language would be a great desideratum; but the same criticism\napplies to it, the destruction of the vitality of the various existing ones.\n", "\n10.  The only solution to be reached was the finding of a great sacred\nlanguage of which all the others would be considered as manifestations, and\nthat was found in the Sanskrit.\n", "\n11. The Dravidian languages may or may not have been originally Sanskritic,\nbut  for practical purposes they are so now, and every day we see them\napproaching the ideal more and more, yet keeping their distinctive vital\npeculiarities.\n", "\n12. A racial background was found \u2014 the \u00c2ryas.\n", "\n13. The speculation whether there was a distinct, separate race called the\nAryas living in Central Asia to the Baltic.\n", "\n14. The so-called types. Races were always mixed.\n", "\n15. The \"blonde\" and the \"brunette\".\n", "\n16. Coming to practical common sense from so-called historical imagination.\nThe Aryas in their oldest records were in the land between Turkistan and the\nPunjab and N. W. Tibet.\n", "\n17. This leads to the attempt at fusion between races and tribes of various\ndegrees of culture.\n", "\n18. Just as Sanskrit has been the linguistic solution, so the Arya the\nracial solution. So the Br\u00e2hminhood is the solution of the varying degrees\nof  progress  and  culture as well as that of all social and political\nproblems.\n", "\n19. The great ideal of India \u2014 Brahminhood.\n", "\n20. Property-less, selfless, subject to no laws, no king except the moral.\n", "\n21. Brahminhood by descent \u2014 various races have claimed and acquired the\nright in the past as well as in the present.\n", "\n22. No claim is made by the doer of great deeds, only by lazy worthless\nfools.\n", "\n23. Degradation of Brahminhood and Kshatriyahood. The Puranas said there\nwill be only non-Brahmins in the Kali Yuga, and that is true, becoming truer\nevery day. Yet a few Brahmins remain, and in India alone.\n", "\n24. Kshatriyahood \u2014 we must pass through that to become a Brahmin. Some may\nhave passed through in the past, but the present must show that.\n", "\n25. But the disclosure of the whole plan is to be found in religion.\n", "\n26. The different tribes of the same race worship similar gods, under a\ngeneric name as the Baals of the Babylonians, the Molochs of the Hebrews.\n", "\n27. The attempt in Babylonia of making all the Baals merge in Baal-Merodach\n\u2014 the attempt of the Israelites to merge all the Molochs in the Moloch Yavah\nor Yahu.\n", "\n28. The Babylonians destroyed by the Persians; and the Hebrews who took the\nBabylonian  mythology  and adapted it to their own needs, succeeded in\nproducing a strict monotheistic religion.\n", "\n29. Monotheism like absolute monarchy is quick in executing orders, and a\ngreat  centralization of force, but it grows no farther, and its worst\nfeature  is its cruelty and persecution. All nations coming within its\ninfluence perish very soon after a flaring up of a few years.\n", "\n30. In India the same problem presented itself - the solution found \u2014\n\u090f\u0915\u0902 \u0938\u0926\u094d\u0935\u093f\u092a\u094d\u0930\u093e \u092c\u0939\u0941\u0927\u093e \u0935\u0926\u0928\u094d\u0924\u093f \u0964\n", "\nThis is the keynote to everything which has succeeded, and the keystone of\nthe arch.\n", "\n31. The result is that wonderful toleration of the Vedantist.\n", "\n32. The great problem therefore is to harmonise and unify without destroying\nthe individuality of these various elements.\n", "\n33. No form of religion which depends Upon persons, either of this earth or\neven of heaven, is able to do that.\n", "\n34. Here is the glory of the Advaita system preaching a principle, not a\nperson, yet allowing persons, both human and divine, to have their full\nplay.\n", "\n35. This has been going on all the time; in this sense we have been always\nprogressing. The Prophets during the Mohammedan rule.\n", "\n36. It was fully conscious and vigorous in old days, and less so of late; in\nthis sense alone we have degenerated.\n", "\n37. This is going to be in the future. If the manifestation of the power of\none tribe utilising the labours of the rest produced wonderful results at\nleast for a certain length of time, here is going to be the accumulation and\nthe concentration of all the races that have been slowly and inevitably\ngetting mixed up in blood and ideas, and in my mind's eye, I see the future\ngiant  slowly maturing. The future of India, the youngest and the most\nglorious of the nations of earth as well as the oldest.\n", "\n38. The way \u2014 we will have to work. Social customs as barriers, some as\nfounded upon the Smritis. But none from the Shrutis. The Smritis must change\nwith time. This is the admitted law.\n", "\n39. The principles of the Vedanta not only should be preached everywhere in\nIndia, but also outside. Our thought must enter into the make-up of the\nminds of every nation, not through writings, but through persons.\n", "\n40. Gift is the only Karma in Kali Yuga. None attaining knowledge until\npurified by Karma.\n", "\n41. Gift of spiritual and secular knowledge.\n", "\n42. Renunciation \u2014 Renouncers \u2014 the national call.\n", "\n", "Introduction\n", "\nBold has been my message to the people of the West, bolder is my message to\nyou, my beloved countrymen. The message of ancient India to new Western\nnations I have tried my best to voice \u2014 ill done or well done the future is\nsure to show; but the mighty voice of the same future is already sending\nforward soft but distinct murmurs, gaining strength as the days go by, the\nmessage of India that is to be to India as she is at present.\n", "\nMany wonderful institutions and customs, and many wonderful manifestations\nof strength and power it has been my good fortune to study in the midst of\nthe various races I have seen, but the most wonderful of all was to find\nthat  beneath all these apparent variations of manners and customs, of\nculture and power, beats the same mighty human heart under the impulsion of\nthe same joys and sorrows, of the same weakness and strength\n", "\nGood and evil are everywhere and the balance is wondrously even; but, above\nall, is the glorious soul of man everywhere which never fails to understand\nany one who knows how to speak its own language. Men and women are to be\nfound in every race whose lives are blessings to humanity, verifying the\nwords  of  the divine Emperor Asoka: \"In every land dwell Br\u00e2hmins and\nShramanas.\"\n", "\nI  am  grateful to the lands of the West for the many warm hearts that\nreceived me with all the love that pure and disinterested souls alone could\ngive; but my life's allegiance is to this my motherland; and if I had a\nthousand lives, every moment of the whole series would be consecrated to\nyour service, my countrymen, my friends.\n", "\nFor to this land I owe whatever I possess, physical, mental, and spiritual;\nand if I have been successful in anything, the glory is yours, not mine.\nMine alone are my weaknesses and failures, as they come through my inability\nof profiting by the mighty lessons with which this land surrounds one, even\nfrom his very birth.\n", "\nAnd what a land! Whosoever stands on this sacred land, whether alien or a\nchild of the soil, feels himself surrounded \u2014 unless his soul is degraded to\nthe level of brute animals \u2014 by the living thoughts of the earth's best and\npurest sons, who have been working to raise the animal to the divine through\ncenturies, whose beginning history fails to trace. The very air is full of\nthe pulsations of spirituality. This land is sacred to philosophy, to ethics\nand  spirituality,  to  all that tends to give a respite to man in his\nincessant struggle for the preservation of the animal to all training that\nmakes man throw off the garment of brutality and stand revealed as the\nspirit immortal, the birthless, the deathless, the ever-blessed \u2014 the land\nwhere the cup of pleasure was full, and fuller has been the cup of misery,\nuntil here, first of all, man found out that it was all vanity; here, first\nof all in the prime of youth, in the lap of luxury, in the height of glory\nand plenitude of power, he broke through the fetters of delusion. Here, in\nthis ocean of humanity, amidst the sharp interaction of strong currents of\npleasure and pain, of strength and weakness, of wealth and poverty, of joy\nand sorrow, of smile and tear, of life and death, in the melting rhythm of\neternal peace and calmness, arose the throne of renunciation! Here in this\nland, the great problems of life and death, of the thirst for life, and the\nvain mad struggles to preserve it only resulting in the accumulation of woes\nwere first grappled with and solved \u2014 solved as they never were before and\nnever will be hereafter; for here and here alone was discovered that even\nlife itself is an evil, the shadow only of something which alone is real.\nThis is the land where alone religion was practical and real, and here alone\nmen and women plunged boldly in to realise the goal, just as in other lands\nthey madly plunge in to realise the pleasures of life by robbing their\nweaker  brethren. Here and here alone the human heart expanded till it\nincluded not only the human, but birds, beasts, and plants; from the highest\ngods to grains of sand, the highest and the lowest, all find a place in the\nheart of man, grown great, infinite. And here alone, the human soul studied\nthe universe as one unbroken unity whose every pulse was his own pulse.\n", "\nWe all hear so much about the degradation of India. There was a time when I\nalso believed in it. But today standing on the vantage-ground of experience,\nwith eyes cleared of obstructive predispositions and above all, of the\nhighly-coloured pictures of other countries toned down to their proper shade\nand light by actual contact, I confess in all humility that I was wrong.\nThou blessed land of the Aryas, thou wast never degraded. Sceptres have been\nbroken and thrown away, the ball of power has passed from hand to hand, but\nin India, courts and kings always touched only a few; the vast mass of the\npeople, from the highest to the lowest, has been left to pursue its own\ninevitable course, the current of national life flowing at times slow and\nhalf-conscious, at others, strong and awakened. I stand in awe before the\nunbroken procession of scores of shining centuries, with here and there a\ndim link in the chain, only to flare up with added brilliance in the next,\nand there she is walking with her own majestic steps \u2014 my motherland \u2014 to\nfulfil her glorious destiny, which no power on earth or in heaven can check\n\u2014 the regeneration of man the brute into man the God.\n", "\nAy, a glorious destiny, my brethren, for as far back as the days of the\nUpanishads we have thrown the challenge to the world:\n\u0928 \u092a\u094d\u0930\u091c\u092f\u093e \u0927\u0928\u0947\u0928 \u0924\u094d\u092f\u093e\u0917\u0947\u0928\u0948\u0915\u0947 \u0905\u092e\u0943\u0924\u0924\u094d\u0935\u092e\u093e\u0928\u0936\u0941\u0903\u2014 \"Not by progeny, not by wealth, but by renunciation alone immortality is reached.\"\nRace after race  has  taken  the challenge up and tried their utmost to solve the\nworld-riddle on the plane of desires. They have all failed in the past \u2014 the\nold ones have become extinct under the weight of wickedness and misery,\nwhich lust for power and gold brings in its train, and the new ones are\ntottering to their fall. The question has yet to be decided whether peace\nwill survive or war; whether patience will survive or non-forbearance,\nwhether goodness will survive or wickedness; whether muscle will survive or\nbrain; whether worldliness will survive or spirituality. We have solved our\nproblem ages ago, and held on to it through good or evil fortune, and mean\nto hold on to it till the end of time. Our solution is unworldliness \u2014\nrenunciation.\n", "\nThis is the theme of Indian life-work, the burden of her eternal songs, the\nbackbone of her existence, the foundation of her being, the raison d'\u00eatre of\nher very existence \u2014 the spiritualisation of the human race. In this her\nlife-course she has never deviated, whether the Tartar ruled or the Turk,\nwhether the Mogul ruled or the English.\n", "\nAnd I challenge anybody to show one single period of her national life when\nIndia was lacking in spiritual giants capable of moving the world. But her\nwork is spiritual, and that cannot be done with blasts of war-trumpets or\nthe march of cohorts. Her influence has always fallen upon the world like\nthat of the gentle dew, unheard and scarcely marked, yet bringing into bloom\nthe  fairest flowers of the earth. This influence, being in its nature\ngentle, would have to wait for a fortunate combination of circumstances, to\ngo out of the country into other lands, though it never ceased to work\nwithin the limits of its native land. As such, every educated person knows\nthat whenever the empire-building Tartar or Persian or Greek or Arab brought\nthis land in contact with the outside world, a mass of spiritual influence\nimmediately flooded the world from here. The very same circumstances have\npresented themselves once more before us. The English high roads over land\nand sea and the wonderful power manifested by the inhabitants of that little\nisland have once more brought India in contact with the rest of the world,\nand the same work has already begun. Mark my words, this is but the small\nbeginning, big things are to follow; what the result of the present work\noutside India will be I cannot exactly state, but this I know for certain\nthat millions, I say deliberately, millions in every civilised land are\nwaiting  for the message that will save them from the hideous abyss of\nmaterialism into which modern money-worship is driving them headlong, and\nmany of the leaders of the new social movements have already discovered that\nVedanta in its highest form can alone spiritualise their social aspirations.\nI shall have to return to this towards the end I take up therefore the other\ngreat subject, the work within the country.\n", "\nThe  problem  assumes  a twofold aspect, not only spiritualisation but\nassimilation of the various elements of which the nation is composed. The\nassimilation of different races into one has been the common task in the\nlife of every nation.\n"]

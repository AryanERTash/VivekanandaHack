He comes of a respectable family of Calcutta. I found him at\nKhandwa where I made his acquaintance, although I knew his family long\nbefore in Calcutta.\n", "He is a very honest and intelligent boy and is an undergraduate of the\nCalcutta University. You know how hard the struggle is in Bengal nowadays,\nand the poor boy has been out in search of some job. Knowing your native\nkindness of heart, I think I am not disturbing you by asking and entreating\nyou to do something for this young man. I need not write more. You will find\nhim an honest and hard-working lad. If a single act of kindness done to a\nfellow creature renders his whole life happy, I need not remind you that\nthis boy is a P\u00e2tra (a person quite deserving of help), noble and kind as\nyou are.\n", "I hope you are not disturbed and troubled by this request of mine. This is\nthe  first  and the last of its kind and made only under very peculiar\ncircumstances. Hoping and relying on your kind nature, I remain,\n", "Yours faithfully,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "CXLV\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "RIDGELY MANOR,\n", "30th October, 1899.\n", "MY DEAR OPTIMIST,\n", "I received your letter and am thankful that something has come to force\noptimistic laissez faire into action. Your questions have tapped the very\nsource of pessimism, however. British rule in modern India has only one\nredeeming feature, though unconscious; it has brought India out once more on\nthe stage of the world; it has forced upon it the contact of the outside\nworld. If it had been done with an eye to the good of the people concerned,\nas circumstances favoured Japan with, the results could have been more\nwonderful  for  India.  No  good  can  be  done  when the main idea is\nblood-sucking. On the whole the old regime was better for the people, as it\ndid not take away everything they had, and there was some justice, some\nliberty.\n", "A few hundred, modernised, half-educated, and denationalised men are all the\nshow of modern English India \u2014 nothing else. The Hindus were 600 million in\nnumber according to Ferishta, the Mohammedan historian, in the 12th century\n\u2014 now less than 200 million.\n", "In spite of the centuries of anarchy that reigned during the struggles of\nthe English to conquer, the terrible massacre the English perpetrated in\n1857 and 1858, and the still more terrible famines that have become the\ninevitable consequence of British rule (there never is a famine in a native\nstate)  and  that take off millions, there has been a good increase of\npopulation,  but  not  yet  what  it was when the country was entirely\nindependent \u2014 that is, before the Mohammedan rule. Indian labour and produce\ncan  support  five times as many people as there are now in India with\ncomfort, if the whole thing is not taken off from them.\n", "This is the state of things \u2014 even education will no more be permitted to\nspread;  freedom of the press stopped already, (of course we have been\ndisarmed long ago), the bit of self-government granted to them for some\nyears is being quickly taken off. We are watching what next! For writing a\nfew words of innocent criticism, men are being hurried to transportation for\nlife, others imprisoned without any trial; and nobody knows when his head\nwill be off.\n", "There has been a reign of terror in India for some years. English soldiers\nare killing our men and outraging our women \u2014 only to be sent home with\npassage and pension at our expense. We are in a terrible gloom \u2014 where is\nthe Lord? Mary, you can afford to be optimistic, can I? Suppose you simply\npublish this letter \u2014 the law just passed in India will allow the English\nGovernment in India to drag me from here to India and kill me without trial.\nAnd I know all your Christian governments will only rejoice, because we are\nheathens. Shall I also go to sleep and become optimistic? Nero was the\ngreatest optimistic person! They don't think it worth while to write these\nterrible things as news items even! If necessary, the news agent of Reuter\ngives the exactly opposite news fabricated to order! Heathen-murdering is\nonly a legitimate pastime for the Christians! Your missionaries go to preach\nGod and dare not speak a word of truth for fear of the English, who will\nkick them out the next day.\n", "All property and lands granted by the previous governments for supporting\neducation have been swallowed up, and the present Government spends even\nless than Russia in education. And what education?\n", "The least show of originality is throttled. Mary, it is hopeless with us,\nunless there really is a God who is the father of all, who is not afraid of\nthe strong to protect the weak, and who is not bribed by wealth. Is there\nsuch a God? Time will show.\n", "Well, I think I am coming to Chicago in a few weeks and talk of things\nfully! Don't quote your authority.\n", "With all love, ever your brother,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. As for religious sects \u2014 the Brahmo Samaj, the Arya Samaj, and other\nsects have been useless mixtures; they were only voices of apology to our\nEnglish masters to allow us to live! We have started a new India \u2014 a growth\n\u2014 waiting to see what comes. We believe in new ideas only when the nation\nwants them, and what will be true for us. The test of truth for this Brahmo\nSamaj is \"what our masters approve\"; with us, what the Indian reasoning and\nexperience approves. The struggle has begun \u2014 not between the Brahmo Samaj\nand us, for they are gone already, but a harder, deeper, and more terrible\none.\n", "V.\n"]

In March I will be able to stay a few weeks at\neach of these places. Of course it is true that [as] yet I have no competent\npersons here to carry on the work like Sturdy in England, nor any sincere\nfriend to stand by me except you.\n", "I will do anything you want me to, and if you think it is good for me to\ncome to Boston in February, I am ready.\n", "Ever yours with gratitude, love, and blessings\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "P.S.

Noble or Sister Nivedita)\n", "It is a lazy life I am leading for the last two months, floating leisurely\nin a boat, which is also my home, up and down the beautiful Jhelum, through\nthe most gorgeous scenery God's world can afford, in nature's own park,\nwhere the earth, air, land, grass, plants, trees, mountains, snows, and the\nhuman form, all express, on the outside at least, the beauty of the Lord \u2014\nwith almost no possessions, scarcely a pen or an inkstand even, snatching up\na meal whenever or wherever convenient, the very ideal of a Rip Van Winkle!\n. . .\n", "Do not work yourself out. It is no use; always remember \u2014 \"Duty is the\nmidday sun whose fierce rays are burning the very vitals of humanity.\" It is\nnecessary for a time as a discipline; beyond that, it is a morbid dream.\nThings go on all right whether we lend them our helping hands or not. We in\ndelusion only break ourselves. There is a false sentiment which goes the\nextreme of unselfishness, only to injure others by its submission to every\nevil. We have no right to make others selfish by our unselfishness; have we?\n. . .\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

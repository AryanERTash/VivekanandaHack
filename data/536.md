["\n", "LXXV[6]*\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "U.S.A.,\n", "(Beginning of?) 1895.\n", "MY DEAR SHASHI,\n", "Yesterday I received a letter from you in which there was a smattering of\nnews, but nothing in detail. I am much better now. Through the grace of the\nLord I am proof against the severe cold for this year. Oh, the terrible\ncold! But these people keep all down through scientific knowledge. Every\nhouse has its cellar underground, in which there is a big boiler whence\nsteam is made to course day and night through every room. This keeps all the\nrooms warm, but it has one defect, that while it is summer indoors, it is 30\nto 40 degrees below zero outside! Most of the rich people of this country\nmake for Europe during the winter, which is comparatively warm.\n", "Now, let me give you some instructions. This letter is meant for you. Please\ngo through these instructions once a day and act up to them. I have got\nSarada's letter \u2014 he is doing good work \u2014 but now we want organization. To\nhim, Brother Tarak, and others please give my special love and blessings.\nThe  reason  why I give you these few instructions is that there is an\norganising power in you \u2014 the Lord has made this known to me \u2014 but it is not\nyet fully developed. Through His blessings it will soon be. That you never\nlose your centre of gravity is an evidence of this, but it must be both\nintensive and extensive.\n", "1. All the Sh\u00e2stras hold that the threefold misery that there is in this\nworld is not natural, hence it is removable.\n", "2. In the Buddha Incarnation the Lord says that the root of the \u00c2dhibhautika\nmisery or, misery arising from other terrestrial beings, is the formation of\nclasses (J\u00e2ti); in other words, every form of class-distinction, whether\nbased on birth, or acquirements, or wealth is at the bottom of this misery.\nIn the Atman there is no distinction of sex, or Varna[7]* or Ashrama,[8]* or\nanything  of  the kind, and as mud cannot be washed away by mud, it is\nlikewise impossible to bring about oneness by means of separative ideas.\n", "3. In the Krishna Incarnation He says that the root of all sorts of misery\nis  Avidy\u00e2 (Nescience) and that selfless work purifies the mind. But \"\nwhat is no-work\" (Gita).\n", "4. Only that kind of work which develops our spirituality is work. Whatever\nfosters materiality is no-work.\n", "5. Therefore work and no-work must be regulated by a person's aptitude, his\ncountry, and his age.\n", "6. Works such as sacrifices were suited to the olden times but are not for\nthe modern times.\n", "7. From the date that the Ramakrishna Incarnation was born, has sprung the\nSatya-Yuga (Golden Age) . . . .\n", "8. In this Incarnation atheistic ideas ... will be destroyed by the sword of\nJnana (knowledge), and the whole world will be unified by means of Bhakti\n(devotion) and Prema (Divine Love). Moreover, in this Incarnation, Rajas, or\nthe desire for name and fame etc., is altogether absent. In other words,\nblessed is he who acts up to His teachings; whether he accepts Him or not,\ndoes not matter.\n", "9. The founders of different sects, in the ancient or modern times, have not\nbeen in the wrong. They have done well, but they must do better. Well \u2014\nbetter \u2014 best.\n", "10. Therefore we must take all up where they are, that is, we must lead them\non to higher and higher ideals, without upsetting their own chosen attitude.\nAs to social conditions, those that prevail now are good, but they shall be\nbetter \u2014 best.\n", "11. There is no chance for the welfare of the world unless the condition of\nwomen is improved. It is not possible for a bird to fly on only one wing.\n", "12. Hence, in the Ramakrishna Incarnation the acceptance of a woman as the\nGuru, hence His practicing in the woman's garb and frame of mind,[9]* hence\ntoo His preaching the motherhood of women as representations of the Divine\nMother.\n", "13.

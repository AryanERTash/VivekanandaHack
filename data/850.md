["\n", "III\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "BAGHBAZAR,\n", "CALCUTTA,\n", "21st March, 1889.\n", "RESPECTED SIR, (Shri Pramadadas Mitra)\n", "It is several days since I received your last letter. Please excuse the\ndelay in replying, which was due to some special reasons. I am very ill at\npresent; there is fever now and then, but there is no disorder in the spleen\nor other organs. I am under homeopathic treatment. Now I have had to give up\ncompletely the intention of going to Varanasi. Whatever God dispenses will\nhappen later on, according to the state of the body. If you meet brother\nJnanananda, please tell him not to be held up there in expectation of my\ncoming. My going there is very uncertain. My regards to you and Jnanananda.\n", "Yours sincerely,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

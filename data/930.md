I have also suggested them that instead of having too many\npapers, they may start by putting in a few sheets in The Brahmavadin \u2014\nwritten in America \u2014 and raise the subscription a little which will cover\nthe American expenses. I had a number\nof newspaper cuttings from America about Saradananda \u2014 I hear from them that\nhe has done very well there. America is a good training ground to bring out\nall that is in a man. There is such a sympathy in the air. I had letters\nfrom Goodwin and Saradananda. S. sends his love to you and Mrs. Sturdy and\nthe baby.\n", "With everlasting love and blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

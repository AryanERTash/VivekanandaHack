["\n", "CXVII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "DELHI,\n", "30th November, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Part  of the money that Miss M\u00fcller promised has reached Calcutta. The\nbalance will come afterwards in a short while. We have also some amount.\nMiss M\u00fcller will deposit the money in your name as well as mine with Messrs.\nGrindlay & Co. As you have got the power of attorney, you alone can draw all\nthe money. As soon as the money is deposited, you yourself with Hari go to\nPatna and meet that gentleman and by some means or other influence him; and\nif the price of the land is reasonable, buy it. If it cannot be had, try for\nsome other plot of ground. I am trying to get some money in these parts too.\nWe must hold the big festival on our own plot of ground \u2014 remember this must\nbe your first and foremost work, come what may.\n", "You have shown great pluck; the work you have done these last eight or nine\nmonths does you great credit. Now you must see to it that a Math and a\ncentre in Calcutta are steadily established before everything else. Work\nhard  to this end but quietly and in secret. Get information about the\nCossipore house also. Tomorrow I am going to Khetri via Alwar. My health is\ngood, even though I have caught a cold. Send all letters to Khetri. My love\nto all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. What about the will I asked you to make in favour of Sharat and Hari? Or\nwill you buy the land and other things in my name, and I shall make a will?\n", "V.\n"]

["\n", "CVIII\n", "\n", "AIRLIE LODGE, RIDGEWAY GARDENS,\n", "WIMBLEDON, ENGLAND,\n", "17th Sept., 1896.\n", "DEAR SISTER, (Miss Harriet Hale.)\n", "Your  very  welcome  news  reached me just now, on my return here from\nSwitzerland. I am very, very happy to learn that at last you have thought it\nbetter to change your mind about the felicity of \"Old Maids Home\". You are\nperfectly right now \u2014 marriage is the truest goal for ninety-nine per cent\nof the human race, and they will live the happiest life as soon as they have\nlearnt and are ready to abide by the eternal lesson \u2014 that we are bound to\nbear and forbear and that life to every one must be a compromise.\n", "Believe  me,  dear  Harriet, perfect life is a contradiction in terms.\nTherefore we must always expect to find things not up to our highest ideal.\nKnowing this, we are bound to make the best of everything. From what I know\nof you, you have the calm power which bears and forbears to a great degree,\nand therefore I am safe to prophesy that your married life will be very\nhappy.\n", "All blessings attend you and your fianc\u00e9 and may the Lord make him always\nremember what good fortune was his in getting such a wife as you \u2014 good,\nintelligent, loving, and beautiful. I am afraid it is impossible for me to\ncross the Atlantic so soon. I wish I could, to see your marriage.\n", "The best I can do in the circumstances is to quote from one of our books:\n\"May you always enjoy the undivided love of your husband, helping him in\nattaining all that is desirable in this life, and when you have seen your\nchildren's children, and the drama of life is nearing its end, may you help\neach other in reaching that infinite ocean of Existence, Knowledge, and\nBliss, at the touch of whose waters all distinctions melt away and we are\nall one!\" (A reminiscence of Kalidasa's Shakuntalam, where Kanva gives his\nbenedictions to Shakuntal\u00e2 on the eve of her departure to her husband's\nplace.)\n", "\"May you be like Um\u00e2, chaste and pure throughout life \u2014 may your husband be\nlike Shiva, whose life was in Uma!\"\n", "Your loving brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

The Christian believes that Jesus Christ died to save him. With\nyou it is belief in a doctrine, and this belief constitutes your salvation.\nWith us, doctrine has nothing whatever to do with salvation. Each one may\nbelieve in whatever doctrine he likes or in no doctrine. With us realization\nis religion, not doctrine. Each one has a special nature peculiar to himself which he must\nfollow and through which he will find his way to freedom. Your teacher\nshould be able to tell you what your particular path in nature is and to put\nyou in it. He should know by your face where you belong and should be able\nto indicate it to you. We should never try to follow another's path for that\nis his way, not yours. When that path is found, you have nothing to do but\nfold your arms and the tide will carry you to freedom.

I will have to remain in New York a few days yet\nto see my books through. I am going to publish another edition of Karma-Yoga\nand the London lectures in a book form. Miss Waldo is editing them, and Mr.\nLeggett will publish.\n", "Then, I think, if I am to remain in this country a few weeks more, it is\nbetter that you get a rest and change. Newport[6]* is a celebrated seaside\nplace \u2014 four hours from New York. I am invited there. I will go there this\nweek and, as promised, I [will] find quiet and retirement and freedom. I\nwill try to find a place for you and wire you as soon as found.\n", "I am sure in Detroit you cannot have rest. A little change of place and\nquiet from time to time is a great factor in renewing one's vigour.\n", "Well, if you think that you would have better rest and quiet in Detroit,\ndrop a line and I come. It is only seventeen hours from New York to Detroit,\nand I am quite strong to undertake it. I am free to go already; only I\nreally want you to take a good, long rest for some weeks at least.\n", "Don't  be  afraid of expenses. Mother has amply provided that and will\nprovide, so long I am unselfish.\n", "Think [over] all the pros and cons, and write at your earliest convenience.\n", "I am going to Newport anyway, just to see what it looks like. I will write\nyou all about [it] as soon as I am there.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

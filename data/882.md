["\n", "XXXV\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "168, BRATTLE STREET,\n", "CAMBRIDGE,\n", "8th December, 1894.\n", "DEAR SISTER,\n", "I have been here three days. We had a nice lecture from Lady Henry Somerset.\nI have a class every morning here on Vedanta and other topics. Perhaps you\nhave got the copy of Vedantism by this time which I left with Mother Temple\nto be sent over. I went to dine with the Spaldings another day. That day\nthey urged me, against my repeated protests, to criticise the Americans. I\nam afraid they did not relish it. It is of course always impossible to do\nit. What about Mother Church and the family at Chicago? I had no letters\nfrom them a long time. I would have run into town to see you before this,\nhad I time. I am kept pretty busy the whole day. Then there is the fear of\nnot meeting you.\n", "If you have time, you may write, and I shall snatch the first opportunity to\nsee you. My time of course is always in the afternoon, so long I shall be\nhere, that is until the 27th or 28th of this month; I will have to be very\nbusy in the morning till 12 or 1.\n", "With my love to you all,\n", "Ever your affectionate brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

Well, do you think there is any religion left in India! The paths of\nknowledge, devotion, and Yoga \u2014 all have gone, and now there remains only\nthat of Don't touchism \u2014 \"Don't touch me! Don't touch me!\" The whole world\nis  impure,  and I alone am pure. Lucid Brahmajn\u00e2na! Bravo! Great God!\nNowadays Brahman is neither in the recesses of the heart, nor in the highest\nheaven, nor in all beings \u2014 now He is in the cooking-pot. Formerly the\ncharacteristic  of  a noble-minded man was \" [26_brahmananda_01.jpg] \u2014\nPleasing the whole universe by one's numerous acts of service\" but now it is\n\u2014 I am pure and the whole world is impure \u2014 go and get money and set it at\nmy feet. . . . Tell the sapient sage who writes to me to finish my preaching\nwork here and return home, . . . that this country is more my home. What is\nthere in Hindusthan? Who appreciates religion? Who appreciates learning?\n", "To return home! Where is the home! I do not care for liberation, or for\ndevotion,   I   would  rather  go  to  a  hundred  thousand  hells,  \"\n\u2014 this is my religion. I do not want to have any connection with lazy,\nhard-hearted, cruel and selfish men. He whose good fortune it is, may help\nin this great cause.\n", ". . . Please convey to all my love, I want the help of everyone. Neither\nmoney pays, nor name, nor fame, nor learning; it is character that can\ncleave through adamantine walls of difficulties. Bear this in mind. . . .\n", "Ever yours in love,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

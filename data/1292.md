["\n", "THE PLAGUE MANIFESTO[6]*\n", "Om Salutations to Bhagavan Shri Ramakrishna<\n", "Brothers of Calcutta!\n", "1.  We  feel  happy when you are happy, and we suffer when you suffer.\nTherefore, during these days of extreme adversity, we are striving and\nceaselessly  praying for your welfare and an easy way to save you from\ndisease and the fear of an epidemic.\n", "2. If that grave disease \u2014 fearing which both the high and the low, the rich\nand the poor are all fleeing the city \u2014 ever really comes in our midst, then\neven if we perish while serving and nursing you, we will consider ourselves\nfortunate because you are all embodiments of God. He who thinks otherwise\n\u2014 out of vanity, superstition or ignorance \u2014 offends God and incurs great\nsin. There is not the slightest doubt about it.\n", "3. We humbly pray to you \u2014 please do not panic due to unfounded fear. Depend\nupon  God  and calmly try to find the best means to solve the problem.\nOtherwise, join hands with those who are doing that very thing.\n", "4. What is there to fear? The terror that has entered people's hearts due to\nthe occurrence of the plague has no real ground. Through God's will, nothing\nof  the  terrible form that plague takes, as seen in other places, has\noccurred in Calcutta. The government authorities have also been particularly\nhelpful to us. So what is there to fear?\n", "5. Come, let us give up this false fear and, having faith in the infinite\ncompassion of God, gird our loins and enter the field of action. Let us live\npure and clean lives. Disease, fear of an epidemic, etc., will vanish into\nthin air by His grace.\n", "6.         (a) Always keep the house and its premises, the rooms, clothes,\nbed, drain, etc., clean.\n", "(b) Do not eat stale, spoiled food; take fresh and nutritious food instead.\nA weak body is more susceptible to disease.\n", "(c) Always keep the mind cheerful. Everyone will die once. Cowards suffer\nthe pangs of death again and again, solely due to the fear in their own\nminds.\n", "(d) Fear never leaves those who earn their livelihoods by unethical means or\nwho cause harm to others. Therefore, at this time when we face the great\nfear of death, desist from all such behaviour.\n", "(e) During the period of epidemic, abstain from anger and from lust \u2014 even\nif you are householders.\n", "(f) Do not pay any heed to rumours.\n", "(g) The British government will not vaccinate anyone by force. Only those\nwho are willing will be vaccinated.\n", "(h) There will be no lack of effort in treating the afflicted patients in\nour hospital under our special care and supervision, paying full respect to\nreligion, caste and the modesty (Purdah) of women. Let the wealthy run away!\nBut we are poor; we understand the heartache of the poor. The Mother of the\nUniverse is Herself the support of the helpless. The Mother is assuring us:\n\"Fear not! Fear not!\"\n", "7.  Brother,  if  there  is  no one to help you, then send information\nimmediately to the servants of Shri Bhagavan Ramakrishna at Belur Math.\nThere will be no dearth of help that is physically possible. By the grace of\nthe Mother, monetary help will also be possible.\n", "\u2014 N. B. In order to remove the fear of the epidemic, you should sing N\u00e2ma\nSankirtanam [the name of the Lord] every evening and in every locality.\n"]

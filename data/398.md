["In answer to a few questions put by Sister Nivedita, Swamiji jotted down the replies on May 24, 1900 at San Francisco.\n", "\n", "VI\n", "IN ANSWER TO NIVEDITA\n", "\nQ.\u2014I cannot remember what parts Prithvi Rai and Ch\u00e2nd disguised themselves\nto play, when they determined to attend the Svayamvara at Kanauj.\n", "A.\u2014Both went as minstrels.\n", "\nQ.\u2014Also did Prithvi Rai determine to marry Samyukt\u00e2 partly because she was\nthe daughter of his rival and partly for the fame of her great beauty? Did\nhe then send a woman-servant to obtain the post of her maid? And did this\nold nurse set herself to make the princess fall in love with Prithvi Rai?\n", "A.\u2014They had fallen in love with each other, hearing deeds and beauty and\nseeing portraits. Falling in love through portraits is an old Indian game.\n", "\nQ.\u2014How did Krishna come to be brought up amongst the shepherds?\n", "A.\u2014His father had to flee with the baby to save it from the tyrant Kamsa,\nwho ordered all the babes (male) from that year to be killed, as (through\nprophecy) he was afraid one of them would be Krishna and dethrone him. He\nkept Krishna's father and mother in prison (who were his cousins) for fear\nof that prophecy.\n", "\nQ.\u2014How did this part of his life terminate?\n", "A.\u2014He came with his brother Baladeva and Nanda, his foster-father, invited\nby the tyrant to a festival. (The tyrant had plotted his destruction.) He\nkilled the tyrant and instead of taking the throne placed the nearest heir\non it. Himself he never took any fruit of action.\n", "\nQ.\u2014Can you give me any dramatic incident of this period?\n", "A.\u2014This period is full of miracles. He as a baby was once naughty and the\ncowherd-mother tried to tie him with her churning string and found she could\nnot bind him with all the strings she had. Then her eyes opened and she saw\nthat she was going to bind him who had the whole universe in his body. She\nbegan to pray and tremble. Immediately the Lord touched her with his Maya\nand she saw only the child.\n", "Brahm\u00e2, the chief of gods, disbelieving that the Lord had become a cowherd,\nstole one day all the cows and cowherd boys and put them to sleep in a cave.\nWhen he came back, he found the same boys and cows round Krishna. Again he\nstole the new lot and hid them away. He came back and saw there the same\nagain. Then his eyes opened and began to see numerous worlds and heavens and\nBrahmans by the thousands, one greater than the preceding, in the body of\nthe Lord.\n", "He danced on the serpent K\u00e2liya who had been poisoning the water of the\nYamun\u00e2, and he held up the mount Govardhana in defiance of Indra whose\nworship he had forbidden and who in revenge wanted to kill all the people of\nVraja by deluge of rain. They were all sheltered by Krishna under the hill\nGovardhana which he upheld with a finger on their head.\n", "He  from  his  childhood  was against snake-worship and Indra-worship.\nIndra-worship is a Vedic ritual. Throughout the Gita he is not favourable to\nVedic ritual.\n", "This is the period of his love to Gopis. He was eleven years of age.\n"]

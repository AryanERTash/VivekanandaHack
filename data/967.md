["\n", "CXX\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Shivananda\n", "JAIPUR,\n", "27th December, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR SHIVANANDA,\n", "Mr. Setlur of Girgaon, Bombay, whom you know very well from Madras writes to\nme to send somebody to Africa to look after the religious needs of the\nIndian emigrants in Africa. He will of course send the man and bear all\nexpenses.\n", "The work will not be congenial at present, I am afraid, but it is really the\nwork for a perfect man. You know the emigrants are not liked at all by the\nwhite people there. To look after the Indians, and at the same time maintain\ncool \u2014 headedness so as not to create more strife \u2014 is the work there. No\nimmediate result can be expected, but in the long run it will prove a more\nbeneficial work for India than any yet attempted. I wish you to try your\nluck in this. If you agree, please write to Setlur, about your willingness\nand ask for more information, mentioning this letter. And godspeed to you! I\nam not very well, but am going to Calcutta in a few days and will be all\nright.\n", "Yours in the Lord,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

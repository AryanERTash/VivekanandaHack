["\n", "XIII\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "DETROIT\n", "16 March 1894\n", "DEAR MOTHER,\n", "Since my last, there has been nothing of interest here. Except that Mr.\nPalmer is a very hearty, jolly, good old man and very rich. He has been\nuniformly kind to me. Tomorrow I go back to Mrs. Bagley's because I am\nafraid she is rather uneasy at my long stay here. I am shrewd enough to know\nthat in every country in general, and America in particular, \"she\" is the\nreal operator at the nose string.\n", "I am going to lecture here on Monday[6]* and in two places near the town on\nTuesday and Wednesday.[7]* I do not remember the lady you refer me to,[8]*\nand she is in Lynn; what is Lynn, where on the globe its position is \u2014 I do\nnot know.[9]* I want to go to Boston. What good would it do me by stopping\nat Lynn? Kindly give me a more particular idea. Nor could I read the name of\nthe lady at whose house you say I met the lady. However, I am in no way very\nanxious. I am taking life very easy in my natural way. I have no particular\nwish to go anywhere, Boston or no Boston. I am just in a nice come-what-may\nmood. Something should turn up, bad or good. I have enough now to pay my\npassage back and a little sight-seeing to boot. As to my plans of work, I am\nfully convinced that at the rate it is progressing I will have to come back\nfour or five times to put it in any shape.\n", "As to informing others and doing good that way, I have failed to persuade\nmyself that I have really anything to convey to the world. So I am very\nhappy just now and quite at my ease. With almost nobody in this vast house\nand a cigar between my lips, I am dreaming just now and philosophising upon\nthat work fever which was upon me. It is all nonsense. I am nothing, the\nworld is nothing, the Lord alone is the only worker. We are simply tools in\nHis hands etc., etc., etc. Have you got the Alaska information? If so,\nkindly send it to me c/o Mrs. Bagley.\n", "Are you coming to the East this summer? With eternal gratitude and love,\n", "Your son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "XXVI\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "GREENACRE INN\n", "ELIOT, MAINE\n", "5 August 1894\n", "DEAR MOTHER\n", "I have received your letter and am very much ashamed at my bad memory. I\nunfortunately forgot all about the cheque. Perhaps you have come to know by\nthis time of my being in Greenacre. I had a very nice time here and am\nenjoying it immensely. In the fall I am going to lecture in Brooklyn, New\nYork. Yesterday I got news that they have completed all the advertising\nthere. I have an invitation today from a friend in New York to go with him\nto some mountains north of this state of Maine. I do not know whether I will\ngo or not. I am doing pretty well. Between lecturing, teaching, picnicking\nand other excitements the time is flying rapidly. I hope you are doing very\nwell and that Father Pope is in good trim. It is a very beautiful spot \u2014\nthis Greenacre \u2014 and [I] have very nice company from Boston: Dr. Everett\nHale,[6]* you know, of Boston, and Mrs. Ole Bull, of Cambridge. I do not\nknow whether I will accept the invitation of my friend of New York or not.\n", "So far only this is sure, that I will go to lecture in New York this coming\nfall. And Boston, of course, is a good field. The people here are mostly\nfrom Boston and they all like me very much. Are you having a good time, and\nFather Pope? Has your house-painting been finished? The Babies, I am sure,\nare enjoying their Mudville.\n", "I am in no difficulty for money. I have plenty to eat and drink.\n", "With my best love and gratitude to you and Father Pope and the Babies.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "Excuse this hasty scrawl. The pen is very bad.\n", "V.\n", "The Harrison people sent me two \"nasty standing\" photos \u2014 that is all I have\nout of them, when they ought to give me 40 minus the 10 or 15 I have got\nalready!!!\n", "V.\n"]

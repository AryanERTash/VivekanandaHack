["\n", "CL\n", "\n", "12th Dec., 1899.\n", "MY DEAR MRS. BULL,\n", "You are perfectly right; I am brutal, very indeed. But about the tenderness\netc., that is my fault. I wish I had less, much less of that \u2014 that is my\nweakness  \u2014 and alas! all my sufferings have come from that. Well, the\nmunicipality is trying to tax us out \u2014 good; that is my fault as I did not\nmake the Math public property by a deed of trust. I am very sorry I use\nharsh language to my boys, but they also know I love them more than anybody\nelse on earth. I may have had Divine help \u2014 true; but oh, the pound of blood\nevery bit of Divine help has been to me!! I would be gladder and a better\nman without that. The present looks very gloomy indeed; but I am a fighter\nand must die fighting, not give way \u2014 that is why I get crazy at the boys. I\ndon't ask them to fight, but not to hinder my fight.\n", "I don't grudge my fate. But oh! now I want a man, one of my boys, to stand\nby me and fight against all odds! Don't you vex yourself; if anything is to\nbe done in India, my presence is necessary; and I am much better in health;\npossibly the sea will make me better. Anyway I did not do anything this time\nin America except bother my friends. Possibly Joe will help me out with the\npassage, and I have some money with Mr. Leggett. I have hopes of collecting\nsome money in India yet. I did not see any of my friends in different parts\nof India. I have hope of collecting the fifteen thousand that will make up\nthe fifty thousand, and a deed of trust will bring down the municipal taxes.\nIf I cannot collect that \u2014 it is better to struggle and die for it than\nvegetate here in America. My mistakes have been great; but everyone of them\nwas from too much love. How I hate love! Would I never had any Bhakti!\nIndeed, I wish I could be an Advaitist, calm and heartless. Well, this life\nis done. I will try in the next. I am sorry, especially now, that I have\ndone more injury to my friends than there have been blessings on them. The\npeace, the quiet I am seeking, I never found.\n", "I  went  years ago to the Himalayas, never to come back; and my sister\ncommitted suicide, the news reached me there, and that weak heart flung me\noff from that prospect of peace! It is the weak heart that has driven me out\nof India to seek some help for those I love, and here I am! Peace have I\nsought, but the heart, that seat of Bhakti, would not allow me to find it.\nStruggle and torture, torture and struggle. Well, be it then. since it is my\nfate, and the quicker it is over, the better. They say I am impulsive, but\nlook at the circumstances!!! I am sorry I have been the cause of pain to\nyou, to you above all, who love me so much, who have been so, so kind. But\nit  is done \u2014 was a fact. I am now going to cut the knot or die in the\nattempt.\n", "Ever your son,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. As Mother wants it, so let it be. I am going to beg of Joe a passage via\nSan Francisco to India. If she gives it, I start immediately via Japan. It\nwould take a month. In India, I think, I can raise some money to keep things\nstraight or on a better footing \u2014 at least to leave things where I get them\nall muddled. The end is getting very dark and very much muddled; well, I\nexpected it so. Don't think I give in in a moment. Lord bless you; if the\nLord has made me His hack to work and die on the streets, let Him have it. I\nam more cheerful just now after your letter than I was for years \u2014 Wah Guru\nki Fateh! Victory unto the Guru!! Yes, let the world come, the hells come,\nthe gods come, let Mother come, I fight and do not give in. R\u00e2vana got his\nrelease in three births by fighting the Lord Himself! It is glorious to\nfight Mother.\n", "All blessings on you and yours. You have done for me more, much more, than I\ndeserved ever.\n", "Love to Christine and Turiyananda.\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

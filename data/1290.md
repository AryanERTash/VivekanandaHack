["\n", "MACROCOSM AND MICROCOSM\n", "(The Life of Swami Vivekananda, Vol. I. p. 250.)\n", "After his experience of the macrocosm within the microcosm while absorbed in\nmeditation under the peepul tree at Kakrighat, in 1890, Swami Vivekananda\njotted down in Bengali fragments of his realization in his notebook.\n", "In the beginning was the Word etc.\n", "The microcosm and the macrocosm are built on the same plan. Just as the\nindividual soul is encased in the living body, so is the universal Soul in\nthe Living Prakriti [Nature] \u2014 the objective universe. Shiv\u00e2 [i.e. K\u00e2li] is\nembracing Shiva: this is not a fancy. This covering of the one [Soul] by the\nother [Nature] is analogous to the relation between an idea and the word\nexpressing  it:  they are one and the same; and it is only by a mental\nabstraction that one can distinguish them. Thought is impossible without\nwords. Therefore, in the beginning was the Word etc.\n", "This dual aspect of the Universal Soul is eternal. So what we perceive or\nfeel is this combination of the Eternally Formed and the Eternally Formless.\n"]

["\n", "XCII\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "ALMORA,\n", "20th May, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "From your letter I got all the important news. I got a letter from Sudhir\nalso and also one from Master Mahashay. I have also got two letters from\nNityananda (Yogen Chatterjee) from the famine areas.\n", "Even now money is floating on the waters, as it were, . . . but it will\nsurely  come.  When  it comes, buildings, land, and a permanent fund \u2014\neverything will come all right. But one can never rest assured until the\nchickens are hatched; and I am not now going down to the hot plains within\ntwo or three months. After that I shall make a tour and shall certainly\nsecure  some money. This being so, if you think that the [land with a]\nfrontage of eight K\u00e2th\u00e2s cannot be acquired . . ., there is no harm in\npaying the earnest money to the middle-man vendor as though you were losing\nit for nothing. In all these matters use your own discretion; I cannot give\nany further advice. There is particularly a chance of making mistake through\nhurry. . . . Tell Master Mahashay that I quite approve of what he had said.\n", "Write to Gangadhar that if he finds it difficult to get alms etc. there, he\nshould feed himself by spending from his own pocket, and that he should\npublish a weekly letter in Upen's paper (The Basumati). In that case others\nalso may help.\n", "I understand from a letter of Shashi . . . he wants Nirbhayananda. If you\nthink this course to be the best, then send Nirbhayananda and bring back\nGupta. . . . Send Sashi a copy of the Bengali Rules and Regulations of the\nMath or an English version of it, and write to him to see that the work\nthere is done in accordance with the Rules and Regulations.\n", "I am glad to learn that the Association in Calcutta is going on nicely. It\ndoes not matter if one or two keep out. Gradually everyone will come. Be\nfriendly and sympathetic with everybody. Sweet words are heard afar; it is\nparticularly necessary to try and make new people come. We want more and\nmore new members.\n", "Yogen is doing well. On account of the great heat in Almora, I am now in an\nexcellent garden twenty miles from there. This place is comparatively cool,\nbut still warm. The heat does not seem to be particularly less than that of\nCalcutta. . . .\n", "The feverishness is all gone. I am trying to go to a still cooler place.\nHeat or the fatigue of walking, I find, at once produces trouble of the\nliver. The air here is so dry that there is a burning sensation in the nose\nall the time, and the tongue becomes, as it were, a chip of wood. You have\nstopped criticising; otherwise I would have gone to a colder place by this\ntime just for the fun of it. \"He constantly neglects diet restrictions\" \u2014\nwhat rot do you talk? Do you really listen to the words of these fools? It\nis just like your not allowing me to take Kal\u00e2i-d\u00e2l (black pulses), because\nit contains starch! And what is more \u2014 there will be no starch if rice and\nRoti (bread) are eaten after frying them! What wonderful knowledge, my dear.\nThe fact of the matter is my old nature is coming back \u2014 this I am seeing\nclearly. In this part of the country now, an illness takes on the colour and\nfashion of this locality; and in that part of the country, it takes on the\ncolour and fashion of the illnesses in that locality. I am thinking of\nmaking my meals at night very light; I shall eat to the full in the morning\nand at noon; at night milk, fruits, etc. That is why I am staying in this\norchard, \"in expectation of fruits\"! Don't you see?\n", "Now don't be alarmed. Does a companion of Shiva die so quickly? Just now the\nevening lamp has been lighted, and singing has to be done throughout the\nwhole night. Nowadays my temper also is not very irritable, and feverishness\nis all due to the liver \u2014 I see this clearly. Well, I shall make that also\ncome under control \u2014 what fear? . . . Bravely brace yourself up and do work;\nlet us create a mighty commotion.\n", "Tender my love to all at the Math. At the next meeting of the Association\ngive my greetings to everybody and tell them that though I am not physically\npresent there, yet my spirit is where the name of our Lord is sung \u2014 \"\ngoes the round on the earth\" \u2014 because, you see, the Atman is omnipresent.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

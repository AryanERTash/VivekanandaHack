["\n", "LXV\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "C/O MISS DUTCHER\n", "THOUSAND ISLAND PARK\n", "30th August [July] '95\n", "DEAR MOTHER,\n", "I  was starting for Chicago, Thursday next [August 1], but your letter\nstopped me. The letter and the package have safely arrived.\n", "Write to me or wire if you want me to come to Chicago. I will then start for\nChicago next week, i.e. on Tuesday next [August 6]. I thought Sister Mary\nwas at home. When are the other babies coming? My going to Europe is not yet\nsettled finally. The babies have not written me a line \u2014 not one of them.\n", "Oh, Mother, my heart is so, so sad. The letters bring the news of the death\nof Dewanji. Haridas Viharidas has left the body. He was as a father to me.\nPoor man, he was the last 5 years seeking the retirement from business life,\nand at last he got it but could not enjoy it long. I pray that he may never\ncome back again to this dirty hole they call the Earth. Neither may he be\nborn in heaven or any other horrid place. May he never again wear a body \u2014\ngood  or bad, thick or thin. What a humbug and illusion this world is,\nMother, what a mockery this life. I pray constantly that all mankind will\ncome to know the reality, i.e. God, and this \"Shop\" here be closed for ever.\n", "My heart is too full to write more. Write to me or wire if you like.\n", "Your ever obedient Son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "P.S. We will think of the coming package [from the Maharaja of Khetri] in\nChicago. How long will you be in Chicago? If it is only a week or so, I need\nnot come. I will meet you in New York. If more than that, I come to see you.\n", "Yours,\n", "V.\n"]

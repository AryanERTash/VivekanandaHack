["\n", "X\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "DETROIT,\n", "20 February 1894.\n", "DEAR MOTHER\n", "My lectures here are over. I have made some very good friends here, amongst\nthem Mr. Palmer,[6]* President of the late World's Fair. I am thoroughly\ndisgusted with this Slayton[7]* business and am trying hard to break loose.\nI have lost at least $5,000 by joining this man. Hope you are all well. Mrs.\nBagley and her daughters are very kind to me. I hope to do some private\nlecturing here and then go to Ada and then back to Chicago. It is snowing\nhere this morning. They are very nice people here, and the different clubs\ntook a good deal of interest in me.\n", "It is rather wearisome, these constant receptions and dinners; and their\nhorrible dinners \u2014 a hundred dinners concentrated into one \u2014 and when in a\nman's club, why, smoking on between the courses and then beginning afresh. I\nthought  the  Chinese  alone make a dinner run through half a day with\nintervals of smoking!!\n", "However,they are very gentlemanly men and, strange to say, an Episcopal\nclergyman[8]* and a Jewish rabbi[9]* take great interest in me and eulogize\nme.  Now  the man who got up the lectures here got at least a thousand\ndollars.  So  in every place. And this is Slayton's duty to do for me.\nInstead, he, the liar, had told me often that he has agents everywhere and\nwould advertise and do all that for me. And this is what he is doing. His\nwill be done. I am going home. Seeing the liking the American people have\nfor  me, I could have, by this time, got a pretty large sum. But Jimmy\nMills[10]* and Slayton were sent by the Lord to stand in the way. His ways\nare inscrutable.\n", "However, this is a secret. President Palmer has gone to Chicago to try to\nget me loose from this liar of a Slayton. Pray that he may succeed. Several\njudges here have seen my contract, and they say it is a shameful fraud and\ncan be broken any moment; but I am a monk \u2014 no self-defence. Therefore, I\nhad better throw up the whole thing and go to India.\n", "My love to Harriets, Mary, Isabelle, Mother Temple, Mr. Matthews, Father\nPope and you all.[11]*\n", "Yours obediently,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "CXIV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "LAHORE,\n", "11th November, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "The lecture at Lahore is over somehow. I shall start for Dehra Dun in a day\nor two. I have now postponed my tour to Sind, as none of you are agreeable\nto it, and also because of various other obstacles. Somebody has opened my\ntwo letters from England on the way. So don't send me letters any further\nfor the present. Send them after I have written for them from Khetri. If you\ngo to Orissa, then make arrangements that some one will do all the work as\nyour representative \u2014 say Hari, especially now, when I am daily expecting\nletters from America.\n", "Perhaps the will that I asked you to make in favour of Hari and Sharat has\nnow been made.\n", "Probably  I shall leave Sadananda and Sudhir here after establishing a\nSociety. Now no more lecturing \u2014 I go in a hurry straight to Rajputana.\n", "The establishment of the Math must have precedence over everything.\n", "Without regular exercise the body does not keep fit; talking, talking all\nthe time brings illness \u2014 know this for certain. My love to all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

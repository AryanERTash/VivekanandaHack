["\n", "CXXXI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "\n", "ALMORA,\n", "24th July, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR AKHANDANANDA,\n", "I am very glad to receive your letter and go through the contents. Your\nwishes  about  the  orphanage  are  very  good  and Shri Maharaj (Shri\nRamakrishna.) will not fail to fulfil them at an early date. Try your best\nto found a permanent centre. ... Never worry about money. Tomorrow I shall\nleave Almora for the plains; and wherever there will be made some stir, I\nshall open a subscription list for famine \u2014 set your mind easy on that\nscore. When in every district there will be a Math on the model of our Math\nin Calcutta, then will my heart's desire be fulfilled. Let not the work of\npreaching, too, be at a standstill, and greater even than preaching, is the\nwork of imparting education. By means of lectures and the like, the village\npeople must be taught religion, history, and such other subjects \u2014 specially\nhistory. To help our educational work there is a Society in England, which,\nas I find from reports, is doing excellent work. In time we shall get help\nof this kind from everywhere, don't be frightened. They only do work who\nthink that help will come, directly they are on the field of work.\n", "All strength is in you, have faith in it. It will not go unmanifested.\nAccept my heartiest love and blessings, and convey them to the Brahmach\u00e2rin.\nWrite now and then fiery letters to the Math, so that all may take heart and\nwork. Victory to the Guru!\n", "Yours affly.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

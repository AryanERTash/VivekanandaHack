I do not\nknow when I am going back to India. It is better to leave everything in the\nhands of Him who is at my back directing me. Try to work without me, as if I\nnever existed. Do not wait for anybody or anything. Do whatever you can.\nBuild your hope on none. Before writing about myself, I will tell you about\nNarasimha. He has proved a complete failure. . . . However he wrote to me\nfor help in the last stage, and I will try to help him as much as is in my\npower. Meanwhile you tell his people to send money as soon as they can for\nhim to go over. . . . He is in distress. Of course I will see that he does\nnot starve.\n", "\nI  have  done  a good deal of lecturing here.... The expenses here are\nterrible; money has to fly, although I have been almost always taken care of\neverywhere by the nicest and the highest families.\n", "\nI do not know whether I shall go away this summer or not. Most probably not.\nIn the meantime try to organise and push on our plans. Believe you can do\neverything. Know that the Lord is with us, and so, onward, brave souls!\n", "\nI  have  had enough appreciation in my own country. Appreciation or no\nappreciation, sleep not, slacken not. You must remember that not a bit even\nof our plans has been as yet carried out.\n", "\nAct on the educated young men, bring them together, and organise them. Great\nthings can be done by great sacrifices only. No selfishness, no name, no\nfame, yours or mine, nor my Master's even! Work, work the idea, the plan, my\nboys, my brave, noble, good souls \u2014 to the wheel, to the wheel put your\nshoulders! Stop not to look back for name, or fame, or any such nonsense.\nThrow self overboard and work. Remember, \"The grass when made into a rope by\nbeing joined together can even chain a mad elephant.\" The Lord's blessings\non you all! His power be in you all\u2014 as I believe it is already. \"Wake up,\nstop not until the goal is reached\", say the Vedas. Up, up, the long night\nis passing, the day is approaching, the wave has risen, nothing will be able\nto resist its tidal fury. The spirit, my boys, the spirit; the love, my\nchildren, the love; the faith, the belief; and fear not! The greatest sin is\nfear.\n", "\nMy blessings on all. Tell all the noble souls in Madras who have helped our\ncause that I send them my eternal love and gratitude, but I beg of them not\nto slacken. Throw the idea broadcast. Do not be proud; do not insist upon\nanything dogmatic; do not go against anything \u2014 ours is to put chemicals\ntogether, the Lord knows how and when the crystal will form. Above all, be\nnot inflated with my success or yours. Great works are to be done; what is\nthis small success in comparison with what is to come? Believe, believe, the\ndecree has gone forth, the fiat of the Lord has gone forth \u2014 India must\nrise, the masses and the poor are to be made happy. Rejoice that you are the\nchosen instruments in His hands. The flood of spirituality has risen. I see\nit is rolling over the land resistless, boundless, all-absorbing. Every man\nto the fore, every good will be added to its forces, every hand will smooth\nits way, and glory be unto the Lord! . . .\n", "\nI do not require any help. Try to get up a fund, buy some magic-lanterns,\nmaps, globes, etc., and some chemicals. Get every evening a crowd of the\npoor and low, even the Pariahs, and lecture to them about religion first,\nand then teach them through the magic-lantern and other things, astronomy,\ngeography, etc., in the dialect of the people. Train up a band of fiery\nyoung men. Put your fire in them and gradually increase the organization,\nletting it widen and widen its circle. Do the best you can, do not wait to\ncross the river when the water has all run down. Printing magazines, papers,\netc., are good, no doubt, but actual work, my boys even if infinitesimal, is\nbetter  than  eternal  scribbling  and  talking.  Call  a  meeting  at\nBhattacharya's. Get a little money and buy those things I have just now\nstated, hire a hut, and go to work. Magazines are secondary, but this is\nprimary. You must have a hold on the masses. Do not be afraid of a small\nbeginning, great things come afterwards. Be courageous. Do not try to lead\nyour brethren, but serve them. The brutal mania for leading has sunk many a\ngreat ship in the waters of life. Take care especially of that, i.e. be\nunselfish even unto death, and work. I could not write all I was going to\nsay, but the Lord will give you all understanding, my brave boys. At it, my\nboys!

["\n", "IL\n", "\n", "U. S. A.,\nAugust 1895.\n", "\nBy the time this reaches you, dear Alasinga, I shall be in Paris. . . . I\nhave done a good deal of work this year and hope to do a good deal more in\nthe next. Don't bother about the missionaries. It is quite natural that they\nshould cry. Who does not when his bread is dwindling away? The missionary\nfunds have got a big gap the last two years, and it is on the increase.\nHowever, I wish the missionaries all success. So long as you have love for\nGod and Guru and faith in truth, nothing can hurt you, my son. But the loss\nof any of these is dangerous. You have remarked well; my ideas are going to\nwork in the West better than in India. . . . I have done more for India than\nIndia ever did for me. . . . I believe in truth, the Lord sends me workers\nby the scores wherever I go \u2014 and they are not like the . . . disciples\neither \u2014 they are ready to give up their lives for their Guru. Truth is my\nGod, the universe my country I do not believe in duty. Duty is the curse of\nthe Sams\u00e2ri (householder), not for the Sanny\u00e2sin. Duty is humbug. I am free,\nmy bonds are cut; what care I where this body goes or does not go. You have\nhelped  me well right along. The Lord will reward you. I sought praise\nneither from India nor from America, nor do I seek such bubbles. I have a\ntruth to teach, I, the child of God And He that gave me the truth will send\nme fellow workers from the earth's bravest and best. You Hindus will see in\na few years what the Lord does in the West. You are like the Jews of old \u2014\ndogs in the manger, who neither eat nor allow others to eat. You have no\nreligion your God is the kitchen, your Bible the cooking-pots. . . . You are\na few brave lads. . . . Hold on, boys, no cowards among my children. . . .\nAre great things ever done smoothly? Time, patience, and indomitable will\nmust show. I could have told you many things that would have made your heart\nleap, but I will not. I want iron wills and hearts that do not know how to\nquake. Hold on. The Lord bless you.\n", "\n", "Ever yours with blessings,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

["\n", "CLI\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "21 WEST 34TH STREET,\n", "NEW YORK,\n", "10th November 1899.\n", "MY DEAR CHRISTINA,\n", "I received your letter just now. I am now in New York. Dr. [Egbert] Guernsey\nanalysed my urine yesterday, and there was no sugar or albumen in it. So my\nkidneys are all right, at least at present. The heart is only nervous,\nrequires calming! \u2014 some cheerful company and good, loving friends and\nquiet. The only difficulty is the dyspepsia, and that is the evil. For\ninstance, I am all right in the morning and can walk miles, but in the\nevening it is impossible to walk after a meal \u2014 the gas \u2014 that depends\nentirely up on food, does it not? I ought to try the Battle Creek food. If I\ncome to Detroit, there will be quiet and Battle Creek food for me.\n", "But if you come to Cambridge with all the instructions of the Battle Creek\nfood, I will have it prepared there; or, between you and me, we will cook\nit. I am a good hand at that. You don't know a thing about cooking. Well,\nyou may help in cleaning the plates etc. I always get money when I need it\nbadly. \"Mother\" always sees to that. So, no danger on that head. I am not in\nthe least danger of life, the Doctors agree \u2014 only if this dyspepsia goes\naway. And that is \"food\", \"food\", \"food\", and no worry. Oh, what a worry I\nhave had! Say we go somewhere else and make a little party and keep house\nourselves. In Cambridge, Mrs. Bull has a quiet separate place \u2014 her studio\nhouse. You can have rooms there. I wish you to know Mrs. Bull. She is a\nsaint, a real saint, if ever there was one. Wait for my next letter. I will\nwrite today again, or tomorrow after seeing Mrs. Bull.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

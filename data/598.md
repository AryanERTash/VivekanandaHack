["\n", "CXXXVII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "MURREE,\n", "10th October, 1897.\n", "DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Reached Murree from Kashmir in the evening of the day before yesterday.\nEverybody had an enjoyable time of it, only Krishnalal (Dhirananda) and\nGupta (Sadananda) suffered now and then from fever, which, however, was but\nslight. This Address is to be sent to the Raja of Khetri. Have it printed in\ngilt etc. The Raja is expected at Bombay about the 21st or 22nd of October.\nNone of us is staying at Bombay at present \u2014 if there be any, send him a\ncopy so that he may present the same to the Raja even on Board the ship, or\nsomewhere in the city of Bombay. Send the superior copy to Khetri. Have this\npassed in a meeting and if any change is needed, no harm. Then sign it, all\nof you, only leaving a blank for my name, and I shall sign it on going to\nKhetri. Let no pains be spared in this.\n", ". . . Captain Sevier says he is very anxious for a site. He wishes to have a\nspot near Mussoorie or in some other central place, as soon as possible. ...\nThe thing is that we do not want a place which is too cold, at the same time\nit must not be too hot. Dehra Dun is unbearable in summer, but pleasant in\nwinter; Mussoorie itself is, I dare say, not the right place for many in\nwinter. Above or below it, that is, in British or Garhwal territory, some\nland is sure to be found. At the same time there must be a supply of water\nat the place throughout the year, for drinking purposes and for everyday\nuse. My plan is this: With only Achyutananda and Gupta I go from Murree to\nRawalpindi, thence to Jammu, thence to Lahore, and from Lahore straight to\nKarachi. ... Give my hearty love and blessings to Shashi Babu. I see that\nMaster Mahashaya has buckled to work after such a long time. Alive him my\nspecial love and greetings. To see him, with his feminine retiringness,\nstirred to work, my courage has gone up by leaps and bounds. I am writing to\nhim tomorrow even. Victory to the Lord! \u2014 To work! To work!\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

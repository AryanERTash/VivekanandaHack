["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "SATURDAY, July 20, 1895.\n", "Perception is our only real knowledge or religion. Talking about it for\nages  will never make us know our soul. There is no difference between\ntheories and atheism. In fact, the atheist is the truer man. Every step I\ntake in the light is mine for ever. When you go to a country and see it,\nthen it is yours. We have each to see for ourselves; teachers can only\n\"bring the food\", we must eat it to be nourished. Argument can never prove\nGod save as a logical conclusion.\n", "It is impossible to find God outside of ourselves. Our own souls contribute\nall the divinity that is outside of us. We are the greatest temple. The\nobjectification is only a faint imitation of what we see within ourselves.\n", "Concentration of the powers of the mind is our only instrument to help us\nsee God. If you know one soul (your own), you know all souls, past, present,\nand to come. The will concentrates the mind, certain things excite and\ncontrol  this  will,  such  as  reason, love, devotion, breathing. The\nconcentrated mind is a lamp that shows us every corner of the soul.\n", "No one method can suit all. These different methods are not steps necessary\nto be taken one after another. Ceremonials are the lowest form; next God\nexternal,  and after that God internal. In some cases gradation may be\nneeded, but in many only one way is required. It would be the height of\nfolly to say to everyone, \"You must pass through Karma and Bhakti before you\ncan reach Jnana.\"\n", "Stick to your reason until you reach something higher; and you will know it\nto  be  higher,  because it will not jar with reason. The stage beyond\nconsciousness is inspiration (Sam\u00e2dhi); but never mistake hysterical trances\nfor  the  real thing. It is a terrible thing to claim this inspiration\nfalsely, to mistake instinct for inspiration. There is no external test for\ninspiration, we know it ourselves; our guardian against mistake is negative\n\u2014 the voice of reason. All religion is going beyond reason, but reason is\nthe only guide to get there. Instinct is like ice, reason is the water, and\ninspiration  is  the  subtlest  form or vapour; one follows the other.\nEverywhere  is this eternal sequence \u2014 unconsciousness, consciousness,\nintelligence \u2014 matter, body, mind \u2014 and to us it seems as if the chain began\nwith the particular link we first lay hold of. Arguments on both sides are\nof equal weight, and both are true. We must reach beyond both, to where\nthere is neither the one nor the other. These successions are all Maya.\n", "Religion is above reason, supernatural. Faith is not belief, it is the grasp\non the Ultimate, an illumination. First hear, then reason and find out all\nthat reason can give about the Atman; let the flood of reason flow over It,\nthen take what remains. If nothing remains, thank God you have escaped a\nsuperstition. When you have determined that nothing can take away the Atman,\nthat It stands every test, hold fast to this and teach it to all. Truth\ncannot be partial; it is for the good of all. Finally, in perfect rest and\npeace meditate upon It, concentrate your mind upon It, make yourself one\nwith It. Then no speech is needed; silence will carry the truth. Do not\nspend your energy in talking, but meditate in silence; and do not let the\nrush of the outside world disturb you. When your mind is in the highest\nstate, you are unconscious of it. Accumulate power in silence and become a\ndynamo of spirituality. What can a beggar give? Only a king can give, and he\nonly when he wants nothing himself.\n", "Hold your money merely as custodian for what is God's. Have no attachment\nfor it. Let name and fame and money go; they are a terrible bondage. Feel\nthe wonderful atmosphere of freedom. You are free, free, free! Oh, blessed\nam I! Freedom am I! I am the Infinite! In my soul I can find no beginning\nand no end. All is my Self. Say this unceasingly.\n"]

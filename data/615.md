["\n", "CLIV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "CALIFORNIA,\n", "21st February, 1900.\n", "MY DEAR AKHANDANANDA,\n", "I am very glad to receive your letter and go through the details of news.\nLearning and wisdom are supersfluities, the surface glitter merely, but it\nis the heart that is the seat of all power. It is not in the brain but in\nthe heart that the Atman, possessed of knowledge, power, and activity, has\nIts  seat. \" \u0936\u0924\u0902 \u091a\u0948\u0915\u093e \u091a \u0939\u0943\u0926\u092f\u0938\u094d\u092f \u0928\u093e\u0921\u094d\u092f: \u2014 The nerves of the heart are a\nhundred and one\" etc. The chief nerve-centre near the heart, called the\nsympathetic ganglia, is where the Atman has Its citadel. The more heart you\nwill be able to manifest, the greater will be the victory you achieve. It is\nonly a few that understand the language of the brain, but everyone from the\nCreator down to a clump of grass, understands the language that comes from\nthe heart. But then, in our country, it is a case of rousing men that are,\nas it were, dead. It will take time, but if you have infinite patience and\nperseverance, success is bound to come. No mistake in that.\n", "How are the English officials to blame? Is the family, of whose unnatural\ncruelty you have written, an isolated one in India? Or, are there plenty of\nsuch? It is the same story all over the country. But then, it is not as a\nresult of pure wickedness that the selfishness commonly met with in our\ncountry has come. This bestial selfishness is the outcome of centuries of\nfailure and repression. It is not real selfishness, but deep-rooted despair.\nIt will be cored at the first inkling of success. It is only this that the\nEnglish officials are noticing all round, so how can they have faith at the\nvery outset? But tell me, do they not sympathise with any real work that\nthey meet with? . . .\n", "In these days of dire famine, flood, disease, and pestilence, tell me where\nyour Congressmen are. Will it do merely to say, \"Hand the government of the\ncountry over to us\"? And who is there to listen to them? If a man does work,\nhas he to open his mouth to ask for anything? If there be two thousand\npeople like you working in several districts, won't it be the turn of the\nEnglish  themselves  to  consult you in matters of political moment? \"\nA\u2014 was not allowed to open a centre, but what of that! Has not Kishengarh\nallowed it?\u2014 Let him work on without ever opening his lips; there is no use\nof either telling anything to anybody, or quarrelling with any. Whoever will\nassist in this work of the Divine Mother of the universe, will have Her\ngrace,   and   whoever   will   oppose   it   will   not   only  be  \"\nlaying the axe to his own prospects.  [154_akhandananda_04.jpg] \u2014 all in\ngood time. Many a little makes a mickle. When a great work is being done,\nwhen the foundations are laid or a road constructed, when superhuman energy\nis needed \u2014 it is one or two extraordinary men who silently and noiselessly\nwork through a world of obstacles and difficulties. When thousands of people\nare benefited, there is a great tomtoming, and the whole country is loud in\nnotes of praise. But then the machine has already been set agoing, and even\na boy can work it, or a fool add to it some impetus. Grasp this that, that\nbenefit done to a village or two, that orphanage with its twenty orphans,\nthose ten or twenty workers \u2014 all these are enough; they form the nucleus,\nnever to be destroyed. From these, hundreds of thousands of people will be\nbenefited in time. Now we want half a dozen lions, then excellent work will\nbe turned out by even hundreds of jackals. . .\n", "If orphan girls happen to come to your hands for shelter, you must take them\nin above all else. Otherwise, Christian missionaries will take them, poor\nthings, away! What matters it that you have no particular arrangements for\nthem? Through the Divine Mother's will, they will be provided for. When you\nget a horse, never you worry about the whip. ... Get together whomsoever you\ncan lay your hands on, no picking and choosing now \u2014 everything will be set\nright in course of time. In every attempt there are many obstacles to cope\nwith, but gradually the path becomes smooth.\n", "Convey to the European officer many thanks from me. Work on fearlessly \u2014\nthere  is a hero! Bravo! Thrice well done! The starting of a centre at\nBhagalpur that you have written about is no doubt a good idea \u2014 enlightening\nthe  schoolboys  and  things  of that sort. But our mission is for the\ndestitute, the poor, and the illiterate peasantry and labouring classes, and\nif, after everything has been done for them first, there is spare time, then\nonly for the gentry. Those peasants and labouring people will be won over by\nlove. Afterwards it will be they who will collect small sums and start\nmissions at their own villages, and gradually, from among those very men,\nteachers will spring.\n", "Teach some boys and girls of the peasant classes the rudiments of learning\nand infuse a number of ideas into their brains. Afterwards the peasants of\neach village will collect funds and have one of these in their village. \"\n\u2014 this holds good in all spheres. We help them to help themselves. That they\nare supplying you with your daily bread is a real bit of work done. The\nmoment  they  will come to understand their own condition and feel the\nnecessity of help and improvement, know that your work is taking effect and\nis in the right direction, while the little good that the moneyed classes,\nout of pity, do to the poor, does not last, and ultimately it does nothing\nbut  harm to both parties. The peasants and labouring classes are in a\nmoribund condition, so what is needed is that the moneyed people will only\nhelp  them  to regain their vitality, and nothing more. Then leave the\npeasants and labourers to look to their own problem, to grapple with and\nsolve it. But then you must rake care not to set up class-strife between the\npoor peasants, the labouring people, and wealthy classes. Make it a point\nnot to abuse the moneyed classes. \" [154_akhandananda_06.jpg] \u2014 The wise man\nshould achieve his own object.\"\n", "Victory to the Guru! Victory to the Mother of the Universe! What fear!\nOpportunity, remedy, and its application will present themselves. I do not\ncare about the result, well or ill. I shall be happy if only you do this\nmuch of work. Wordy warfares, texts and scriptures, doctrines and dogmas \u2014\nall these I am coming to loathe as poison in this my advanced age. Know this\nfor certain that he who will work will be the crown on my head. Useless\nbandying of words and making noise is taking away our time, is consuming our\nlife-energy, without pushing the cause of humanitarianism a step further.\n\u2014 Away with fear! Bravo! There is a hero indeed! May the blessed Guru be\nenthroned in your heart, and the Divine Mother guide your hands.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["XV*\n", "Salutation to Shri Ramakrishna!\n", "5th January, 1890.\n", "MY DEAR SIR, (Sj. Balaram Bose)\n", "I am very sorry to hear of your illness from your kind note. The gist of the\nletter I wrote to you about your change to Baidyanath was that it would be\nimpossible for a man of weak and extremely delicate physique like you to\nlive in that place unless you spent a good deal of money. If change be\nreally advisable for you, and if you have deferred it so long simply to\nselect a cheaper place and that sort of thing, it is certainly a matter of\nregret. . . . Baidyanath is excellent so far as the air is concerned, but\nthe water is not good, it upsets the stomach. I used to suffer from acidity\nevery day. I have already written you a letter; have you got it, or finding\nit a bearing letter, have you left it to its fate? In my opinion, if you\nhave to go away for a change, the sooner the better. But, pardon me, you\nhave a tendency to expect that everything should fit in exactly with your\nrequirements, but unfortunately, such a state of things is very rare in this\nworld. \"\u0906\u0924\u094d\u092e\u093e\u0928\u0902 \u0938\u0924\u0924\u0902 \u0930\u0915\u094d\u0937\u0947\u0924\u094d\u2014One must save oneself under any circumstances.\"\n\"Lord have mercy\", is all right, but He helps him who helps himself. If you\nsimply try to save your purse, will the Lord arrange the change for you by\ndrawing on His ancestral capital? If you think you have so much reliance on\nthe Lord, don't call in the doctor, please. . . . If that does not suit you,\nyou should go to Varanasi. I would have already left this place, but the\nlocal gentlemen would not give me leave to depart! . . . But let me repeat\nonce more, if change is actually decided upon, please do not hesitate out of\nmiserliness. That would be suicide. And not even God can save a suicide.\nPlease convey my compliments to Tulasi Babu and the rest.\n", "With best regards,\n", "Yours affectionately,\nVivekananda.\n"]

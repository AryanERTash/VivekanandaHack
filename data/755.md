["\n", "X\n", "CHICAGO,\n", "2nd October, 1893.\n", "DEAR ADHYAPAKJI (Prof. John Henry Wright),\n", "\nI do not know what you are thinking of my long silence. In the first place I\ndropped in on the Congress in the eleventh hour, and quite unprepared; and\nthat kept me very very busy for some time. Secondly, I was speaking almost\nevery day in the Congress and had no time to write; and last and greatest of\nall \u2014 my kind friend, I owe so much to you that it would have been an insult\nto your ahetuka (unselfish) friendship to have written you business-like\nletters in a hurry. The Congress is now over.\n", "Dear brother, I was so so afraid to stand before that great assembly of fine\nspeakers and thinkers from all over the world and speak; but the Lord gave\nme strength, and I almost every day heroically (?) faced the platform and\nthe audience. If I have done well, He gave me the strength for it; if I have\nmiserably failed \u2014 I knew that beforehand \u2014 for I am hopelessly ignorant.\n", "Your friend Prof. Bradley was very kind to me and he always cheered me on.\nAnd oh! everybody is so kind here to me who am nothing \u2014 that it is beyond\nmy power of expression. Glory unto Him in the highest in whose sight the\npoor ignorant monk from India is the same as the learned divines of this\nmighty land. And how the Lord is helping me every day of my life, brother\n\u2014 I sometimes wish for a life of [a] million million ages to serve Him\nthrough the work, dressed in rags and fed by charity.\n", "Oh, how I wished that you were here to see some of our sweet ones from India\n\u2014 the tender-hearted Buddhist Dharmapala, the orator Mazoomdar \u2014 and realise\nthat in that far-off and poor India there are hearts that beat in sympathy\nto yours, born and brought up in this mighty and great country.\n", "My eternal respects to your holy wife; and to your sweet children my eternal\nlove and blessings.\n", "Col. I am going to\nEvanston tomorrow and hope to see Prof. Bradley there.\n", "May He make us all more and more pure and holy so that we may live a perfect\nspiritual life even before throwing off this earthly body.\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "[The letter continues on a separate sheet of paper:]\n", "I am now going to be reconciled to my life here. All my life I have been\ntaking every circumstance as coming from Him and calmly adapting myself to\nit. At first in America I was almost out of my water. I was afraid I would\nhave to give up the accustomed way of being guided by the Lord and cater for\nmyself \u2014 and what a horrid piece of mischief and ingratitude was that. I now\nclearly see that He who was guiding me on the snow tops of the Himalayas and\nthe burning plains of India is here to help me and guide me. Glory unto Him\nin the highest. So I have calmly fallen into my old ways. Somebody or other\ngives me a shelter and food, somebody or other comes to ask me to speak\nabout Him, and I know He sends them and mine is to obey. And if He\ndoes not, I only would take for granted that He wants that I should lay\naside this three minutes' body of clay \u2014 and hope to lay it down gladly.\n", "We may or may not meet, brother.

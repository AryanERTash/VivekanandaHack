I got a letter from A__ stating that\nhe was going to leave New York. He thought Mrs. Bull and you have written\nlots against him to me. I wrote him back to be patient and wait, and that\nMrs. Bull and Miss MacLeod wrote only good things about him.\n", "Well, Joe Joe, you know my method in all these rows; to leave all rows\nalone! \"Mother\" sees to all such things. I could\ndo it today, but I am waiting to make it a thousand. I expect to make a\nthousand in Frisco by the end of this week. I will buy a draft on New York\nand send it or ask the bank the best way to do it.\n", "I  have plenty of letters from the Math and the Himalayan centre. This\nmorning came one from Swarupananda. Yesterday one from Mrs. Sevier.\n", "I told Mrs. Hansborough about the photos.\n", "You tell Mr. Leggett from me to do what is best about the Vedanta Society\nmatter. The only thing I see is that in every country we have to follow its\nown method. As such, if I were you, I would convene a meeting of all the\nmembers and sympathisers and ask them what they want to do. Whether they\nwant to organise or not, what sort of organisation they want if any, etc.\nBut Lordy, do it on your own hook. I am quits. Only if you think my presence\nwould be of any help I can come in fifteen days.\n", "I have finished my work here; only, out of San Francisco, Stockton is a\nlittle city I want to work a few days in; then I go East. I think I should\nrest now, although I can have $100 a week average in this city, all along.\nThis time I want to let upon New York the charge of the Light Brigade.\n", "With all love,\n", "Ever yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. If the workers are all averse to organising, do you think there is any\nbenefit in it? You know best. Do what you think best. I have a letter from\nMargot from Chicago. She asks some questions; I am going to reply.\n", "V.\n"]

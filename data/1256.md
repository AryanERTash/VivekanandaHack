["\n", "CCXII\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "THE MATH, BELUR,\n", "DIST. HOWRAH, BENGAL, INDIA,\n", "18th December 1901.\n", "DEAR CHRISTINE,\n", "I am much better, and the rest is doing me good. I have found out that lying\nin bed all the time gives me as much sleep as wanted and good digestion too.\nAlbumen and sugar vanish immediately [when] I begin taking rest.\n", "Mrs. Bull and Nivedita start for India from Marseilles today, and unless\nthey change their plan, [they] must be in India before this reaches you \u2014\ntwo weeks before.\n", "Herewith I send you four hundred and eighty dollars by cheque drawn on\nThomas  Cook  & Son, Broadway, New York. They have no branch office in\nDetroit. On receipt of this, you write to Thomas Cook & Son, Broadway, New\nYork, that you have got a cheque from India \u2014 mentioning the amount and\nnumber \u2014 drawn by Thomas Cook & Son on the firm of Thomas Cook & Son, and\nwant  to be advised as to how to cash it. Don't send the cheque ahead.\n(Excuse all these details. I feel you are a baby in business, though I am\nworse.) This is to pay your \"passage to India\"[6]* if you think fit to\naccept Mrs. Sevier's invitation. If you get leave and come, I am sure you\nwill find somebody who is coming to England, at least. Then from there,\nagain, somebody who is coming to Egypt. You come with them as far as Italy,\nthence direct on a boat to India.\n", "Second-class passage across the Atlantic is all right, but the second class\nfrom Italy to Bombay is rather bad. There are always a few rough men and\nfast women. There is money enough for travelling first class all through, if\nyou so like.\n", "The Mother will see to it, even as [She did when] this money came. Drop me a\nline as soon as you engage your passage \u2014better a week ahead; otherwise I\ndon't see how the letter can reach me. The vessel to India you get from\nLondon; and possibly a letter may reach me with the name of the vessel, etc.\nIn any case, however, you wire me as soon as you land and get into a good\nhotel.  You  will  find many persons to receive you \u2014 and me too, most\nprobably.\n", "In case, however, things take another turn and you cannot come, no matter.\nDo with the money just as it pleases you.\n", "It is very probable that after Miss [Josephine] MacLeod and Mrs. [Ole] Bull\nhave been through India, they are going home via Japan; and, of course, I am\ngoing with them. In that case I will be in California next fall.\n", "It will be a nice trip, and would it not be a fine tour round the world if\nyou get leave and come?\n", "Do just as the Mother opens the way for you, and do not worry.\n", "Yours with all love and blessings,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

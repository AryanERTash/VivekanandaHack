["\n", "CCXXVII\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "THE MATH, BELUR, DIST. HOWRAH,\n", "21st June 1902.\n", "MY DEAR CHRISTINE,\n", "You have not the least cause to be anxious. I am getting on anyhow and am\nquite strong. As to diet, I find I have to restrict myself and not follow\nthe prescription of my doctor to eat anything I like. The pills continue,\nhowever. Will you ask the boys if they can get \"Amalaki\" [Emblic myrobalan]\nfruits in the place now? We cannot get them in the plains now. They are\nrather sour and puckery eaten raw; but make marmalade of whole [ones] \u2014\ndelicious.

The joy of the\ndevotees of Shri Ramakrishna knows no bounds at enjoying his holy presence\nafter a long time. And the well-to-do among them are considering themselves\nblessed to cordially invite Swamiji to their own houses. This afternoon\nSwamiji had an invitation to the house of Srijut Priyanath Mukhopadhyaya, a\ndevotee of Shri Ramakrishna, at Rajballabhpara in Baghbazar. But by the preaching of this universal religion of Vedanta, a\nreligion which gives equal rights to acquire spirituality to men of all\ncreeds and all paths of religious practice, the civilised West would come to\nknow what a wonderful degree of spirituality once developed in India and how\nthat is still existing. In this way, if we have their real sympathy and regard, we\nwould learn from them the sciences bearing on our material life, thereby\nqualifying ourselves better for the struggle for existence. If you people believe that we shall in case of conflict with\nthem gain freedom by applying those material forces, you are profoundly\nmistaken. Just as a little piece of stone figures before the Himalayas, so\nwe differ from them in point of skill in the use of those forces. Do you\nknow what my idea is? By preaching the profound secrets of the Vedanta\nreligion in the Western world, we shall attract the sympathy and regard of\nthese mighty nations, maintaining for ever the position of their teacher in\nspiritual  matters,  and they will remain our teachers in all material\nconcerns. When there will grow a link of sympathy and regard between both\nnations by this give-and-take intercourse, there will be then no need for\nthese noisy cries. They will do everything of their own accord. I believe\nthat by this cultivation of religion and the wider diffusion of Vedanta,\nboth this country and the West will gain enormously. To me the pursuit of\npolitics is a secondary means in comparison with this. I will lay down my\nlife to carry out this belief practically. If you believe in any other way\nof accomplishing the good of India, well, you may go on working your own\nway.\"\n", "Narendra  Babu shortly left, expressing his unqualified agreement with\nSwamiji's  ideas. He\nwas dressed almost like a Sannyasin, if not fully so\u2014with a Geru\u00e2 turban\non the head; he was evidently an up-country Indian. At the announcement of\nthis preacher of cow-protection Swamiji came out to the parlour room. The\npreacher saluted Swamiji and presented him with a picture of the mother-cow.\nSwamiji  took that in his hand and, making it over to one standing by,\ncommenced the following conversation with the preacher:\n", "Swamiji: What is the object of your society\u00a0?\n", "Preacher: We protect the mother-cows of our country from the hands of the\nbutcher.  Cow-infirmaries  have  been founded in some places where the\ndiseased,  decrepit  mother-cows or those bought from the butchers are\nprovided for.\n", "Swamiji: That is very good indeed. What is the source of your income?\n", "Preacher: The work of the society is carried on only by gifts kindly made by\ngreat men like you.\n", "Swamiji: What amount of money have you now laid by?\n", "Preacher: The Marwari traders' community are the special supporters of this\nwork.

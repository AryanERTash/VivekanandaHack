["\n", "XXIV\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "C/O DR. E. GUERNSEY\n", "CEDAR LAWN, FISHKILL ON THE HUDSON\n", "19 July 1894\n", "DEAR MOTHER,\n", "Your kind note reached me here yesterday evening. I am so glad to hear the\nbabies are enjoying. I got the Interior and am very glad to see my friend\nMazoomdar\u2019s (Pratap Chandra Mazumdar.) book spoken of so highly. Mazoomdar\nis a great and a good man and has done much for his fellow beings.\n", "It is a lovely summer place, this Cedar Lawn of the Guernseys. Miss Guernsey\nhas gone on a visit to Swampscott. I had also an invitation there, but I\nthought [it] better to stay here in the calm and silent place full of trees\nand with the beautiful Hudson flowing by and mountain in the background.\n", "I am very thankful for Miss Howe\u2019s suggestion, and I am also thinking of it.\nMost probably I will go to England very soon. But between you and me, I am a\nsort of mystic and cannot move without orders, and that has not come yet.\nMr. [Charles M.] Higgins, a rich young lawyer and inventor of Brooklyn, is\narranging some lectures for me. I have not settled whether I will stop for\nthem or not.\n", "My eternal thanks to you for your kindness. My whole life cannot repay my\ndebt to you. (Original letter: your debt.) You may see from the letter from\nMadras that there is not a word about Narasimha. What can I do more? I did\nnot get the cheque cashed yet, for there was no necessity. Miss Phillips was\nvery kind to me. She is an old lady, about 50 or more. You need not feel any\nworry  about my being taken care of. The Lord always takes care of His\nservants; and so long as I am really His servant and not the world\u2019s, I am\nvery  confident  of  getting everything that would be good for me. The\nGuernseys love me very much, and there are many families in New York and\nBrooklyn who would take the best care of me.\n", "I had a beautiful letter from Mr. Snell,[6]* saying that a sudden change for\nthe better has taken place in his fortunes and offering me thrice the money\nI lent him as a contribution to my work. And he also has beautiful letters\nfrom Dharmapala and others from India. But, of course, I politely refused\nhis repayment.\n", "So far so good. I have seen Mr. [Walter Hines] Page, the editor of the Forum\nhere. He was so sorry not to get the article on missionaries. But I have\npromised to write on other interesting subjects. Hope I will have patience\nto do so.\n", "I had a letter yesterday from Miss Harriet, (Mrs. Hale\u2019s daughter.) from\nwhich I learn that they are enjoying Kenosha (A port in southwest Wisconsin,\non Lake Michigan.) very much. Lord bless you and yours, Mother Church, for\never and ever. I cannot even express my gratitude to you.\n", "As for me, you need not be troubled in the least. My whole life is that of a\nvagabond \u2014 homeless, roving tramp; any fare, good or bad, in any country, is\ngood enough for me.\n", "Yours ever in love and obedience,\n", "SWAMI VIVEKANANDA\n"]

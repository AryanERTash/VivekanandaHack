["\n", "XCIX\n", "\n", "63 ST. GEORGE'S ROAD, LONDON S.W.,\n", "5th June, 1896.\n", "DEAR MRS. BULL,\n", "The Raja-Yoga book is going on splendidly. Saradananda goes to the States\nsoon.\n", "I do not like any one whom I love to become a lawyer, although my father was\none. My Master was against it, and I believe that that family is sure to\ncome to grief where there are several lawyers. Our country is full of them;\nthe universities turn them out by the hundreds. What my nation wants is\npluck and scientific genius. So I want Mohin to be an electrician. Even if\nhe fails in life, still I will have the satisfaction that he strove to\nbecome great and really useful to his country. ... In America alone there is\nthat something in the air which brings out whatever is best in every one.\n... I want him to be daring, bold, and to struggle to cut a new path for\nhimself and his nation. An electrical engineer can make a living in India.\n", "Yours with love,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Goodwin is writing to you this mail with reference to a magazine in\nAmerica.  I  think something of the sort is necessary to keep the work\ntogether, and shall of course do all that I can to help it on in the line he\nsuggests.   . . . I think it very probable that he will come over with\nSaradananda.\n"]

Indeed, one should pay homage to such a\nman. If you happen to know this man, please\nask him to bless me, so that even a dog or a jackal may be my Guru \u2014 not to\nspeak of a great soul like Pavhari Baba.\n", "I have many things to learn. My master used to say: \"As long as I live, so\nlong do I learn\". Also please tell this fellow that unfortunately I do not\nhave the time to \"cross the seven seas and thirteen rivers\" or to go to Sri\nLanka in order to sleep after having put oil in the nostrils.[6]*\n", "Your servant,\n", "NARENDRA\n", "P.S. Please have the rose-water brought from Ishan Babu's [Ishan Chandra\nMukherjee's]  residence  if there is delay [in their sending it to the\nBaranagore Math]. The roses are still not in bloom.

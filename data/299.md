["\n", "LXIV\n", "\n", "\nDear Alasinga,\n", "\nSince writing to you a few days ago I have found my way to let you know that\nI am in a position to do this for the Brahmavadin. I will give you Rs. 100 a\nmonth for a year or two, i.e. \u00a360 or \u00a370 a year, i.e. as much as would cover\nRs. 100 a month. That will set you free to work for the Brahmavadin and make\nit a better success. Mr. Mani Iyer and a few friends can help in raising\nfund  that  would  cover  the  printing  etc.  What is the income from\nsubscription? Can these be employed to pay the contributors and get a fine\nseries of articles? It is not necessary that everybody should understand all\nthat  is written in the Brahmavadin, but that they must subscribe from\npatriotism and good Karma \u2014 the Hindus I mean.\n", "\nSeveral things are necessary. First there should be strict integrity. Not\nthat I even hint that any of you would digress from it, but the Hindus have\na  peculiar  slovenliness  in business matters, not being sufficiently\nmethodical and strict in keeping accounts etc.\n", "\nSecondly, entire devotion to the cause, knowing that your salvation depends\nupon making the Brahmavadin a success. Let this paper be your Ishtadevata,\nand then you will see how success comes. I have already sent for Abhed\u00e2nanda\nfrom India. I hope there will be no delay with him as it was with the other\nSwami. On receipt of this letter you send me a clear account of all the\nincome and the expenses of the Brahmavadin so that I may judge from it what\nbest can be done. Remember that perfect purity, disinterestedness, and\nobedience to the Guru are the secret of all success. . . .\n", "\nA big foreign circulation of a religious paper is impossible. It must be\nsupported by the Hindus if they have any sense of virtue or gratitude left\nto them.\n", "\nBy the by, Mrs. Annie Besant invited me to speak at her Lodge, on Bhakti. I\nlectured there one night. Col. Olcott also was there. I did it to show my\nsympathy for all sects. . . . Our countrymen must remember that in things of\nthe Spirit we are the teachers, and not foreigners \u2014 but in things of the\nworld we ought to learn from them.\n", "\nI have read Max M\u00fcller's article, which is a good one, considering that when\nhe wrote it, six months ago, he had no material except Mazoomdar's leaflet.\nNow he writes me a long and nice letter offering to write a book on Shri\nRamakrishna. I have already supplied him with much material, but a good deal\nmore is needed from India.\n", "\nWork on! Hold on! Be brave! Dare anything and everything!\n", "\n. . . It is all misery, this Sams\u00e2ra, don't you see!\n", "\n", "Yours with blessings and love,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

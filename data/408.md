## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "THE BELUR MATH: AN APPEAL\n", "\nThe success which attended the labours of the disciples of Shri Ramakrishna\nParamahamsa in diffusing the principles of Hindu religion and obtaining some\nrespect for our much abused faith in the West, gave rise to the hope of\ntraining a number of young Sanny\u00e2sins to carry on the propaganda, both in\nand out of India. And an attempt is being made to educate a number of young\nmen according to the Vedic principle of students living in touch with the\nGuru.\n", "A Math has already been started on the Ganga near Calcutta, through the\nkindness of some European and American friends.\n", "The work, to produce any visible results in a short time, requires funds and\nhence this appeal to those who are in sympathy with our efforts.\n", "It is intended to extend the operations of the Math, by educating in the\nMath as many young men as the funds can afford, in both Western science and\nIndian spirituality, so that in addition to the advantages of a University\neducation, they will acquire a manly discipline by living in contact with\ntheir teachers.\n", "The central Math near Calcutta will gradually start branches in other parts\nof the country as men become ready and the means are forthcoming.\n", "It is a work which will take time to bring forth any permanent result and\nrequires a great deal of sacrifice on the part of our young men and on those\nwho have the means of helping this work.\n", "We believe the men are ready, and our appeal therefore is to those who\nreally love their religion and their country and have the means to show\ntheir sympathy practically by helping the cause.\n"]

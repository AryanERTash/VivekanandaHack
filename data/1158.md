["\n", "CXIV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Shivananda\n", "ALMORA,\n", "The 9th July 1897\n", "DEAR SHIVANANDA, (This address was written in English.)\n", "I haven't received any word of your arrival yet. I heard that Alasinga has\ngone there with his relations by way of Jaipur. We stayed at the Binsar Dak\nBungalow [rest-house] for two or three days, and then I left for Shyamdhura.\nAt  this,  Miss [Henrietta] M\u00fcller got infuriated and left for Almora.\nTerribly upset, Miss M\u00fcller accused Shivananda of telling her first that I\nshall live with a friend as his guest and of renting later such a big house\nfor  the  season  at 80 rupees without consulting her. Very cross with\neverybody, she has been reproving one and all but has cooled down a little\nwhen I said I would pay half of the rent. . . .\n", "Shashi himself [Swami Ramakrishnananda] should handle the entire amount of\n100 rupees which the Raja of Ramnad is donating (every month); he should\nsend a detailed account of the monthly income and expenditure to the Math\n\u2014 otherwise there won't be any check. Advise him to spend as little as\nnecessary  on  Thakur's[6]* worship, for the money is [primarily] \"for\npropagation of Truth\". (\nThe phrase \u201cfor propagation of Truth\u201d was written in English.)\n", "In case Gupta [Swami Sadananda] has lost his mental balance, ask him to come\nto Almora \u2014 but only when the boy selected for Shashi reaches there. I\nreceived a letter from R. A. [Rajam Aiyer?]. The money he sent has reached\nthe Math. I have received two volumes of Ramanuja's commentary. Advise him\nto  send  me  the third. Ask G. G. [Narasimhachari] to send me similar\ncommentaries by Madhva and others, if he can.\n", "A public meeting will have to be organized at Madras to present an address\nof welcome to the Raja [Ajit Singh] of Khetri and to Pratap Singh of Jodhpur\nfor their boldness in visiting England as well as for representing their\nprincipalities in India in the Jubilee celebration. This has to be done on\ntheir return to India, but for that you have to endeavour from now on.\nPlease go to Colombo and arrange a similar public meeting there.\n", "Give my love to Kidi [Singaravelu Mudaliar] and Doctor [Nanjunda Rao]; ask\nKidi why he hasn't written to me. What is wrong with him? Has he lost his\ndevotion? Bear this in mind that you should not assume a teacher's place in\nthe beginning. Do all your work with humility; otherwise everything will\ncrumble to pieces. Please see that there is no opposition, criticism or\nobstacles  to Shashi's work in Madras, for everybody should obey him \u2014\nwhoever may be in charge of a particular centre. If Shashi goes to Ceylon,\nhe will have to obey your authority, etc. Make sure that every centre sends\na weekly report to the Math. I have not seen a single one from Shashi yet.\n\"O Rama! How hard it is to turn a donkey into a horse, even by beating!\"\n", "Above all \"obedience\" and \"esprit de corps\".[1] The work cannot succeed\nunless  there  is  perfect obedience to the authority of the Order and\nsacrifice of individual views for the sake of the Order. Trinair gunatvam\n\u00e2pannair badhyante mattadantinah \u2014 \"Blades of grass woven into a rope can\nrestrain even mad elephants\".\n", "With love to Sashi and Gupta,[8]*\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

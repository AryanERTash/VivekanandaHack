["\n", "CXXXVI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "MURREE,\n", "10th October, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR AKHANDANANDA,\n", "I am very glad to receive your letter. You need not make a big plan for the\npresent,  but  do  only what is possible under existing circumstances.\nGradually the way will open to you. We must certainly have the orphanage, no\nhesitating in that. We must not leave the girls in the lurch either. But\nthen we must have a lady superintendent for an orphanage of girls. I believe\nMother will be a very good hand for that. Or engage for this task some aged\nwidow of the village who has no issue. And there must be separate places for\nthe boys and girls. Captain Sevier is ready to send you money to help in\nthis. Nedou's Hotel, Lahore \u2014 that is his address. If you write to him,\nwrite  the words, \"To wait arrival\", on the letter. I am soon going to\nRawalpindi, tomorrow or the day after; then I visit Lahore and other places\nvia Jammu, and return to Rajputana via Karachi etc.\n", "I am doing well.\n", "Yours,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS.  You  must admit Mohammedan boys, too, but never tamper with their\nreligion.  The  only  thing  you  will  have to do is to make separate\narrangements for their food etc., and teach them so that they may be moral,\nmanly, and devoted to doing good to others. This indeed is religion.\n", "Shelve your intricate philosophical speculations for the present. In our\ncountry we at present need manhood and kindness. \" [136_akhandananda_01.jpg]\n\u2014 The Lord is the Essence of unutterable love.\" But instead of saying \"\nsay,  \" [136_akhandananda_03.jpg] \u2014 He is ever manifest as Love in all\nbeings.\" What other God \u2014 the creation of your mind \u2014 are you then going to\nworship! Let the Vedas, the Koran, the Puranas, and all scriptural lumber\nrest now for some time \u2014 let there be worship of the visible God of Love and\nCompassion  in the country. All idea of separation is bondage, that of\nnon-differentiation is Mukti. Let not the words of people dead-drunk with\nworldliness terrify you. \" [136_akhandananda_04.jpg] \u2014 Be fearless\" \"Ignore\nthe  ordinary  critics as worms!\" Admit boys of all religions \u2014 Hindu,\nMohammedan, Christian, or anything; but begin rather gently \u2014 I mean, see\nthat they get their food and drink a little separately, and teach them only\nthe universal side of religion.\n", "Be mad over this, and strike others with this madness! This life has no\nother end. Preach His name, let His teachings penetrate the world to the\nvery  bone.  Never  forget. Repeat this Mantra in your heart of hearts\nunceasingly, as you go the round of your daily duties.\n", "Yours,\n", "V.\n"]

## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "WORLD-WIDE UNITY\n", "\nSpeaking of the world-wide unity, before the Oak Beach Christian Unity,\nSwami Vivekananda said:\n", "All religions are, at the bottom, alike. This is so, although the Christian\nChurch, like the Pharisee in the parable, thanks God that it alone is right\nand thinks that all other religions are wrong and in need of Christian\nlight. Christianity must become tolerant before the world will be willing to\nunite  with the Christian Church in a common charity. God has not left\nHimself without a witness in any heart, and men, especially men who follow\nJesus Christ, should be willing to admit this. In fact, Jesus Christ was\nwilling to admit every good man to the family of God. It is not the man who\nbelieves a certain something, but the man who does the will of the Father in\nheaven, who is right. On this basis\u2014being right and doing right\u2014the whole world can unite.\n"]

["\n", "CLXI\n", "(Translated from Bengali.)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "SAN FRANCISCO,\n", "12th March, 1900.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I got a letter from you some time ago. A letter from Sharat reached me\nyesterday.  I  saw  a  copy of the invitation letters for the birthday\nanniversary of Gurudeva (Divine Master). I am frightened hearing that Sharat\nis troubled by rheumatism. Alas, sickness, sorrow, and pain have been my\ncompanions for the last two years. Tell Sharat that I am not going to work\nso hard any more. But he who does not work enough to earn his food will have\nto  starve  to death! . . . I hope Durgaprasanna has done by this time\nwhatever  was  necessary for the compound wall. . . . The raising of a\ncompound wall is not, after all, a difficult thing. If I can, I shall build\na small house there and serve my old grandmother and mother. Evil actions\nleave none scot-free; Mother never spares anybody. I admit my actions have\nbeen wrong. Now, brother, all of you are S\u00e2dhus and great saints, kindly\npray to the Mother that I do not have to shoulder all this trouble and\nburden any longer. Now I desire a little peace \u2014 it seems there is no more\nstrength left to bear the burden of work and responsibility \u2014 rest and peace\nfor the few days that I shall yet live! Victory to the Guru! Victory to the\nGuru! . . . No more lectures or anything of that sort. Peace!\n", "As soon as Sharat sends the trust-deed of the Math, I shall put my signature\nto  it.  You all manage \u2014 truly I require rest. This disease is called\nneurasthenia, a disease of the nerves. Once it comes, it continues for some\nyears. But after a complete rest for three or four years it is cured. This\ncountry is the home of the disease, and here it has caught me. However, it\nis not only no fatal disease, but it makes a man live long. Don't be anxious\non my account. I shall go on rolling. But there is only this sorrow that the\nwork of Gurudeva is not progressing; there is this regret that I have not\nbeen able to accomplish anything of his work. How much I abuse you all and\nspeak harshly! I am the worst of men! Today, on the anniversary of his\nbirthday, put the dust of your feet on my head \u2014 and my mind will become\nsteady again. Victory to the Guru! Victory to the Guru! You are my only\nrefuge \u2014 you are my only refuge! Now that my mind is steady, let me tell you\nthat this resignation is the permanent attitude of my mind. All other moods\nthat come are, you should know, only disease. Please don't allow me to work\nat all any longer. Now I shall quietly do Japa and meditation for some time\n\u2014  nothing  more.  Mother knows all else. Victory to the Mother of the\nUniverse!\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

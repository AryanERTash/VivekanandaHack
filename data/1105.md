Kindly take one more\ntrouble for me and get it out of the express company. And keep it with you\ntill I come. The goods have arrived in New York and I had a notice of that.\nThey are on their way to Chicago.\n", "In two or three days I will send the bill of lading and the receipt for duty\npaid,  to  you.  I foolishly asked Miss Phillips, as soon as I got the\nCompany's (Original letter: Companies\u2019.) notice, to get them out before I\ngot the bill of lading. Now the bill of lading shows that it is bound for\nChicago. So I am bound to give you this trouble. I am so sorry. Again with\nmy usual business instincts \u2014 I forgot to note down the name of the express\ncompany. So I have written to New York for the letters of the Company. As\nsoon as that comes I will send over to you.\n", "I am going to Europe by the end of August or a little later.\n", "I will come to see you by the end of August.\n", "Lord bless you and yours for ever and ever.\n", "Your ever affectionate Son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

A.)\n", "\nQ.\u2014How can you reconcile your optimistic views with the existence of evil,\nwith the universal prevalence of sorrow and pain?\n", "A. \u2014I can only answer the question if the existence of evil be first\nproved; but this the Vedantic religion does not admit. Eternal pain unmixed\nwith pleasure would be a positive evil; but temporal pain and sorrow, if\nthey have contributed an element of tenderness and nobility tending towards\neternal bliss, are not evils: on the contrary, they may be supreme good. We\ncannot assert that anything is evil until we have traced its sequence into\nthe realm of eternity.\n", "Devil worship is not a part of the Hindu religion. The human race is in\nprocess of development; all have not reached the same altitude. Therefore\nsome are nobler and purer in their earthly lives than others. Every one has\nan opportunity within the limits of the sphere of his present development of\nmaking himself better. We cannot unmake ourselves; we cannot destroy or\nimpair  the  vital force within us, but we have the freedom to give it\ndifferent directions.\n", "\nQ.\u2014Is not the reality of cosmic matter simply the imagining of our own\nminds?\n", "A.\u2014In my opinion the external world is certainly an entity and has an\nexistence outside of our mental conceptions. All creation is moving onwards\nand  upwards,  obedient to the great law of spirit evolution, which is\ndifferent from the evolution of matter. The latter is symbolical of, but\ndoes not explain, the process of the former. We are not individuals now, in\nour present earthly environment. We shall not have reached individuality\nuntil we shall have ascended to the higher state, when the divine spirit\nwithin us will have a perfect medium for the expression of its attributes.\n", "\nQ.\u2014What is your explanation of the problem presented to Christ, as to\nwhether it was the infant itself or its parents that had sinned, that it was\nborn blind?\n", "A.\u2014While the question of sin does not enter into the problem, I am\nconvinced that the blindness was due to some act on the part of the spirit\nof the child in a previous incarnation. In my opinion such problems are only\nexplicable on the hypothesis of a prior earthly existence.\n", "\nQ.\u2014Do our spirits pass at death into a state of happiness?\n", "A.\u2014Death is only a change of condition: time and space are in you, you are\nnot in time and space. It is enough to know that as we make our lives purer\nand nobler, either in the seen or the unseen world, the nearer we approach\nGod, who is the centre of all spiritual beauty and eternal joy.\n", "Q.\u2014What is the Hindu theory of the transmigration of souls?\n", "A.\u2014It is on the same basis as the theory of conservation is to the\nscientist. This theory was first produced by a philosopher of my country.\nThe  ancient  sages  did not believe in a creation. A creation implies\nproducing  something  out of nothing. That is impossible. There was no\nbeginning of creation as there was no beginning of time. God and creation\nare as two lines without end, without beginning, and parallel. Our theory of\ncreation is \"It is, it was, and is to be\". All punishment is but reaction.\nPeople of the West should learn one thing from India and that is toleration.\nAll the religions are good, since the essentials are the same.\n", "\nQ.\u2014Why are the women of India not much elevated?\n", "A.\u2014It is in a great degree owing to the barbarous invaders through\ndifferent ages; it is partly due to the people of India themselves.\n", "When  it  was pointed out to Swamiji in America that Hinduism is not a\nproselytising religion, he replied:\n", "\"I have a message to the West as Buddha had a message to the East.\"\n", "\nQ.\u2014Do you intend to introduce the practices and rituals of the Hindu\nreligion into this country (America)?\n", "A.\u2014I am preaching simply philosophy.\n", "\nQ.\u2014Do you not think if the fear of future hell-fire were taken from man\nthere would be no controlling him?\n", "A.\u2014No! On the contrary, I think he is made far better through love and\nhope than through fear.\n", "\n"]

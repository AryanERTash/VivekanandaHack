["\n", "CHAPTER IV\n", "THE FORMS OF LOVE \u2014 MANIFESTATION\n", "\nHere are some of the forms in which love manifests itself. First there is\nreverence. Why do people show reverence to temples and holy places? Because\nHe is worshipped there, and His presence is associated with all such places.\nWhy do people in every country pay reverence to teachers of religion? It is\nnatural for the human heart to do so, because all such teachers preach the\nLord. At bottom, reverence is a growth out of love; we can none of us revere\nhim whom we do not love. Then comes Priti \u2014 pleasure in God. What an immense\npleasure  men take in the objects of the senses. They go anywhere, run\nthrough any danger, to get the thing which they love, the thing which their\nsenses like. What is wanted of the Bhakta is this very kind of intense love\nwhich has, however, to be directed to God. Then there is the sweetest of\npains, Viraha, the intense misery due to the absence of the beloved. When a\nman feels intense misery because he has not attained to God, has not known\nthat which is the only thing worthy to be known, and becomes in consequence\nvery dissatisfied and almost mad \u2014 then there is Viraha; and this state of\nthe mind makes him feel disturbed in the presence of anything other than the\nbeloved (Ekarativichikits\u00e2). In earthly love we see how often this Viraha\ncomes. Again, when men are really and intensely in love with women or women\nwith men, they feel a kind of natural annoyance in the presence of all those\nwhom they do not love. Exactly the same state of impatience in regard to\nthings that are not loved comes to the mind when Para-Bhakti holds sway over\nit; even to talk about things other than God becomes distasteful then.\n\"Think  of  Him, think of Him alone, and give up all other vain words\"\n\u0905\u0928\u094d\u092f\u093e \u0935\u093e\u091a\u094b \u0935\u093f\u092e\u0941\u0902\u091a\u0925\u0964  \u2014 Those who talk of Him alone, the\nBhakta finds to be friendly to him; while those who talk of anything else\nappear to him to be unfriendly. A still higher stage of love is reached when\nlife itself is maintained for the sake of the one Ideal of Love, when life\nitself is considered beautiful and worth living only on account of that Love\n(\u0924\u0926\u0930\u094d\u0925\u092a\u094d\u0930\u093e\u0923\u0938\u0902\u0938\u094d\u0925\u093e\u0928\u0902) . Without it, such a life would not\nremain even for a moment. Life is sweet, because it thinks of the Beloved.\nTadiyat\u00e2 (His-ness) comes when a man becomes perfect according to Bhakti \u2014\nwhen he has become blessed, when he has attained God, when he has touched\nthe  feet  of  God,  as it were. Then his whole nature is purified and\ncompletely changed. All his purpose in life then becomes fulfilled. Yet many\nsuch  Bhaktas live on just to worship Him. That is the bliss, the only\npleasure in life which they will not give up. \"O king, such is the blessed\nquality of Hari that even those who have become satisfied with everything,\nall the knots of whose hearts have been cut asunder, even they love the Lord\nfor love's sake\" \u2014 the Lord \"Whom all the gods worship \u2014 all the lovers of\nliberation,    and    all    the    knowers    of   the   Brahman\"   \u2014\n\u092f\u0902 \u0938\u0930\u094d\u0935\u0947 \u0926\u0947\u0935\u093e \u0928\u092e\u0928\u094d\u0924\u093f \u092e\u0941\u092e\u0941\u0915\u094d\u0937\u0935\u094b \u092c\u094d\u0930\u0939\u094d\u092e\u0935\u093e\u0926\u093f\u0928\u0936\u094d\u091a\u0947\u0924\u093f  (Nri. Tap. Up.). Such is the power\nof love. When a man has forgotten himself altogether, and does not feel that\nanything belongs to him, then he acquires the state of Tadiyata; everything\nis sacred to him, because it belongs to the Beloved. Even in regard to\nearthly love, the lover thinks that everything belonging to his beloved is\nsacred and so dear to him. He loves even a piece of cloth belonging to the\ndarling of his heart In the same way, when a person loves the Lord, the\nwhole universe becomes dear to him, because it is all His.\n"]

["\n", "XLVII\n", "LOS ANGELES,\n", "6th Dec., 1899.\n", "DEAR MARGOT,\n", "Your sixth has arrived, but with it yet no change in my fortune. Would\nchange be any good, do you think? Some people are made that way, to love\nbeing miserable. If I did not break my heart over people I was born amongst,\nI would do it for somebody else. I am sure of that. This is the way of some,\nI am coming to see it. We are all after happiness, true, but that some are\nonly happy in being unhappy \u2014 queer, is it not? There is no harm in it\neither, except that happiness and unhappiness are both infectious. Ingersoll\nsaid once that if he were God, he would make health catching, instead of\ndisease, little dreaming that health is quite as catching as disease, if not\nmore! That is the only danger. No harm in the world in my being happy, in\nbeing miserable, but others must not catch it. This is the great fact. No\nsooner a prophet feels miserable for the state of man than he sours his\nface, beats his breast, and calls upon everyone to drink tartaric acid,\nmunch charcoal, sit upon a dung-heap covered with ashes, and speak only in\ngroans and tears! \u2014 I find they all have been wanting. Yes, they have. If\nyou are really ready to take the world's burden, take it by all means. But\ndo not let us hear your groans and curses. Do not frighten us with your\nsufferings, so that we came to feel we were better off with our own burdens.\nThe man who really takes the burden blesses the world and goes his own way.\nHe has not a word of condemnation, a word of criticism, not because there\nwas  no  evil but that he has taken it on his own shoulders willingly,\nvoluntarily. It is the Saviour who should \"go his way rejoicing, and not the\nsaved\".\n", "This is the only light I have caught this morning. This is enough if it has\ncome to live with me and permeate my life.\n", "Come ye that are heavy laden and lay all your burden on me, and then do\nwhatever you like and be happy and forget that I ever existed.\n", "Ever with love,\n", "Your father,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

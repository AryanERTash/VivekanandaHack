Go on then straight to thy work without heeding either\" \u2014 thus saith\none of our great Sannyasins, an old emperor of India, King Bhartrihari, who\njoined the order in old times.\n", "May the Lord bless you for ever and ever. My love to all your children and\nmy respects to your noble wife.\n", "\nI remain ever your friend,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. \u2014 I had connection with Pundit Shiva Nath Shastri's party \u2014 but only on\npoints of social reform. Of course\nin religious matters even with my friend Punditji I differed much, the chief\nbeing, I thinking Sannyasa or (giving up the world) the highest ideal, and\nhe, a sin. So the Brahmo Samajists consider becoming a monk a sin!!\n", "Yours,\n", "\n", "V.\n", "The Brahmo Samaj, like Christian Science in your country, spread in Calcutta\nfor a certain time and then died out. I am not sorry, neither glad that it\ndied. It has done its work \u2014 viz social reform. Its religion was not worth a\ncent, and so it must die out. If Mazoomdar thinks I was one of the causes of\nits death, he errs. I am even now a great sympathiser of its reforms; but\nthe \"booby\" religion could not hold its own against the old \"Vedanta\". What\nshall I do? Is that my fault? Mazoomdar has become childish in his old age\nand  takes  to  tactics  not a whit better than some of your Christian\nmissionaries. Lord bless him and show him better ways.\n", "Yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "When are you going to Annisquam? My love to Austin and Bime. My respects to\nyour wife; and for you my love and gratitude is too deep for expression.\n", "\nYours ever affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

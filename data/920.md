**LXXIII**

To the Hale Sisters

**6 WEST 43RD STREET,**

**NEW YORK,**

14th April, 1896.

DEAR SISTERS,

I arrived safe on Sunday and on account of illness could not write earlier.
I sail on board the White Star Line Germanic tomorrow at 12 noon. With
everlasting memory of love, gratitude and blessings,

I am, your ever loving brother,

VIVEKANANDA.

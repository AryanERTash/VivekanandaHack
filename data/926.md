Sturdy\n", "SWITZERLAND,\n", "5th August, 1896.\n", "BLESSED AND BELOVED,\n", "A letter came this morning from Prof. Max M\u00fcller telling me that the article\nof Shri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa has been published in The XIX Century August\nnumber. Have you read it? He asked my opinion about it. Not having seen it\nyet, I can't write anything to him. If you have it, kindly send it to me.\nAlso The Brahmavadin, if any have arrived. Max M\u00fcller wants to know about\nour plans . . . and again about the magazine. He promises a good deal of\nhelp and is ready to write a book on Shri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa.\n", "I think it is better that you should directly correspond with him about the\nmagazine etc. You will see from his letter which I shall send you as soon as\nI have replied (after reading The XIX Century) that he is very much pleased\nwith our movement and is ready to help it as much as he can. . . .\n", "Yours with blessings and love,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. I hope you will consider well the plan for the big magazine. Some money\ncan be raised in America, and we can keep the magazine all to ourselves at\nthe same time. I intend to write to America on hearing about the plan you\nand Prof. Max Muller decide upon. \"A great tree is to be taken refuge in,\nwhen it has both fruits and shade. If, however, we do not get the fruit, who\nprevents our enjoyment of the shade?\" So ought great attempts to be made, is\nthe moral.\n"]

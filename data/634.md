Straying away from the true path of the Shastras,\nthe country has been overwhelmed with usages and observances originating in\nparticular localities, or popular opinion, or with the womenfolk! That's why\nI ask you to proceed along the path of the Shastras as in olden times. Have\nfaith within yourselves and thereby bring it back into the country.

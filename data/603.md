## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "CXLII\n", "(Written to Mohammed Sarfaraz Husain of Naini Tal)\n", "ALMORA,\n", "10th June, 1898.\n", "MY DEAR FRIEND,\n", "I appreciate your letter very much and am extremely happy to learn that the\nLord is silently preparing wonderful things for our motherland.\n", "Whether we call it Vedantism or any ism, the truth is that Advaitism is the\nlast word of religion and thought and the only position from which one can\nlook upon all religions and sects with love. I believe it is the religion of\nthe future enlightened humanity. The Hindus may get the credit of arriving\nat it earlier than other races, they being an older race than either the\nHebrew or the Arab; yet practical Advaitism, which looks upon and behaves to\nall  mankind  as  one's own soul, was never developed among the Hindus\nuniversally.\n", "On the other hand, my experience is that if ever any religion approached to\nthis equality in an appreciable manner, it is Islam and Islam alone.\n", "Therefore I am firmly persuaded that without the help of practical Islam,\ntheories of Vedantism, however fine and wonderful they may be, are entirely\nvalueless to the vast mass of mankind. We want to lead mankind to the place\nwhere there is neither the Vedas, nor the Bible, nor the Koran; yet this has\nto be done by harmonising the Vedas, the Bible and the Koran. Mankind ought\nto be taught that religions are but the varied expressions of THE RELIGION,\nwhich is Oneness, so that each may choose that path that suits him best.\n", "For our own motherland a junction of the two great systems, Hinduism and\nIslam \u2014 Vedanta brain and Islam body \u2014 is the only hope.\n", "I see in my mind's eye the future perfect India rising out of this chaos and\nstrife, glorious and invincible, with Vedanta brain and Islam body.\n", "Ever praying that the Lord may make of you a great instrument for the help\nof mankind, and especially of our poor, poor motherland.\n", "Yours with love,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

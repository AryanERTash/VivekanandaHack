["\n", "CXXI\n", "\n", "RAMNAD,\n", "30th Jan., 1897.\n", "MY DEAR MARY,\n", "Things are turning out most curiously for me. From Colombo in Ceylon, where\nI landed, to Ramnad, the nearly southernmost point of the Indian continent\nwhere I am just now as the guest of the Raja of Ramnad, my journey has been\na huge procession \u2014 crowds of people, illuminations, addresses, etc., etc. A\nmonument forty feet high is being built on the spot where I landed. The Raja\nof Ramnad has presented his address to \"His most Holiness\" in a huge casket\nof solid gold beautifully worked. Madras and Calcutta are on the tiptoe of\nexpectation as if the whole nation is rising to honour me. So you see, Mary,\nI am on the very height of my destiny, yet the mind turns to quietness and\npeace, to the days we had in Chicago, of rest, of peace, and love; and that\nis why I write just now, and may this find you all in health and peace! I\nwrote a letter to my people from London to receive Dr. Barrows kindly. They\naccorded him a big reception, but it was not my fault that he could not make\nany impression there. The Calcutta people are a hard-headed lot! Now Barrows\nthinks a world of me, I hear! Such is the world.\n", "With all love to mother, father, and you all,\n", "I remain, yours affly.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "XC\n", "\n", "NEW YORK,\n", "29th Dec., 1895.\n", "DEAR SISTER, (Miss S. Farmer)\n", "In this universe where nothing is lost, where we live in the midst of death\nin life, every thought that is thought, in public or in private, in crowded\nthoroughfares or in the deep recesses of primeval forests, lives. They are\ncontinuously trying to become self-embodied, and until they have embodied\nthemselves, they will struggle for expression, and any amount of repression\ncannot kill them. Nothing can be destroyed \u2014 those thoughts that caused evil\nin the past are also seeking embodiment, to be filtered through repeated\nexpression and, at last, transfigured into perfect good.\n", "As such, there is a mass of thought which is at the present time struggling\nto get expression. This new thought is telling us to give up our dreams of\ndualism,  of  good  and evil in essence, and the still wilder dream of\nsuppression. It teaches us that higher direction and not destruction is the\nlaw. It teaches us that it is not a world of bad and good, but good and\nbetter \u2014 and still better. It stops short of nothing but acceptance. It\nteaches that no situation is hopeless, and as such accepts every form of\nmental, moral, or spiritual thought where it already stands, and without a\nword of condemnation tells it that so far it has done good, now is the time\nto do better. What in old times was thought of as the elimination of bad, it\nteaches as the transfiguration of evil and the doing of better. It, above\nall, teaches that the kingdom of heaven is already in existence if we will\nhave it, that perfection is already in man if he will see it.\n", "The Greenacre meetings last summer were so wonderful, simply because you\nopened yourself fully to that thought which has found in you so competent a\nmedium  of  expression, and because you took your stand on the highest\nteaching of this thought that the kingdom of heaven already exists.\n", "You have been consecrated and chosen by the Lord as a channel for converting\nthis thought into life, and every one that helps you in this wonderful work\nis serving the Lord.\n", "Our scripture teaches that he who serves the servants of the Lord is His\nhighest worshipper. You are a servant of the Lord, and as a disciple of\nKrishna I will always consider it a privilege and worship to render you any\nservice in the carrying out of your inspired mission wherever I be.\n", "Ever your affectionate brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

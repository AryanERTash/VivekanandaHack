["\n", "CXXVI\n", "\n", "ALMORA,\n", "3rd June, 1897.\n", "DEAR MISS NOBLE,\n", ". . . As for myself I am quite content. I have roused a good many of our\npeople, and that was all I wanted. Let things have their course and Karma\nits sway. I have no bonds here below. I have seen life, and it is all self\n\u2014 life is for self, love for self, honour for self, everything for self. I\nlook back and scarcely find any action I have done for self \u2014 even my wicked\ndeeds  were not for self. So I am content; not that I feel I have done\nanything specially good or great, but the world is so little, life so mean a\nthing, existence so, so servile \u2014 that I wonder and smile that human beings,\nrational souls, should be running after this self \u2014 so mean and detestable a\nprize.\n", "This is the truth. We are caught in a trap, and the sooner one gets out, the\nbetter for one. I have seen the truth \u2014 let the body float up or down, who\ncares?\n", "It is a beautiful mountain park I am living in now. On the north, extending\nalmost all along the horizon, are peak after peak of the snow-clad Himalayas\n\u2014 forests abounding. It is not cold here, neither very warm; the evenings\nand mornings are simply delicious. I should like to be here this summer, and\nwhen the rains set in, I go down to the plains to work.\n", "I was born for the life of a scholar \u2014 retired, quiet, poring over my books.\nBut the Mother dispenses otherwise \u2014 yet the tendency is there.\n", "\nYours etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

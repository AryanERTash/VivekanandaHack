["\n", "CXXVI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "DARJEELING,\n", "23rd April, 1898.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "My health was excellent on my return from Sandukphu (11,924 ft.) and other\nplaces; but after returning to Darjeeling, I had first an attack of fever,\nand after recovering from that, I am now suffering from cough and cold. I\ntry to escape from this place every day; but they have been constantly\nputting it off for a long time. However, tomorrow, Sunday, I am leaving;\nafter halting at Kharsana for a day I start again for Calcutta on Monday. I\nshall send you a wire after starting. We should hold an annual meeting of\nthe Ramakrishna Mission, and also one for the Math. In both the meetings the\naccounts of famine relief must be submitted, and the report of the famine\nrelief must be published. Keep all this ready.\n", "Nityagopal says, managing an English magazine will not cost much. So let us\nfirst get this one out, and we shall see to the Bengali magazine afterwards.\nAll these points will have to be discussed. Is Yogen willing to shoulder the\nresponsibility of running the paper? Shashi writes that if Sharat goes some\ntime to Madras, they may make a lecture tour jointly. Oh, how hot it is now!\nAsk Sharat if G. G., Sarada, Shashi Babu, and others have got their articles\nready. Give my love and blessing to Mrs. Bull, Miss MacLeod, and Nivedita.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "XXXIV\n", "541 DEARBORN AVENUE,\n", "CHICAGO,\n", "November(?), 1894.\n", "DEAR DIWANJI (Shri Haridas Viharidas Desai),\n", "Your letter pleased me extremely. I, of course, understand the joke, but I\nam not the baby to be put off with a joke; now take more.\n", "The secret of success of the Westerners is the power of organisation and\ncombination. That is only possible with mutual trust and co-operation and\nhelp. Now here is Virchand Gandhi, the Jain, whom you well knew in Bombay.\nThis man never takes anything but pure vegetables even in this terribly cold\nclimate, and tooth and nail tries to defend his countrymen and religion. The\npeople of this country like him very well, but what are they doing who sent\nhim over? They are trying to outcast him. Jealousy is a vice necessarily\ngenerated in slaves. Again it is jealousy that holds them down.\n", "Here were . . .; they were all trying to lecture and get money thereby. They\ndid something, but I succeeded better than they \u2014 why, I did not put myself\nas a bar to their success. It was the will of the Lord. But all these . . .\nexcept . . . have fabricated and circulated the most horrible lies about me\nin this country, and behind my back. Americans will never stoop to such\nmeanness.\n", ". . . If any man tries to move forward here, everybody is ready to help him.\nIn India you may try tomorrow by writing a single line of praise for me in\nany of our papers (Hindu), and the next day they would be all against me.\nWhy? It is the nature of slaves. They cannot suffer to see any one of their\nbrethren putting his head the least above their rank. . . . Do you mean to\ncompare such stuff with these children of liberty, self-help, and brotherly\nlove? The nearest approach to our people are the freed slaves of the U.S.A.,\nthe Negroes. Why, in the South they are about twenty millions and are now\nfree. The whites are a handful, still the whites hold them down all the\nsame. Why, even when they have every right by law, a bloody war between the\nbrothers has been fought to free these slaves? The same defect \u2014 jealousy.\nNot one of these Negroes would bear to see his brother-Negro praised or\npushing on. Immediately they would join the whites to crush him down. You\ncan have no idea about it until you come out of India. It is all right for\nthose who have plenty of money and position to let the world roll on such,\nbut I call him a traitor who, having been educated, nursed in luxury by the\nheart's blood of the downtrodden millions of toiling poor, never even takes\na  thought  for  them. Where, in what period of history your rich men,\nnoblemen, your priests and potentates took any thought for the poor \u2014 the\ngrinding of whose faces is the very life-blood of their power?\n", "But the Lord is great, the vengeance came sooner or later, and they who\nsucked  the  life-blood of the poor, whose very education was at their\nexpense, whose very power was built on their poverty, were in their turn\nsold  as  slaves  by hundreds and thousands, their wives and daughters\ndishonoured, their property robbed for the last 1,000 years, and do you\nthink it was for no cause?\n", "Why amongst the poor of India so many are Mohammedans? It is nonsense to\nsay, they were converted by the sword. It was to gain their liberty from the\n. . . zemindars and from the . . . priest, and as a consequence you find in\nBengal there are more Mohammedans than Hindus amongst the cultivators,\nbecause there were so many zemindars there. Who thinks of raising these\nsunken downtrodden millions? A few thousand graduates do not make a nation,\na few rich men do not make a nation. True, our opportunities are less, but\nstill  there  is  enough to feed and clothe and made 300 millions more\ncomfortable, nay, luxurious. Ninety per cent of our people are without\neducation \u2014 who thinks of that? \u2014 these Babus, the so-called patriots?\n", "Now, let me tell you \u2014 still there is a God, no joke. He is ordering our\nlives, and although I know a nation of slaves cannot but try to bite at the\nhand that wants to give them medicine, yet, pray with me, you \u2014 one of the\nfew that have real sympathy for everything good, for everything great, one\nat least whom I know to be a man of true ring, nobility of nature, and a\nthorough sincerity of head and heart \u2014 pray with me:\n", "\"Lead, kindly Light,\namid th' encircling gloom.\"\n", "I do not care what they say. I love my God, my religion, my country, and\nabove  all,  myself, a poor beggar. I love the poor, the ignorant, the\ndowntrodden, I feel for them \u2014 the Lord knows how much. He will show me the\nway. I do not care a fig for human approbation or criticism. I think of most\nof them as ignorant, noisy children \u2014 they have not penetrated into the\ninner nature of sympathy, into the spirit which is all love.\n", "I have that insight through the blessing of Ramakrishna. I am trying to work\nwith my little band, all of these poor beggars like me, you have seen them.\nBut the Lord's works have been always done by the lowly, by the poor. You\nbless me that I may have faith in my Guru, in my God, and in myself.\n", "The only way is love and sympathy. The only worship is love.\n", "May He help you and yours ever and ever!\n", "With prayers and blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

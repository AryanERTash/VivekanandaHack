BULL,\n", "I received your last note duly \u2014 and you already know my gratitude and love\nfor you and that I perfectly agree with most of your ideas and work.\n", "I did not understand, however, one point. You speak of Sturdy and myself\nbeing members. Members of what? I, as you well know, can not become a member\nof any society.\n", "I  am  very  glad  to learn that you have been favourably impressed by\nSaradananda. There is one big mistake you are labouring under. What do you\nmean of [my] writing to my workers more confidentially and not to you? I\nseldom  write  to anyone \u2014 I have no time to write. I have no workers.\nEveryone is independent to work as one likes. I do not bother my head about\nthese little things at all. I can give ideas \u2014 that is all; let people work\nthem out any way they like, and Godspeed to all.\n", "\"He who works unattached to persons and giving up the fruits of work is a\ngenuine worker\" \u2014 Git\u00e2.\n", "Yours Ever with love and gratitude,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

["\n", "CLXXXV\n", "To Mrs. Ole Bull\n", "66, RUE AMPERE\n", "22nd October 1900\n", "DEAR MOTHER,\n", "I am sorry to learn you are not well. Hope you will soon be better. Things\nseem to turn out better for me.\n", "Mr. Maxim of the gun fame is very much interested in me, and he wants to put\nin his book on China and the Chinese something about my work in America.[6]*\nI have not any documents with me; if you have, kindly give them to him. He\nwill come to see you and talk it over with you. Canon Hawes [Reverend Hugh\nReginald Haweis] also keeps track of my work in England. So much about that.\nIt may be that Mother will now work up my original plan of international\nwork. In that case, you will find your work of the Conference[7]* has not\nbeen in vain.\n", "It seems that after this fall in my health, physical and mental, it is going\nto open out that way \u2014 larger and more international work. Mother knows\nbest.\n", "My whole life has been divided into successive depressions and rises \u2014 and\nso, I believe, is the life of everyone. I am glad, rather than not, these\nfalls come. I understand it all; still, I suffer and grumble and rage!!\nPerhaps that is a part of the cause of the next upheaval.\n", "I think you will be in America by the time we return; if not, I will see you\nin London again. Anyhow, adieu for the present. We start day after tomorrow\nfor Egypt etc. And all blessings ever be on you and yours is, as always, my\nprayer.\n", "Your son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "PS: To Margot [Sister Nivedita] my love, and I am sure she will succeed.\n", "V.\n"]

["\n", "VI\n", "To Swami Saradananda\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna\n", "CALCUTTA\n", "32 ASHADHA [JULY 15, 1890]\n", "MY DEAR SHARAT\n", "I am sorry to learn that [Vaikunthanath] Sanyal's habits are as yet not\nPucca [firm]; and what about Brahmacharya? I don't understand you. If so,\nthe best thing for you both is to come down and live here. The widow of\nMohindra Mukherjee is trying head and heart to erect a Math for you, and\nSurendranath Mitra has left another thousand so that you are very likely\nsoon to get a beautiful place on the river. As for all the hardships up\nthere, I reserve my own opinions.\n", "It was not at all my intention to come down, only the death of Balaram Bose\nhad made me have a peep here and go back. If the mountains be so bad, there\nis more than enough place for me; only I leave Bengal. If one does not suit,\nanother will. So that is my determination. Everyone here will be so glad at\nyour return here, and from your letter I see it would be downright injurious\nto you if you didn't come down. So come down at your earliest opportunity. I\nwill leave this place before this letter reaches you; only I won't go to\nAlmora. I have my own plans for the future and they shall be a secret.\n", "As for Sanyal, I do not see how I can benefit him. Of course, you are at\nliberty to hold your own opinion about the Sanga [holy company] here. That I\ncan find places Sudrishya [having scenic beauty] and Subhiksha [where alms\nare  available]  is enough. Sanga is not much, or, I think, not at all\nnecessary for me.\n", "Yours, etc.,\n", "NARENDRA.\n"]

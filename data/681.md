["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "FRIDAY, August 2, 1895.\n", "Nishth\u00e2 (devotion to one ideal) is the beginning of realisation. \"Take the\nhoney out of all flowers; sit and be friendly with all, pay reverence to\nall, say to all, 'Yes, brother, yes, brother', but keep firm in your own\nway.\" A higher stage is actually to take the position of the other. If I am\nall, why can I not really and actively sympathise with my brother and see\nwith his eyes? While I am weak, I must stick to one course (Nishth\u00e2), but\nwhen I am strong, I can feel with every other and perfectly sympathise with\nhis ideas.\n", "The old idea was: \"Develop one idea at the expense of all the rest\". The\nmodern way is \"harmonious development\". A third way is to \"develop the mind\nand control it\", then put it where you will; the result will come quickly.\nThis is developing yourself in the truest way. Learn concentration and use\nit in any direction. Thus you lose nothing. He who gets the whole must have\nthe parts too. Dualism is included in Advaitism (monism).\n", "\"I first saw him and he saw me. There was a flash of eye from me to him and\nfrom him to me.\"\n", "This went on until the two souls became so closely united that they actually\nbecame one. . . .\n", "There are two kinds of Samadhi \u2014 I concentrate on myself, then I concentrate\nand there is a unity of subject and object.\n", "You must be able to sympathise fully with each particular, then at once to\njump back to the highest monism. After having perfected yourself, you limit\nyourself voluntarily. Take the whole power into each action. Be able to\nbecome a dualist for the time being and forget Advaita, yet be able to take\nit up again at will.\n", "*            *            *\n", "Cause and effect are all Maya, and we shall grow to understand that all we\nsee is as disconnected as the child's fairy tales now seem to us. There is\nreally no such thing as cause and effect and we shall come to know it. Then\nif you can, lower your intellect to let any allegory pass through your mind\nwithout questioning about connection. Develop love of imagery and beautiful\npoetry and then enjoy all mythologies as poetry. Come not to mythology with\nideas of history and reasoning. Let it flow as a current through your mind,\nlet it be whirled as a candle before your eyes, without asking who holds the\ncandle, and you will get the circle; the residuum of truth will remain in\nyour mind.\n", "The writers of all mythologies wrote in symbols of what they saw and heard,\nthey painted flowing pictures. Do not try to pick out the themes and so\ndestroy the pictures; take them as they are and let them act on you. Judge\nthem only by the effect and get the good out of them.\n", "*            *            *\n", "Your own will is all that answers prayer, only it appears under the guise of\ndifferent religious conceptions to each mind. We may call it Buddha, Jesus,\nKrishna, Jehovah, Allah, Agni, but it is only the Self, the \"I\". . . .\n", "Concepts grow, but there is no historical value in the allegories which\npresent them. Moses' visions are more likely to be wrong than ours are,\nbecause  we  have more knowledge and are less likely to be deceived by\nillusions.\n", "Books are useless to us until our own book opens; then all other books are\ngood so far as they confirm our book. It is the strong that understand\nstrength, it is the elephant that understands the lion, not the rat. How can\nwe understand Jesus until we are his equals? It is all in the dream to feed\nfive thousand with two loaves, or to feed two with five loaves; neither is\nreal and neither affects the other. Only grandeur appreciates grandeur, only\nGod realises God. The dream is only the dreamer, it has no other basis. It\nis not one thing and the dreamer another. The keynote running through the\nmusic is \u2014 \"I am He, I am He\", all other notes are but variations and do not\naffect the real theme. We are the living books and books are but the words\nwe have spoken. Everything is the living God, the living Christ; see it as\nsuch. Read man, he is the living poem. We are the light that illumines all\nthe Bibles and Christs and Buddhas that ever were. Without that, these would\nbe dead to us, not living.\n", "Stand on your own Self.\n", "The dead body resents nothing; let us make our bodies dead and cease to\nidentify ourselves with them.\n"]

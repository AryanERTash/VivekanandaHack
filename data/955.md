["\n", "CVIII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Ramakrishnananda\n", "SRINAGAR, KASHMIR,\n", "30th September, 1897\n", "MY DEAR SHASHI,\n", "Now I am returning from a visit to places in Kashmir. In a day or two I\nshall leave for the Punjab. As my health is now much better, I have decided\nto tour again in the same way as before. Not too much lecturing \u2014 one or two\nlectures, perhaps, in the Punjab, otherwise none. The people of our country\nhave not yet offered me even as much as a pice for my travelling expenses\n\u2014 and to cap it all, to take with you a whole party, well, you can easily\nunderstand how troublesome it all is. It is also a matter of shame to have\nto draw upon only the English disciples. So, as before, I start out \"with\nonly a blanket\". In this place there is no need for any person like Goodwin,\nas you can see.\n", "A monk from Ceylon, P. C. Jinawar Vamar by name, has written to me among\nother things that he wants to visit India. Perhaps he is the same monk who\ncomes of the Siamese royal family. His address is Wellawatta, Ceylon. If\nconvenient, invite him to Madras. He believes in the Vedanta. It will not be\nso difficult to send him to other places from Madras. It is also good to\nhave  such a person in the Order. My love and blessings to you and all\nothers.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. The Maharaja of Khetri is reaching Bombay on the 10th October. Don't\nforget to present him an address of welcome.\n", "V.\n"]

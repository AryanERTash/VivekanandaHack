I try to keep down the\nspring, but something or other happens, and the spring goes whirr, and there\nyou are \u2014 thinking, remembering, scribbling, scrawling, and all that!\n", "Well, about the rains \u2014 they have come down now in right earnest, and it is\na deluge, pouring, pouring, pouring night and day. The rain-water stands at places some feet high. My\nhuge stork is full of glee, and so are the ducks and geese. My tame antelope\nfled from the Math and gave us some days of anxiety in finding him out. One\nof my ducks unfortunately died yesterday. She had been gasping for breath\nmore than a week. One of my waggish old monks says, \"Sir, it is no use\nliving in this Kali-Yuga when ducks catch cold from damp and rain, and frogs\nsneeze!\"\n", "One of the geese had her plumes falling off. Knowing no other method, I left\nher some minutes in a tub of water mixed with mild carbolic, so that it\nmight either kill or heal; and she is all right now.\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

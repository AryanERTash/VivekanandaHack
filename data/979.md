["\n", "CXXXII\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "SRINAGAR, KASHMIR,\n", "28th August, 1898.\n", "MY DEAR MARY,\n", "I could not make an earlier opportunity of writing you, and knowing that you\nwere in no hurry for a letter, I will not make apologies. You are learning\nall about Kashmir and ourselves from Miss MacLeod's letter to Mrs. Leggett,\nI hear \u2014 therefore needless going into long rigmaroles about it.\n", "The search for Heinsholdt's Mahatmas in Kashmir will be entirely fruitless;\nand  as  the  whole thing has first to be established as coming from a\ncreditable source, the attempt will also be a little too early. How are\nMother Church and Father Pope and where? How are you ladies, young and old?\nGoing on with the old game with more zest now that one has fallen off the\nranks? How is the lady that looks like a certain statue in Florence? (I have\nforgotten the name) I always bless her arms when I think of the comparison.\n", "I have been away a few days. Now I am going to join the ladies. The party\nthen goes to a nice quiet spot behind a hill, in a forest, through which a\nmurmuring stream flows, to have meditation deep and long under the deodars\n(trees of God) cross-legged \u00e0 la Buddha.\n", "This will be for a month or so, when by that time our good work will have\nspent its powers and we shall fall from this Paradise to earth again; then\nwork out our Karma a few months and then will have to go to hell for bad\nKarma in China, and our evil deeds will make us sink in bad odours with the\nworld in Canton and other cities. Thence Purgatory in Japan? And regain\nParadise once more in the U.S. of America. This is what Pumpkin Swami,\nbrother of the Coomra Swami, foretells (in Bengali Coomra means squash). He\nis very clever with his hands. In fact his cleverness with his hands has\nseveral times brought him into great dangers.\n", "I wished to send you so many nice things, but alas! the thought of the\ntariff makes my desires vanish \"like youth in women and beggars' dreams\".\n", "By the by, I am glad now that I am growing grey every day. My head will be a\nfull-blown white lotus by the time you see me next.\n", "Ah! Mary, if you could see Kashmir \u2014 only Kashmir; the marvellous lakes full\nof lotuses and swans (there are no swans but geese \u2014 poetic licence) and the\nbig black bee trying to settle on the wind-shaken lotus (I mean the lotus\nnods  him  off  refusing  a kiss \u2014 poetry), then you could have a good\nconscience on your death-bed. As this is earthly paradise and as logic says\none bird in the hand is equal to two in the bush, a glimpse of this is\nwiser, but economically the other better; no trouble, no labour, no expense,\na little namby-pamby dolly life and later, that is all.\n", "My letter is becoming a bore . . . so I stop. (It is sheer idleness). Good\nnight.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "My address always is:\nMath, Belur,\nHowrah Dist., Bengal, India.\n"]

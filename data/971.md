["\n", "CXXIV\n", "To Swami Ramakrishnananda\n", "MATH, BELUR,\n", "(Howrah),\nMarch, 1898.\n", "MY DEAR SHASHI,\n", "I  forgot  to write you about two things. 1. That Tulsi ought to learn\nshorthand from Goodwin, at least the beginning. 2. I had to write a letter\nalmost every mail to Madras while I was out of India. I have in vain written\nfor a copy of those letters. Send me all those letters. I want to write out\nmy travels. Do not fail, and I shall send them back as soon as they have\nbeen used up. The Dawn can manage with 200 subscribers to come out regularly\non Rs. 40/- an issue expenditure. This is a great fact to know. The P.B.\n(Prabuddha Bh\u00e2rata) seems to be very disorganised; try best to organise it.\nPoor  Alasinga, I am sorry for him. Only thing I can do is to make him\nentirely  free  for a year so that he may devote all his energy to the\nBrahmavadin work. Tell him not to worry; I have him always in mind, poor\nchild; his devotion I can never repay.\n", "I am thinking of going to Kashmir again with Mrs. Bull and Miss MacLeod. (I)\nreturn to Calcutta and start for America from here.\n", "Miss Noble is really an acquisition. She will soon surpass Mrs. Besant as a\nspeaker, I am sure.\n", "Do look after Alasinga. I have an idea that he is breaking himself with\nwork. Tell him, the best work is only done by alternate repose and work.\nGive him all my love. We had two public lectures in Calcutta, one from Miss\nNoble and the other from our Sharat. Both of them did very well indeed;\nthere was great enthusiasm, which shows that the Calcutta public has not\nforgotten us. Some of the members of the Math had a touch of influenza. They\nare all right now. The thing is working nicely. Shri (Holy) Mother is here,\nand the European and American ladies went the other day to see her, and what\ndo you think, Mother ate with them even there! Is not that grand? The Lord\nis watching over us; there is no fear; do not lose your nerves, keep your\nhealth and take things easy. It is always good to give a few strong strokes\nand rest on your oars. Rakhal is living with the new land and buildings. I\nwas not satisfied with the Mahotsava this year. What it should be is a grand\nmixture of all the different phases here. We shall try it next year \u2014 I\nshall send instructions. With love to all of you there and blessings.\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

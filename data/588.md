["\n", "CXXVII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "ALMORA,\n", "14th June, 1897.\n", "DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I am wholly in sympathy with the subject-matter of the letter of Charu that\nyou have sent me.\n", "In the proposed Address to the Queen-Empress the following points should be\nnoted:\n", "1. That it must be free from exaggeration, in other words, statements to the\neffect that she is God's regent and so forth, which are so common to us\nnatives.\n", "2. That all religions having been protected during her reign, we have been\nable fearlessly to preach our Vedantic doctrines both in India and England.\n", "3. Her kindness towards the Indian poor \u2014 as, for instance, her inspiring\nthe English to unique acts of charity by contributing herself to the cause\nof famine-relief.\n", "4. Prayer for her long life and for the continual growth of happiness and\nprosperity among the people of her dominions.\n", "Have this written in correct English and send it to me at Almora, and I\nshall  sign  it and send it to Simla. Let me know to whom it should be\naddressed at Simla.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Let Shuddhananda preserve a copy of the weekly letters that he writes to\nme from the Math.\n", "V.\n"]

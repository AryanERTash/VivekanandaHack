["LXXIV\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "228 W. 39TH STREET\n", "[NEW YORK\n", "Dec. 10, 1895\n", "D CHRISTINA,\n", "Perhaps by this time you have received my first letter. I received yours\njust now.\n", "I had a splendid success in England and have left a nucleus there to work\ntill my arrival next summer. You will be astonished to learn that some of my\nstrongest friends are big \"guns\" of the Church of England.\n", "This Christmas I am going away a week, from 24th Decem., to the country with\nMr. and Mrs. Leggett \u2014 after that I resume my work. In the meanwhile the\nclasses have begun.\n", "I have written to you my intention of taking a quick turn through Detroit\nand Chicago in the meanwhile and [then] return back.\n", "Give Mrs. Phelps my love and kindly arrange the classes [in Detroit] with\nher. The best thing is to arrange for a public lecture where I give out my\ngeneral plan of work. The Unitarian church is available; and if the lecture\nis free, there will be a big crowd. The collection most possibly will cover\nthe expenses. Then out of this we will get the materials of a big class and\nthen hurry them through, leaving Mrs. Phelps and you and Mrs. Funkey [Funke]\nto work on with them.\n", "This plan is entirely feasible and if Mrs. Phelps and Mrs. Bagley desire it,\nthey can work it out very quickly.\n", "Ever yours with love and blessings,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

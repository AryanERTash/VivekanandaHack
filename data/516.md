["\n", "LV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "\n(March or April?) 1894.\n", "MY DEAR AKHANDANANDA,\n", "I am very glad to receive your letter. It is a great pleasure to me to learn\nthat you have regained your health to a great extent by your stay at Khetri.\n", "Brother Tarak (Shivananda) has done a good deal of work in Madras. Very\nagreeable news indeed! I heard much praise of him from the people of Madras.\n. . .\n", "Try to develop spirituality and philanthropy amongst the Thakurs in the\ndifferent places of Rajputana. We must work, and this cannot be done by\nmerely sitting idle. Make a trip now and then to Malsisar, Alsisar, and all\nthe other \"sars\" that are there. And carefully learn Sanskrit and English.\nGunanidhi is in the Punjab, I presume. Convey my special love to him and\nbring him to Khetri. Learn Sanskrit with his help, and teach him English.\nLet me have his address by all means. ...\n", "Go from door to door amongst the poor and lower classes of the town of\nKhetri  and  teach  them religion. Also, let them have oral lessons on\ngeography and such other subjects. No good will come of sitting idle and\nhaving princely dishes, and saying \"Ramakrishna, O Lord!\" \u2014 unless you can\ndo some good to the poor. Go to other villages from time to time, and teach\nthe people the arts of life as well as religion. Work, worship, and Jnana\n(knowledge)  \u2014  first  work, and your mind will be purified; otherwise\neverything will be fruitless like pouring oblations on a pile of ashes\ninstead of in the sacred fire. When Gunanidhi comes, move from door to door\nof the poor and the destitute in every village of Rajputana. If people\nobject  to  the  kind  of food you take, give it up immediately. It is\npreferable to live on grass for the sake of doing good to others. The Geru\u00e2\nrobe is not for enjoyment. It is the banner of heroic work. You must give\nyour body, mind, and speech to \"the welfare of the world\". You have read\u2014 \"\u092e\u093e\u0924\u0943\u0926\u0947\u0935\u094b \u092d\u0935, \u092a\u093f\u0930\u0943\u0926\u0947\u0935\u094b \u092d\u0935 \u2014 Look upon your mother as God, look upon your father as God\" \u2014 but I say \"\u0926\u0930\u093f\u0926\u094d\u0930\u0926\u0947\u0935\u094b \u092d\u0935, \u092e\u0942\u0930\u094d\u0916\u0926\u0947\u0935\u094b \u092d\u0935 \u2014 The poor, the\nilliterate, the ignorant, the afflicted \u2014 let these be your God.\" Know that\nservice to these alone is the highest religion.\n", "Ever yours, with blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

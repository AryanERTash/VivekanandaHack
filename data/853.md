## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "VI\n", "BARODA,\n", "26th April,1892.\n", "DEAR DIWANJI SAHEB, (Shri Haridas Viharidas Desai)\n", "Very  happy to receive your kind letter even here. I had not the least\ndifficulty in reaching your house from the station of Nadiad. And your\nbrothers, they are what they should be, your brothers. May the Lord shower\nhis choicest blessings on your family. I have never found such a glorious\none in all my travels. Your friend Mr. Manibhai has provided every comfort\nfor me; but, as to his company, I have only seen him twice; once for a\nminute, the other for ten minutes at the most when he talked about the\nsystem  of  education here. Of course, I have seen the Library and the\npictures of Ravi Varma, and that is about all worth seeing here. So I am\ngoing off this evening to Bombay. My thanks to the Diwanji here (or to you)\nfor his kind behaviour. More from Bombay.\n", "Yours in affection,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. At Nadiad I met Mr. Manilal Nabhubhai. He is a very learned and pious\ngentleman, and I enjoyed his company much.\n"]

["\n", "XXI\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "541 DEARBORN AVENUE\n", "CHICAGO\n", "9 June 1894\n", "DEAR MOTHER,\n", "We are all doing very well here. Last night the sisters (The daughters of\nMrs. Hale: Mary and Harriet.) invited me and Mrs. Norton and Miss Howe and\nMr. Frank Howe. We had a grand dinner and softshell crab and many other\nthings, and a very nice time. Miss Howe left this morning.\n", "The sisters and Mother Temple (Mrs. James Matthews, Mr. Hale\u2019s sister.>) are\ntaking very good care of me. Just now I am going to see my \"oh-my-dear\"\nGandhi.[6]* Narasimha was here yesterday; he wanted to go to Cincinnati\nwhere he says he has more chances of success than anywhere else in the\nworld. I gave him the passage, and so I hope I have got the white elephant\nout of my hands for the time being. How is Father Pope doing now? Hope he\nhas been much benefited by the mudfish business.[7]*\n", "I had a very beautiful letter from Miss Guernsey of New York, giving you her\nregards. I am going downtown to buy a new pair of shoes as well as to get\nsome money, my purse having been made empty by Narasimha.\n", "Nothing more to write. Yes, we went to see the \"Charley's Aunt\".[8]* I\nnearly killed myself with laughing. Father Pope will enjoy it extremely. I\nhad never seen anything so funny.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "CLXXV\n", "To Mrs. Alice (Shanti) Hansbrough\n", "THE VEDANTA SOCIETY,\n", "102 EAST 58TH STREET, NEW YORK, N.Y.\n", "[End of June 1900]\n", "DEAR MRS. HANSBROUGH \u2014\n", "I have not written you a line since you left San Francisco. I am well and\nthings are going on well with me.\n", "I am in New York once more, where they have got now a home for the Society\nand their headquarters. I and the other Swamis also live there.\n", "A San Francisco lady [Miss Minnie C. Boock] now here owns a plot of land\nnear Mt. Hamilton, 12 miles east of Lick observatory \u2014 160 acres in area.\nShe is going to make us a present of it. It would be nice for a summer\ngathering for us in California. If friends like to go there now, I will send\nthem the written authority. Will you write to Mrs. Aspinall and Miss Bell\netc., about it? I am rather desirous it should be occupied this summer as\nsoon as possible. There is only a log cabin on the land; for the rest they\nmust have tents.\n", "I am sorry I can not spare a Swami yet.\n", "With all love to you and Mrs. [Carrie Mead] Wyckoff and the baby of the\nfamily.\n", "Ever yours in the Truth,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "P.S.  Tell Helen [the youngest Mead sister] \u2014 I thank her for her kind\ninvitation, but [am] so sorry [I] can not accept it now. After all, you\nthree sisters have become a part of my mind forever. What about the club?\n", "V.\n"]

["\n", "CLXVIII\n", "To Mrs. Ole Bull (in London)\n", "SAN FRANCISCO\n", "18 May 1900\n", "MY DEAR MOTHER,\n", "Many thanks for Joe's [Miss Josephine MacLeod's] and your letters. I have\nagain  a  bad  relapse \u2014 and [am] struggling out of it. This time I am\nperfectly certain that with me all diseases are nervous. I want rest for\ntwo, three years \u2014 and not the least bit of work between. I will take rest\nwith the Seviers in the Himalayas.\n", "Mrs.  [James  Henry]  Sevier  gave  me 6,000 Rs. for family \u2014 this was\ndistributed between my cousin, aunt, etc. The 5,000 Rs. for buying the house\nwas borrowed from the Math funds. Do not stop the remittance you send to my\ncousin, whatever Saradananda may say to the contrary. Of course I do not\nknow what he says.\n", "I have long given up the idea of a little house on the Ganges, as I have not\nthe money.\n", "But I have got some in Calcutta and some with the Leggetts, and if you give\na thousand more, that will be a fund for my own personal expenses (as you\nknow I never took Math money) as well as for my mother. Kindly write to\nSaradananda to give up the little house plan. I am not going to write any\nmore for weeks yet \u2014 till I completely recover. I hope to get over [it] in a\nfew weeks from now \u2014 it was a terrible relapse. I am with a Doctor friend\n[Dr. Milburn H. Logan], and he is taking every care of me.\n", "Tell Joe that going amongst different people with a message also does not\nbelong to the Sannyasin; for a Sannyasin, [there] is quiet and retirement,\nscarcely seeing the face of man.\n", "I am now ripe for that, physically at least. If I don't go into retirement,\nnature will force me to it. Many thanks that temporal things have been so\nwell arranged by you.\n", "With all love to Joe and yourself \u2014\n", "Your Son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

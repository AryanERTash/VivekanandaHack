GEORGE'S ROAD, LONDON,\n", "7th June, 1896.\n", "DEAR MISS NOBLE,\n", "My ideal indeed can be put into a few words and that is: to preach unto\nmankind their divinity, and how to make it manifest in every movement of\nlife.\n", "This world is in chain of superstition. I pity the oppressed, whether man or\nwoman, and I pity more the oppressors.\n", "One idea that I see clear as daylight is that misery is caused by ignorance\nand nothing else. Who will give the world light? Sacrifice in the past has\nbeen the Law, it will be, alas, for ages to come. The earth's bravest and\nbest will have to sacrifice themselves for the good of many, for the welfare\nof all. Buddhas by the hundred are necessary with eternal love and pity.\n", "Religions of the world have become lifeless mockeries. What the world wants\nis character. The world is in need of those whose life is one burning love,\nselfless. That love will make every word tell like thunderbolt.\n", "It is no superstition with you, I am sure, you have the making in you of a\nworld-mover, and others will also come. Bold words and bolder deeds are what\nwe want. Awake, awake, great ones! The world is burning with misery. Can you\nsleep? Let us call and call till the sleeping gods awake, till the god\nwithin answers to the call. What more is in life? What greater work? The\ndetails  come  to  me as I go. I never make plans. Plans grow and work\nthemselves. I only say, awake, awake!\n", "May all blessings attend you for ever!\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

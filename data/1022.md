["\n", "CLXXV\n", "To an American friend\n", "ALAMEDA, CALIFORNIA,\n", "12th April, 1900.\n", "Mother is becoming propitious once more. Things are looking up. They must.\n", "Work always brings evil with it. I have paid for the accumulated evil with\nbad  health.  I  am glad. My mind is all the better for it. There is a\nmellowness and a calmness in life now, which was never there before. I am\nlearning now how to be detached as well as attached, and mentally becoming\nmy own master. . . .\n", "Mother is doing Her own work; I do not worry much now. Moths like me die by\nthe  thousand every instant. Her work goes on all the same. Glory unto\nMother! . . . Alone and drifting about in the will-current of the Mother has\nbeen my whole life. The moment I have tried to break this, that moment I\nhave been hurt. Her will be done! . . .\n", "I am happy, at peace with myself, and more of the Sannyasin than I ever was\nbefore. The love for my own kith and kin is growing less every day, and that\nfor  Mother  increasing.  Memories  of  long nights of vigil with Shri\nRamakrishna under the Dakshineswar Banyan are waking up once more. And work?\nWhat is work? Whose work? Whom shall I work for?\n", "I am free. I am Mother's child. She works, She plays. Why should I plan?\nWhat should I plan? Things came and went, just as She liked, without my\nplanning. We are Her automata. She is the wirepuller.\n"]

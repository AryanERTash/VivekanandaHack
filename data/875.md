["\n", "XXVIII\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "HOTEL BELLEVUE,\n", "BEACON ST., BOSTON,\n", "13th September, 1894.\n", "DEAR SISTER,\n", "Your kind note reached me this morning. I have been in this hotel for about\na  week. I will remain in Boston some time yet. I have plenty of gowns\nalready, in fact, more than I can carry with ease. When I had that drenching\nin Annisquam, I had on that beautiful black suit you appreciate so much, and\nI do not think it can be damaged any way; it also has been penetrated with\nmy deep meditations on the Absolute. I am very glad that you enjoyed the\nsummer so well. As for me, I am vagabondising. I was very much amused the\nother day at reading Abe Hue's description of the vagabond lamas of Tibet\n\u2014 a true picture of our fraternity. He says they are queer people. They come\nwhen they will, sit at everybody's table, invitation or no invitation, live\nwhere they will, and go where they will. There is not a mountain they have\nnot climbed, not a river they have not crossed, not a nation they do not\nknow, not a language they do not talk. He thinks that God must have put into\nthem  a part of that energy which makes the planets go round and round\neternally. Today this vagabond lama was seized with a desire of going right\nalong scribbling, and so I walked down and entering a store bought all sorts\nof writing material and a beautiful portfolio which shuts with a clasp and\nhas even a little wooden inkstand. So far it promises well. Hope it will\ncontinue. Last month I had mail enough from India and am greatly delighted\nwith my countrymen at their generous appreciation of my work. Good enough\nfor them. I cannot find anything more to write. Prof. Wright, his wife, and\nchildren were as good as ever. Words cannot express my gratitude to them.\n", "Everything so far is not going bad with me except that I had a bad cold. Now\nI think the fellow is gone. This time I tried Christian Science for insomnia\nand really found it worked very well. Wishing you all happiness, I remain,\never your affectionate brother,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Kindly tell Mother that I do not want any coat now.\n"]

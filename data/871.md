["\n", "XXIV\n", "To the Hale Sisters\n", "SWAMPSCOTT,\n", "26th July, 1894.\n", "DEAR BABIES,\n", "Now don't let my letters stray beyond the circle, please. I had a beautiful\nletter from sister Mary. See how I am getting the dash, sister Jeany teaches\nme all that. She can jump and run and play and swear like a devil and talk\nslang at the rate of 500 a minute; only she does not much care for religion,\nonly a little. She is gone today home, and I am going to Greenacre. I had\nbeen to see Mrs. Breed. Mrs. Stone was there, with whom is residing Mrs.\nPullman and all the golden bugs, my old friends hereabouts. They are kind as\nusual. On my way back from Greenacre I am going to Annisquam to see Mrs.\nBagley for a few days.\n", "Darn it, forget everything. I had duckings in the sea like a fish. I am\nenjoying every bit of it. What nonsense was the song Harriet taught me \"dans\nla plaine\" the deuce take it. I told it to a French scholar and he laughed\nand laughed till the fellow was well-nigh burst at my wonderful translation.\nThat is the way you would have taught me French! You are a pack of fools and\nheathens,  I tell you. Now are you gasping for breath like a huge fish\nstranded? I am glad that you are sizzling. Oh! how nice and cool it is here,\nand it is increased a hundred-fold when I think about the gasping, sizzling,\nboiling, frying four old maids, and how cool and nice I am here. Whoooooo!\n", "Miss Phillips has a beautiful place somewhere in N.Y. State \u2014 mountain,\nlake, river, forest altogether \u2014 what more? I am going to make a Himalayas\nthere and start a monastery as sure as I am living \u2014 I am not going to leave\nthis country without throwing one more apple of discord into this already\nroaring, fighting, kicking, mad whirlpool of American religion. Well, dear\nold maids, you sometimes have a glimpse of the lake and on every hot noon,\nthink of going down to the bottom of the lake, down, down, down, until it is\ncool and nice, and then to lie down on the bottom, with that coolness above\nand around, and lie there still, silent, and just doze \u2014 not sleep, but\ndreamy dozing half unconscious sort of bliss \u2014 very much like that which\nopium brings; that is delicious; and drinking lots of iced water. Lord bless\nmy soul \u2014 I had such cramps several times as would have killed an elephant.\nSo I hope to keep myself away from the cold water.\n", "May you be all happy, dear fin de si\u00e8cle young ladies, is the constant\nprayer of VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

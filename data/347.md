["\n", "CXII\n", "\n", "\nBlessed And Beloved,[1] \n", "\nI had to consult Brahmananda and others, and they were everyone in Calcutta,\nhence the delay in replying to your last.\n", "\nThe  idea  of  taking a house for a whole year must be worked out with\ndeliberation. As on the one hand there is some risk of catching malaria in\nBelur this month, in Calcutta on the other hand there is the danger of\nplague. Then again one is sure to avoid fever if one takes good care not to\ngo into the interior of this village, the immediate bank of the river being\nentirely free from fever. Plague has not come to the river yet, and all the\navailable places in this village are filled with Marwaris during the plague\nseason.\n", "\nThen again you ought to mention the maximum rent you can pay, and we seek\nthe house accordingly. The quarter in the city is another suggestion. For\nmyself, I have almost become a foreigner to Calcutta. But others will soon\nfind a house after your mind. The sooner you decide these two points: (1)\nWhether mother stays at Belur or Calcutta, (2) If Calcutta, what rent and\nquarter, the better, as it can be done in a trice after receiving your\nreply.\n", "\n", "Yours with love and blessings,\nVivekananda.\n", "\nPS.  We are all right here. Moti has returned after his week's stay in\nCalcutta. It is raining here day and night last three days. Two of our cows\nhave calved.\n"]

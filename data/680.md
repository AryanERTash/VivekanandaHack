["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "THURSDAY, August 1, 1895.\n", "The real Guru is the one through whom we have our spiritual descent. He is\nthe channel through which the spiritual current flows to us, the link which\njoins us to the whole spiritual world. Too much faith in personality has a\ntendency to produce weakness and idolatry, but intense love for the Guru\nmakes rapid growth possible, he connects us with the internal Guru. Adore\nyour Guru if there be real truth in him; that Guru-bhakti (devotion to the\nteacher) will quickly lead you to the highest.\n", "Sri Ramakrishna's purity was that of a baby. He never touched money in his\nlife,  and  lust was absolutely annihilated in him. Do not go to great\nreligious teachers to learn physical science, their whole energy has gone to\nthe spiritual. In Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa the man was all dead and only\nGod remained; he actually could not see sin, he was literally \"of purer eyes\nthan to behold iniquity\". The purity of these few Paramahamsa (Monks of the\nhighest order) is all that holds the world together. If they should all die\nout and leave it, the world would go to pieces. They do good by simply\nbeing, and they know it not; they just are. . . .\n", "Books suggest the inner light and the method of bringing that out, but we\ncan only understand them when we have earned the knowledge ourselves. When\nthe inner light has flashed for you, let the books go, and look only within.\nYou have in you all and a thousand times more than is in all the books.\nNever lose faith in yourself, you can do anything in this universe. Never\nweaken, all power is yours.\n", "If religion and life depend upon books or upon the existence of any prophet\nwhatsoever, then perish all religion and books! Religion is in us. No books\nor teachers can do more than help us to find it, and even without them we\ncan get all truth within. You have gratitude for books and teachers without\nbondage to them; and worship your Guru as God, but do not obey him blindly;\nlove him all you will, but think for yourself. No blind belief can save you,\nwork out your own salvation. Have only one idea of God \u2014 that He is an\neternal help.\n", "Freedom  and  highest love must go together, then neither can become a\nbondage. We can give nothing to God; He gives all to us. He is the Guru of\nGurus. Then we find that He is the \"Soul of our souls\", our very Self. No\nwonder we love Him, He is the Soul of our souls; whom or what else can we\nlove? We want to be the \"steady flame, burning without heat and without\nsmoke\". To whom can you do good, when you see only God? You cannot do good\nto God! All doubt goes, all is, \"sameness\". If you do good at all, you do it\nto yourself; feel that the receiver is the higher one. You serve the other\nbecause you are lower than he, not because he is low and you are high. Give\nas the rose gives perfume, because it is its own nature, utterly unconscious\nof giving.\n", "The great Hindu reformer, Raja Ram Mohan Roy, was a wonderful example of\nthis unselfish work. He devoted his whole life to helping India. It was he\nwho stopped the burning of widows. It is usually believed that this reform\nwas due entirely to the English; but it was Raja Ram Mohan Roy who started\nthe agitation against the custom and succeeded in obtaining the support of\nthe Government in suppressing it. Until he began the movement, the English\nhad done nothing. He also founded the important religious Society called the\nBrahmo-Samaj,  and  subscribed  a  hundred thousand dollars to found a\nuniversity. He then stepped out and told them to go ahead without him. He\ncared nothing for fame or for results to himself.\n", "THURSDAY AFTERNOON.\n", "There are endless series of manifestations, like \"merry-go-round\", in which\nthe souls ride, so to speak. The series are eternal; individual souls get\nout, but the events repeat themselves eternally; and that is how one's past\nand future can be read, because all is really present. When the soul is in a\ncertain chain, it has to go through the experiences of that chain. From one\nseries souls go to other series; from some series they escape for ever by\nrealising that they are Brahman. By getting hold of one prominent event in a\nchain and holding on to it, the whole chain can be dragged in and read. This\npower is easily acquired, but it is of no real value; and to practise it\ntakes just so much from our spiritual forces. Go not after these things,\nworship God.\n"]

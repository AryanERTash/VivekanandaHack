["\n", "CXCVI\n", "To Miss Josephine MacLeod\n", "PORT TEWFICK\n", "26th November, 1900.\n", "DEAR JOE,\n", "The steamer was late; so I am waiting. Thank goodness, it entered the Canal\nthis  morning at Port Said. That means it will arrive some time in the\nevening if everything goes right.\n", "Of course it is like solitary imprisonment these two days, and I am holding\nmy soul in patience.\n", "But they say the change is thrice dear. Mr. Gaze's agent gave me all wrong\ndirections. In the first place, there was nobody here to tell me a thing,\nnot to speak of receiving me. Secondly, I was not told that I had to change\nmy Gaze's ticket for a steamer one at the agent's office, and that was at\nSuez, not here. It was good one way, therefore, that the steamer was late;\nso I went to see the agent of the steamer and he told me to exchange Gaze's\npass for a regular ticket.\n", "I hope to board the steamer some time tonight. I am well and happy and am\nenjoying the fun immensely. How is Mademoiselle? Where is Bois? Give my\neverlasting gratitude and good wishes to Mme. Calve. She is a good lady.\n", "Hoping you will enjoy your trip.\n", "Ever affectionately yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

Mixing\nwith Natives is damning to the Europeans.\n", "There are no good hotels to speak of even at the capitals. You will have to\ntravel with a number of servants about you (cost cheaper than hotels). You\nwill have to bear with people who wear only a loin cloth; you will see me\nwith  only a loin cloth about me. Dirt and filth everywhere, and brown\npeople. But you will have plenty of men to talk to you philosophy. If you\nmix with the English much here, you will have more comforts but see nothing\nof the Hindus as they are. Possibly I will not be able to eat with you, but\nI promise that I will travel to good many places with you and do everything\nin my power to make your journey pleasant. These are what you expect; if\nanything good comes, so much the better. Perhaps Mary Hale may come over\nwith  you. There is a young lady, Miss Campbell, Orchard Lake, Orchard\nIsland, Michigan, who is a great worshipper of Krishna and lives alone in\nthat Island, fasting and praying. She will give anything to be able to see\nIndia once, but she is awfully poor. If you bring her with you, I will\nanyhow manage to pay her expenses. If Mrs. Bull brings old Landsberg with\nher, that will be saving that fool's life as it were.\n", "Most probably I may accompany you back to America. Kiss Holister for me and\nthe baby. My love to Alberta, to the Leggetts, and to Mabel. What is Fox\ndoing? Give him my love when you see him. To Mrs. Bull and S. Saradananda my\nlove. I am as strong as ever, but it all depends upon leading a quiet life\never afterwards. No hurly-burly any more.\n", "I had a great mind to go to Tibet this year; but they would not allow me, as\nthe road is dreadfully fatiguing. However, I content myself with galloping\nhard over precipices on mountain ponies. (This is more exciting than your\nbicycle even, although I had an experience of that at Wimbledon.) Miles and\nmiles of uphill and miles and miles of downhill, the road a few feet broad\nhanging over sheer precipices several thousand feet deep below.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS.

["\n", "XC\n", "\n", "\nMy Dear Joe,[1] \n", "\nYou must have reached N.Y. by this time and are in the midst of your own\nafter a long absence. Fortune has favoured you at every step of this journey\n\u2014 even the sea was smooth and calm, and the ship nearly empty of undesirable\ncompany. Well, with me it is doing otherwise. I am almost desperate I could\nnot accompany you. Neither did the change at Vaidyanath do me any good. I\nnearly died there, was suffocating for eight days and nights!! I was brought\nback to Calcutta more dead than alive, and here I am struggling to get back\nto life again.\n", "\nDr. Sarkar is treating me now.\n", "\nI am not so despondent now as I was. I am reconciled to my fate. This year\nseems to be very hard for us. Yogananda, who used to live in Mother's house,\nis suffering for the last month and every day is at death's door. Mother\nknows best. I am roused to work again, though not personally but am sending\nthe boys all over India to make a stir once more. Above all, as you know,\nthe chief difficulty is of funds. Now that you are in America, Joe, try to\nraise some funds for our work over here.\n", "\nI hope to rally again by March, and by April I start for Europe. Again\nMother knows best.\n", "\nI have suffered mentally and physically all my life, but Mother's kindness\nhas  been  immense. The joy and blessings I had infinitely more than I\ndeserve. And I am struggling not to fail Mother, but that she will always\nfind me fighting, end my last breath will be on the battlefield.\n", "\nMy best love and blessings for you ever and ever.\n", "\n", "Ever yours in the Truth,\nVivekananda.\n"]

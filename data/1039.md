["\n", "CXCII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Turiyananda\n", "6 PLACE DES ETATS UNIS,\n", "DA FOREST P.O., SANTA CLARA CO.,\n", "PARIS, FRANCE,\n", "1st September, 1900.\n", "MY DEAR HARI,\n", "I learnt everything from your letter. Earlier I had an inkling of some\ntrouble between the full-fledged Vedantist and the Home of Truth  \u2014 someone\nwrote that. Such things do occur; wisdom consists in carrying on the work by\ncleverly keeping all in good humour.\n", "For some time now I have been living incognito. I shall stay with the French\nto pick up their language. I am somewhat freed from worries; that is to say,\nI have signed the trust-deed and other things and sent them to Calcutta. I\nhave  not  reserved any right or ownership for myself. You now possess\neverything and will manage all work by the Master's grace.\n", "I have no longer any desire to kill myself by touring. For the present I\nfeel like settling down somewhere and spending my time among books. I have\nsomewhat mastered the French language; but if I stay among the French for a\nmonth or two, I shall be able to carry on conversation well. If one can\nmaster this language and German sufficiently, one can virtually become well\nacquainted  with  European  learning.  The  people  of France are mere\nintellectualists, they run after worldly things and firmly believe God and\nsouls  to  be  superstitious; they are extremely loath to talk on such\nsubjects. This is a truly materialistic country!\nLet me see what that Lord does. But this country is at the head of Western\nculture, and Paris is the capital of that culture.\n", "Brother, free me from all work connected with preaching. I am now aloof from\nall that, you manage it yourselves. It is my firm conviction that Mother\nwill get work done through all of you a hundredfold more than through me.\n", "Many days ago I received a letter from Kali. He must have reached New York\nby now. Miss Waldo sends news now and then.\n", "I keep sometimes well and sometimes bad. Of late I am again having that\nmassage treatment by Mrs. Milton, who says, \"You have already recovered!\"\nThis much I see \u2014 whatever the flatulence, I feel no difficulty in moving,\nwalking, or even climbing. In the morning I take vigorous exercise, and then\nhave a dip in cold water.\n", "Yesterday I went to see the house of the gentleman with whom I shall stay.\nHe is a poor scholar, has his room filled with books and lives in a flat on\nthe fifth floor. And as there are no lifts in this country as in America,\none has to climb up and down. But it is no longer trying to me.\n", "There is a beautiful public park round the house. The gentleman cannot speak\nEnglish; that is a further reason for my going. I shall have to speak French\nperforce. It is all Mother's will. She knows best what She wants to have\ndone. She never speaks out, \"only keeps mum\". But this much I notice that\nfor a month or so I have been having intense meditation and repetition of\nthe Lord's name.\n", "Please convey my love to Miss Boocke, Miss Bell, Mrs. Aspinel, Miss Beckham,\nMr. George, Dr. Logan, and other friends and accept it yourself. My love to\nall in Los Angeles also.\n", "Yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

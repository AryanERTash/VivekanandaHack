["\n", "XLIII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "BELUR MATH,\n", "16th April, 1899.\n", "DEAR MADAM (Shrimati Sarala Ghosal, B. A.),\n", "Very glad to receive your kind note. If by the sacrifice of some specially\ncherished object of either myself or my brother-disciples many pure and\ngenuinely patriotic souls come forward to help our cause, rest assured, we\nwill not hesitate in the least to make that sacrifice nor shed a tear-drop\n\u2014 you will see this verified in action. But up till now I have seen nobody\ncoming forward to assist in this way. Only some have wished to put their own\nhobby in place of ours \u2014 that is all. If it really help our country or\nhumanity \u2014 not to speak of giving up Guru-worship \u2014 believe me, we are\nprepared to commit any dire iniquity and suffer the eternal damnation of the\nChristians. But my hairs have turned grey since I began the study of man.\nThis world is a most trying place, and it is long since I have taken to\nwandering with the lantern of the Grecian Philosopher in hand. A popular\nsong my Master often used to sing comes to my mind:\n", "\"He who's a man after one's heart\nBetrays himself by his very looks.\nRare indeed is such a one!\nHe's a man of aesthetic perceptions\nWho treads a path contrary to others.\"\n", "This much from my side. Please know that not one word of it is exaggerated\n\u2014 which you will find to be actually the case.\nBut then I have some doubts about those patriotic souls who can join with us\nif only we give up the worship of the Guru. Well, if, as they pose, they are\nindeed panting and struggling so much \u2014 almost to the point of dissolution\nfrom their body \u2014 to serve the country, how can the single accident of\nGuru-worship stop everything!\n", "This impetuous river with rolling waves which bade fair to sweep away whole\nhills and mountains \u2014 was a bit of Guru-worship sufficient to turn it back\nto the Himalayas! I put it to you, do you think anything great will come of\nsuch patriotism, or any substantial good proceed from such assistance? It is\nfor you to say; I can make nothing out of it. For a thirsty man to weigh so\nmuch the merits of water, or for a man about to die of hunger to cogitate so\nmuch and turn up his nose at the food presented! Well, people have strange\nways of thinking. I, for one, am inclined to think that those people were\nbest in a glass-case; the more they keep away from actual work, the better.\n", "\"Love stops not for questions of birth.\nNor the hungry man for stale food.\"\n", "This is what I know. But I may be wholly mistaken. Well, if this trifle of\nGuru-worship sticks in one's throat to choke one to death, we had better\nextricate him from this predicament.\n", "However,  I have a great longing to talk over these points with you in\ndetail. For talking these things over, affliction and death have given me\nleave till now, and I hope they will do so yet.\n", "May all your wishes be fulfilled in this New Year!\n", "Yours sincerely,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

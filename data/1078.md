I am glad that they appreciate me \u2014 not\nfor my sake, but that I am firmly persuaded that a man is never improved by\nabuse but by praise, and so with nations. Think how much of abuse has been\nquite unnecessarily hurled at the head of my devoted, poor country, and for\nwhat?  They  never  injured  the Christians or their religion or their\npreachers.

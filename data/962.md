## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "CXV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "LAHORE,\n", "15th November, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I hope you and Hari are now in good health. The work in Lahore went off with\ngreat \u00e9clat. Now I go to Dehra Dun. The Sind tour is postponed. I have yet\nno  news  whether Dinu, Latu, and Krishnalal have reached Jaipur. Babu\nNagendranath Gupta will collect subscriptions and donations from here and\nsend them to the Math to meet expenses. Send him regular receipts. Let me\nknow if you have received anything from Murree, Rawalpindi, and Sialkot.\n", "Reply to me C/o Post Master, Dehra Dun. Other letters you may send me after\nhearing from me from Dehra Dun. My health is good; only I have to get up at\nnight once or twice. I am having sound sleep; sleep is not spoiled even\nafter exhausting lectures; and I am doing exercise every day. . . . There is\nno trouble at all. Now, come on, work with redoubled energy. Keep an eye on\nthat big piece of land \u2014 in all secrecy. We are making regular efforts so\nthat big Utsava (Celebration \u2014 of Shri Ramakrishna's birthday.)can be held\nthere. My love to all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. It will be a very good thing if Master Mahashay will write now and then\nabout us in The Tribune, so that Lahore will not become cold again \u2014 now it\nis quite warmed up. Spend money a little economically; pilgrimage expenses\nshould be borne by you personally; preaching and propaganda expenses should\nbe charged to the Math.\n", "V.\n"]

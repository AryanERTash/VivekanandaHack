["\n", "CHAPTER III\nSPIRITUAL REALISATION, THE AIM OF BHAKTI-YOGA\n", "\nTo the Bhakta these dry details are necessary only to strengthen his will;\nbeyond that they are of no use to him. For he is treading on a path which is\nfitted  very soon to lead him beyond the hazy and turbulent regions of\nreason, to lead him to the realm of realisation. He, soon, through the mercy\nof the Lord, reaches a plane where pedantic and powerless reason is left far\nbehind, and the mere intellectual groping through the dark gives place to\nthe daylight of direct perception. He no more reasons and believes, he\nalmost perceives. He no more argues, he senses. And is not this seeing God,\nand feeling God, and enjoying God higher than everything else? Nay, Bhaktas\nhave not been wanting who have maintained that it is higher than even Moksha\n\u2014 liberation. And is it not also the highest utility? There are people \u2014 and\na good many of them too \u2014 in the world who are convinced that only that is\nof use and utility which brings to man creature-comforts. Even religion,\nGod, eternity, soul, none of these is of any use to them, as they do not\nbring them money or physical comfort. To such, all those things which do not\ngo to gratify the senses and appease the appetites are of no utility. In\nevery mind, utility, however, is conditioned by its own peculiar wants. To\nmen, therefore, who never rise higher than eating, drinking, begetting\nprogeny, and dying, the only gain is in sense enjoyments; and they must wait\nand go through many more births and reincarnations to learn to feel even the\nfaintest  necessity for anything higher. But those to whom the eternal\ninterests of the soul are of much higher value than the fleeting interests\nof this mundane life, to whom the gratification of the senses is but like\nthe thoughtless play of the baby, to them God and the love of God form the\nhighest and the only utility of human existence. Thank God there are some\nsuch still living in this world of too much worldliness.\n", "\nBhakti-Yoga, as we have said, is divided into the Gauni or the preparatory,\nand the Par\u00e2 or the supreme forms. We shall find, as we go on, how in the\npreparatory stage we unavoidably stand in need of many concrete helps to\nenable us to get on; and indeed the mythological and symbological parts of\nall religions are natural growths which early environ the aspiring soul and\nhelp it Godward. It is also a significant fact that spiritual giants have\nbeen produced only in those systems of religion where there is an exuberant\ngrowth of rich mythology and ritualism. The dry fanatical forms of religion\nwhich attempt to eradicate all that is poetical, all that is beautiful and\nsublime, all that gives a firm grasp to the infant mind tottering in its\nGodward way \u2014 the forms which attempt to break down the very ridge-poles of\nthe spiritual roof, and in their ignorant and superstitious conceptions of\ntruth try to drive away all that is life-giving, all that furnishes the\nformative material to the spiritual plant growing in the human soul \u2014 such\nforms of religion too soon find that all that is left to them is but an\nempty shell, a contentless frame of words and sophistry with perhaps a\nlittle flavour of a kind of social scavengering or the so-called spirit of\nreform.\n", "\nThe  vast  mass of those whose religion is like this, are conscious or\nunconscious materialists \u2014 the end and aim of their lives here and hereafter\nbeing enjoyment, which indeed is to them the alpha and the omega of human\nlife,  and  which  is  their Isht\u00e2purta; work like street-cleaning and\nscavengering, intended for the material comfort of man is, according to\nthem,  the  be-all  and end-all of human existence; and the sooner the\nfollowers of this curious mixture of ignorance and fanaticism come out in\ntheir  true colours and join, as they well deserve to do, the ranks of\natheists and materialists, the better will it be for the world. One ounce of\nthe practice of righteousness and of spiritual Self-realisation outweighs\ntons and tons of frothy talk and nonsensical sentiments. Show us one, but\none gigantic spiritual genius growing out of all this dry dust of ignorance\nand fanaticism; and if you cannot, close your mouths, open the windows of\nyour hearts to the clear light of truth, and sit like children at the feet\nof those who know what they are talking about \u2014 the sages of India. Let us\nthen listen attentively to what they say.\n"]

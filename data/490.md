["\n", "XXIX[6]*\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "GHAZIPUR,\n", "31st March, 1890.\n", "DEAR SIR,\n", "I haven't been here for the last few days and am again going away today. I\nhave asked brother Gangadhar to come here; and if he comes, we go over to\nyou together. For some special reasons, I shall continue to stay in secret\nin a village some distance from this place, and there's no facility for\nwriting any letter from that place, owing to which I could not reply to your\nletter so long. Brother Gangadhar is very likely to come, otherwise the\nreply to my note would have reached me. Brother Abhedananda is putting up\nwith Doctor Priya at Varanasi. Another brother of mine had been with me, but\nhas left for Abhedananda's place. The news of his arrival has not yet been\nreceived, and, his health being bad, I am rather anxious for his sake. I\nhave behaved very cruelly towards him \u2014 that is, I have harassed him much to\nmake  him  leave  my  company.  There's no help, you see; I am so very\nweak-hearted, so much overmastered by the distractions of love! Bless me\nthat I may harden. What shall I say to you about the condition of my mind!\nOh, it is as if the hell-fire is burning there day and night! Nothing,\nnothing could I do yet! And this life seems muddled away in vain; I feel\nquite helpless as to what to do! The Babaji throws out honeyed words and\nkeeps me from leaving. Ah, what shall I say? I am committing hundreds of\noffenses against you \u2014 please excuse them as so many misdoings of a man\ndriven mad with mental agonies. Abhedananda is suffering from dysentery. I\nshall be very much obliged if you will kindly inquire about his condition\nand send him down to our Math in case he wants to go there with our brother\nwho has come from here. My Gurubhais must be thinking me very cruel and\nselfish. Oh, what can I do? Who will see deep down into my mind? Who will\nknow how much I am suffering day and night? Bless me that I may have the\nmost unflinching patience and perseverance.\n", "With countless greetings,\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Abhedananda is staying in Doctor Priya's house at Sonarpura. My lumbago\nis as before.\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "MONDAY, August 5, 1895.\n", "The question is: Is it necessary to pass through all the lower stages to\nreach the highest, or can a plunge be taken at once? The modern American boy\ntakes twenty-five years to attain that which his forefathers took hundreds\nof years to do. The present-day Hindu gets in twenty years to the height\nreached in eight thousand years by his ancestors. On the physical side, the\nembryo goes from the amoeba to man in the womb. These are the teachings of\nmodern science. Vedanta goes further and tells us that we not only have to\nlive  the  life  of all past humanity, but also the future life of all\nhumanity. The man who does the first is the educated man, the second is the\nJivanmukta, for ever free (even while living).\n", "Time is merely the measure of our thoughts, and thought being inconceivably\nswift, there is no limit to the speed with which we can live the life ahead.\nSo it cannot be stated how long it would take to live all future life. It\nmight be in a second, or it might take fifty lifetimes. It depends on the\nintensity of the desire. The teaching must therefore be modified according\nto the needs of the taught. The consuming fire is ready for all, even water\nand chunks of ice quickly consume. Fire a mass of bird-shot, one at least\nwill strike; give a man a whole museum of truths, he will at once take what\nis suited to him. Past lives have moulded our tendencies; give to the taught\nin  accordance  with his tendency. Intellectual, mystical, devotional,\npractical \u2014 make one the basis, but teach the others with it. Intellect must\nbe balanced with love, the mystical nature with reason, while practice must\nform part of every method. Take every one where he stands and push him\nforward. Religious teaching must always be constructive, not destructive.\n", "Each tendency shows the life-work of the past, the line or radius along\nwhich that man must move. All radii lead to the centre. Never even attempt\nto  disturb anyone's tendencies; to do that puts back both teacher and\ntaught. When you teach Jnana, you must become a Jnani and stand mentally\nexactly where the taught stands. Similarly in every other Yoga. Develop\nevery faculty as if it were the only one possessed, this is the true secret\nof so-called harmonious development. That is, get extensity with intensity,\nbut not at its expense. We are infinite. There is no limitation in us, we\ncan be as intense as the most devoted Mohammedan and as broad as the most\nroaring atheist.\n", "The way to do this is not to put the mind on any one subject, but to develop\nand control the mind itself; then you can turn it on any side you choose.\nThus you keep the intensity and extensity. Feel Jnana as if it were all\nthere was, then do the same with Bhakti, with Raja (-Yoga), with Karma. Give\nup the waves and go to the ocean, then you can have the waves as you please.\nControl the \"lake\" of your own mind, else you cannot understand the lake of\nanother's mind.\n", "The true teacher is one who can throw his whole force into the tendency of\nthe taught. Without real sympathy we can never teach well. Give up the\nnotion that man is a responsible being, only the perfect man is responsible.\nThe ignorant have drunk deep of the cup of delusion and are not sane. You,\nwho know, must have infinite patience with these. Have nothing but love for\nthem and find out the disease that has made them see the world in a wrong\nlight, then help them to cure it and see aright. Remember always that only\nthe free have free will; all the rest are in bondage and are not responsible\nfor what they do. Will as will is bound. The water when melting on the top\nof the Himalayas is free, but becoming the river, it is bound by the banks;\nyet the original impetus carries it to the sea, and it regains its freedom.\nThe first is the \"fall of man\", the second is the \"resurrection\". Not one\natom can rest until it finds its freedom.\n", "Some imaginations help to break the bondage of the rest. The whole universe\nis imagination, but one set of imaginations will cure another set. Those\nwhich  tell us that there is sin and sorrow and death in the world are\nterrible; but the other set which says ever, \"I am holy, there is God, there\nis no pain\", these are good and help to break the bondage of the others. The\nhighest imagination that can break all the links of the chain is that of\nPersonal God.\n", "\"Om tat sat\" is the only thing beyond Maya, but God exists eternally. As\nlong as the Niagara Falls exist, the rainbow will exist; but the water\ncontinually flows away. The falls are the universe, and the rainbow is\npersonal God; and both are eternal. While the universe exists, God must\nexist. God creates the universe, and the universe creates God; and both are\neternal. Maya is neither existence nor non-existence. Both the Niagara Falls\nand the rainbow are eternally changeable. . . . Brahman seen through Maya.\nPersians and Christians split Maya into two and call the good half \"God\" and\nthe bad half the \"devil\". Vedanta takes Maya as a whole and recognises a\nunity beyond it \u2014 Brahman. . . .\n", "Mohammed found that Christianity was straying out from the Semitic fold and\nhis  teachings were to show what Christianity ought to be as a Semitic\nreligion, that it should hold to one God. The Aryan idea that \"I and my\nFather are one\" disgusted and terrified him. In reality the conception of\nthe Trinity was a great advance over the dualistic idea of Jehovah, who was\nfor ever separate from man. The theory of incarnation is the first link in\nthe chain of ideas leading to the recognition of the oneness of God and man.\nGod appearing first in one human form, then re-appearing at different times\nin other human forms, is at last recognised as being in every human form, or\nin all men. Monistic is the highest stage, monotheistic is a lower stage.\nImagination will lead you to the highest even more rapidly and easily than\nreasoning.\n", "Let a few stand out and live for God alone and save religion for the world.\nDo not pretend to be like Janaka when you are only the \"progenitor\" of\ndelusions. (The name Janaka means \"progenitor\" and belonged to a king who,\nalthough he still held his kingdom for the sake of his people, had given up\neverything mentally.) Be honest and say, \"I see the ideal but I cannot yet\napproach it\"; but do not pretend to give up when you do not. If you give up,\nstand fast. If a hundred fall in the fight, seize the flag and carry it on.\nGod is true for all that, no matter who fails. Let him who falls hand on the\nflag to another to carry on; it can never fall.\n", "When I am washed and clean, why shall impurity be added on to me? Seek first\nthe kingdom of Heaven, and let everything else go. Do not want anything\n\"added into you\"; be only glad to get rid of it. Give up and know that\nsuccess will follow, even if you never see it. Jesus left twelve fishermen,\nand yet those few blew up the Roman Empire.\n", "Sacrifice on God's altar earth's purest and best. He who struggles is better\nthan he who never attempts. Even to look on one who has given up has a\npurifying effect. Stand up for God; let the world go. Have no compromise.\nGive up the world, then alone you are loosened from the body. When it dies,\nyou are \u00e2z\u00e2d, free. Be free. Death alone can never free us. Freedom must be\nattained by our own efforts during life; then, when the body falls, there\nwill be no rebirth for the free.\n", "Truth is to be judged by truth and by nothing else. Doing good is not the\ntest of truth; the Sun needs no torch by which to see it. Even if truth\ndestroys the whole universe, still it is truth; stand by it.\n", "Practising the concrete forms of religion is easy and attracts the masses;\nbut really there is nothing in the external.\n", "\"As the spider throws her web out of herself and draws it in, even so this\nuniverse is thrown out and drawn in by God.\"\n"]

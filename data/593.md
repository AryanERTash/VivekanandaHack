["\n", "CXXXII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "ALMORA,\n", "30th July, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR AKHANDANANDA,\n", "According to your instructions, I write a letter to Mr. Levinge, the Dist.\nMagistrate. Besides, you will write a big letter to the Indian Mirror,\ndescribing in detail his method of work (having got the same revised by Dr.\nShashi), and send a copy of it to the gentleman named above. Our fools only\nsearch for people's shortcomings. Let them see some virtues too.\n", "I am leaving this place next Monday. ...\n", "What do you talk of the difficulty in getting orphans? Better ask for four\nor five men from the Math, if you like; you can find some orphans in two\ndays, if you seek from village to village.\n", "Of course we must have a permanent centre. And can anything be done in this\ncountry  unless the \u2014\u2014 help? Do not mix in politics etc., nor have any\nconnection with them. At the same time you need not have any quarrel with\nanybody. You must put your body, mind, and all you have to some one work.\nHere I gave a lecture to a European audience in English, and another to the\nIndian residents in Hindi. This was my maiden speech in Hindi, but everyone\nliked it for all that. Of course the Westerners, as is their wont, were in\nraptures over it, as coming from a \"nigger\"! \"Oh, how wonderful!\" and that\nsort  of  thing.  Next  Saturday there will be another lecture for the\nEuropeans. A big Association has been set on foot here \u2014 let us wait and see\nhow far it works in future. The object of the Association is to impart\neducation and religion.\n", "Monday next, trip to Bareilly then to Saharanpur, next to Ambala, thence,\nmost probably, to Mussoorie with Captain Sevier, and as soon as it is a\nlittle  cool, return to the plains and journey to Rajputana etc. Go on\nworking at top speed. Never fear! I, too, have determined to work. The body\nmust go, no mistake about that. Why then let it go in idleness? It is better\nto wear out than rust out. Don't be anxious even when I die, my very bones\nwill work miracles. We must spread over the whole of India in ten years,\nshort of this it is no good. To work like an athlete! Victory to the Guru!\nMoney and all will come of themselves, we want men, not money. It is man\nthat makes everything, what can money do? \u2014 Men we want, the more you get,\nthe better. ... Here, for instance, was M\u2014 who brought together a lot of\nmoney, but there was no man, and what good did he achieve?\n", "Yours affly.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "ON BHAKTI-YOGA\n", "\nThe dualist thinks you cannot be moral unless you have a God with a rod in\nHis hand, ready to punish you. How is that? Suppose a horse had to give us a\nlecture on morality, one of those very wretched cab-horses who move only\nwith the whip, to which he has become accustomed. He begins to speak about\nhuman beings and says that they must be very immoral. Why? \"Because I know\nthey are not whipped regularly.\" The fear of the whip only makes one more\nimmoral.\n", "\nYou all say there is a God and that He is an omnipresent Being. Close your\neyes and think what He is. What do you find? Either you are thinking, in\nbringing the idea of omnipresence in your mind, of the sea, or the blue sky,\nor an expanse of meadow, or such things as you have seen in your life. If\nthat is so, you do not mean anything by omnipresent God; it has no meaning\nat all to you. So with every other attribute of God. What idea have we of\nomnipotence or omniscience? We have none. Religion is realising, and I shall\ncall you a worshipper of God when you have become able to realise the Idea.\nBefore that it is the spelling of words and no more. It is this power of\nrealisation that makes religion; no amount of doctrines or philosophies, or\nethical books, that you may have stuffed into your brain, will matter much\n\u2014 only what you are and what you have realised.\n", "\nThe Personal God is the same Absolute looked at through the haze of M\u00e2y\u00e2.\nWhen  we approach Him with the five senses, we can see Him only as the\nPersonal God. The idea is that the Self cannot be objectified. How can the\nKnower know Itself\u00a0? But It can cast a shadow, as it were, if that can be\ncalled objectification. So the highest form of that shadow, that attempt at\nobjectifying Itself, is the Personal God. The Self is the eternal subject,\nand we are struggling all the time to objectify that Self. And out of that\nstruggle has come this phenomenal universe and what we call matter, and so\non. But these are very weak attempts, and the highest objectification of the\nSelf possible to us is the Personal God. This objectification is an attempt\nto reveal our own nature. According to the S\u00e2nkhya, nature is showing all\nthese experiences to the soul, and when it has got real experience it will\nknow  its  own nature. According to the Advaita Vedantist, the soul is\nstruggling to reveal itself. After long struggle, it finds that the subject\nmust always remain the subject; and then begins non-attachment, and it\nbecomes free.\n", "\nWhen a man has reached that perfect state, he is of the same nature as the\nPersonal  God. \"I and my Father are one.\" He knows that he is one with\nBrahman, the Absolute, and projects himself as the Personal God does. He\nplays \u2014 as even the mightiest of kings may sometimes play with dolls.\n", "\nSome imaginations help to break the bondage of the rest. The whole universe\nis imagination, but one set of imaginations will cure another set. Those\nthat  tell  us that there is sin and sorrow and death in the world are\nterrible. But the other set \u2014 thou art holy, there is God, there is no pain\n\u2014 these are good, and help to break the bondage of the others. The highest\nimagination  that  can break all the links of the chain is that of the\nPersonal God.\n", "\nTo go and say, \"Lord, take care of this thing and give me that; Lord, I give\nyou my little prayer and you give me this thing of daily necessity; Lord,\ncure my headache\", and all that \u2014 these are not Bhakti. They are the lowest\nstates of religion. They are the lowest form of Karma. If a man uses all his\nmental energy in seeking to satisfy his body and its wants, show me the\ndifference between him and an animal. Bhakti is a higher thing higher than\neven desiring heaven. The idea of heaven is of a place of intensified\nenjoyment. How can that be God?\n", "\nOnly the fools rush after sense-enjoyments. It is easy to live in the\nsenses. It is easier to run in the old groove, eating and drinking; but what\nthese modern philosophers want to tell you is to take these comfortable\nideas and put the stamp of religion on them. Such a doctrine is dangerous.\nDeath lies in the senses. Life on the plane of the Spirit is the only life,\nlife on any other plane is mere death; the whole of this life can be only\ndescribed as a gymnasium. We must go beyond it to enjoy real life.\n", "\nAs long as touch-me-not-ism is your creed and the kitchen-pot your deity,\nyou cannot rise spiritually. All the petty differences between religion and\nreligion are mere word-struggles, nonsense. Everyone thinks, \"This is my\noriginal idea\", and wants to have things his own way. That is how struggles\ncome.\n", "\nIn criticising another, we always foolishly take one especially brilliant\npoint as the whole of our life and compare that with the dark ones in the\nlife of another. Thus we make mistakes in judging individuals.\n", "\nThrough fanaticism and bigotry a religion can be propagated very quickly, no\ndoubt, but the preaching of that religion is firm-based on solid ground,\nwhich gives everyone liberty to his opinions and thus uplifts him to a\nhigher path, though this process is slow\n", "\nFirst deluge the land (India) with spiritual ideas, then other ideas will\nfollow The gift of spirituality and spiritual knowledge is the highest, for\nit saves from many and many a birth; the next gift is secular knowledge, as\nit opens the eyes of human beings towards that spiritual knowledge; the next\nis the saving of life; and the fourth is the gift of food.\n", "\nEven if the body goes in practicing S\u00e2dhan\u00e2s (austerities for realisation),\nlet it go; what of that? Realisation will come in the fullness of time, by\nliving constantly in the company of S\u00e2dhus (holy men). A time comes when one\nunderstands that to serve a man even by preparing a Chhilam (earthen pipe)\nof tobacco is far greater than millions of meditations. He who can properly\nprepare a Chhilam of tobacco can also properly meditate.\n", "\nGods are nothing but highly developed dead men. We can get help from them.\n", "\nAnyone and everyone cannot be an \u00c2ch\u00e2rya (teacher of mankind); but many may\nbecome  Mukta  (liberated).  The whole world seems like a dream to the\nliberated, but the Acharya has to take up his stand between the two states.\nHe must have the knowledge that the world is true, or else why should he\nteach? Again, if he has not realised the world as a dream, then he is no\nbetter than an ordinary man, and what could he teach? The Guru has to bear\nthe disciple's burden of sin; and that is the reason why diseases and other\nailments  appear even in the bodies of powerful Acharyas. For the former knows the world as a dream and has no concern with\nit; but an Acharya knows it as a dream and yet has to remain in it and work.\nIt is not possible for everyone to be an Acharya. He is an Acharya through\nwhom the divine power acts. The body in which one becomes an Acharya is very\ndifferent from that of any other man. There is a science for keeping that\nbody  in  a  perfect  state.  His  is the most delicate organism, very\nsusceptible, capable of feeling intense joy and intense suffering. He is\nabnormal.\n", "\nIn every sphere of life we find that it is the person within that triumphs,\nand that personality is the secret of all success.\n", "\nNowhere is seen such sublime unfoldment of feeling as in Bhagav\u00e2n Shri\nKrishna Chaitanya, the Prophet of Nadia.\n", "\nShri Ramakrishna is a force. You should not think that his doctrine is this\nor that. But he is a power, living even now in his disciples and working in\nthe  world.  I saw him growing in his ideas. He is still growing. Shri\nRamakrishna was both a Jivanmukta and an Acharya.\n", "\n"]

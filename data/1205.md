["\n", "CLXI\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "921 WEST 21ST STREET,\n", "LOS ANGELES,\n", "27th December 1899.\n", "DEAR CHRISTINA,\n", "So you are awake and can't go to sleep any more. Good! Keep awake, wide\nawake. It was good I came here. For, in the first place, I am cured. What do\nyou think of this \u2014 able to walk, and every day walk three miles after a\nheavy dinner! Good! Isn't it?\n", "I am making money fast \u2014 twenty-five dollars a day now. Soon I will work\nmore and get fifty dollars a day. In San Francisco I hope to do still better\n\u2014 where I go in two or three weeks. Good again \u2014 better, say I \u2014 as I am\ngoing to keep the money all to myself and not squander it any more. And then\nI will buy a little place in the Himalayas \u2014 a whole hill \u2014 about say, six\nthousand feet high with a grand view of the eternal snows. There must be\nsprings and a tiny lake. Cedars \u2014 the Himalayan cedar forests \u2014 and flowers,\nflowers  everywhere.  I  will have a little cottage; in the middle, my\nvegetable gardens, which I will work myself \u2014 and \u2014 and \u2014 and \u2014 my books \u2014\nand see the face of man only once in a great while. And the world may go to\nruin round about my ears, I would not care. I will have done with all my\nwork \u2014 secular or spiritual \u2014 and retire. My! how restless I have been all\nmy life! Born nomad. I don't know; this is the present vision. The future is\nto come yet. Curious \u2014all my dreams about my own happiness are, as it were,\nbound to come to nothing; but about others' well-being \u2014 they as a rule\nprove true.\n", "I am so glad you are happy and peaceful under Mrs. Bull's hospitable roof.\nShe is a great, great woman \u2014 one whom to see is a pilgrimage.\n", "No snow here \u2014 exactly like northern India in winter. Some days, even warmer\n\u2014 cool in the morning and evening, in the middle of the day, warm, in the\nsun, hot. The roses are about us, gardens everywhere, and the beautiful\npalms. But I like the snow: crisp, crackling under the feet, white, white,\nwhite \u2014 all round white!\n", "I don't think I have anything with the kidneys or the heart. The whole thing\nwas about indigestion and it is now nearly cured. A month more, and I will\nbe strong like a lion and hardy like a mule. The poor English are getting it\nhot from the Boers. Mourning in every home in England and still the war goes\non. Such is human folly. How long will it take for man to become civilized!\nWill wars ever cease? Mother knows! The New Year is sure to bring about a\ngreat change. Pray some good may come to India. I send you all joy, all\nlove, all success for the New Year and many, many more to come.\n", "So you did well, you think, by coming to Mrs. Bull. I am glad. I wanted you\nto know Mrs. Bull thoroughly. Remain there as long as you can. It will do\nyou good, I am sure. Take heart and be of cheer, for next year is sure to\nbring many joys and a hundred blessings.\n", "Yours truly,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

**LXXIV**

**WAVENEY MANSIONS,**

**FAIRHAZEL GARDENS,**

**LONDON N.W.**

April, 1896
Thursday Afternoon.

DEAR STURDY,

I forgot to tell you in the morning that Prof. Max Müller also offered in
his letter to me to do everything he could if I went to lecture at Oxford.

Yours affectionately,

VIVEKANANDA.

PS. Have you written for the Artharva-Veda Samhita edited by Shankara
Pandurang?

V.

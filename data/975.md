["\n", "CXXVIII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "ALMORA,\n", "20th May, 1898.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I have got all the news from your letter and have replied to your wire\nalready. Niranjan and Govindalal Shah will wait at Kathgodam for Yogen-Ma.\nAfter I reached Naini Tal, Baburam went from here to Naini Tal on horseback\nagainst everybody's advice, and while returning, he also accompanied us on\nhorseback. I was far behind as I was in a Dandi. When I reached the dak\nbungalow at night, I heard that Baburam had again fallen from the horse and\nhad hurt one of his arms \u2014 though he had no fractures. Lest I should rebuke\nhim, he stayed in a private lodging house. Because of his fall, Miss MacLeod\ngave him her Dandi and herself came on the horse. He did not meet me that\nnight. Next day I was making arrangements for a Dandi for him, when I heard\nthat he had already left on foot. Since then I have not heard of him. I have\nwired to one or two places, but no news. Perhaps he is putting up at some\nvillage. Very well! They are experts in increasing one's worries.\n", "There will be a Dandi for Yogen-Ma; but all the rest will have to go on\nfoot.\n", "My health is much better, but the dyspepsia has not gone, and again insomnia\nhas set in. It will be very helpful if you can soon send some good Ayurvedic\nmedicine for dyspepsia.\n", "Since only one or two sporadic cases of plague have occurred there, there is\nplenty of accommodation in the Government plague hospital, and there is a\ntalk of having hospitals in every Ward. Taking all this into consideration,\ndo what the situation demands. But remember that something said by somebody\nin Baghbazar does not constitute public opinion. . . . Take care that funds\ndo not run short in times of need and that there is no waste of money. For\nthe present buy a plot of ground for Ramlal in the name of Raghuvir (The\nfamily deity of Shri Ramakrishna's birthpalce, Kamarpukur, Ramlal being his\nnephew.) after careful consideration. . . . Holy Mother will be the Seb\u00e2it\n(worshipper-in-charge); after her will come Ramlal, and Shibu will succeed\nthem as Sebait; or make any other arrangement that seems best. You can, if\nyou think it right, begin the construction of the building even now. For it\nis not good to live in a new house for the first one or two months, as it\nwill be damp. . . . The anti-erosion wall can be completed afterwards. I am\ntrying to raise money for the magazine. See that the sum of Rs. 1,200 which\nI gave for the magazine is kept only for that account.\n", "All the others are well here. Sadananda sprained his foot yesterday. He says\nhe will be all right by the evening. The climate at Almora is excellent at\nthis time. Moreover the bungalow rented by Sevier is the best in Almora. On\nthe  opposite  side  Annie  Besant is staying in a small bungalow with\nChakravarty. Chakravarty is now the son-in-law of Gagan (of Ghazipur). One\nday I went to see him. Annie Besant told me entreatingly that there should\nbe friendship between her organisation and mine all over the world, etc.,\netc. Today Besant will come here for tea. Our ladies are in a small bungalow\nnear by and are quite happy. Only Miss MacLeod is a little unwell today.\nHarry Sevier is becoming more and more a Sadhu as the days pass by. . . .\nBrother Hari sends you his greetings and Sadananda, Ajoy, and Suren send you\ntheir respectful salutations. My love to you and all the others.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Give my love to Sushil and Kanai and all the others.\n", "V.\n"]

BELUR, HOWRAH\n", "27th November 1901.\n", "DEAR CHRISTINE,\n", "It is almost sure, I did not write any letter to you that week in which [I]\nmade  that  infamous  blunder.  As  I wrote you two letters a few days\npreviously, it is not at all probable that I wrote you another. Then Miss\n[Josephine] MacLeod [would have] sent the letter back. I must have written\nonly one letter that week to Miss MacLeod, giving her my reasons for not\ngoing to Japan; and somehow it so happened that the hand wrote the most\nfamiliar name on the envelope. So you need not expect any letter of yours\nback from Japan, as there was none; and if there were, you shall have it.\n", "I am just under another spell of catarrh and asthma. Yesterday a cyclone\nblew over the place, and several trees and a bit of the roof are damaged. It\nis gloomy yet and cold. You know it is almost impossible to write with the\nasthma on. So au revoir [good-bye].\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

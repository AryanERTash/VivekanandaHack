["\n", "XCVII\n", "\n", "My Dear Shashi, (Swami Ramakrishnananda) \n", "\nI am going with my mother to Rameswaram, that is all. I don't know whether I\nshall go to Madras at all. If I go, it will be strictly private. My body and\nmind are completely worked out; I cannot stand a single person. I do not\nwant anybody. I have neither the strength nor the money, nor the will to\ntake up anybody with me. Bhaktas (devotees) of Guru Maharaj or not, it does\nnot matter. It was very foolish of you even to ask such a question. Let me\ntell  you again, I am more dead than alive, and strictly refuse to see\nanybody. If you cannot manage this, I don't go to Madras. I have to become a\nbit selfish to save my body.\n", "\nLet Yogin-Ma and others go their own way. I shall not take up any company in\nmy present state of health.\n", "\n", "Yours in love,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

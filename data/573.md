["\n", "CXII\n", "\n", "14 GREYCOAT GARDENS, WESTMINSTER,\n", "LONDON, ENGLAND,\n", "1st November, 1896.\n", "MY DEAR MARY, (Miss Mary Hale.)\n", "\"Silver and gold\", my dear Mary, \"have I none; but such as I have give I\nthee'\" freely, and that is the knowledge that the goldness of gold, the\nsilverness  of silver, the manhood of man, the womanhood of woman, the\nreality of everything is the Lord \u2014 and that this Lord we are trying to\nrealise from time without beginning in the objective, and in the attempt\nthrowing up such \"queer\" creatures of our fancy as man, woman, child, body,\nmind, the earth, sun, moon, stars, the world, love, hate, property, wealth,\netc.; also ghosts, devils, angels and gods, God etc.\n", "The fact being that the Lord is in us, we are He, the eternal subject, the\nreal ego, never to be objectified, and that all this objectifying process is\nmere waste of time and talent. When the soul becomes aware of this, it gives\nup objectifying and falls back more and more upon the subjective. This is\nthe evolution, less and less in the body and more and more in the mind \u2014 man\nthe highest form, meaning in Sanskrit manas, thought \u2014 the animal that\nthinks and not the animal that \"senses\" only. This is what in theology is\ncalled  \"renunciation\".  The  formation of society, the institution of\nmarriage, the love for children, our good works, morality, and ethics are\nall different forms of renunciation. All our lives in every society are the\nsubjection of the will, the thirst, the desire. This surrender of the will\nor the fictitious self \u2014 or the desire to jump out of ourselves, as it were\n\u2014 the struggle still to objectify the subject \u2014 is the one phenomenon in\nthis world of which all societies and social forms are various modes and\nstages. Love is the easiest and smoothest way towards the self-surrender or\nsubjection of the will and hatred, the opposite.\n", "People have been cajoled through various stories or superstitions of heavens\nand hells and Rulers above the sky, towards this one end of self-surrender.\nThe philosopher does the same knowingly without superstition, by giving up\ndesires.\n", "An objective heaven or millennium therefore has existence only in the fancy\n\u2014 but a subjective one is already in existence. The musk-deer, after vain\nsearch for the cause of the scent of the musk, at last will have to find it\nin himself.\n", "Objective society will always be a mixture of good and evil \u2014 objective life\nwill always be followed by its shadow death, and the longer the life, the\nlonger will also be the shadow. It is only when the sun is on our own head\nthat there is no shadow. When God and good and everything else is in us,\nthere is no evil. In objective life, however, every bullet has its billet\n\u2014 evil goes with every good as its shadow. Every improvement is coupled with\nan equal degradation. The reason being that good and evil are not two things\nbut one, the difference being only in manifestation \u2014 one of degree, not\nkind.\n", "Our  very lives depend upon the death of others \u2014 plants or animals or\nbacilli! The other great mistake we often make is that good is taken as an\never-increasing item, whilst evil is a fixed quantity. From this it is\nargued that evil being diminished every day, there will come a time when\ngood  alone will remain. The fallacy lies in the assumption of a false\npremise. If good is increasing, so is evil. My desires have been much more\nthan the desires of the masses among my race. My joys have been much greater\nthan  theirs  \u2014  but my miseries a million times more intense The same\nconstitution that makes you feel the least touch of good makes you feel the\nleast of evil too. The same nerves that carry sensations of pleasure carry\nthe sensations of pain too \u2014 and the same mind feels both. The progress of\nthe world means more enjoyment and more misery too. This mixture of life and\ndeath, good and evil, knowledge and ignorance is what is called Maya \u2014 or\nthe  universal phenomenon. You may go on for eternity inside this net,\nseeking for happiness \u2014 you find much, and much evil too. To have good and\nno evil is childish nonsense. Two ways are left open \u2014 one by giving up all\nhope to take up the world as it is and bear the pangs and pains in the hope\nof a crumb of happiness now and then. The other, to give up the search for\npleasure, knowing it to be pain in another form, and seek for truth \u2014 and\nthose that dare try for truth succeed in finding that truth as ever present\n\u2014  present  in themselves. Then we also discover how the same truth is\nmanifesting itself both in our relative error and knowledge \u2014 we find also\nthat the same truth is bliss which again is manifesting itself as good and\nevil, and with it also we find real existence which is manifesting itself as\nboth death and life.\n", "Thus we realise that all these phenomena are but the reflections, bifurcated\nor manifolded, of the one existence, truth-bliss-unity \u2014 my real Self and\nthe reality of everything else. Then and then only is it possible to do good\nwithout evil, for such a soul has known and got the control of the material\nof which both good and evil are manufactured, and he alone can manifest one\nor the other as he likes, and we know he manifests only good. This is the\nJivan-mukta  \u2014  the  living free \u2014 the goal of Vedanta as of all other\nphilosophies.\n", "Human society is in turn governed by the four castes \u2014 the priests, the\nsoldiers, the traders, and the labourers. Each state has its glories as well\nas its defects. When the priest (Brahmin) rules, there is a tremendous\nexclusiveness on hereditary grounds; the persons of the priests and their\ndescendants are hemmed in with all sorts of safeguards \u2014 none but they have\nany knowledge \u2014 none but they have the right to impart that knowledge. Its\nglory is that at this period is laid the foundation of sciences. The priests\ncultivate the mind, for through the mind they govern.\n", "The military (Kshatriya) rule is tyrannical and cruel, but they are not\nexclusive; and during that period arts and social culture attain their\nheight.\n", "The commercial (Vaishya) rule comes next. It is awful in its silent crushing\nand  blood-sucking power. Its advantage is, as the trader himself goes\neverywhere, he is a good disseminator of ideas collected during the two\nprevious  states. They are still less exclusive than the military, but\nculture begins to decay.\n", "Last  will come the labourer (Shudra) rule. Its advantages will be the\ndistribution  of  physical comforts \u2014 its disadvantages, (perhaps) the\nlowering  of  culture.  There will be a great distribution of ordinary\neducation, but extraordinary geniuses will be less and less.\n", "If it is possible to form a state in which the knowledge of the priest\nperiod,  the  culture  of the military, the distributive spirit of the\ncommercial, and the ideal of equality of the last can all be kept intact,\nminus their evils, it will be an deal state. But is it possible?\n", "Yet the first three have had their day. Now is the time for the last \u2014 they\nmust have it \u2014 none can resist it. I do not know all the difficulties about\nthe gold or silver standards (nobody seems to know much as to that), but\nthis much I see that the gold standard has been making the poor poorer, and\nthe rich richer. Bryan was right when he said, \"We refuse to be crucified on\na cross of gold.\" The silver standard will give the poor a better chance in\nthis unequal fight. I am a socialist not because I think it is a perfect\nsystem, but half a loaf is better than no bread.\n", "The other systems have been tried and found wanting. Let this one be tried\n\u2014 if for nothing else, for the novelty of the thing. A redistribution of\npain and pleasure is better than always the same persons having pains and\npleasures. The sum total of good and evil in the world remains ever the\nsame. The yoke will be lifted from shoulder to shoulder by new systems, that\nis all.\n", "Let every dog have his day in this miserable world, so that after this\nexperience of so-called happiness they may all come to the Lord and give up\nthis vanity of a world and governments and all other botherations.\n", "With love to you all,\n", "Ever your faithful brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

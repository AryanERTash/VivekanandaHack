["\n", "XXXVI\n", "MOUNT ABU,\n", "30th April, 1891.\n", "DEAR GOVINDA SAHAY,\n", "Have you done the Upanayana of that Brahmin boy? Are you studying Sanskrit?\nHow far have you advanced? I think you must have finished the first part.\n... Are you diligent in your Shiva Puj\u00e2\u00a0? If not, try to be so. \"Seek ye\nfirst the kingdom of God and all good things will be added unto you.\" Follow\nGod and you shall have whatever you desire. ... To the two Commander Sahebs\nmy best regards; they being men of high position were very kind to a poor\nfakir like me. My children, the secret of religion lies not in theories but\nin practice. To be good and to do good \u2014 that is the whole of religion. \"Not\nhe that crieth 'Lord', 'Lord', but he that doeth the will of the Father\".\nYou are a nice band of young men, you Alwaris, and I hope in no distant\nfuture many of you will be ornaments of the society and blessings to the\ncountry you are born in.\n", "Yours with blessings,\n", "V.\n", "PS. Don't be ruffled if now and then you get a brush from the world; it will\nbe over in no time, and everything will be all right.\n"]

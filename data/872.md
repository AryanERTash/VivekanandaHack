["\n", "XXV\n", "To the Hale Sisters\n", "GREENACRE,\n", "11th August, 1894.\n", "DEAR SISTERS,\n", "I have been all this time in Greenacre. I enjoyed this place very much. They\nhave been all very kind to me. One Chicago lady, Mrs. Pratt of Kenilworth,\nwanted to give me $500; she became so much interested in me; but I refused.\nShe has made me promise that I would send word to her whenever I need money,\nwhich I hope the Lord will never put me in. His help alone is sufficient for\nme. I have not heard anything from you nor from Mother. Neither have I any\nnews from India as to the arrival of the phonograph.\n", "If there was anything in my letter to you which was offensive, I hope you\nall  know that I meant everything in love. It is useless to express my\ngratitude to you for your kindness. Lord bless you and shower His choicest\nblessings  on  you  and those you love. To your family I am ever, ever\nbeholden. You know it. You feel it. I cannot express it. On Sunday I am\ngoing to lecture at Plymouth at the \"Sympathy of Religions\" meetings of Col.\nHigginson. Herewith I send a photograph Cora Stockham took of the group\nunder the tree. It is only a proof and will fade away under exposure, but I\ncannot get anything better at present. Kindly tender my heartfelt love and\ngratitude  to Miss Howe. She has been so, so kind to me. I do not need\nanything  at  present.  I shall be very glad to let you know if I need\nanything. I think I am going to Fishkill from Plymouth, where I will be only\na couple of days. I will write you again from Fishkill. Hope you are all\nhappy, or rather I know you are. Pure and good souls can never be unhappy. I\nshall have a very nice time the few weeks I am here. I will be in New York\nnext fall. New York is a grand and good place. The New York people have a\ntenacity of purpose unknown in any other city. I had a letter from Mrs.\nPotter Palmer asking me to see her in August. She is a very gracious and\nkind lady, etc. I have not much to say. There is my friend Dr. Janes of New\nYork, President of the Ethical Culture Society, who has begun his lectures.\nI must go to hear him. He and I agree so much. May you be always happy!\n", "Ever your well-wishing brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

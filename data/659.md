["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "WEDNESDAY, July 10, 1895.\n", "There are sixty-five million Mohammedans in India, some of them Sufis.[6]*\nSufis identify man with God, and through them this idea came into Europe.\nThey  say,  \"I am that Truth\"; but they have an esoteric as well as an\nexoteric doctrine, although Mohammed himself did not hold it.\n", "\"Hashshashin\"[7]* has become our word \"assassin\", because an old sect of\nMohammedanism killed nonbelievers as a part of its creed.\n", "A pitcher of water has to be present in the Mohammedan worship as a symbol\nof God filling the universe.\n", "The Hindus believe that there will be ten Divine Incarnations. Nine have\nbeen and the tenth is still to come.\n", "*    *    *\n", "Shankara sometimes resorts to sophistry in order to prove that the ideas in\nthe books go to uphold his philosophy. Buddha was more brave and sincere\nthan any teacher. He said: \"Believe no book; the Vedas are all humbug. If\nthey agree with me, so much the better for the books. I am the greatest\nbook; sacrifice and prayer are useless.\" Buddha was the first human being to\ngive to the world a complete system of morality. He was good for good's\nsake, he loved for love's sake.\n", "Shankara says: God is to be reasoned on, because the Vedas say so. Reason\nhelps inspiration; books and realised reason \u2014 or individualized perception\n\u2014  both  are proofs of God. The Vedas are, according to him, a sort of\nincarnation of universal knowledge. The proof of God is that He brought\nforth the Vedas, and the proof of the Vedas is that such wonderful books\ncould  only  have  been given out by Brahman. They are the mine of all\nknowledge,  and  they  have come out of Him as a man breathes out air;\ntherefore we know that He is infinite in power and knowledge. He may or may\nnot have created the world, that is a trifle; to have produced the Vedas is\nmore important! The world has come to know God through the Vedas; no other\nway there is.\n", "And so universal is this belief, held by Shankara, in the all-inclusiveness\nof the Vedas that there is even a Hindu proverb that if a man loses his cow,\nhe goes to look for her in the Vedas!\n", "Shankara further affirms that obedience to ceremonial is not knowledge.\nKnowledge of God is independent of moral duties, or sacrifice or ceremonial,\nor what we think or do not think, just as the stump is not affected when one\nman takes it for a ghost and another sees it as it is.\n", "Vedanta is necessary because neither reasoning nor books can show us God. He\nis only to be realised by superconscious perception, and Vedanta teaches how\nto attain that. You must get beyond personal God (Ishvara) and reach the\nAbsolute Brahman. God is the perception of every being: He is all there is\nto he perceived. That which says \"I\" is Brahman, but although we, day and\nnight, perceive Him; we do not know that we are perceiving Him. As soon as\nwe become aware of this truth, all misery goes; so we must get knowledge of\nthe truth. Reach unity; no more duality will come. But knowledge does not\ncome by sacrifice, but by seeking, worshipping, knowing the Atman.\n", "Brahmavidy\u00e2 is the highest knowledge, knowing the Brahman; lower knowledge\nis science. This is the teaching of the Mundakopanishad or the Upanishad for\nSanny\u00e2sins. There are two sorts of knowledge \u2014 principal and secondary. The\nunessential is that part of the Vedas dealing with worship and ceremonial,\nalso all secular knowledge. The essential is that by which we reach the\nAbsolute.  It (the Absolute) creates all from Its own nature; there is\nnothing to cause, nothing outside. It is all energy, It is all there is. He\nwho makes all sacrifices to himself, the Atman, he alone knows Brahman.\nFools think outside worship the highest; fools think works can give us God.\nOnly those who go through the Sushumn\u00e2 (the \"path\" of the Yogis) reach the\nAtman. They must go to a Guru to learn. Each part has the same nature as the\nwhole; all springs from the Atman. Meditation is the arrow, the whole soul\ngoing out to God is the bow, which speeds the arrow to its mark, the Atman.\nAs finite, we can never express the Infinite, but we are the Infinite.\nKnowing this we argue with no one.\n", "Divine wisdom is to be got by devotion, meditation, and chastity. \"Truth\nalone triumphs, and not untruth. Through truth alone the way is spread to\nBrahman\" \u2014 where alone love and truth are.\n"]

["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "WEDNESDAY, July 24, 1895.\n", "The powers acquired by the practice of Yoga are not obstacles for the Yogi\nwho is perfect, but are apt to be so for the beginner, through the wonder\nand pleasure excited by their exercise. Siddhis are the powers which mark\nsuccess in the practice; and they may be produced by various means, such as\nthe repetition of a Mantra, by Yoga practice, meditation, fasting, or even\nby the use of herbs and drugs. The Yogi, who has conquered all interest in\nthe powers acquired and who renounces all virtue arising from his actions,\ncomes into the \"cloud of virtue\" (name of one of the states of Samadhi) and\nradiates holiness as a cloud rains water.\n", "Meditation is on a series of objects, concentration is on one object.\n", "Mind is cognised by the Atman, but it is not self-illuminated. The Atman\ncannot be the cause of anything. How can it be? How can the Purusha join\nitself to Prakriti (nature)? It does not; it is only illusively thought to\ndo so. . . .\n", "Learn to help without pitying or feeling that there is any misery. Learn to\nbe the same to enemy and to friend; then when you can do that and no longer\nhave any desire, the goal is attained.\n", "Cut down the banyan tree of desire with the axe of non-attachment, and it\nwill vanish utterly. It is all illusion. \"He from whom blight and delusion\nhave fallen, he who has conquered the evils of association, he alone is \u00e2z\u00e2d\n(free).\"\n", "To love anyone personally is bondage. Love all alike, then all desires fall\noff.\n", "Time,  the \"eater of everything\", comes, and all has to go. Why try to\nimprove the earth, to paint the butterfly? It all has to go at last. Do not\nbe mere white mice in a treadmill, working always and never accomplishing\nanything. Every desire is fraught with evil, whether the desire itself be\ngood or evil. It is like a dog jumping for a piece of meat which is ever\nreceding from his reach, and dying a dog's death at last. Do not be like\nthat. Cut off all desire.\n", "*            *            *\n", "Param\u00e2tman as ruling Maya is Ishvara; Param\u00e2tman as under Maya is Jiv\u00e2tman.\nMaya is the sum total of manifestation and will utterly vanish.\n", "Tree-nature is Maya, it is really God-nature which we see under the veil of\nMaya. The \"why\" of anything is in Maya. To ask why Maya came is a useless\nquestion, because the answer can never be given in Maya, and beyond Maya who\nwill ask it? Evil creates \"why\", not \"why\" the evil, and it is evil that\nasks \"why\". Illusion destroys illusion. Reason itself, being based upon\ncontradiction, is a circle and has to kill itself. Sense-perception is an\ninference, and yet all inference comes from perception.\n", "Ignorance reflecting the light of God is seen; but by itself it is zero. The\ncloud would not appear except as the sunlight falls on it.\n", "There were four travellers who came to a high wall. The first one climbed\nwith difficulty to the top and without looking back, jumped over. The second\nclambered up the wall, looked over, and with a shout of delight disappeared.\nThe third in his turn climbed to the top, looked where his companions had\ngone, laughed with joy, and followed them. But the fourth one came back to\ntell what had happened to his fellow-travellers. The sign to us that there\nis something beyond is the laugh that rings back from those great ones who\nhave plunged from Maya's wall.\n", "*            *            *\n", "Separating ourselves from the Absolute and attributing certain qualities to\nIt give us Ishvara. It is the Reality of the universe as seen through our\nmind. Personal devil is the misery of the world seen through the minds of\nthe superstitious.\n"]

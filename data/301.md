Now I am going\nto Germany. I have an invitation from Prof. Deussen to visit him at Kiel.\nFrom thence I go back to England. Possibly I will return to India this\nwinter.\n", "\nWhat I objected to in the design for the Prabuddha Bh\u00e2rata was not only its\ntawdriness, but the crowding in of a number of figures without any purpose.\nA design should be simple, symbolical, and condensed. I will try to make a\ndesign for Prabuddha Bharata in London and send it over to you. . . .\n", "\nThe work is going on beautifully, I am very glad to say. . . . I will give\nyou one advice however. All combined efforts in India sink under the weight\nof one iniquity \u2014 we have not yet developed strict business principles.\nBusiness is business, in the highest sense, and no friendship \u2014 or as the\nHindu  proverb says \"eye-shame\" \u2014 should be there. One should keep the\nclearest account of everything in one's charge \u2014 and never, never apply the\nfunds intended for one thing to any other use whatsoever \u2014 even if one\nstarves the next moment. This is business integrity. Next, energy unfailing.\nWhatever you do let that be your worship for the time. Let this paper be\nyour God for the time, and you will succeed.\n", "\nWhen you have succeeded in this paper, start vernacular ones on the same\nlines  in  Tamil, Telugu, Canarese, etc. We must reach the masses. The\nMadrasis are good, energetic, and all that, but the land of Shankar\u00e2ch\u00e2rya\nhas lost the spirit of renunciation, it seems.\n", "\nMy children must plunge into the breach, must renounce the world \u2014 then the\nfirm foundation will be laid.\n", "\nGo on bravely \u2014 never mind about designs and other details at present \u2014\n\"With the horse will come the reins\". Work unto death \u2014 I am with you, and\nwhen I am gone, my spirit will work with you. This life comes and goes \u2014\nwealth, fame, enjoyments are only of a few days. It is better, far better to\ndie on the field of duty, preaching the truth, than to die like a worldly\nworm. Advance!\n", "\n", "Yours with all love and blessings,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

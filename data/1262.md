["\n", "CCXVIII\n", "To Sister Nivedita\n", "GOPAL LAL VILLA,\n", "BENARAS CANTONMENT,\n", "4th March 1902.\n", "MY DEAR MARGO [MARGOT],\n", "It is night now, and I can hardly sit up or write, yet still feel duty bound\nto write to you this letter, fearing lest it becomes my last, it may put\nothers to trouble.\n", "My condition is not at all serious, but it may become [so] any time; and I\ndon't know what is meant by a low fever that almost never leaves me and the\ndifficulty of breathing.\n", "Well, I sent Christina [Greenstidel] \u00a3100 from Mrs. [Charlotte] Sevier for a\ntravel  to India, as she lost her mother at that time. Her last letter\ninforms me that she sails on February 15th. In that case, her reaching India\nis very near. I expect, of course, some information as to the port and\nsteamer next week. In case I pass away, which I would like very much to do\nin this city of Shiva, do you open her letters directed to me, receive the\ngirl, and send her back home. If she has no money to go back, give her a\npassage \u2014 even if you have to beg.\n", "I have spent the little money I brought from Europe in feeding my mother and\npaying her debts. What little remains I cannot touch, as that is the expense\nfor the pending lawsuit.\n", "In case I rally, I will inform you of the time of her arrival, and, in that\ncase,  you  will have to see that she comes in safe to some station in\nBareilly,  where I [will] meet her. And she is to be the guest of Mrs.\n[Charlotte] Sevier. I am also going to take another chance in Almora.\n", "Ramakrishnananda came a few weeks before I came away, and the first thing he\ndid was to lay down at my feet 400 Rs. he had collected in so many years of\nhard work!!! It was the first time such a thing has happened in my life. I\ncan  scarcely suppress my tears. Oh, Mother!! Mother! There is not all\ngratitude, all love, all manliness dead!!! And, dear child, one is enough\n\u2014 one seed is enough to reforest the world.\n", "Well, that money is in deposit in the Math. I never mean to touch a penny of\nthat. When I asked Ramakrishnananda to give that money to his people, he\nreplied he did not care a hang to give to anybody except me and was only\nsorry he could scrape that little in four years! Well, if I pass away, see\nthat  400  Rs.  is  paid back \u2014 every rupee to him. Lord bless you and\nRamakrishnananda.\n", "I am quite satisfied with my work. To have left two true souls is beyond the\nambition of the greatest.\n", "Ever your loving father,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "CXX\n", "To Mrs. Ole Bull\n", "DARJEELING\n", "the 4th April '98\n", "MY DEAR DHIRA MATA \u2014\n", "I am afraid you are getting roasted down there in the heat of Calcutta. Here\nit is nice and cool and rather chill when it rains, which it does almost\nevery day. Yesterday the view of the snows was simply superb, and it is the\nmost  picturesque  city  in  the world; there is such a mass of colour\neverywhere, especially in the dress of the Lepchas and Bhutias and the\nPaharees. Had it not been for the awful, corrugated iron roofs everywhere,\nit would have been twenty times more picturesque.\n", "My health was not bad in Calcutta; here it is the same \u2014 only, the sugar has\nentirely disappeared, the specific gravity being only 13. I slept very well\nlast night too; but the morning ride up, or climb, of a few miles is proving\ntoo much for my adipose tissues. The flannel clothes only made me worse, so\nI have given them up and have gone to my summer dress and am all right.\n", "I have sent you Sturdy's letter already \u2014 poor fellow \u2014 I do not know what\nto do for him. He is really \"living in a desert of his own making\" \u2014 you\nsee, one thing is not good for every one. Marriage has indeed proved a hell\nfor Sturdy. And he can not come, although \"he is skirting the coast of\nIndia\". Lord help the poor boy. May He cut all his bonds and make him free\nsoon. Aye, it is good that he is feeling the bondage \u2014 and not \"hugging and\nkissing its spokes of agony\".\n", "I gave a little lecture to the Hindus here yesterday, and I told them all\ntheir defects purposely and with their permission. I hope it will make them\nhowl.\n", "Miss M\u00fcller has taken a bungalow here and she is coming on Wednesday. I do\nnot know whether Miss Noble is coming with her. She [Miss Noble] had better\nbe your guest in Kashmir as according to our plan.\n", "Have you got that place yet or changed [places]? I am going to Kashmir\nanyway, as I have promised.\n", "I will be here only a few days and then I come to Calcutta, to be there only\na week \u2014 and [then] I start for the N.W. Of course this is not the time to\nsee anything in the N.W.P.; (North-West Provinces, now Uttar Pradesh.)\neverything is burning there. Yet that heat is much healthier than that of\nBengal.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

["\n", "XII\n", "C/O J. LYON,\n", "262 MICHIGAN AVENUE, CHICAGO,\n", "26th October, 1893.\n", "DEAR ADHYAPAKJI (Prof. John Henry Wright),\n", "\nYou  would  be  glad to know that I am doing well here and that almost\neverybody has been very kind to me, except of course the very orthodox. Many\nof the men brought together here from far-off lands have got projects and\nideas and missions to carry out, and America is the only place where there\nis a chance of success for everything. But I thought better and have given\nup speaking about my project entirely \u2014 because I am sure now \u2014 the heathen\ndraws more than his project. So I want to go to work earnestly for my own\nproject only keeping the project in the background and working like any\nother lecturer.\n", "He who has brought me hither and has not left me yet will not leave me ever\nI am here. You will be glad to know that I am doing well and expect to do\nvery well in the way of getting money. Of course I am too green in the\nbusiness but would soon learn my trade. I am very popular in Chicago. So I\nwant to stay here a little more and get money.\n", "Tomorrow I am going to lecture on Buddhism at the ladies' fortnightly club\n\u2014 which is the most influential in this city. How to thank you my kind\nfriend or Him who brought you to me; for now I think the success of my\nproject probable, and it is you who have made it so.\n", "May blessings and happiness attend every step of your progress in this\nworld.\n", "My love and blessings to your children.\n", "Yours affectionately ever,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

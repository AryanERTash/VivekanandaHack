["\n", "CXXXIX\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "LAHORE,\n", "15th November, 1897.\n", "DEAR MOTHER, (Shrimati Indumati Mitra)\n", "It is a matter of deep regret that in spite of my earnest wishes, I do not\nfind it feasible to go to Karachi this time and see you. First, because\nCaptain and Mrs. Sevier, who have come from England and are travelling with\nme for the last nine months nearly, are very anxious to buy some land at\nDehra Dun and start an orphanage there. It is their special request that I\nshould go and open the work. This makes it unavoidable to go to Dehra Dun.\n", "Secondly, owing to my kidney troubles I cannot count upon a long life. Even\nnow it is one of my desires to start a Math in Calcutta, towards which as\nyet I could do nothing. Moreover, the people of my country have withheld the\nlittle help that they used to give to our Math of late. They have got a\nnotion that I have brought plenty of money from England! Over and above\nthat, it is impossible to celebrate Shri Ramakrishna's festival this year,\nfor the proprietors of Rasmani's garden would not let me go there, as I am\nreturned from the West! Hence my first duty lies in seeing the few friends\nwe have in Rajputana and trying my best to have a centre in Calcutta. For\nthese  reasons I have been very sorry to postpone my tour to Sindh tat\npresent. I shall try my best to go there via Rajputana and Kathiawar. Please\ndo not be sorry. Never for a day do I forget you all. But duty must be\ndone first. It will ease me of my anxiety when a Math is established in\nCalcutta. Then I can hope that the work for which I struggled all my life\nthrough all sorts of privation and suffering will not die out after I cease\nto live in this body. I start for Dehra Dun this very day. After a week's\nstay there, to Rajputana, thence to Kathiawar, and so on.\n", "With blessings,\n", "Yours sincerely,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "LXXI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "1895.\n", "DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I have now got lots of newspapers etc., and you need not send any more. Let\nthe movement now confine itself to India....\n", "It isn't much use getting up a sensation every day. But avail yourselves of\nthis stir that is rife all over the country, and scatter yourselves in all\nquarters. In other words, try to start branches at different places. Let it\nnot  be  an  empty  sound merely. You must join the Madrasis and start\nassociations etc., at different places. What about the magazine which I\nheard was going to be started? Why are you nervous about conducting it? ...\nCome? Do something heroic! Brother, what if you do not attain Mukti, what if\nyou suffer damnation a few times? Is the saying untrue? \u2014\n", "\n\u2014 There are some saints who full of holiness in thought, word, and bleed,\nplease the whole world by their numerous beneficent acts, and who develop\ntheir own hearts by magnifying an atom of virtue in others as if it were as\ngreat as a mountain\" (Bhartrihari, Nitishataka).\n", "What if you don't get Mukti? What childish prattle! Lord! They say even the\nvenom of a snake loses its power by firmly denying it. Isn't it true? What\nqueer humility is this to say, \"I know nothing\u00a0!\" \"I am nothing\u00a0!\" This is\npseudo-renunciation  and  mock  modesty,  I  tell you. Off with such a\nself-debasing spirit! \"If  I do not know, who on earth does!\" What have you\nbeen doing so long if you now plead ignorance? These are the words of an\natheist \u2014 the humility of a vagabond wretch. We can do everything, and will\ndo everything! He who is fortunate enough will heroically join us, letting\nthe worthless mew like cats from their corner. A saint writes, \"Well, you\nhave had enough of blazoning. Now come back home.\" I would have called him a\nman if he could build a house and call me. Ten years' experience of such\nthings has made me wiser. I am no more to be duped by words. Let him who has\ncourage in his mind and love in his heart come with me. I want none else.\nThrough Mother's grace, single-handed I am worth a hundred thousand now and\nwill be worth two millions.... There is no certainty about my going back to\nIndia. I shall have to lead a wandering life there also, as I am doing here.\nBut here one lives in the company of scholars, and there one must live among\nfools \u2014 there is this difference as of the poles. People of this country\norganise and work, while our undertakings all come to dust clashing against\nlaziness \u2014 miscalled \"renunciation,\" \u2014 and jealousy, etc. \u2014 writes me big\nletters now and then, half of which I cannot decipher, which is a blessing\nto me. For a great part of the news is of the following description \u2014 that\nin such and such a place such and such a man was speaking ill of me, and\nthat he, being unable to bear the same, had a quarrel with him, and so\nforth. Many thanks for his kind defence of me. But what seriously hinders me\nfrom listening to what particular people may be saying about me is \u2014 \"\n", "An organised society is wanted. Let Shashi look to the household management,\nSanyal  take  charge of money matters and marketing, and Sharat act as\nsecretary, that is, carry on correspondence etc. Make a permanent centre \u2014\nit is no use making random efforts as you are doing now. Do you see my\npoint? I have quite a heap of newspapers, now I want you to do something. If\nyou can build a Math, I shall say you are heroes; otherwise you are nothing.\nConsult the Madras people when you work. They have a great capacity for\nwork. Celebrate this year's Shri Ramakrishna festival with such \u00e9clat as to\nmake it a record. The less the feeding propaganda is, the better. It is\nenough if you distribute Pras\u00e2da in earthen cups to the devotees standing in\nrows....\n", "I  am going to write a very short sketch of Shri Ramakrishna's life in\nEnglish, which I shall send you. Have it printed and translated into Bengali\nand sell it at the festival \u2014 people do not read books that are distributed\nfree. Fix some nominal price. Have the festival done with great\npomp. . . .\n", "You must have an all-sided intellect to do efficient work. In any towns or\nvillages you may visit, start an association wherever you find a number of\npeople  revering  Shri Ramakrishna. Have you travelled through so many\nvillages all for nothing? We must slowly absorb the Hari Sabh\u00e2s and such\nother associations. Well, I cannot tell you all \u2014 if I could but get another\ndemon like me! The Lord will supply me everything in time.... If one has got\npower, one must manifest it in action. ... Off with your ideas of Mukti and\nBhakti! There is only one way in the world,\n", "\n\u2014 \"The good live for others alone\", \"The wise man should sacrifice himself\nfor others\". I can secure my own good only by doing you good. There is no\nother way, none whatsoever.... You are God, I am God, and man is God. It is\nthis God manifested through humanity who is doing everything in this world.\nIs there a different God sitting high up somewhere? To work, therefore!\n", "Bimala has sent me a book written by Shashi (Sanyal). ... From a perusal of\nthat work Bimala has come to know that all the people of this world are\nimpure and that they are by their very nature debarred from having a jot of\nreligion; that only the handful of Brahmins that are in India have the sole\nright to it, and among these again, Shashi (Sanyal) and Bimala are the sun\nand moon, so to speak. Bravo! What a powerful religion indeed! In Bengal\nspecially, that sort of religion is very easy to practice. There is no\neasier  way than that. The whole truth about austerities and spiritual\nexercises is, in a nutshell, that I am pure and all the rest are impure! A\nbeastly, demoniac, hellish religion this! If the American people are unfit\nfor religion, if it is improper to preach religion here, why then ask their\nhelp? . . . What can remedy such a disease? Well, tell Shashi (Sanyal )to go\nto Malabar. The Raja there has taken his subjects' land and offered it at\nthe feet of Brahmins. There are big monasteries in every village where\nsumptuous dinners are given, supplemented by presents in cash. ... There is\nno harm in touching the non-Brahmin classes when it serves one's purpose;\nand when you have done with it, you bathe, for the non-Brahmins are as a\nclass  unholy  and must never be touched on other occasions! Monks and\nSannyasins and Brahmins of a certain type have thrown the country into ruin.\nIntent all the while on theft and wickedness, these pose as preachers of\nreligion! They will take gifts from the people and at the same time cry,\n\"Don't touch me!\" And what great things they have been doing! \u2014 \"If a potato\nhappens to touch a brinjal, how long will the universe last before it is\ndeluged?\" \"If they do not apply earth a dozen times to clean their hands,\nwill fourteen generations of ancestors go to hell, or twenty-four?\" \u2014 For\nintricate  problems  like  these they have been finding out scientific\nexplanations for the last two thousand years \u2014 while one fourth of the\npeople are starving. A girl of eight is married to a man of thirty, and the\nparents are jubilant over it.... And if anyone protests against it, the plea\nis put forward, \"Our religion is being overturned.\" What sort of religion\nhave they who want to see their girls becoming mothers before they attain\npuberty even and offer scientific explanations for it? Many, again, lay the\nblame at the door of the Mohammedans. They are to blame, indeed! Just read\nthe Grihya-Sutras through and see what is given as the marriageable age of a\ngirl. ... There it is expressly stated that a girl must be married before\nattaining puberty. The entire Grihya-Sutras enjoin this. And in the Vedic\nAshvamedha  sacrifice worse things would be done.... All the Br\u00e2hmanas\nmention them, and all the commentators admit them to be true. How can you\ndeny them?\n", "What I mean by mentioning all this is that there were many good things in\nthe ancient times, but there were bad things too. The good things are to be\nretained,  but the India that is to be, the future India. must be much\ngreater than ancient India. From the day Shri Ramakrishna was born dates the\ngrowth of modern India and of the Golden Age. And you are the agents to\nbring about this Golden Age. To work, with this conviction at heart!\n", "Hence, when you call Shri Ramakrishna an Incarnation and in the same breath\nplead your ignorance unhesitatingly, I say, \"You are false to the backbone!\"\nIf Ramakrishna Paramahamsa be true, you also are true. But you must show it.\n...  In you all there is tremendous power. The atheist has nothing but\nrubbish in him. Those who are believers are heroes. They will manifest\ntremendous power. The world will be swept before them. \"Sympathy and help to\nthe poor\"; \"Man is God, he is N\u00e2r\u00e2yana\"; \"In Atman there is no distinction\nof male or female, of Brahmin or Kshatriya, and the like\"; \"All is Narayana\nfrom the Creator down to a clump of grass.\" The worm is less manifested, the\nCreator more manifested. Every action that helps a being manifest its divine\nnature more and more is good, every action that retards it is evil.\n", "The only way of getting our divine nature manifested is by helping others to\ndo the same.\n", "If there is inequality in nature, still there must be equal chance for all\n\u2014 or if greater for some and for some less \u2014 the weaker should be given more\nchance than the strong.\n", "In other words, a Brahmin is not so much in need of education as a Chand\u00e2la.\nIf the son of a Brahmin needs one teacher, that of a Chandala needs ten. For\ngreater help must be given to him whom nature has not endowed with an acute\nintellect from birth. It is a madman who carries coals to Newcastle. The\npoor, the downtrodden, the ignorant, let these be your God.\n", "A dreadful slough is in front of you \u2014 take care; many fall into it and die.\nThe slough is this, that the present religion of the Hindus is not in the\nVedas,  nor in the Puranas, nor in Bhakti, nor in Mukti \u2014 religion has\nentered into the cooking-pot. The present religion of the Hindus is neither\nthe path of knowledge nor that of reason \u2014 it is \"Don't-touchism\". \"Don't\ntouch me!\" \"Don't touch me!\" \u2014 that exhausts its description. See that you\ndo not lose your lives in this dire irreligion of \"Don't-touchism\". Must the\nteaching, \" [071_rakhal_04.jpg] \u2014 Looking upon all beings as your own self\"\n\u2014 be confined to books alone? How will they grant salvation who cannot feed\na hungry mouth with a crumb of bread? How will those who become impure at\nthe mere breath of others purify others? Don't-touchism is a form of mental\ndisease. Beware! All expansion is life, all contraction is death. All love\nis expansions all selfishness is contraction. Love is therefore the only law\nof life. He who loves lives, he who is selfish is dying. Therefore love for\nlove's sake, because it is the only law of life, just as you breathe to\nlive. This is the secret of selfless love, selfless action and the rest. ...\nTry to help Shashi (Sanyal) if you can, in any ways He is a very good and\npious man, but of a narrow heart. It does not fall to the lot of all to feel\nfor the misery of others. Good Lord! Of all Incarnations Lord Chaitanya was\nthe  greatest,  but  he was comparatively lacking in knowledge; in the\nRamakrishna Incarnation there is knowledge, devotion and love \u2014 infinite\nknowledge, infinite love, infinite work, infinite compassion for all beings.\nYou have not yet been able to understand him. \" [071_rakhal_05.jpg] \u2014 Even\nafter hearing about Him, most people do not understand Him.\" What the whole\nHindu race has thought in ages, he lived in one life. His life is the living\ncommentary to the Vedas of all nations. People will come to know him by\ndegrees. My old watchword \u2014 struggle, struggle up to light! Onward!\n", "Yours in service,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

Last time I got from collection $30. I\nam going to have classes at 50 cents admission each.\n", "San Francisco had one lecture the other night [February 23] at 50\u00a2 each. It\npaid its expenses. This Monday [Sunday?] I am going to speak free \u2014 after\nthat a class.\n", "I went to see Mrs. Hurst [Hearst].[8]* She was not at home. I left a card\n\u2014 so with Prof. Le Conte.[9]*\n", "Mary [Hale] writes that you wrote her of my coming any day to the East. I\ndon't know. Here I have a large following \u2014ready \u2014 made by my books. Will\nget some money, not much. St. Francis [Francis Leggett] may put the money in\nthe bank for me \u2014 but can that be done without my signature? And I am here?\nIt is good if it can be done. Did you see any possibility of my books being\nsold for good to any publisher?\n", "The French invitation[10]* is all right. But it seems impossible to write\nany decent paper on the subject we chose. Because if I have to lecture and\nmake money, very little time will be left for anything else. Again, I can\nnot find any books (Sanskrit) here. So let me try to make a little money if\nI can and go to France all the same, but send them no paper. No scholarly\nwork can be done in this haphazard and hurried fashion. It means time and\nstudy.\n", "Shall I write to Mr. [Gerald] Nobel an acknowledgement and thanks? Write to\nme fully on these subjects if you can before you leave [for Europe]. My\nhealth is going on the same way. The gas is there more or less and this city\nis all climbing up[hill] \u2014 that tires me much.\n", "With all love,\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "P.S. Did anybody else respond to Mrs. Leggett's call?\n"]

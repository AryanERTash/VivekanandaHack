["\n", "THE RAMAKRISHNA HOME OF SERVICE VARANASI: AN APPEAL\n", "\n(Letter written by Swamiji, to accompany the First Report of the Ramakrishna\nHome of Service, Varanasi, February, 1902.)\n", "\nDear\u2014\n", "We beg your acceptance of the past year's Report of the Ramakrishna Home of\nService, Varanasi, embodying a short statement of our humble efforts towards\nthe amelioration, however little, of the miserable state into which a good\nmany of our fellow-beings, generally old men and women, are cast in this\ncity.\n", "In  these days of intellectual awakening and steadily asserting public\nopinion, the holy places of the Hindus, their condition, and method of work\nhave not escaped tile keen eye of criticism; and this city, being the holy\nof  holies  to all Hindus, has not failed to attract its full share of\ncensure.\n", "In other sacred places people go to purify themselves from sin, and their\nconnection with these places is casual, and of a few day's duration. In\nthis, the nicest ancient and living centre of Aryan religious activity,\nthere come men and women, and as a rule, old and decrepit, waiting to pass\nunto Eternal Freedom, through the greatest of all sanctifications, death\nunder the shadow of the temple of the Lord of the universe.\n", "And then there are those who have renounced everything for the good of the\nworld and have for ever lost the helping hands of their own flesh and blood\nand childhood's associations.\n", "They too are overtaken by the common lot of humanity, physical evil in the\nform of disease.\n", "It may be true that some blame attaches to the management of the place. It\nmay be true that the priests deserve a good part of the sweeping criticism\ngenerally heaped upon them; yet we must not forget the great truth\u2014like\npeople, like priests. If the people stand be with folded hands and watch the\nswift current of misery rushing past their doors, dragging men, women and\nchildren, the Sanny\u00e2sin and the householder into one common whirlpool of\nhelpless suffering, and make not the least effort to save any from the\ncurrent, only waxing eloquent at the misdoings of the priests of the holy\nplaces not one particle of suffering can ever be lessened, not one ever be\nhelped.\n", "Do we want to keep up the faith of our forefathers in the efficacy of the\nEternal City of Shiva towards salvation?\n", "If we do, we ought to be glad to see the number of those increase from year\nto year who come here to die.\n", "And blessed be the name of the Lord that the poor have this eager desire for\nsalvation, the same as ever.\n", "The poor who come here to die have voluntarily cut themselves off from any\nhelp they could have received in the places of their birth, and when disease\novertakes them, their condition we leave to your imagination and to your\nconscience as a Hindu to feel and to rectify.\n", "Brother, does it not make you pause and think of the marvellous attraction\nof this wonderful place of preparation for final rest? Does it not strike\nyou with a mysterious sense of awe\u2014this age-old and never-ending stream of\npilgrims marching to salvation through death?\n", "If it does\u2014come and lend us a helping hand.\n", "Never mind if your contribution is only a mite, your help only a little;\nblades of grass united into a rope will hold in confinement the maddest of\nelephants\u2014says the old proverb.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord of the universe,\nVivekananda.\n"]

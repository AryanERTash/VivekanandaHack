["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "THURSDAY, July 18, 1895.\n", "(The lesson today was mainly Shankara's argument against the conclusion of\nthe S\u00e2nkhya philosophy.)\n", "The Sankhyas say that consciousness is a compound, and beyond that, the last\nanalysis gives us the Purusha, Witness, but that there are many Purushas \u2014\neach of us is one. Advaita, on the contrary, affirms that Purushas can be\nonly  One,  that Purusha cannot be conscious, unconscious, or have any\nqualification,  for  either  these qualities would bind, or they would\neventually cease; so the One must be without any qualities, even knowledge,\nand  It  cannot  be  the cause of the universe or of anything. \"In the\nbeginning, existence only, One without a second\", says the Vedas.\n", "*    *    *\n", "The presence of Sattva with knowledge does not prove that Sattva is the\ncause of knowledge; on the contrary, Sattva calls out what was already\nexisting in man, as the fire heats an iron ball placed near it by arousing\nthe heat latent in it, not by entering into the ball.\n", "Shankara says, knowledge is not a bondage, because it is the nature of God.\nThe world ever is, whether manifested or unmanifested; so an eternal object\nexists.\n", "Jn\u00e2na-bala-kriy\u00e2 (knowledge, power, activity) is God. Nor does He need form,\nbecause the finite only needs form to interpose as an obstruction to catch\nand hold infinite knowledge; but God really needs no such help. There is no\n\"moving  soul\", there is only one Atman. Jiva (individual soul) is the\nconscious ruler of this body, in whom the five life principles come into\nunity, and yet that very Jiva is the Atman, because all is Atman. What you\nthink  about it is your delusion and not in the Jiva. You are God, and\nwhatever else you may think is wrong. You must worship the Self in Krishna,\nnot Krishna as Krishna. Only by worshipping the Self can freedom be won.\nEven personal God is but the Self objectified. \"Intense search after my own\nreality is Bhakti\", says Shankara.\n", "All the means we take to reach God are true; it is only like trying to find\nthe pole-star by locating it through the stars that are around it.\n", "*    *    *\n", "The Bhagavad-Gita is the best authority on Vedanta.\n"]

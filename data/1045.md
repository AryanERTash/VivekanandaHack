["\n", "I\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Balaram Bose\n", "Glory to Ramakrishna\n", "GHAZIPUR\n", "February 6, 1890\n", "RESPECTED SIR,\n", "I have talked with Pavhari Baba. He is a wonderful saint \u2014 the embodiment of\nhumility, devotion, and Yoga. Although he is an orthodox Vaishnava, he is\nnot prejudiced against others of different beliefs. He has tremendous love\nfor Mah\u00e2prabhu Chaitanya, and he [Pavhari Baba] speaks of Shri Ramakrishna\nas \"an incarnation of God\". He loves me very much, and I am going to stay\nhere for some days at his request.\n", "Pavhari Baba can live in Sam\u00e2dhi for from two to six months at a stretch. He\ncan read Bengali and has kept a photograph of Shri Ramakrishna in his room.\nI have not yet seen him face to face, since he speaks from behind a door,\nbut I have never heard such a sweet voice. I have many things to say about\nhim but not just at present.\n", "Please  try  to  get a copy of Chaitanya-Bh\u00e2gavata for him and send it\nimmediately to the following address: Gagan Chandra Roy, Opium Department,\nGhazipur. Please don't forget.\n", "Pavhari Baba is an ideal Vaishnava and a great scholar; but he is reluctant\nto reveal his learning. His elder brother acts as his attendant, but even he\nis not allowed to enter his room.\n", "Please send him a copy of Chaitanya-Mangala also, if it is still in print.\nAnd remember that if Pavhari Baba accepts your presents, that will be your\ngreat fortune. Ordinarily, he does not accept anything from anybody. Nobody\nknows what he eats or even what he does.\n", "Please don't let it be known that I am here and don't send news of anyone to\nme. I am busy with an important work.\n", "Your servant,\n", "NARENDRA\n"]

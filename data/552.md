["\n", "XCI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "Jan., 1896.\n", "DEAR SARADA,\n", ". . .Your idea of the paper is very good indeed. Apply yourself to it heart\nand  soul.  . . . Never mind the funds. . . . There are many to preach\nChristianity and Mohammedanism \u2014 you just go through the preaching of your\nown country's religion. But then if you can get hold of a Mohammedan who is\nversed in Arabic and have old Arabic books translated, it will be a good\nplan. There is much of Indian history in the Persian language. If you can\nhave the books translated bit by bit, it will be a good regular item. We\nwant quite a number of writers, then there is the difficult task of getting\nsubscribers. The way out is this: You lead a wandering life; wherever you\nfind Bengali language spoken, thrust the paper on whomsoever you can lay\nyour hands on. Enlist them by vehemence! \u2014 they would always turn tail the\nmoment they have to spend something. Never mind anything! Push it on! Begin\nto contribute articles, all of you who can. It won't do merely to sit idle.\nYou have done a heroic deed! Bravo! Those who falter and vacillate will lag\nbehind, and you will jump straight on top of all! Those that are working for\ntheir own salvation will neither have their own nor that of others. Let the\ncommotion that you make be such as to resound to the world's end. There are\npeople who are ready to pick holes in everything, but when it comes to the\nquestion of work, not a scent of them can be had! To work! \u2014 as far as in\nyou lies! Then I shall go to India and move the whole country. What fear!\n\"Even a snake loses its venom if it is insisted that it has none.\" These\npeople will go on the negative track, till they are actually reduced to\nnothing! . . .\n", "Gangadhar has done right heroic work! Well done! Kali has joined him in work\n\u2014 thrice well done!! Let one go to Madras, and another to Bombay, let the\nworld shake on its hinges! Oh, the grief! If I could get two or three like\nme, I could have left the world convulsed. As it is, I have to proceed\ngently. Move the world to its foundations! Send one to China, another to\nJapan! What will the poor householders do, with their little bits of life?\nIt is for the Sannyasins, Shiva's demons, to rend the skies with their\nshouts of \"Hara! Hara! Shambho!\"\n", "Yours affectionately\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

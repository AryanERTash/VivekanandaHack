["\n", "CLXXXIX\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Turiyananda\n", "6 PLACE DES ETATS UNIS,\n", "PARIS,\n", "13th August, 1900.\n", "DEAR BROTHER HARI,\n", "I got your letter from California. So three persons are getting spiritual\ntrances; well, it is not bad. Even out of that much good will come. Shri\nRamakrishna knows! Let things happen as they will. His work He knows, you\nand I are but servants and nothing else.\n", "I am sending this letter to San Francisco \u2014 care of Mrs. C. Panel. Just now\nI got some news from New York. They are well. Kali is on tour. Write in\ndetail about your health and work in San Francisco. And don't be indifferent\nto the question of sending money to the Math. See that money goes certainly\nevery month, from Los Angeles and San Francisco.\n", "I am on the whole doing well. I am shortly starting for England. I get news\nof Sharat. Recently he had an attack of dysentery. The rest are all well.\nThis time few got malaria; nor is it so prevalent on the banks of the Ganga.\nThis year, owing to the scarcity of rain, there is fear of famine in Bengal\nalso.\n", "By the grace of Mother, go on doing work, brother. Mother knows, and you\nknow \u2014 but I am off! Now I am going to take a rest.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

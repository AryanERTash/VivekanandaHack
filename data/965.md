## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "CXVIII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "DELHI,\n", "8th December, 1897.\n", "MYDEAR RAKHAL,\n", "We  shall start for Khetri tomorrow. Gradually the luggage has greatly\nincreased. After Khetri I intend to send everybody to the Math. I could get\ndone through them none of the work which I had hoped. That is to say, it is\nquite certain that none of them can do anything if he always remains with\nme.  Unless  each  goes about independently, he will not be able to do\nanything. The fact is, who will care for them if they are in my company?\nOnly waste of time. So I am sending them to the Math.\n", "Keep as a fund for some permanent work the balance of the money left after\nthe famine relief. Do not spend that money for any other purpose, and after\ngiving  the full accounts of the famine work, note down thus, \"So much\nbalance is left for some other good work\". . . .\n", "Work I want \u2014 I don't want any humbug. To those who have no desire to work I\nsay, \"My dear fellow, now go and follow your own way.\" As soon as I reach\nKhetri, I will send you the power of attorney with my signature if the\ndocument has reached there meanwhile. Open only those letters from America\nwhich bear the Boston postmark, not the others. Send all my letters to\nKhetri. I shall get money in Rajputana itself; no cause for anxiety on that\nscore. Try energetically for the piece of land; we must have the celebration\non our own ground this time.\n", "Is the money in the Bengal Bank, or have you kept it elsewhere? Be very\ncareful about money matters; keep detailed accounts, and regarding money\nknow for certain that one cannot rely even on one's own father.\n", "Give my love to all. Write to me how Hari is doing. Recently I met at Dehra\nDun the Ud\u00e2si S\u00e2dhu, Kaly\u00e2n Dev, and a few others. I hear the people at\nHrishikesh are very eager to see me and are asking again and again about me.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

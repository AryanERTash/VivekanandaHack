["\n", "XIX\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "DETROIT,\n", "30th March, 1894.\n", "DEAR SISTER,\n", "Your and Mother Church's letters came together just now, acknowledging the\nreceipt of the money. I am very glad to receive the Khetri letter, which I\nsend  back for your perusal. You would find from it that he wants some\nnewspaper clippings. I do not think I have any except the Detroit one, which\nI will send to him. If you can get hold of some others, kindly send some\nover to him if it be possible and convenient. You know his address \u2014 H. H.\nthe Maharajah of Khetri, Rajputana, India. Of course, this letter is for the\nperusal of the holy family alone. Mrs. Breed wrote to me a stiff burning\nletter first, and then today I got a telegram from her inviting me to be her\nguest for a week. Before this I got a letter from Mrs. Smith of New York\nwriting on her behalf and another lady Miss Helen Gould and another Dr.__ to\ncome over to New York. As the Lynn Club wants me on the 17th of next month,\nI am going to New York first and come in time for their meeting at Lynn.\n", "Next summer, if I do not go away, which Mrs. Bagley insists I should not, I\nmay go to Annisquam where Mrs. Bagley has engaged a nice house. Mrs. Bagley\nis a very spiritual lady, and Mr. Palmer a spirituous gentleman but very\ngood. What shall I write more? I am all right in nice health of body and\nmind. May you all be blessed, ever blessed, my dear, dear sisters. By the\nby, Mrs. Sherman has presented me with a lot of things amongst which is a\nnail  set and letter holder and a little satchel etc., etc. Although I\nobjected, especially to the nail set, as very dudish with mother-of-pearl\nhandles, she insisted and I had to take them, although I do not know what to\ndo with that brushing instrument. Lord bless them all. She gave me one\nadvice \u2014 never to wear this Afrikee dress in society. Now I am a society\nman!  Lord!  What  comes  next? Long life brings queer experiences! My\ninexpressible love for you all, my holy family.\n", "Your brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

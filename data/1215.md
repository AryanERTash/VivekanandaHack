["\n", "CLXXI\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "VEDANTA SOCIETY,\n", "102 EAST 58TH STREET,\n", "NEW YORK,\n", "9th June 1900.\n", "DEAR CHRISTINA,\n", "I could not write more, as the last few weeks of my stay in California was\none more relapse and great suffering. However, I got one great benefit out\nof it inasmuch as I came to know I have really no disease, except worry and\nfear. My kidneys are as sound as any other healthy man's. All the symptoms\nof Bright's disease etc., are only brought on by nerves.\n", "I wrote you one, however, from 770 Oak Street, San Francisco, to which I did\nnot get any reply. Of course, I was bedridden then and my address book was\nnot in the place I was in. There was a mistake in number. I cannot believe\nyou did not reply willingly.\n", "As you see, now I am in New York, and will be here a few days. I have an\ninvitation from Mrs. Walton of Cleveland, Ohio. I have accepted it. She\nwrites me you are also invited and have accepted her invitation. Well, we\nwill meet in Cleveland then. I am sure to see you before I go to Europe \u2014\neither there or anywhere you wish. If you don't think it would be possible\nfor you to come to Ohio, I will come to any other place you want me to come\nto say goodbye.\n", "When is your school going to close? Write me all about your plans \u2014 do!\n", "Miss Noble wants me very much to go to Cleveland. I would be very, very glad\nto get a few weeks' seclusion and rest before I start with friends who do\nnot disturb me at all. I know I will find rest and peace that way, and you\ncan help me any amount in that. In Cleveland, of course, there will be a few\nfriends always and much talkee-talkee as a matter of course. So if you think\nI will have real peace and rest elsewhere, just write all about it.\n", "My reply to the Cleveland lady depends on your letter.\n", "How I wish I were in Detroit or elsewhere just now, among friends who I know\nare good and true always. This is weakness; but when the physical vitality\nis lowered and the nerves all unstrung, I feel so, so much to depend upon\nsomebody. You will be glad to learn I made a little money in the West. So I\nwill be quite able to pay my expenses.\n", "Write soon.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

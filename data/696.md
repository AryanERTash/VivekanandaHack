If you tell a man, \"You are low, you are vile\", in season and out of season, then he is bound to believe in course of time that he is really such. This is called hypnotism. The non - brahmin classes are now slowly rousing themselves. Their faith in Brahminical scriptures and Mantras is getting shaken. The Brahmins, in fact, gradually took a course of gross immorality and oppression. Through selfishness they introduced a large number of strange, non - vedic, immoral, and unreasonable doctrines -- simply to keep intact their own prestige. And the fruits of that they are reaping forthwith.\n", "Disciple: What may these fruits be, sir?\n", "Swamiji: Don't you perceive them? Popular customs, local usages, and observances prevalent among women only -- have not these taken the place of the Smritis everywhere? Who obeys, and whom?

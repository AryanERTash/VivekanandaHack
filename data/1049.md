["\n", "V\n", "To Tulsiram Ghosh\n", "GHAZIPUR\n", "10 May 1890\n", "DEAR TULSIRAM:[6]*\n", "A basket of roses will be sent to you in a few days at Chitpur. Do you\nplease send them up immediately to Shashi [Swami Ramakrishnananda, at the\nBaranagore Math]. I too am going off from this place very\nsoon. I go perhaps to Bareilly and up. What is Balaram Babu [Balaram Bose]\ndoing?\n", "My Pran\u00e2ms etc. to you all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "NARENDRA\n"]

["\n", "CXL\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "LONDON,\n", "10th August, 1899.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I got a lot of news from your letter. My health was much better on the ship,\nbut, after landing, owing to flatulence it is rather bad now. . . . There is\na lot of difficulty here \u2014 all friends have gone out of town for the summer.\nIn addition my health is not so good, and there is a lot of inconvenience\nregarding food etc. So in a few days I leave for America. Send an account to\nMrs.  Bull  as  to how much was spent on purchase of land, how much on\nbuildings, how much on maintenance etc.\n", "Sarada writes that the magazine is not going well. . . . Let him publish the\naccount of my travels, and thoroughly advertise it beforehand \u2014 he will have\nsubscribers rushing in. Do people like a magazine if three-fourths of it are\nfilled with pious stuff? Anyway pay special attention to the magazine.\nMentally take it as though I were not. Act independently on this basis. \"We\ndepend on the elder brother for money, learning, everything\" \u2014 such an\nattitude is the road to ruin. If all the money even for the magazine is to\nbe collected by me and all the articles too are from my pen \u2014 what will you\nall do? What are our Sahibs then doing? I have finished my part. You do what\nremains to be done. Nobody is there to collect a single penny, nobody to do\nany  preaching,  none has brains enough to take proper care of his own\naffairs, none has the capacity to write one line, and all are saints for\nnothing!  .  .  .  If this be your condition, then for six months give\neverything into the hands of the boys \u2014 magazine, money, preaching work,\netc. If they are also not able to do anything, then sell off everything, and\nreturning the proceeds to the donors go about as mendicants. I get no news\nat all from the Math. What is Sharat doing? I want to see work done. Before\ndying, I want to see that what I have established as a result of my lifelong\nstruggle is put in a more or less running condition. Consult the Committee\nin every detail regarding money matters. Get the signatures of the Committee\nfor every item of expenditure. Otherwise you also will be in for a bad name.\nThis much is customary that people want some time or other an account of\ntheir donations. It is very wrong not to have it ready at every turn. . . .\nBy such lethargy in the beginning, people finally become cheats. Make a\ncommittee of all those who are in the Math, and no expenditure will be made\nwhich is not countersigned by them \u2014 none at all! I want work, I want vigour\n\u2014 no matter who lives or dies. What are death and life to a Sannyasin?\n", "If Sharat cannot rouse up Calcutta, . . . if you are not able to construct\nthe embankment this year, then you will see the fun! I want work \u2014 no humbug\nabout it. My respectful salutations to Holy Mother.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "LXXXV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "LONDON,\n", "13th Nov., 1895.\n", "MY DEAR AKHANDANANDA,\n", "I am very glad to receive your letter. It is excellent work that you are\ndoing. R\u2014 is very liberal and openhanded, but no advantage should be taken\nover him for that reason. About the raising of funds by Shrim\u00e2n \u2014, well, it\nis a fair enterprise; but my boy, this is a very queer world, where even the\nWorld-Gods Brahm\u00e2 and Vishnu find it difficult to evade the clutches of lust\nand gold. Wherever there is any the least concern with money, there is the\nchance for misunderstanding. Let therefore nobody undertake such work as\nraising  money  on  behalf  of  the Math. ... Whenever you hear of any\nhouseholder collecting funds in my or our name on the plea of erecting a\nMath, or some such thing, the first thing you should do is to distrust him,\nand never set your hand to it. The more so, as householders of poor means\ntake to various tricks to supply their wants. Therefore, if ever a trusty\ndevotee or a householder with a heart, being of affluent circumstances,\nundertakes such works as the founding of a Math, or if the funds raised be\nkept in the custody of a trusty householder of wealth \u2014 well and good,\notherwise never have a hand in it. On the contrary, you must dissuade others\nfrom such a thing. You are but a boy and are ignorant of the snare of gold.\nOpportunities will turn even a staunch moralist into a cheat. This is the\nway of the world.\n", "It is not at all in our nature to do a work conjointly. It is to this that\nour  miserable condition is due. He who knows how to obey knows how to\ncommand. Learn obedience first. Among these Western nations, with such a\nhigh spirit of independence, the spirit of obedience is equally strong. We\nare  all  of  us self-important \u2014 which never produces any work. Great\nenterprise, boundless courage, tremendous energy, and, above all, perfect\nobedience \u2014 these are the only traits that lead to individual and national\nregeneration. These traits are altogether lacking in us.\n", "Go on with the work as you are doing it, but then you must pay particular\nattention to study. J\u2014 Babu has sent a Hindi magazine, in which Pundit R\u2014 of\nAlwar has published a translation of my Chicago Address. Please convey my\nspecial indebtedness and thanks to both.\n", "Let me now address myself to you \u2014 take particular care to start a centre in\nRajputana. It must be in some central place like Jaipur or Ajmer. Then\nbranches must be established in towns like Alwar and Khetri. You must mix\nwith all, we do not want to quarrel with any. Give my loving embrace to\nPundit N\u2014; the man is very energetic, and will be a very practical man in\ntime.  Tender  my  loving  regards  to Mr. M\u2014 and \u2014ji too. A Religious\nAssociation or something of the kind has been afoot at Ajmer \u2014 what is it?\nLet me know all about it. M\u2014 Babu writes that he and others have written me\nletters; but I have not received any up till now. . . . About Maths, or\ncentres, or anything of the kind, it is no use starting them in Calcutta;\nVaranasi is the place for them. I have many plans like that, but all depends\non funds. You will know of them by degrees. You might have noticed from the\npapers  that our movement is steadily gaining ground in England. Every\nenterprise in this country takes some time to have a go. But once John Bull\nsets his hand to a thing, he will never let it go. The Americans are quick,\nbut they are somewhat like straw on fire, ready to be extinguished. Do not\npreach to the public that Ramakrishna Paramahamsa was an Incarnation, and\nthings of that sort. I have some followers at \u2014 look after them. . . .\nInfinite power will come unto you \u2014 never fear. Be pure, have faith, be\nobedient.\n", "Teach against the marriage of boys. No scripture ever sanctions it. But for\nthe present say nothing against little girls being married. Directly you\nstop the marriage of boys, that of girls will stop of itself. Girls surely\nare not going to marry among themselves! Write to the Secretary, Arya Samaj,\nLahore, asking the whereabouts of a Sannyasin named Achyutananda who used to\nlive with them. Make special inquiry of the man. . . . Never fear.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

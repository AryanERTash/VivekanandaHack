I had some beautiful letters from India.\nThe letter from Ajit Singh (The Raja of Khetri, a very devoted disciple of\nthe Swami.) shows that the phonograph has not reached yet, and it was dated\n8th  June. So I do not think it is time yet to get an answer. I am not\nastonished at my friends' asking Cook & Sons to hunt for me; I have not\nwritten for a long time.\n", "I  have  a  letter from Madras which says they will soon send money to\nNarasimha (Narasimhacharya. Vide the letter dated [6]February 14, 1894.) \u2014\nin fact, as soon as they get a reply to their letter written to Narasimha.\nSo kindly let Narasimha know it. The photographs have not reached me \u2014\nexcept two of Fishkill when I was there last. Landsberg (Leon Landsberg.\nVide the letter dated [7]June 28, 1894.) has kindly sent over the letters.\nFrom here I will probably go over to Fishkill. The meerschaum[8]* was not\nsent over by me direct, but I left it to the Guernseys. And they are a lazy\nfamily in that respect.\n", "I have beautiful letters from the sisters.\n", "By the by, your missionaries try to make me a malcontent before the English\ngovernment in India, and the Lieutenant Governor of Bengal in a recent\nspeech  hinted  that  the  recent  revival of Hinduism was against the\ngovernment.  Lord bless the missionary. Everything is fair in love and\n(religion?).\n", "The word Shri means \"of good fortune\", \"blessed\", etc. Paramahamsa is a\ntitle for a Sanny\u00e2si who has reached the goal, i.e. realized God. Neither am\nI blessed nor have I reached the goal; but they are courteous, that is all.\nI will soon write to my brothers in India. I am so lazy, and I cannot send\nover the newspaper nonsense day after day.\n", "I want a little quiet, but it is not the will of the Lord, it seems. At\nGreenacre I had to talk on an average 7 to 8 hours a day \u2014 that was rest, if\nit ever was. But it was of the Lord, and that brings vigour along with it.\n", "I have not much to write, and I do not remember anything of what I said or\ndid all these places over. So I hope to be excused.\n", "I will be here a few days more at least, and therefore I think it would be\nbetter to send over my mail here.\n", "I have now almost become dizzy through the perusal of a heavy and big mail,\nso excuse my hasty scrawl.\n", "Ever affectionately yours,\n", "SWAMI VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

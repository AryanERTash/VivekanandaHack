["\n", "XXX\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "ANNISQUAM\n", "DATE DO NOT KNOW\n", "[Postmarked: August 28, 1894]\n", "DEAR MOTHER\n", "I  have  been  for three days at Magnolia. Magnolia is one of the most\nfashionable and beautiful seaside resorts of this part. I think the scenery\nis better than that of Annisquam. The rocks there are very beautiful, and\nthe  forests  run  down to the very edge of the water. There is a very\nbeautiful pine forest. A lady of Chicago and her daughter, Mrs. Smith and\nMrs.  Sawyer, were the friends that invited me up there. They had also\narranged a lecture for me, out of which I got $43. I met a good many Boston\npeople \u2014 Mrs. Smith Junior, who said she knows Harriet, and Mrs. Smith the\nelder, [who] knows you well.\n", "In Boston the other day I met a Unitarian clergyman who said he lives next\nto you in Chicago. I have unfortunately forgotten his name. Mrs. Smith is a\nvery nice lady and treated me with all courtesy. Mrs. Bagley is kind as\never, and I will have to remain here a few days more, I am afraid. Prof.\nWright and I are having a good time. Prof. Bradley of Evanston[6]* has gone\nhome. If you ever meet him at Evanston, give him my best love and regards.\nHe is really a spiritual man.\n", "I do not find anything more to write.\n", "Some  unknown friend has sent me from New York a fountain pen. So I am\nwriting with it to test it. It is working very smoothly and nicely as you\ncan judge from the writing. Perhaps Narasimha's difficulties have been\nsettled by this time, and \"heathen India\" has helped him out yet, I hope.\n", "What is Father Pope doing? What the Babies are doing and where are they?\nWhat news of our Sam?[7]* Hope he is prospering. Kindly give him my best\nlove. Where is Mother Temple now?\n", "Well, after all, I could fill up two pages. Yes, there was a Miss Barn (?)\nwho said she met me at your house. She is a young lady of Chicago.\n", "Magnolia is a good bathing place and I had two baths in the sea. A large\nconcourse of men and women go to bathe there every day \u2014 the most part men.\nAnd strange, women do not give up their coat of mail even while bathing.\nThat is how these mailclad she-warriors of America have got the superiority\nover men.\n", "Our Sanskrit poets lavish all the power of expression they have upon the\nsoft body of women \u2014 the Sanskrit word for women is \"Komala\", the soft body;\nbut the mailclad ones of this country are \"armadillas\", I think. You cannot\nimagine how ludicrous it appears to a foreigner who never saw it before.\nShiva, Shiva.\n", "Now Narasimha's Mrs. Smith does not torture you anymore with letters, I\nhope. Did I tell you I met your friend Mrs. H. O. Quarry at Swampscott? \u2014\nshe can swamp a house for all that, not to speak of a cott \u2014 and that I met\nthere the woman that pulls by the nose Mr. Pullman?[8]* And I also heard\nthere the best American singer, (Miss Emma Thursby.) they said \u2014 she sang\nbeautifully; she sang \"Bye Baby Bye\". I am having a very, very good time all\nthe time, Lord be praised.\n", "I have written to India not to bother me with constant letters. Why, when I\nam travelling in India nobody writes to me. Why should they spend all their\nsuperfluous energy in scrawling letters to me in America? My whole life is\nto be that of a wanderer \u2014 here or there or anywhere. I am in no hurry. I\nhad a foolish plan in my head unworthy of a Sannyasin. I have given it up\nnow and mean to take life easy. No indecent hurry. Don't you see, Mother\nChurch? You must always remember, Mother Church, that I cannot settle down\neven  at the North Pole, that wander about I must \u2014 that is my vow, my\nreligion. So India or North Pole or South Pole \u2014 don't care where. Last two\nyears I have been travelling among races whose language even I cannot speak.\n\"I have neither father nor mother nor brothers nor sisters nor friends nor\nfoes, nor home nor country \u2014 a traveller in the way of eternity, asking no\nother help, seeking no other help but God.\"\n", "Yours ever affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

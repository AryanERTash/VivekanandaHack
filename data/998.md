I had a very hopeful note from Margo. I am mentally very well; indeed I never really was\nso calm as of late. The lectures for one thing do not disturb my sleep, that\nis some gain. I am doing some writing too. The lectures here were taken down\nby a stenographer, the people here want to print them.\n", "I  learn  they  are  well and doing good work at the Math \u2014 from Swami\nSaradananda's letter to Joe. Slowly as usual plans are working; but Mother\nknows, as I say. May She give me release and find other workers for Her\nplans. By the by, I have made a discovery as to the mental method of really\npractising what the Gita teaches, of working without an eye to results. I\nhave  seen  much  light  on concentration and attention and control of\nconcentration, which if practised will take us out of all anxiety and worry.\nIt is really the science of bottling up our minds whenever we like. Now what\nabout yourself, poor Dhira Mata! This is the result of motherhood and its\npenalties; we all think of ourselves, and never of the Mother. How are you?\nHow are things going on with you? What about your daughter? about Mrs.\nBriggs?\n", "I  hope Turiyananda is completely recovered now and working. Poor man,\nsuffering is the lot! Never mind; there is a pleasure in suffering even,\nwhen it is for others, is there not? Mrs. Leggett is doing well; so is Joe;\nI \u2014 they say \u2014 I too am. May be they are right. I work anyway and want to\ndie in harness; if that be what Mother wants, I am quite content.\n", "Ever your son,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

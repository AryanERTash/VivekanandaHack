["\n", "CXXVII\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "THE MATH, BELOOR, HOWRAH DIST.,\n", "25th October, 1898.\n", "MY DEAR CHRISTINA,\n", "How are you? I am very anxious about your health. I have long not had any\nletter from you.\n", "My health again failed badly. I had, therefore, to leave Kashmir in haste\nand come to Calcutta. The doctors say I ought not go tramping again this\nwinter. That is such a disappointment, you know. However, I am coming to the\nU. S. this summer. Mrs. Bull and Miss MacLeod enjoyed this year's trip to\nKashmir immensely, and now they are having a glimpse of the old monuments\nand buildings of Delhi, Agra, Jeypore [Jaipur], etc.\n", "Do write a nice, long letter if you have time, and do not work yourself to\ndeath. Duty is duty, no doubt; but we have our duties, not only to our\nmother  etc., but to others also. Sometimes one duty asks for physical\nsacrifice, whilst the other insists on great care for our health. Of course,\nwe follow the stronger motive, and [I] do not know which will prove stronger\nin your case. Anyhow, take great care of your body, now that your sisters\nhave come to your help.\n", "How do you manage the family? \u2014 the expenses etc? Write me all you like to\nwrite. Give me a long chat, will you? Do!\n", "I am getting better every day \u2014 and then the long months before I can start\nfor the U.S. Never mind, \"Mother\" knows what is best for us. She will show\nthe way. I am now in Bhakti. As I am growing old, Bhakti is taking the place\nof Jn\u00e2na. Did you get the new Awakened India? How do you like it?\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

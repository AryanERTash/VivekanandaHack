["\n", "CII\n", "(Translated from Bengali.)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "AMRITSAR,\n", "2nd September, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Yogen tells me in a letter to buy the house at Baghbazar for Rs. 20,000.\nEven  if we buy that house, there are still a lot of difficulties; for\nexample, we shall have to break it down in part and make the drawing room\ninto a big hall, and similar alterations and repairs. Moreover the house is\nvery old and ramshackle. However, consult Girish Babu and Atul and do what\nyou decide to be best. Today I am leaving by the two o'clock train with all\nmy party for Kashmir. The recent stay at Dharamsala Hills has improved my\nhealth much, and the tonsillitis, fever, etc. have completely disappeared.\nFrom a letter of yours I got all the news. Niranjan, Latu, Krishnalal,\nDinanath, Gupta, and Achyut are all going to Kashmir with me.\n", "The gentleman from Madras who donated Rs. 1,500 for famine relief wants an\naccount of how exactly the money was expended. Send him such an account. We\nare doing more or less well.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Give my love to all at the Math.\n", "V.\n"]

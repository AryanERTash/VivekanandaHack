Those that are weak must make themselves great\nworkers, great heroes \u2014 never mind money, it will drop from the heavens. It is the heart, the heart that conquers, not the brain.\nBooks and learning, Yoga and meditation and illumination \u2014 all are but dust\ncompared with love. It is love that gives you the supernatural powers, love\nthat gives you Bhakti, love that gives illumination, and love, again, that\nreads to emancipation. This indeed is worship, worship of the Lord in the\nhuman  tabernacle,  \" \u0928\u0947\u0926\u0902 \u092f\u0926\u093f\u0926\u092e\u0941\u092a\u093e\u0938\u0924\u0947 \u2014 not this that people\nworship\". (That is things other than God.) This is but the beginning, and\nunless we spread over the whole of India, nay, the whole earth, in that way,\nwhere lies the greatness of our Lord!\n", "Let people see whether or not the touch of our Lord's feet confers divinity\non man! It is this that is called liberation-in-life \u2014 when the last trace\nof egoism and selfishness is gone. Well done! Glory to the Lord! Gradually\ntry to spread. If you can, go to Calcutta, and raise a fund with the help of\nanother band of boys; set one or two of them to work at some place, and\nbegin somewhere else. Spread in that way, and go on inspecting them. You\nwill  see that the work will gradually become permanent, and spread of\nreligion and education will follow as a matter of course. I have given\nparticular instructions to them in Calcutta. Do that kind of work, and I\nshall carry you on my shoulders \u2014 bravo! You will see that by degrees every\ndistrict will become a centre \u2014 and that a permanent one. I am soon going\ndown to the plains. I am a fighter, and shall die in the battlefield. Does\nit behave me to sit up here like a zenana lady?\n", "Yours with all love,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

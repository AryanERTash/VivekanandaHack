The babies are doing well\nin Mudville (Kenosha, Wisconsin) \u2014 in their nunnery, I am sure. It is very\nhot here, but now and then a breeze comes up which cools it down. I am now\nwith Miss [Mary A.] Phillips. Will move off from here on Tuesday to another\nplace.\n", "Here I find a quotation from a speech by Sir Monier Williams, professor of\nSanskrit in the Oxford University. It is very strange as coming from one who\nevery day expects to see the whole of India converted to Christianity. \"And\nyet it is a remarkable characteristic of Hinduism that it neither requires\nnor attempts to make converts. Nor is it at present by any means decreasing\nin  numbers,  nor  is  it  being  driven  out of the field by two such\nproselytizing religions as Mahomedanism [sic] and Christianity. On the\ncontrary, it is at present rapidly increasing. And far more remarkable than\nthis is that, it is all-receptive, all-embracing and all-comprehensive. It\nclaims to be the one religion of humanity, of human nature, of the entire\nworld. It cares not to oppose the progress of Christianity nor of any other\nreligion. For it has no difficulty in including all other religions within\nits all-embracing arms and ever-widening fold. And in real fact Hinduism has\nsomething to offer which is suited to all minds. Its very strength lies in\nits infinite adaptability to the infinite diversity of human characters and\nhuman tendencies. It has its highly spiritual and abstract side suited to\nthe philosophical higher classes. Its practical and concrete side suited to\nthe man of affairs and the man of the world. Its aesthetic and ceremonial\nside suited to the man of poetic feeling and imagination. Its quiescent and\ncontemplative side suited to the man of peace and lover of seclusion.\n", "\"Indeed, the Hindus were Spinozists 2,000 years before the birth of Spinoza,\nDarwinians centuries before the birth of Darwin, and evolutionists centuries\nbefore the doctrine of evolution had been accepted by the Huxleys of our\ntime, and before any word like evolution existed in any language of the\nworld.\"\n", "This, as coming from one of the staunchest defenders of Christianity, is\nwonderful indeed. But he seems to have got the idea quite correct.\n", "Now I am going to send up the orange coat today; as for the books that came\nto me from Philadelphia, I do not think they are worthy of being sent at\nall. I do not know what I am going to do next. Patiently wait and resign\nmyself unto His guidance \u2014 that is my motto. My love to you all.\n", "Your affectionate son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "THURSDAY, July 25, 1895. (Patanjali's Yoga Aphorisms)\n", "\"Things may be done, caused to be done, or approved of\", and the effect upon\nus is nearly equal.\n", "Complete  continence gives great intellectual and spiritual power. The\nBrahmach\u00e2rin must be sexually pure in thought, word, and deed. Lose regard\nfor the body; get rid of the consciousness of it so far as possible.\n", "\u00c2sana  (posture)  must  be steady and pleasant; and constant practice,\nidentifying the mind with the Infinite, will bring this about.\n", "Continual attention to one object is contemplation.\n", "When  a  stone is thrown into still water, many circles are made, each\ndistinct but all interacting; so with our minds; only in us the action is\nunconscious, while with the Yogi it is conscious. We are spiders in a web,\nand Yoga practice will enable us like the spider to pass along any strand of\nthe web we please. Non-Yogis are bound to the particular spot where they\nare.\n", "*            *            *\n", "To injure another creates bondage and hides the truth. Negative virtues are\nnot enough; we have to conquer Maya, and then she will follow us. We only\ndeserve things when they cease to bind us. When the bondage ceases, really\nand truly, all things come to us. Only those who want nothing are masters of\nnature.\n", "Take refuge in some soul who has already broken his bondage, and in time he\nwill free you through his mercy. Higher still is to take refuge in the Lord\n(Ishvara), but it is the most difficult; only once in a century can one be\nfound who has really done it. Feel nothing, know nothing, do nothing, have\nnothing, give up all to God, and say utterly, \"Thy will be done\". We only\ndream this bondage. Wake up and let it go. Take refuge in God, only so can\nwe cross the desert of Maya. \"Let go thy hold, Sannyasin bold, say, Om tat\nsat, Om!\"\n", "It is our privilege to be allowed to be charitable, for only so can we grow.\nThe poor man suffers that we may be helped; let the giver kneel down and\ngive thanks, let the receiver stand up and permit. See the Lord back of\nevery being and give to Him. When we cease to see evil, the world must end\nfor us, since to rid us of that mistake is its only object. To think there\nis any imperfection creates it. Thoughts of strength and perfection alone\ncan cure it. Do what good you can, some evil will inhere in it; but do all\nwithout regard to personal result, give up all results to the Lord, then\nneither good nor evil will affect you.\n", "Doing work is not religion, but work done rightly leads to freedom. In\nreality all pity is darkness, because whom to pity? Can you pity God? And is\nthere  anything  else?  Thank God for giving you this world as a moral\ngymnasium to help your development, but never imagine you can help the\nworld. Be grateful to him who curses you, for he gives you a mirror to show\nwhat cursing is, also a chance to practise self-restraint; so bless him and\nbe glad. Without exercise, power cannot come out; without the mirror, we\ncannot see ourselves.\n", "Unchaste imagination is as bad as unchaste action. Controlled desire leads\nto the highest result. Transform the sexual energy into spiritual energy,\nbut do not emasculate, because that is throwing away the power. The stronger\nthis force, the more can be done with it. Only a powerful current of water\ncan do hydraulic mining.\n", "What we need today is to know there is a God and that we can see and feel\nHim here and now. A Chicago professor says, \"Take care of this world, God\nwill take care of the next.\" What nonsense! If we can take care of this\nworld, what need of a gratuitous Lord to take care of the other!\n"]

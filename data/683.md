["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "SUNDAY, August 4, 1895.\n", "\"Whom the ignorant worship, Him I preach unto thee.\"\n", "This one and only God is the \"knownest\" of the known. He is the one thing we\nsee everywhere. All know their own Self, all know, \"I am\", even animals. All\nwe know is the projection of the Self. Teach this to the children, they can\ngrasp it. Every religion has worshipped the Self, even though unconsciously,\nbecause there is nothing else.\n", "This indecent clinging to life as we know it here, is the source of all\nevil. It causes all this cheating and stealing. It makes money a god and all\nvices and fears ensue. Value nothing material and do not cling to it. If you\ncling to nothing, not even life, then there is no fear. \"He goes from death\nto death who sees many in this world.\" There can be no physical death for us\nand no mental death, when we see that all is one. All bodies are mine; so\neven body is eternal, because the tree, the animal, the sun, the moon, the\nuniverse itself is my body; then how can it die? Every mind, every thought\nis mine, then how can death come? The Self is never born and never dies.\nWhen we realise this, all doubts vanish. \"I am, I know, I love\" \u2014 these can\nnever be doubted. There is no hunger, for all that is eaten is eaten by me.\nIf a hair falls out, we do not think we die; so if one body dies, it is but\na hair falling. . . .\n", "The  superconscious  is  God,  is beyond speech beyond thought, beyond\nconsciousness. . . . There are three states, \u2014 brutality (Tamas), humanity\n(Rajas), and divinity (Sattva). Those attaining the highest state simply\nare.  Duty dies there; they only love and as a magnet draw others to them.\nThis is freedom. No more you do moral acts, but whatever you do is moral.\nThe Brahmavit (knower of God) is higher than all gods. The angels came to\nworship Jesus when he had conquered delusion and had said, \"Get thee behind\nme, Satan.\" None can help a Brahmavit, the universe itself bows down before\nhim. His every desire is fulfilled, his spirit purifies others; therefore\nworship the Brahmavit if you wish to attain the highest. When we have the\nthree great \"gifts of God\" \u2014 a human body, intense desire to be free, and\nthe help of a great soul to show us the way \u2014 then liberation is certain for\nus. Mukti is ours.\n", "*            *            *\n", "Death of the body for ever is Nirvana. It is the negative side and says, \"I\nam not this, nor this, nor this.\" Vedanta takes the further step and asserts\nthe positive side \u2014 Mukti or freedom. \"I am Existence absolute, Knowledge\nabsolute, Bliss absolute, I am He\", this is Vedanta, the cap-stone of the\nperfect arch.\n", "The great majority of the adherents of Northern Buddhism believe in Mukti\nand  are  really  Vedantists.  Only  the  Ceylonese  accept Nirvana as\nannihilation.\n", "No belief or disbelief can kill the \"I\". That which comes with belief and\ngoes with disbelief is only delusion. Nothing teaches the Atman. \"I salute\nmy own Self.\" \"Self-illuminated, I salute myself, I am Brahman.\" The body is\na dark room; when we enter it, it becomes illuminated, it becomes alive.\nNothing can ever affect the illumination; it cannot be destroyed. It may be\ncovered, but never destroyed.\n", "*            *            *\n", "At the present time God should be worshipped as \"Mother\", the Infinite\nEnergy. This will lead to purity, and tremendous energy will come here in\nAmerica. Here no temples weigh us down, no one suffers as they do in poorer\ncountries. Woman has suffered for aeons, and that has given her infinite\npatience and infinite perseverance. She holds on to an idea. It is this\nwhich makes her the support of even superstitious religions and of the\npriests in every land, and it is this that will free her. We have to become\nVedantists and live this grand thought; the masses must get it, and only in\nfree America can this be done. In India these ideas were brought out by\nindividuals like Buddha, Shankara, and others, but the masses did not retain\nthem. The new cycle must see the masses living Vedanta, and this will have\nto come through women.\n", "\"Keep the beloved beautiful Mother in the heart of your hearts with all\ncare.\"\n", "\"Throw out everything but the tongue, keep that to say, \"Mother, Mother!\"\n", "\"Let no evil counsellors enter; let you and me, my heart, alone see Mother.\"\n", "\"Thou art beyond all that lives!\"\n", "\"My Moon of life, my Soul of soul!\"\n", "SUNDAY AFTERNOON.\n", "Mind is an instrument in the hand of Atman, just as body is an instrument in\nthe hand of mind. Matter is motion outside, mind is motion inside. All\nchange begins and ends in time. If the Atman is unchangeable, It must be\nperfect; if perfect, It must be infinite; and if It be infinite, It must be\nonly One; there cannot be two infinites. So the Atman, the Self, can be only\nOne. Though It seems to be various, It is really only One. If a man were to\ngo toward the sun, at every step he would see a different sun, and yet it\nwould be the same sun after all.\n", "Asti, \"isness\", is the basis of all unity; and just as soon as the basis is\nfound, perfection ensues. If all colour could be resolved into one colour,\npainting  would  cease.  The  perfect  oneness  is  rest; we refer all\nmanifestations to one Being. Taoists, Confucianists, Buddhists, Hindus,\nJews, Mohammedans, Christians, and Zoroastrians, all preached the golden\nrule  and in almost the same words; but only the Hindus have given the\nrationale, because they saw the reason: Man must love others because those\nothers are himself. There is but One.\n", "Of all the great religious teachers the world has known, only Lao-tze,\nBuddha, and Jesus transcended the golden rule and said, \"Do good to your\nenemies\", \"Love them that hate you.\"\n", "Principles  exist; we do not create them, we only discover them. . . .\nReligion  consists  solely  in realisation. Doctrines are methods, not\nreligion.  All the different religions are but applications of the one\nreligion adapted to suit the requirements of different nations. Theories\nonly lead to fighting; thus the name of God that ought to bring peace has\nbeen the cause of half the bloodshed of the world. Go to the direct source.\nAsk God what He is. Unless He answers, He is not; but every religion teaches\nthat He does answer.\n", "Have something to say for yourself, else how can you have any idea of what\nothers have said? Do not cling to old superstitions; be ever ready for new\ntruths. \"Fools are they who would drink brackish water from a well that\ntheir forefathers have digged and would not drink pure water from a well\nthat others have digged.\" Until we realise God for ourselves, we can know\nnothing  about  Him.  Each man is perfect by his nature; prophets have\nmanifested this perfection, but it is potential in us. How can we understand\nthat Moses saw God unless we too see Him? If God ever came to anyone, He\nwill come to me. I will go to God direct; let Him talk to me. I cannot take\nbelief as a basis; that is atheism and blasphemy. If God spake to a man in\nthe deserts of Arabia two thousand years ago, He can also speak to me today,\nelse how can I know that He has not died? Come to God any way you can; only\ncome. But in coming do not push anyone down.\n", "The knowing ones must have pity on the ignorant. One who knows is willing to\ngive up his body even for an ant, because he knows that the body is nothing.\n"]

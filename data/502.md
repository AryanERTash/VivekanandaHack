["XLI\n", "(Translated from Bengali) \nSalutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "541 DEARBORN AVENUE, CHICAGO,\n19th March, 1894.\n", "MY DEAR SHASHI (RAMAKRISHNANANDA),\n", "I  have  not  written to you since coming to this country. But Haridas\nBhai's[1]  letter  gives  me all the news. It is excellent that G. C.\nGhosh[2] and all of you have treated him with due consideration.\n", "I have no wants in this country, but mendicancy has no vogue here, and I\nhave to labour, that is, lecture in places. It is as cold here as it is hot.\nThe summer is not a bit less hot than in Calcutta. And how to describe the\ncold in winter! The whole country is covered with snow, three or four feet\ndeep, nay, six or seven feet at places! In the southern parts there is no\nsnow. Snow, however, is a thing of little consideration here. For it snows\nwhen the mercury stands at 32\u00b0 F. In Calcutta it scarcely comes down to\n60,\u00b0 and it rarely approaches zero in England. But here, your mercury sinks\nto minus 4\u00b0 or 5\u00b0. In Canada, in the north, mercury becomes condensed, when\nthey have to use the alcohol thermometer. When it is too cold, that is, when\nthe mercury stands even below 20\u00b0F, it does not snow. I used to think that\nit must be an exceedingly cold day on which the snow falls. But it is not\nso, it snows on comparatively warm days. Extreme cold produces a sort of\nintoxication. No carriages would run; only the sledge, which is without\nwheels, slides on the ground! Everything is frozen stiff\u2014even an elephant\ncan walk on rivers and canals and lakes. The massive falls of Niagara, of\nsuch tremendous velocity, are frozen to marble!! But I am doing nicely. I\nwas a little afraid at first, but necessity makes me travel by rail to the\nborders of Canada one day, and the next day finds me lecturing in south\nU.S.A.! The carriages are kept quite warm, like your own room, by means of\nsteam pipes, and all around are masses of snow, spotlessly white. Oh, the\nbeauty of it!\n", "I was mortally afraid that my nose and ears would fall off, but to this day\nthey are all right. I have to go out, however, dressed in a heap of warm\nclothing surmounted by a fur-coat, with boots encased in a woollen jacket,\nand so on. No sooner do you breathe out than the breath freezes among the\nbeard and moustache! Notwithstanding all this, the fun of it is that they\nwon't drink water indoors without putting a lump of ice into it. This is\nbecause it is warm indoors. Every room and the staircase are kept warm by\nsteam pipes. They are first and foremost in art and appliances, foremost in\nenjoyment and luxury, foremost in making money, and foremost in spending it.\nThe daily wages of a coolie are six rupees, as also are those of a servant;\nyou cannot hire a cab for less than three rupees, nor get a cigar for less\nthan four annas. A decent pair of shoes costs twenty-four rupees, and a\nsuit, five hundred rupees. As they earn, so they spend. A lecture fetches\nfrom  two  hundred  up to three thousand rupees. I have got up to five\nhundred.[3] Of course now I am in the very heyday of fortune. They like me,\nand thousands of people come to hear me speak.\n", "As it pleased the Lord, I met here Mr. Mazoomdar. He was very cordial at\nfirst,  but  when the whole Chicago population began to flock to me in\noverwhelming numbers, then grew the canker in his mind! . . . The priests\ntried their utmost to snub me. But the Guru (Teacher) is with me, what could\nanybody do? And the whole American nation loves and respects me, pays my\nexpenses, and reveres me as a Guru. ... It was not in the power of your\npriests to do anything against me. Moreover, they are a nation of scholars.\nHere it would no longer do to say, \"We marry our widows\", \"We do not worship\nidols\", and things of that sort. What they want is philosophy, learning; and\nempty talk will no more do.\n", "Dharmapala is a fine boy. He has not much of learning but is very gentle. He\nhad a good deal of popularity in this country.\n", "Brother, I have been brought to my senses. . . . \u092f\u0947 \u0928\u093f\u0927\u094d\u0928\u0928\u094d\u0924\u093f \u0928\u093f\u0930\u0930\u094d\u0925\u0915\u0902 \u092a\u0930\u0939\u093f\u0924\u0902 \u0924\u0947 \u0915\u0947 \u0928 \u091c\u093e\u0928\u0940\u092e\u0939\u0947\u2014We\ndo not know what sort of people they are who for nothing hinder the welfare\nof others\" (Bhartrihari). Brother, we can get rid of everything, but not of\nthat cursed jealousy. . . . That is a national sin with us, speaking ill of\nothers, and burning at heart at the greatness of others. Mine alone is the\ngreatness, none else should rise to it!!\n", "Nowhere  in  the world are women like those of this country. How pure,\nindependent, self-relying, and kindhearted! It is the women who are the life\nand soul of this country. All learning and culture are centred in them. The\nsaying, \" \u092f\u093e \u0936\u094d\u0930\u0940: \u0938\u094d\u0935\u092f\u0902 \u0938\u0941\u0915\u0943\u0924\u093f\u0928\u093e\u0902 \u092d\u0935\u0928\u0947\u0937\u0941\u2014Who is the Goddess of Fortune Herself in\nthe families of the meritorious\" (Chandi)\u2014holds good in this country,\nwhile that other, \" \u0905\u0932\u0915\u094d\u0937\u094d\u092e\u0940\u0903 \u092a\u093e\u092a\u093e\u0924\u094d\u092e\u0928\u093e\u0902\u2014The Goddess of ill luck in the\nhomes of the sinful\" (ibid.)\u2014applies to ours. Just think on this. Great\nGod! I am struck dumb with wonderment at seeing the women of America. \"\u0924\u094d\u0935\u0902 \u0936\u094d\u0930\u0940\u0938\u094d\u0924\u094d\u0935\u092e\u0940\u0936\u094d\u0935\u0930\u0940 \u0924\u094d\u0935\u0902 \u0939\u094d\u0930\u0940\u0903 etc.\n\u2014 Thou art the Goddess of Fortune, Thou art the supreme Goddess, Thou art Modesty\" (ibid.),\"\n\"\u092f\u093e \u0926\u0947\u0935\u0940 \u0938\u0930\u094d\u0935\u092d\u0942\u0924\u0947\u0937\u0941 \u0936\u0915\u094d\u0924\u093f\u0930\u0942\u092a\u0947\u0923 \u0938\u0902\u0938\u094d\u0925\u093f\u0924\u093e\u2014The Goddess who\nresides in all beings as Power\" (ibid.)\u2014all this holds good here. There\nare thousands of women here whose minds are as pure and white as the snow of\nthis country. And look at our girls, becoming mothers below their teens!!\nGood Lord! I now see it all. Brother, \"\u092f\u0924\u094d\u0930 \u0928\u093e\u0930\u094d\u092f\u0938\u094d\u0924\u0941 \u092a\u0942\u091c\u094d\u092f\u0928\u094d\u0924\u0947 \u0930\u092e\u0928\u094d\u0924\u0947 \u0924\u0924\u094d\u0930 \u0926\u0947\u0935\u0924\u093e\u0903\u2014The gods are\npleased where the women are held in esteem\"\u2014says the old Manu. We are\nhorrible  sinners,  and  our  degradation  is due to our calling women\n\"despicable worms\", \"gateways to hell\", and so forth. Goodness gracious!\nThere is all the difference between heaven and hell!! \"\u092f\u093e\u0925\u093e\u0924\u0925\u094d\u092f\u0924\u094b\u093d\u0930\u094d\u0925\u093e\u0928\u094d \u092c\u094d\u092f\u0926\u0927\u093e\u0924\u094d\u2014He adjudges gifts according to the merits of the case\" (Isha, 8). Is the\nLord to be hoodwinked by idle talk? The Lord has said, \"\u0924\u094d\u0935\u0902 \u0938\u094d\u0924\u094d\u0930\u093f \u0924\u094d\u092c\u0902 \u092a\u0941\u092e\u093e\u0928\u0938\u093f \u0924\u094d\u0935\u0902 \u0915\u0941\u092e\u093e\u0930 \u0909\u0924 \u0935\u093e \u0915\u0941\u092e\u093e\u0930\u0940\u2014Thou art the woman, Thou art the man, Thou art the boy and the girl as\nwell.\"  (Shvet\u00e2shvatara  Upa.)  And  we  on  our  part  are  crying, \"\u0926\u0942\u0930\u092e\u092a\u0938\u0930 \u0930\u0947 \u091a\u0923\u094d\u0921\u093e\u0932\u2014Be off, thou outcast!\" \n\"\u0915\u0947\u0928\u0948\u0937\u093e \u0928\u093f\u0930\u094d\u092e\u093f\u0924\u093e \u0928\u093e\u0930\u0940 \u092e\u094b\u0939\u093f\u0928\u0940 etc.\u2014Who has made the bewitching woman?\" My brother, what experiences I have had\nin the South, of the upper classes torturing the lower! What Bacchanalian\norgies within the temples! Is it a religion that fails to remove the misery\nof the poor and turn men into gods! Do you think our religion is worth the\nname? Ours is only Don't touchism, only \"Touch me not\", \"Touch me not.\" Good\nheavens! A country, the big leaders of which have for the last two thousand\nyears been only discussing whether to take food with the right hand or the\nleft, whether to take water from the right-hand side or from the left, ...\nif   such  a  country  does  not  go  to  ruin,  what  other  will?  \"\u0915\u093e\u0932\u0903 \u0938\u0941\u092a\u094d\u0924\u0947\u0937\u0941 \u091c\u093e\u0917\u0930\u094d\u0924\u093f \u0915\u093e\u0932\u094b \u0939\u093f \u0926\u0941\u0930\u0924\u093f\u0915\u094d\u0930\u092e\u0903\u2014Time keeps wide awake when all else sleeps. Time is \ninvincible indeed!\" He knows it; who is there to throw dust in His eyes, my\nfriend?\n", "A country where millions of people live on flowers of the Mohu\u00e2 plant, and a\nmillion or two of Sadhus and a hundred million or so of Brahmins suck the\nblood out of these poor people, without even the least effort for their\namelioration\u2014is that a country or hell? Is that a religion, or the devil's\ndance? My brother, here is one thing for you to understand fully\u2014I have\ntravelled all over India, and seen this country too\u2014can there be an effect\nwithout cause? Can there be punishment without sin?\n", "\u0938\u0930\u094d\u0935\u0936\u093e\u0938\u094d\u0924\u094d\u0930\u092a\u0941\u0930\u093e\u0923\u0947\u0937\u0941  \u0935\u094d\u092f\u093e\u0938\u0938\u094d\u092f \u0935\u091a\u0928\u0902 \u0927\u094d\u0930\u0941\u0935\u092e\u094d \u0964\n\u092a\u0930\u094b\u092a\u0915\u093e\u0930\u0903 \u092a\u0941\u0923\u094d\u092f\u093e\u092f \u092a\u093e\u092a\u093e\u092f \u092a\u0930\u092a\u0940\u0921\u0928\u092e\u094d \u0965\n", "\u2014\"Amidst all the scriptures and Pur\u00e2nas, know this statement of Vy\u00e2sa to be\ntrue, that doing good to others conduces to merit, and doing harm to them\nleads to sin.\"\n", "Isn't it true?\n", "My brother, in view of all this, specially of the poverty and ignorance, I\nhad no sleep. At Cape Comorin sitting in Mother Kumari's temple, sitting on\nthe last bit of Indian rock\u2014I hit upon a plan: We are so many Sannyasins\nwandering about, and teaching the people metaphysics\u2014it is all madness.\nDid not our Gurudeva use to say, \"An empty stomach is no good for religion\"?\nThat those poor people are leading the life of brutes is simply due to\nignorance. We have for all ages been sucking their blood and trampling them\nunderfoot.\n", ". . . Suppose some disinterested Sannyasins, bent on doing good to others,\ngo from village to village, disseminating education and seeking in various\nways to better the condition of all down to the Chand\u00e2la, through oral\nteaching, and by means of maps, cameras, globes, and such other accessories\u2014can't that bring forth good in time? All these plans I cannot write out in\nthis short letter. The long and the short of it is\u2014if the mountain does\nnot come to Mohammed, Mohammed must go to the mountain. The poor are too\npoor to come to schools and P\u00e2thash\u00e2l\u00e2s, and they will gain nothing by\nreading poetry and all that sort of thing. We, as a nation, have lost our\nindividuality, and that is the cause of all mischief in India. We have to\ngive back to the nation its lost individuality and raise the masses. The\nHindu, the Mohammedan, the Christian, all have trampled them underfoot.\nAgain the force to raise them must come from inside, that is, from the\northodox Hindus. In every country the evils exist not with, but against,\nreligion. Religion therefore is not to blame, but men.\n", "To effect this, the first thing we need is men, and the next is funds.\nThrough the grace of our Guru I was sure to get from ten to fifteen men in\nevery town. I next travelled in search of funds, but do you think the people\nof India were going to spend money! . . . . Selfishness personified\u2014are\nthey to spend anything? Therefore I have come to America, to earn money\nmyself, and then return to my country and devote the rest of my days to the\nrealisation of this one aim of my life.\n", "As our country is poor in social virtues, so this country is lacking in\nspirituality. I give them spirituality, and they give me money. I do not\nknow  how long I shall take to realise my end. ...These people are not\nhypocrites, and jealousy is altogether absent in them. I depend on no one in\nHindusthan. I shall try to earn the wherewithal myself to the best of my\nmight and carry out my plans, or die in the attempt.\n\"\u0938\u0928\u094d\u0928\u093f\u092e\u093f\u0924\u094d\u0924\u0947 \u0935\u0930\u0902 \u0924\u094d\u092f\u093e\u0917\u094b \u0935\u093f\u0928\u093e\u0936\u0947 \u0928\u093f\u092f\u0924\u0947 \u0938\u0924\u093f\u2014When death is certain, it is best to sacrifice oneself for a good cause.\"\n", "You may perhaps think what Utopian nonsense all this is! You little know\nwhat is in me. If any of you help me in my plans, all right, or Gurudeva\nwill show me the way out. ... We cannot give up jealousy and rally together.\nThat is our national sin!! It is not to be met with in this country, and\nthis is what has made them so great.\n", "Nowhere in the world have I come across such \"frogs-in-the-well\" as we are.\nLet anything new come from some foreign country, and America will be the\nfirst to accept it. But we?\u2014oh, there are none like us in the world, we\nmen of Aryan blood!! Where that heredity really expresses itself, I do not\nsee. ...Yet they are descendants of the Aryans?\n", "Ever yours,\nVivekananda.\n"]

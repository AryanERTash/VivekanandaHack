["\n", "CXXIX\n", "To Maharaja Ajit Singh, the Raja of Khetri\n", "MATH BELOOR\n", "HOWRAH DISTRICT\n", "1 December 1898\n", "YOUR HIGHNESS \u2014\n", "Your telegram has pleased me beyond description, and it is worthy of your\nnoble self. I herewith give you the details of what I want.\n", "The lowest possible estimate of building a little home in Calcutta is at\nleast ten thousand rupees. With that it is barely possible to buy or build a\nhouse in some out-of-the-way quarter of the town \u2014 a little house fit for\nfour or five persons to live in.\n", "As  for the expenses of living, the 100 Rs. a month your generosity is\nsupplying my mother is enough for her. If another 100 Rs. a month be added\nto it for my lifetime for my expenses \u2014 which unfortunately this illness has\nincreased, and which, I hope, will not be for long a source of trouble to\nyou, as I expect only to live a few years at best \u2014 I will be perfectly\nhappy. One thing more will I beg of you \u2014 if possible, the 100 Rs. a month\nfor  my  mother  be made permanent, so that even after my death it may\nregularly reach her. Or even if your Highness ever gets reasons to stop your\nlove  and  kindness  for me, my poor old mother may be provided [for],\nremembering the love you once had for a poor S\u00e2dhu.\n", "This is all. Do this little work amongst the many other noble deeds you have\ndone, knowing well whatever else can be proved or not, the power of Karma is\nself-evident to all. The blessings of this good Karma shall always follow\nyou and yours. As for me, what shall I say \u2014 whatever I am in the world has\nbeen almost all through your help. You made it possible for me to get rid of\na terrible anxiety and face the world and do some work. It may be that you\nare destined by the Lord to be the instrument again of helping yet grander\nwork, by taking this load off my mind once more.\n", "But whether you do this or not, \"once loved is always loved\". Let all my\nlove and blessings and prayers follow you and yours, day and night, for what\nI owe you already; and may the Mother, whose play is this universe and in\nwhose hands we are mere instruments, always protect you from all evil.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

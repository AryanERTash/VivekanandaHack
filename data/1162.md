["\n", "CXVIII\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "THE MATH, BELOOR, HOWRAH DIST.,\n", "BENGAL, INDIA,\n", "11th March 1898.\n", "MY DEAR CHRISTINA,\n", "I simply wonder what has become of you. It is an age [that] I did not hear\nfrom you, and I expected so much after Sturdy's visit to Detroit. How did\nyou like the man? What about Baby and the Devendorfs? How is Mrs. Funkey\n[Funke]? What are you going to do this summer? Take rest, dear Christina; I\nam sure you require it badly.\n", "Mrs. Bull of Boston and Miss MacLeod of New York are now in India. We have\nchanged our Math from the old, nasty house to a house on the banks of the\nGanges. This is much more healthy and beautiful. We have also got a good\npiece of land very near on the same side where Mrs. Bull and Miss MacLeod\nare putting up now. It is wonderful how they accommodate themselves to our\nIndian life of privation and hardship! My, these Yanks can do anything!\nAfter the luxuries of Boston and New York, to be quite content and happy in\nthis wretched little house!! We intend to travel a bit together in Kashmir,\nand then I come to America with them and am sure to get a hearty welcome\nfrom my friends. What do you think? Is it welcome news to you? Of course, I\ncannot undergo the same amount of work as before; that, dear Christina, I am\nsorry, I will no more be able to do. I will do a little work and [take] a\ngood deal of rest. No more getting crowds and making noise, but quiet,\nsilent, personal work will be all I intend to do.\n", "This  time I will quietly come and quietly go away, seeing only my old\nfriends, and no noise.\n", "Write soon, as I am so anxious.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "\"There are two sorts of persons \u2014 one sort has the heart of water, the other\nof stone. The one easily takes an impression, and as easily throws it off;\nthe other seldom takes an impression, but once it takes, it is there for\never. Nay, the more they struggle to cast it off, the more it cuts deep into\nthe stone soul.\" \u2014 R. K. [Ramakrishna] Paramahamsa\n"]

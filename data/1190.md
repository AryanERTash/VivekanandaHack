["\n", "CXLVI\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "RIDGELY MANOR\n", "5 October 1899\n", "MY DEAR MOTHER CHURCH,\n", "Many, many thanks for your kind words.\n", "I am so glad you are working on as ever. I am glad because the wave of\noptimism has not caught you yet. It is all very well to say everything is\nright, but that is apt to degenerate into a sort of laissez-faire. I believe\nwith you that the world is evil \u2014 made more hideous with a few dashes of\ngood.\n", "All our works have only this value, that they awaken some to the reality of\nthis horror \u2014 and [those] flee for refuge to some place beyond, which is\ncalled God, or Christ, or Brahma, or Buddha, etc. Names do not make much\ndifference.\n", "Again,  we must always remember ours is only to work \u2014 we never attain\nresults. How can we? Good can never be done without doing evil. We cannot\nbreathe a breath without killing thousands of poor little animals. National\nprosperity is another name for death and degradation to millions of other\nraces. So is individual prosperity the beggaring of many. The world is evil\n\u2014 and will ever remain so. It is its nature, and cannot be changed \u2014 \"Which\none of you by taking thought . . .\" etc. (Matthew 6.27.)\n", "Such is truth. The wisdom is therefore in renunciation, that is, to make the\nLord our all in all. Be a true Christian, Mother \u2014 like Christ, renounce\neverything and let the heart and soul and body belong to Him and Him alone.\nAll this nonsense which people have built round Christ's name is not His\nteaching. He taught to renounce. He never says the earth is an enjoyable\nplace. And your time has come to get rid of all vanities \u2014 even the love of\nchildren and husband \u2014 and think of the Lord and Him alone.\n", "Ever your Son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

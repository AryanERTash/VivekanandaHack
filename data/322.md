["\n", "LXXXVII\n", "\n", "\nYour Highness, (Maharaja Of Khetri.)\n", "\nVery glad to learn that you and the Kumar are enjoying good health As for\nme, my heart has become very weak. Change, I do not think, will do me any\ngood, as for the last 14 years I do not remember to have stopped at one\nplace for 3 months at a stretch. On the other hand if by some chance I can\nlive for months in one place, I hope it will do me good. I do not mind this.\nHowever, I feel that my work in this life is done. Through good and evil,\npain and pleasure, my life-boat has been dragged on. The one great lesson I\nwas taught is that life is misery, nothing but misery. Mother knows what is\nbest. Each one of us is in the hands of Karma; it works itself out \u2014 and no\nnay. There is only one element in life which is worth having at any cost,\nand it is love. Love immense and infinite, broad as the sky and deep as the\nocean \u2014 this is the one great gain in life. Blessed is he who gets it.\n", "\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

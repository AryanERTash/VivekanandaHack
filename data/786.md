["\n", "XLI\n", "ALMORA,\n", "23rd July, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR MISS NOBLE,\n", "Excuse these few lines. I shall write more fully as soon as I reach some\nplace. I am on my way from the hills to the plains.\n", "I do not understand what you mean by frankness without familiarity \u2014 I for\none will give anything to get rid of the last lingering bit of Oriental\nformality in me and speak out like a child of nature. Oh, to live even for a\nday in the full light of freedom, to breathe the free air of simplicity! Is\nnot that the highest purity?\n", "In this world we work through fear of others, we talk through fear, we think\nthrough fear, alas! we are born in a land of enemies. Who is there who has\nbeen able to get rid of this feeling of fear, as if everyone is a spy set\nspecially to watch him? And woe unto the man who pushes himself forward!\nWill it ever be a land of friends? Who knows? We can only try.\n", "The work has already begun and at present famine-relief is the thing next to\nhand. Several centres have been opened and the work goes on; famine-relief,\npreaching,  and  a  little  teaching. As yet of course it is very very\ninsignificant, the boys in training are being taken out as opportunity is\noffering itself. The sphere of action at present is Madras and Calcutta. Mr.\nGoodwin working in Madras. Also one has gone to Colombo. From the next week\na monthly report of the whole work will be forwarded to you if it has not\nalready reached you. I am away from the centre of work, so things go a\nlittle slow, you see; but the work is satisfactory on the whole.\n", "You can do more work for us from England than by coming here. Lord bless you\nfor your great self-sacrifice for the poor Indians.\n", "I entirely agree with you that the work in England will look up when I am\nthere. But all the same it is not proper to leave India before the machine\nis moving at some rate and I am sure that there are many to guide it in my\nabsence. That will be done in a few months. \"God willing\", as the Mussulmans\nsay. One of my best workers is now in England, the Raja of Khetri. I expect\nhim soon in India, and he will be of great service to me no doubt.\n", "With everlasting love and blessings,\n", "Yours,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

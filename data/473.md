["\n", "XII[6]*\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "BARANAGORE, CALCUTTA,\n", "13th Dec., 1889.\n", "DEAR SIR,\n", "\nI have all particulars from your letter; and from Rakhal's which followed, I\ncame to know of your meeting. I have received the pamphlet written by you. A\nkind of scientific Advaitism has been spreading in Europe ever since the\ntheory  of  the conservation of energy was discovered, but all that is\nParin\u00e2mav\u00e2da, evolution by real modification. It is good you have shown the\ndifference   between  this  and  Shankara's  Vivartav\u00e2da  (progressive\nmanifestation by unreal superimposition). I can't appreciate your citing\nSpencer's parody on the German transcendentalists; he himself is fed much on\ntheir doles. It is doubtful whether your opponent Gough understands his\nHegel sufficiently. Anyway, your rejoinder is very pointed and thrashing.\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

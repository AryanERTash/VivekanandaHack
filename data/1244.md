["\n", "CC\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "THE MATH, BELUR\n", "DIST. HOWRAH, BENGAL, INDIA,\n", "[End of June 1901]\n", "DEAR CHRISTINA,\n", "Your very welcome letter just reached. A few days ago a precious little bit\nof poem also reached. I wish it ever so much you were the writer thereof.\nAnyhow, most of us feel, though unable to express; and then, \"There are\nthoughts that lie too deep for tears\". Regularity in anything is not in my\nline of life, but that need not make you irregular. I pray you to drop a few\nlines every now and then. Of course, when I am not in this body, I am sure\nthe news will reach you, and then you will have to stop writing.\n", "Miss  MacLeod  wishes me to join her in Japan, but I am not sure. Most\nprobably I am not going, especially as I expect both her and Mrs. Ole Bull\nin India, in November. Two whole months consumed in coming and going; only\none month's stay in Japan \u2014 that does not pay, I am afraid.\n", "Say, I am getting enormously fat about the middle \u2014 alas!\n", "Mrs. [Charlotte] Sevier, who is now in England, returns in a few months to\nIndia. She has invited Mrs. Bull etc. to be her guests in the Himalayas. I\nwish they could be there during summer.\n", "I have manfully borne the terrific heat of my country in the plains, and now\nI am facing the deluging rains of my country. Do you know how I am taking\nrest? I have got a few goats and sheep and cows and dogs and cranes! And I\nam taking care of them the whole day! It is not trying to be happy; what\nfor? Why should one not be unhappy as well \u2014 both being nonsense? \u2014 but just\nto kill time.\n", "Do you correspond with Mrs. Bull or Nivedita?\n", "Don't worry, don't be anxious; for me the \"Mother\" is my protection and\nrefuge; and everything must come round soon, better than my fondest dreams\ncan paint.\n", "With all love,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "SATURDAY, August 3, 1895.\n", "Individuals who are to get freedom in this life have to live thousands of\nyears in one lifetime. They have to be ahead of their times, but the masses\ncan only crawl. Thus we have Christs and Buddhas. . . .\n", "There was once a Hindu queen, who so much desired that all her children\nshould attain freedom in this life that she herself took all the care of\nthem; and as she rocked them to sleep, she sang always the one song to them\n\u2014 \"Tat tvam asi, Tat tvam asi\" (\"That thou art, That thou art\").\n", "Three of them became Sannyasins, but the fourth was taken away to be brought\nup elsewhere to become a king. As he was leaving home, the mother gave him a\npiece of paper which he was to read when he grew to manhood. On that piece\nof paper was written, \"God alone is true. All else is false. The soul never\nkills or is killed. Live alone or in the company of holy ones.\" When the\nyoung prince read this, he too at once renounced the world and became a\nSannyasin.\n", "Give up, renounce the world. Now we are like dogs strayed into a kitchen and\neating a piece of meat, looking round in fear lest at any moment some one\nmay come and drive them out. Instead of that, be a king and know you own the\nworld. This never comes until you give it up and it ceases to bind. Give up\nmentally, if you do not physically. Give up from the heart of your hearts.\nHave Vair\u00e2gya (renunciation). This is the real sacrifice, and without it, it\nis impossible to attain spirituality. Do not desire, for what you desire you\nget, and with it comes terrible bondage. It is nothing but bringing \"noses\non us,\"[6]* as in the case of the man who had three boons to ask. We never\nget freedom until we are self-contained. \"Self is the Saviour of self, none\nelse.\"\n", "Learn to feel yourself in other bodies, to know that we are all one. Throw\nall other nonsense to the winds. Spit out your actions, good or bad, and\nnever think of them again. What is done is done. Throw off superstition.\nHave no weakness even in the face of death. Do not repent, do not brood over\npast deeds, and do not remember your good deeds; be \u00e2z\u00e2d  (free). The weak,\nthe fearful, the ignorant will never reach Atman. You cannot undo, the\neffect must come, face it, but be careful never to do the same thing again.\nGive up the burden of all deeds to the Lord; give all, both good and bad. Do\nnot keep the good and give only the bad. God helps those who do not help\nthemselves.\n", "\"Drinking the cup of desire, the world becomes mad.\" Day and night never\ncome together, so desire and the Lord can never come together. Give up\ndesire.\n", "*            *            *\n", "There  is a vast difference between saying \"food, food\" and eating it,\nbetween saying \"water, water\" and drinking it. So by merely repeating the\nwords \"God, God\" we cannot hope to attain realisation. We must strive and\npractise.\n", "Only by the wave falling back into the sea can it become unlimited, never as\na wave can it be so. Then after it has become the sea, it can become the\nwave again and as big a one as it pleases. Break the identification of\nyourself with the current and know that you are free.\n", "True philosophy is the systematising of certain perceptions. Intellect ends\nwhere religion begins. Inspiration is much higher than reason, but it must\nnot contradict it. Reason is the rough tool to do the hard work; inspiration\nis the bright light which shows us all truth. The will to do a thing is not\nnecessarily inspiration. . . .\n", "Progression in Maya is a circle that brings you back to the starting point;\nbut you start ignorant and come to the end with all knowledge. Worship of\nGod, worship of the holy ones, concentration and meditation, and unselfish\nwork, these are the ways of breaking away from Maya's net; but we must first\nhave the strong desire to get free. The flash of light that will illuminate\nthe darkness for us is in us; it is the knowledge that is our nature \u2014 there\nis no \"birthright\", we were never born. All that we have to do is to drive\naway the clouds that cover it.\n", "Give up all desire for enjoyment in earth or heaven. Control the organs of\nthe senses and control the mind. Bear every misery without even knowing that\nyou are miserable. Think of nothing but liberation. Have faith in Guru, in\nhis teachings, and in the surety that you can get free. Say \"Soham, Soham\"\nwhatever comes. Tell yourself this even in eating, walking, suffering; tell\nthe mind this incessantly \u2014 that what we see never existed, that there is\nonly \"I\". Flash \u2014 the dream will break! Think day and night, this universe\nis zero, only God is. Have intense desire to get free.\n", "All relatives and friends are but \"old dry wells\"; we fall into them and get\ndreams of duty and bondage, and there is no end. Do not create illusion by\nhelping anyone. It is like a banyan tree, that spreads on and on. If you are\na dualist, you are a fool to try to help God. If you are a monist, you know\nthat you are God; where find duty? You have no duty to husband, child,\nfriend. Take things as they come, lie still, and when your body floats, go;\nrise with the rising tide, fall with falling tide. Let the body die; this\nidea of body is but a worn-out fable. \"Be still and know that you are God.\"\n", "The present only is existent. There is no past or future even in thought,\nbecause to think it, you have to make it the present. Give up everything,\nand let it float where it will. This world is all a delusion, do not let it\nfool you again. You have known it for what it is not, now know it for what\nit is. If the body is dragged anywhere, let it go; do not care where the\nbody is. This tyrannical idea of duty is a terrible poison and is destroying\nthe world.\n", "Do not wait to have a harp and rest by degrees; why not take a harp and\nbegin  here?  Why wait for heaven? Make it here. In heaven there is no\nmarrying or giving in marriage; why not begin at once and have none here?\nThe  yellow robe of the Sannyasin is the sign of the free. Give up the\nbeggar's dress of the world; wear the flag of freedom, the ochre robe.\n"]

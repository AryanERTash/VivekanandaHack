["\n", "CV\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "DARJEELING,\n", "[RETURN ADDRESS: ALAMBAZAR MATH, CALCUTTA]\n", "16th March 1897.\n", "DEAR CHRISTINA,\n", "Many, many thanks for the photograph and the poem. I never saw anything half\nas beautiful. The work I had to do to reach Calcutta from Ceylon was so\nimmense that I could not earlier acknowledge your precious gift. The work\nhas broken me down completely, and I have got \"diabetes\", an incurable\ndisease, which must carry me off \u2014 at least in a few years.\n", "I  am  now writing to you from Darjeeling, the nearest hill station to\nCalcutta, with a climate as cool as London. It has revived me a bit. If I\nlive, I will come to America next year or so.\n", "How are things going on with you all? How are Mrs. Funkey [Funke] and Mrs.\nPhelps?\n", "Are you laying by a few dollars whenever you can? That is very important.\n", "I am in a hurry for the mail. You will be glad to know that the Indian\npeople have, as it were, risen in a mass to honour me. I am the idol of the\nday. Mr. Goodwin is going to publish in book form all the addresses given to\nme and the speeches in reply. The demonstrations all over have been simply\nunique.\n", "Yours with all love,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

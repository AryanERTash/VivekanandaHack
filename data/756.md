## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "XI\n", "CHICAGO,\n", "10th October, 1893.\n", "DEAR MRS. TANNATT WOODS,\n", "\nI received your letter yesterday. Just now I am lecturing about Chicago \u2014\nand am doing as I think very well; it is ranging from 30 to 80 dollars a\nlecture, and just now I have been so well advertised in Chicago gratis by\nthe Parliament of Religions that it is not advisable to give up this field\nnow. To which I am sure you will agree. However I may come soon to Boston,\nbut when I cannot say. Yesterday I returned from Streator where I got 87\ndollars for a lecture. I have engagements every day this week. And hope more\nwill come by the end of the week. My love to Mr. Woods and compliments to\nall our friends.\n", "Yours truly,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

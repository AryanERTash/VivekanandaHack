["\n", "IX\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "(From the Diary of a Disciple)\n", "(The disciple is Sharatchandra Chakravarty, who published his records in a\nBengali book, Swami-Shishya-Samv\u00e2da, in two parts. The present series of\n\"Conversations and Dialogues\" is a revised translation from this book. Five\ndialogues of this series have already appeared in the Complete Works,Volume 5)\n", "[Place: Calcutta. year: 1897.]\n", "For the last ten days, the disciple had been studying S\u00e2yana's commentary on\nthe Rig-Veda with Swamiji, who was staying then at the house of the late\nBabu Balaram Bose at Baghbazar. Max M\u00fcller's volumes on the Rig-Veda had\nbeen brought from a wealthy friend's private library. Swamiji was correcting\nthe disciple every now and then and giving him the true pronunciation or\nconstruction as necessary. Sometimes while explaining the arguments of\nSayana to establish the eternity of the Vedas, Swamiji was praising very\nhighly the commentator's wonderful ingenuity; sometimes again while arguing\nout  the deeper significance of the doctrine, he was putting forward a\ndifference in view and indulging in an innocent squib at Sayana.\n", "While our study had proceeded thus for a while, Swamiji raised the topic\nabout Max M\u00fcller and continued thus: Well, do you know, my impression is\nthat  it  is  Sayana who is born again as Max M\u00fcller to revive his own\ncommentary  on  the  Vedas? I have had this notion for long. It became\nconfirmed in my mind, it seems, after I had seen Max M\u00fcller. Even here in\nthis  country,  you don't find a scholar so persevering, and so firmly\ngrounded in the Vedas and the Vedanta. Over and above this, what a deep,\nunfathomable respect for Sri Ramakrishna! Do you know, he believes in his\nDivine Incarnation! And what great hospitality towards me when I was his\nguest! Seeing the old man and his lady, it seemed to me that they were\nliving their home-life like another Vasishtha and Arundhati! At the time of\nparting with me, tears came into the eyes of the old man.\n", "Disciple: But, sir, if Sayana himself became Max M\u00fcller, then why was he\nborn as a Mlechchha instead of being born in the sacred land of India?\n", "Swamiji: The feeling and the distinction that I am an Aryan and the other is\na  Mlechchha  come  from ignorance. But what are Varn\u00e2shrama and caste\ndivisions to one who is the commentator of the Vedas, the shining embodiment\nof knowledge? To him they are wholly meaningless, and he can assume human\nbirth wherever he likes for doing good to mankind. Specially, if he did not\nchoose to be born in a land which excelled both in learning and wealth,\nwhere would he secure the large expenses for publishing such stupendous\nvolumes? Didn't you hear that the East India Company paid nine lakhs of\nrupees in cash to have the Rig-Veda published? Even this money was not\nenough. Hundreds of Vedic Pundits had to be employed in this country on\nmonthly stipends. Has anybody seen in this age, here in this country, such\nprofound yearning for knowledge, such prodigious investment of money for the\nsake  of  light and learning? Max M\u00fcller himself has written it in his\npreface, that for twenty-five years he prepared only the manuscripts. Then\nthe printing took another twenty years! It is not possible for an ordinary\nman to drudge for fortyfive years of his life with one publication. Just\nthink of it! Is it an idle fancy of mine to say he is Sayana himself?\n", "After this talk about Max M\u00fcller the leading of the Vedas was resumed. Now\nSwamiji  began  variously  to support the view of Sayana that creation\nproceeded out of the Vedas. He said: Veda means the sum total of eternal\ntruths; the Vedic Rishis experienced those truths; they can be experienced\nonly by seers of the supersensuous and not by common men like us. That is\nwhy  in  the  Vedas the term Rishi means \"the seer of the truth of the\nMantras\", and not any Brahmin with the holy thread hanging down the neck.\nThe division of society into castes came about later on. Veda is of the\nnature of Shabda or of idea. It is but the sum total of ideas. Shabda,\naccording to the old Vedic meaning of the term, is the subtle idea, which\nreveals  itself  by  taking  the  gross form later on. So owing to the\ndissolution of the creation the subtle seeds of the future creation become\ninvolved in the Veda. Accordingly, in the Puranas you find that during the\nfirst Divine Incarnation, the Min\u00e2vat\u00e2ra, the Veda is first made manifest.\nThe Vedas having been first revealed in this Incarnation, the other creative\nmanifestations followed. Or in other words, all the created objects began to\ntake concrete shape out of the Shabdas or ideas in the Veda. For in Shabda\nor idea, all gross objects have their subtle forms. Creation had proceeded\nin the same way in all previous cycles or Kalpas. This you find in the\nSandhy\u00e2 Mantra of the Vedas:\n\" \u0938\u0942\u0930\u094d\u092f\u093e\u091a\u0928\u094d\u0926\u094d\u0930\u092e\u0938\u094c \u0927\u093e\u0924\u093e \u092f\u0925\u093e\u092a\u0942\u0930\u094d\u0935\u092e\u0915\u0932\u094d\u092a\u092f\u0924\u094d \u092a\u0943\u0925\u093f\u0935\u0940\u0902 \u0926\u093f\u0935\u0902 \u091a\u093e\u0928\u094d\u0924\u0930\u0940\u0915\u094d\u0937\u092e\u0925\u094b \u0938\u094d\u0935\u0903\n\u2014 The Creator projected the sun, the moon, the earth, the atmosphere, the\nheaven, and the upper spheres in the same manner and process as in previous\ncycles.\" Do you understand?\n", "Disciple: But, sir, how in the absence of an actual concrete object can the\nShabda or idea be applied and for what? And how can the names too be given\nat all?\n", "Swamiji: Yes. that is what on first thought seems to be the difficulty. But\njust think of this. Supposing this jug breaks into pieces; does the idea of\na jug become null and void? No. Because, the jug is the gross effect, while\nthe idea, \"jug\", is the subtle state or the Shabda-state of the jug. In the\nsame way, the Shabda-state of every object is its subtle state, and the\nthings  we  see,  hear, touch, or perceive in any manner are the gross\nmanifestations of entities in the subtle or Shabda-state. Just as we may\nspeak  of  the  effect  and its cause. Even when the whole creation is\nannihilated, the Shabda, as the consciousness of the universe or the subtle\nreality of all concrete things, exists in Brahman as the cause. At the point\nof creative manifestation, this sum total of causal entities vibrates into\nactivity, as it were, and as being the sonant, material substance of it all,\nthe eternal, primal sound of \"Om\" continues to come out of itself. And then\nfrom the causal totality comes out first the subtle image or Shabda-form of\neach particular thing and then its gross manifestation. Now that causal\nShabda, or word-consciousness, is Brahman, and it is the Veda. This is the\npurport of Sayana. Do you now understand?\n", "Disciple: No, sir, I can't clearly comprehend it.\n", "Swamiji: Well, you understand, I suppose, that even if all the jugs in the\nuniverse were to be destroyed, the idea or Shabda, \"jug\", would still exist.\nSo if the universe be destroyed \u2014 I mean if all the things making up the\nuniverse  be  smashed  to  atoms \u2014 why should not the ideas or Shabdas\nrepresenting all of them in consciousness, be still existing; And why cannot\na second creation be supposed to come out of them in time?\n", "Disciple: But, sir, if one cries out \"jug\", \"jug\", that does not cause any\njug to be produced!\n", "Swamiji: No, nothing is produced if you or I cry out like that; but a jug\nmust be revealed if the idea of it rises in Brahman which is perfect in Its\ncreative determinations. When we see even those established in the practice\nof religion (S\u00e2dhakas) bring about by will-power things otherwise impossible\nto happen, what to speak of Brahman with perfect creativeness of will? At\nthe point of creation Brahman becomes manifest as Shabda (Idea), and then\nassumes  the form of \"N\u00e2da\" or \"Om\". At the next stage, the particular\nShabdas or ideas, that variously existed in former cycles, such as Bhuh,\nBhuvah, Svah, cow, man, etc., begin to come out of the \"Om\". As soon as\nthese ideas appear in Brahman endowed with perfect will, the corresponding\nconcrete things also appear, and gradually the diversified universe becomes\nmanifest. Do you now understand how Shabda is the source of creation?\n", "Disciple:  Yes,  I  just  form  some idea of it, but there is no clear\ncomprehension in the mind.\n", "Swamiji: Well, clear comprehension, inward realisation, is no small matter,\nmy son. In the process of entering into Samadhi, first the universe appears\nas one mass of ideas; then the whole thing loses itself in a profound \"Om\".\nThen  even  that  melts  away, even that seems to be between being and\nnon-being.

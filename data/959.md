## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "CXII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "MURREE,\n", "12th October, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I wrote at length in yesterday's letter. I think it desirable to give you\nspecial directions about certain matters. . . . (1) To all those who collect\nmoney and send it to the Math . . . the acknowledgment of the amounts will\nbe issued from the Math. (2) The acknowledgment must be in duplicate, one\nfor the sender, and one for filing in the Math. (3) There must be a big\nregister in which all the names and addresses of the donors will be entered.\n(4) Accounts, accurate to the last pie, must be kept of the amounts that are\ndonated to the Math Fund, and fully accurate accounts should be obtained\nfrom  Sarada  and  others to whom money is given. For lack of accurate\naccount-keeping . . . see that I am not accused as a cheat. These accounts\nshould afterwards be published. (5) Immediately go and register a will under\nlawyer's advice to the effect that in case you and I die then Hari and\nSharat will succeed to all that there is in our Math.\n", "I have not yet got any news from Ambala, whether Hariprasanna and others\nhave reached there or not. Give the other half-sheet of this letter to\nMaster Mahashay.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

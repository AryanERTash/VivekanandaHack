["\n", "III[6]*\n", "(Translated from [7]Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "THE BARANAGORE MATH,\n", "19th Nov., 1888.\n", "RESPECTED SIR,\n", "I have received the two books sent by you and am filled with joy to read\nyour wonderfully affectionate letter which betokens your broad, generous\nheart. No doubt, it is due to good merit of my previous births that you\nshow, sir, so much kindness to a mendicant like me who lives on begging. By\nsending your gift of the \"Vedanta\", you have laid under lifelong obligation\nnot only myself but the whole group of Shri Ramakrishna's Sannyasins. They\nall bow down to you in respect. It is not for my own sake alone that I asked\nof you the copy of P\u00e2nini's grammar; a good deal of study, in fact, is given\nto Sanskrit scriptures in this Math. The Vedas may well be said to have\nfallen quite out of vogue in Bengal. Many here in this Math are conversant\nwith Sanskrit, and they have a mind to master the Samhit\u00e2 portions of the\nVedas.  They are of opinion that what has to be done must be done to a\nfinish.  So, believing that a full measure of proficiency in the Vedic\nlanguage is impossible without first mastering Panini's grammar, which is\nthe best available for the purpose, a copy of the latter was felt to be a\nnecessity.  The  grammatical work Mugdhabodha, which we studied in our\nboyhood, is superior in many respects to Laghukaumudi. You are yourself,\nhowever, a deeply learned man and, therefore, the best judge we can have in\nthis matter. So if you consider the Asht\u00e2dhy\u00e2yi (Panini's) to be the most\nsuitable in our case, you will lay us under a debt of lifelong gratitude by\nsending the same (provided you feel it convenient and feel so inclined).\nThis Math is not wanting in men of perseverance, talent, and penetrative\nintellect. I may hope that by the grace of our Master, they will acquire in\na short time Panini's system and then succeed in restoring the Vedas to\nBengal. I beg to send you two photographs of my revered Master and two parts\nof  some  of  his teachings as given in his homely style compiled, and\npublished by a certain gentleman \u2014 hoping you will give us the pleasure of\nyour acceptance. My health is now much improved, and I expect the blessings\nof meeting you within two or three months. . . .\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

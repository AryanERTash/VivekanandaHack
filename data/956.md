## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "CIX\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "SRINAGAR, KASHMIR,\n", "30th September, 1897\n", "DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "I understand from a letter of Gopal Dada that you have seen that piece of\nland at Konnagar. It seems that that site is rent-free and measures 16\nbighas (about 5 acres), and that the price is below eight or ten thousand\nrupees. Do what you think best after considering the healthiness and other\nfactors. In a day or two I shall leave for the Punjab. So don't write any\nmore  letters  to me at this address. I shall telegraph to you my next\naddress. Don't forget to send Hariprasanna. Tell Gopal Dada thus: \"Your\nhealth will soon be all right \u2014 winter is coming, what fear? Eat well and be\nmerry.\" Write a letter to Mrs. C. Sevier at Spring Dale, Murree, as to\nYogen's present state of health, marking on the cover \"to await arrival\".\nGive my love and blessings to all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. The Maharaja of Khetri reaches Bombay on the 10th October. Don't forget\nto give him an address of welcome.\n", "V.\n"]

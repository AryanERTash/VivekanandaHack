["\n", "CIV\n", "To Swami Shuddhananda\n", "C/O RISHIBAR MUKHOPADHYAYA,\n", "CHIEF JUSTICE,\n", "SRINAGAR, KASHMIR,\n", "15th September, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR SHUDDHANANDA,\n", "We are in Kashmir at last. I need not tell you of all the beauties of the\nplace. It is the one land fit for Yogis, to my mind. But the land is now\ninhabited  by  a  race who though possessing great physical beauty are\nextremely dirty. I am going to travel by water for a month seeing the sights\nand getting strong. But the city is very malarious just now, and Sadananda\nand Kristolal have got fever. Sadananda is all right today, but Kristolal\nhas fever yet. The doctor came today and gave him a purgative. He will be\nall right by tomorrow, we hope; and we start also tomorrow. The State has\nlent me one of its barges, and it is fine and quite comfortable. They have\nalso sent orders to the Tahsildars of different districts. The people here\nare crowding in banks to see us and are doing everything they can to make us\ncomfortable.\n", "A clipping from The Indian Mirror, quoting passages from an article written\nby Dr. Barrows in an American paper, has been sent over to me by somebody\nwithout a name and asking me what reply to give. I send back the cutting to\nBrahmananda with my answer to the passages which are damned lies!\n", "I am glad to learn you are doing well there and going on with your usual\nwork. I also had a letter from Shivananda giving the details of work there.\n", "After a month I go back to the Punjab, and I will expect three of you at\nAmbala. In case a centre is founded, one of you will be left in charge.\nNiranjan, Latu, and Kristolal will be sent back.\n", "I intend to make a rapid march through the Punjab and Sind via Kathiawar and\nBaroda, back to Rajputana, and thence to Nepal and last Calcutta.\n", "Write to me C/o Rishibar Babu at Srinagar. I will get the letter on my way\nback.\n", "With love to all and blessings,\n", "Yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "CCI\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "THE MATH, BELUR,\n", "DIST. HOWRAH, BENGAL,\n", "6th August 1901.\n", "Letters are sometimes, dear Christina, like mercy \u2014 good to the one that\nsends and the other that receives.\n", "I am so happy that you are calm and resigned as ever. You are ever that.\n\"Mother knows\", indeed; only I know that \"Mother\" not only knows, but does\n\u2014 and is going to do something very fine for me in the near future. What do\nyou think will be very good for me on earth? Silver? Gold? Pooh! I have got\nsomething infinitely better; but a little gold will not be amiss to keep my\njewel in proper surroundings, and it is coming, don't you think so?\n", "I am a man who frets much, but waits all the same; and the apple comes to my\nmouth by itself. So, it is coming, coming, coming.\n", "Now, how are you? Growing ever thinner, thinner, thinner, eh? Do have a very\ngood appetite and good sleep in anticipation of the coming good time \u2014 to be\nin trim for welcoming its advent.\n", "How did the heat feel this year? We read all sorts of horrible stories about\nAmerican heat waves. You have beaten the world's records, even in heat \u2014\nthat's Yankee push, surely.\n", "Well, you are right as about taste: I renounce the yellow of gold and the\nwhite of silver, but stick to amber always \u2014 that is to my taste.\n", "Amber and corals I always hated; but of late I am awakening to their beauty.\nOne learns as he lives, is it not?\n", "I am going to Darjeeling tomorrow for a few days and will write to you from\nthere. Now gute Nacht [good night] and au revoir [good-bye] for the present.\n", "Ever yours truly,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

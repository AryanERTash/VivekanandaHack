Swamiji  commenced singing the famous Nativity Hymn on Shri\nRamakrishna \u2014 \"Who art Thou laid on the lap of a poor Brahmin mother\", etc.,\nand headed a procession, himself playing on the Khol. All the devotees assembled there\nfollowed, joining in the; chorus.\n", "Shortly after the procession reached its destination, Swamiji went upstairs\nto see the chapel. The chapel was floored with marble. In the centre was the\nthrone  and  upon  it was the porcelain image of Shri Ramakrishna. The\narrangement of materials was perfect and Swamiji was much pleased to see\nthis.\n", "The wife of Navagopal Babu prostrated herself before Swamiji with the other\nfemale members of the house and then took to fanning him. Hearing Swamiji\nspeaking highly of every arrangement, she addressed him and said, \"What have\nwe got to entitle us to the privilege of worshipping Th\u00e2kur (the Master,\nLord)? \u2014 A poor home and poor means! Do bless us please by installing him\nhere out of your own kindness!\n", "In reply to this, Swamiji jocosely said, \"Your Thakur never had in his\nfourteen generations such a marble floored house to live in! He had his\nbirth in that rural thatched cottage and lived his days on indifferent\nmeans. And if he does not live here so excellently served, where else should\nhe live?\" Swamiji's words made everybody laugh out.\n", "Now, with his body rubbed with ashes and gracing the seat of the priest,\nSwamiji himself conducted the worship, with Swami Prakashananda to assist\nhim. After the worship was over, Swamiji while still in the worship-room\ncomposed  extempore  this  Mantra for prostration before Bhagavan Shri\nRamakrishna:\n", "[xi_sharat_chakravarty_01.jpg]\n", "\u2014 \"I bow down to Ramakrishna, who established the religion, embodying in\nhimself the reality of all religions and being thus the foremost of divine\nIncarnations.\"\n", "All prostrated before Shri Ramakrishna with this Mantra. In the evening\nSwamiji returned to Baghbazar.\n"]

## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "XXXI\n", "U.S.A.\n", "March, 1896.\n", "DEAR ALASINGA,\n", "Last week I wrote you about the Brahmav\u00e2din. I forgot to write about the\nBhakti lectures. They ought to be published in a book all together. A few\nhundreds may be sent to America to Goodyear in New York. Within twenty days\nI sail for England. I have other big books on Karma, Jnana, and Raja Yogas\n\u2014 the Karma is out already, the Raja will be a very big book and is already\nin the Press. The Jnana will have to be published, I think, in England.\n", "A  letter  you published from Kripananda in the Brahmavadin was rather\nunfortunate. Kripananda is smarting under the blows the Christians have\ngiven him and that sort of letter is vulgar, pitching into everybody. It is\nnot in accord with the tone of the Brahmavadin. So in future when Kripananda\nwrites, tone down everything that is an attack upon any sect, however cranky\nor crude. Nothing which is against any sect, good or bad, should get into\nthe Brahmavadin. Of course, we must not show active sympathy with frauds.\nAgain  let  me  remind you that the paper is too technical to find any\nsubscriber here. The average Western neither knows nor cares to know all\nabout jaw-breaking Sanskrit terms and technicalities. The paper is well\nfitted for India \u2014 that I see. Every word of special pleading should be\neliminated from the Editorials, and you must always remember that you are\naddressing the whole world, not India alone, and that the same world is\nentirely ignorant of what you have got to tell them. Use the translation of\nevery Sanskrit term carefully and make things as easy as possible.\n", "Before  this reaches you I will be in England. So address me c/o E. T.\nSturdy, Esq., High View, Caversham, Eng.\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "XXXIX\n", "ALAMBAZAR MATH, CALCUTTA,\n", "May 5th, 1897.\n", "DEAR MRS. BULL,\n", "I have been to Darjeeling for a month to recuperate my shattered health. I\nam very much better now. The disease disappeared altogether in Darjeeling. I\nam  going  tomorrow  to  Almora, another hill station, to perfect this\nimprovement.\n", "Things are looking not very hopeful here as I have already written you \u2014\nthough the whole nation has risen as one man to honour me and people went\nalmost mad over me! The practical part cannot be had in India. Again, the\nprice of the land has gone up very much near Calcutta. My idea at present is\nto start three centres at three capitals. These would be my normal schools,\nfrom thence I want to invade India.\n", "India is already Ramakrishna's whether I live a few years more or not.\n", "I have a very kind letter from Prof. Janes in which he points out my remarks\nabout degraded Buddhism. You also write that Dharmapala is very wroth about\nit. Mr. Dharmapala is a good man, and I love him; but it would be entirely\nwrong for him to go into fits over things Indian.\n", "I am perfectly convinced that what they call modern Hinduism with all its\nugliness is only stranded Buddhism. Let the Hindus understand this clearly,\nand then it would be easier for them to reject it without murmur. As for the\nancient form which the Buddha preached, I have the greatest respect for it,\nas well as for His person. And you well know that we Hindus worship Him as\nan Incarnation. Neither is the Buddhism of Ceylon any good. My visit to\nCeylon has entirely disillusioned me, and the only living people there are\nthe Hindus. The Buddhists are all much Europeanised \u2014 even Mr. Dharmapala\nand his father had European names, which they have since changed. The only\nrespect the Buddhists pay to their great tenet of non-killing is by opening\n\"butcher-stalls\" in every place! And the priests encourage this. The real\nBuddhism, I once thought, would yet do much good. But I have given up the\nidea entirely, and I clearly see the reason why Buddhism was driven out of\nIndia, and we will only be too glad if the Ceylonese carry off the remnant\nof this religion with its hideous idols and licentious rites.\n", "About the Theosophists, you must remember first that in India Theosophists\nand Buddhists are nonentities. They publish a few papers and make a lot of\nsplash and try to catch Occidental ears. . .\n", "I was one man in America and another here. Here the whole nation is looking\nupon me as their authority \u2014 there I was a much reviled preacher. Here\nPrinces draw my carriage, there I would not be admitted to a decent hotel.\nMy utterances here, therefore, must be for the good of the race, my people\n\u2014  however  unpleasant  they  might appear to a few. Acceptance, love,\ntoleration for everything sincere and honest \u2014 but never for hypocrisy. The\nTheosophists tried to fawn upon and flatter me as I am the authority now in\nIndia, and therefore it was necessary for me to stop my work giving any\nsanction to their humbugs, by a few bold, decisive words; and the thing is\ndone. I am very glad. If my health had permitted, I would have cleared India\nby this time of these upstart humbugs, at least tried my best. . . . Let me\ntell you that India is already Ramakrishna's and for a purified Hinduism I\nhave organised my work here a bit.\n", "Yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

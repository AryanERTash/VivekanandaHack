["\n", "CCXXV\n", "To Sister Christine\n", "THE MATH, BELUR, DIST. HOWRAH,\n", "14th June 1902.\n", "MY DEAR CHRISTINE,\n", "Your letters had to wait a few days, as I was out of town in a village.[6]*\nWell, many thanks for all the information I got. Mr. Okakura [Kakuzo] has\nbeen to the Math, but I was away. He will be in Calcutta a few weeks more\nand then goes to Bombay. He intends taking a house near the city to learn\nintimately the customs of Bengalees. I am so glad to learn Margo's [Sister\nNivedita's] intention to stop at Mayavati longer. She really requires good\nrest, and she had none in Europe, I am sure of that. If she were amenable to\nmy advice as of old, I would take away every book and every scrap of paper\nfrom her, make her walk some, eat a lot and sleep a lot more. As to talking,\nI would have the merriest conversation all the while.\n", "I have a beautiful letter from Mrs. Sevier, and [am] so happy to learn that\nshe loves you more and more. But plumpness is the criterion, mon amie [my\nfriend], for a' [all] that.\n", "So there was a great flutter in our dovecote owing to my letters, but things\nmust have assumed their old form by this time. The boy, my nephew, is going\nto be sometime yet in the Ashrama; make him talk English with a good accent\n\u2014 do. No foreign language can be learnt properly unless you talk in it from\nchildhood.\n", "Mr. Bose[7]* is still there, I hope; and you must have liked him immensely.\nHe is a man, a brick. Tender him my best regards, will you?\n", "Have you any water in the lakes now? Do you get the snows clearer? It has\nbeen raining all through this summer here. We had very few burning days,\nonly a number of stuffy ones. Our rains also have nearly set in. In a week\nthe deluge will commence in earnest.\n", "As for me, I am much stronger than before; and when seven miles of jolting\nin a bullock-cart and railway travel of thirty-four miles did not bring back\nthe dropsy to the feet, I am sure it is not going to return.\n", "But anyway, it is the Math that suits me best just now.\n", "With all love,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "CIX\n", "C/O MISS MULLER,\n", "AIRLIE LODGE, RIDGEWAY GARDENS,\n", "WIMBLEDON, ENGLAND,\n", "7th October, 1896.\n", "Once more in London, dear Joe Joe, and the classes have begun already.\nInstinctively I looked about for one familiar face which never had a line of\ndiscouragement,  never  changed, but was always helpful, cheerful, and\nstrengthening \u2014 and my mind conjured up that face before me, in spite of a\nfew thousand miles of space. For what is space in the realm of spirit? Well,\nyou are gone to your home of rest and peace. For me, ever-increasing mad\nwork; yet I have your blessings with me always, have I not? My natural\ntendency is to go into a cave and be quiet, but a fate behind pushes me\nforward and I go. Whoever could resist fate?\n", "Why did not Christ say in the Sermon on the Mount, \"Blessed are they that\nare always cheerful and always hopeful for they have already the kingdom of\nheaven\"? I am sure, He must have said it, He with the sorrows of a whole\nworld in His heart, He who likened the saintly soul with the child \u2014 but it\nwas not noted down; of a thousand things they noted down only one, I mean,\nremembered.\n", "Most of our friends came \u2014 one of the Galsworthys too \u2014 i.e. the married\ndaughter. Mrs. Galsworthy could not come today; it was very short notice. We\nhave a hall now, a pretty big one holding about 200 or more. There is a big\ncorner which will be fitted up as the Library. I have another man from India\nnow to help me.\n", "I enjoyed Switzerland immensely, also Germany. Prof. Deussen was very kind\n\u2014 we came together to London and had great fun here. Prof. Max M\u00fcller is\nvery, very friendly too. In all, the English work is becoming solid \u2014 and\nrespectable too, seeing that great scholars are sympathising. Probably I go\nto India this winter with some English friends. So far about my own sweet\nself.\n", "Now what about the holy family? Everything is going on first-rate, I am\nsure. You must have heard of Fox by this time. I am afraid I rather made him\ndejected the day before he sailed by telling him that he could not marry\nMabel, until he began to earn a good deal of money! Is Mabel with you now?\nGive her my love. Also give me your present address.\n", "How is Mother? Frankincense, same solid sterling gold as ever, I am sure.\nAlberta, working at her music and languages, laughing a good deal and eating\na good many apples as usual? By the by, I now live mostly on fruits and\nnuts. They seem to agree with me well. If ever the old doctor, with \"land\"\nup somewhere, comes to see you, you may confide to him this secret. I have\nlost a good deal of my fat. But on days I lecture, I have to go on solid\nfood. How is Hollis? I never saw a sweeter boy \u2014 may all blessings ever\nattend him through life.\n", "I hear your friend Cola is lecturing on Zoroastrian philosophy \u2014 surely the\nstars  are  not  smiling  on him. What about your Miss Andreas and our\nYoganandla?  What news about the brotherhood of the ZZZ's and our Mrs.\n(forgotten!)?  I hear that half a shipload of Hindus and Buddhists and\nMohammedans and Brotherhoods and what not have entered the U.S., and another\ncargo of Mahatma-seekers, evangelists etc. have entered India! Good. India\nand the U.S. seem to be two countries for religious enterprise. Have a care,\nJoe; the heathen corruption is dreadful. I met Madam Sterling in the street\ntoday. She does not come any more for my lectures, good for her. Too much of\nphilosophy is not good. Do you remember that lady who used to come to every\nmeeting too late to hear a word but button-holed me immediately after and\nkept me talking, till a battle of Waterloo would be raging in my internal\neconomy through hunger? She came. They are all coming and more. That is\ncheering.\n", "It is getting late in the night. So goodnight, Joe. (Is strict etiquette to\nbe followed in New York too?) And Lord bless you ever and ever.\n", "\"Man's all-wise maker, wishing to create a faultless form whose matchless\nsymmetry should far transcend creation's choicest works, did call together\nby his mighty will, and garner up in his eternal mind, a bright assemblage\nof all lovely things, and then, as in a picture, fashioned them into one\nperfect and ideal form. Such the divine, the wondrous prototype whence her\nfair shape was moulded into being.\" (Shakuntalam by Kalidasa, translated by\nMonier Williams).\n", "That is you, Joe Joe; only I would add, the same the creator did with all\npurity and nobility and other qualities and then Joe was made.\n", "Ever yours, with love and blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "\nPS. Mrs. & Mr. Sevier in whose house (flat) I am writing now, send their\nkindest regards.\n"]

["\n", "CCXVII\n", "To Mrs. Alice (Shanti) Hansbrough\n", "BENARAS\n", "14 Feb. 1902\n", "MY DEAR MRS. HANSBROUGH,\n", "I am eternally indebted to you for what you did for me in the past, and\ninfinitely more now for what you are doing for Turiyananda.\n", "A gloom came over the Math when news reached Calcutta of his severe illness.\nNow, I hope, he has recovered completely, and I will be so glad to get the\nnews from you.\n", "It seems the American climate does not suit him. In that case it will be\nbetter for him to come over to India whenever he thinks fit.\n", "In all probability I am going to Japan in a month or two. Ramakrishnananda\naccompanies me. Turiyananda may come over to Japan and I go to America.\n\"Mother\" knows best, however, and we obey.\n", "I am just now in Benaras for a few days. All letters should, however, be\naddressed to the Belur Math.\n", "Kindly convey my best love to Turiyananda and to yourself, the holy family\nand the other friends.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "PS. Let Turiyananda take rest all the time now. He must not work at all till\nI reach Japan or America.\n"]

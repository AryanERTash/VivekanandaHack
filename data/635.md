["\n", "VI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "(From the Diary of a Disciple)\n", "(The disciple is Sharatchandra Chakravarty, who published his records in a\nBengali book, Swami-Shishya-Samv\u00e2da, in two parts. The present series of\n\"Conversations and Dialogues\" is a revised translation from this book. Five\ndialogues of this series have already appeared in the Complete Works,Volume 5)\n", "\n[Place: Baghbazar, Calcutta. Year: 1897.]\n", "\nSwamiji has been staying for some days at the house of the late Balaram\nBabu. At his wish, a large number of devotees of Shri Ramakrishna have\nassembled  at the house at 3 p.m. (on May 1, 1897). Swami Yogananda is\namongst those present here. The object of Swamiji is to form an Association.\nWhen  all present had taken their seats, Swamiji proceeded to speak as\nfollows:\n", "\"The conviction has grown in my mind after all my travels in various lands\nthat no great cause can succeed without an organisation. In a country like\nours,  however,  it  does not seem quite practicable to me to start an\norganisation at once with a democratic basis or work by general voting.\nPeople in the West are more educated in this respect, and less jealous of\none another than ourselves. They have learnt to respect merit. Take for\ninstance my case. I was just an insignificant man there, and yet see how\ncordially  they  received  and entertained me. When with the spread of\neducation the masses in our country grow more sympathetic and liberal, when\nthey learn to have their thoughts expanded beyond the limits of sect or\nparty,  then  it  will be possible to work; on the democratic basis of\norganization. For this reason it is necessary to have a dictator for this\nSociety. Everybody should obey him, and then in time we may work on the\nprinciple of general voting.\n", "\"Let this Association be named after him, in whose name indeed, we have\nembraced the monastic life, with whom as your Ideal in life you all toil on\nthe field of work from your station in family life, within twenty years of\nwhose passing away a wonderful diffusion of his holy name and extraordinary\nlife has taken place both in the East and the West. We are the servants of\nthe Lord. Be you all helpers In this cause.\"\n", "When Srijut Girish Chandra Ghosh and all other householder disciples present\nhad approved of the above proposal, the future programme of the Society of\nShri Ramakrishna was taken up for discussion. The Society was named the\nRamakrishna Mission.\n", "Swamiji  himself became the general president of the Mission and other\noffice-bearers  also  were  elected.  The  rule was laid down that the\nAssociation should hold meetings at the house of Balaram Babu every Sunday\nat  4  p.m. Needless to say that Swamiji used to attend these meetings\nwhenever convenient.\n", "When the meeting had broken up and the members departed, addressing Swami\nYogananda, Swamiji said, \"So the work is now begun this way; let us see how\nfar it succeeds by the will of Shri Ramakrishna.\"\n", "Swami Yogananda. You are doing these things with Western methods. Should you\nsay Shri Ramakrishna left us any such instructions?\n", "Swamiji: Well, how do you know that all this is not on Shri Ramakrishna's\nlines? He had an infinite breadth of feeling, and dare you shut him up\nwithin your own limited views of life. I will break down these limits and\nscatter  broadcast  over the earth his boundless inspiration. He never\ninstructed me to introduce any rites of his own worship. We have to realise\nthe  teachings  he  has left us about religious practice and devotion,\nconcentration and meditation, and such higher ideas and truths, and then\npreach these to all men. The infinite number of faiths are only so many\npaths. I haven't been born to found one more sect in a world already teeming\nwith sects. We have been blessed with obtaining refuge at the feet of the\nMaster, and we are born to carry his message to the dwellers of the three\nworlds.\n", "Swami Yogananda uttered no word of dissent, and so Swamiji continued: Time\nand again have I received in this life marks of his grace. He stands behind\nand gets all this work done by me. When lying helpless under a tree in an\nagony of hunger, when I had not even a scrap of cloth for Kaupina, when I\nwas resolved on travelling penniless round the world, even then help came in\nall ways by the grace of Shri Ramakrishna. And again when crowds jostled\nwith  one  another  in  the streets of Chicago to have a sight of this\nVivekananda, then also, just because I had his grace, I could digest without\ndifficulty all that honour \u2014 a hundredth part of which would have been\nenough to turn mad any ordinary man; and by his will, victory followed\neverywhere. Now I must conclude by doing something in this country. So\ncasting all doubt away, please help my work; and you will find everything\nfulfilled by his will.\n", "Swami Yogananda: Yes, whatever you will, shall be fulfilled; and are we not\nall ever obedient to you? Now and then I do clearly see how Shri Ramakrishna\nis getting all these things done through you. And yet, to speak plainly,\nsome misgiving rises at intervals, for as we saw it, his was of doing things\nwas different. So I question myself: \"Are we sure that we are not going\nastray  from Shri Ramakrishna's teachings?\" And so I take the opposing\nattitude and warn you.\n", "Swamiji: You see, the fact is that Shri Ramakrishna is not exactly what the\nordinary followers have comprehended him to be. He had infinite moods and\nphases. Even if you might form an idea of the limits of Brahmajn\u00e2na, the\nknowledge of the Absolute, you could not have any idea of the unfathomable\ndepths of his mind! Thousands of Vivekanandas may spring forth through one\ngracious glance of his eyes! But instead of doing that, he has chosen to get\nthings done this time through me as his single instrument, and what can I do\nin this matter you see?\n", "Saying this, Swamiji left to attend to something else waiting for him, and\nSwami Yogananda went on praising Swamiji's versatile gifts.\n", "Meanwhile Swamiji returned and asked the disciple, \"Do the people in your\npart of the country know much of Shri Ramakrishna?\"\n", "Disciple: Only one man, N\u00e2g Mah\u00e2shaya, came to Shri Ramakrishna from our\npart of Bengal;[1] it is from him that many came to hear of him and had\ntheir curiosity excited to know more. But that Shri Ramakrishna was the\nIncarnation of God, the people there have not yet come to know and some\nwould not believe it even if told so.\n", "Swamiji: Do you think it is an easy matter to believe so? We who had actual\ndealings with him in every respect we who heard of that fact again and again\nfrom his own lips, we who lived and stayed with him for twenty-four hours of\nthe day \u2014 even we off and on have doubts about it coming over us! So what to\nspeak of others!\n", "Disciple: Did Shri Ramakrishna, out of his own lips ever say that he was\nGod, the all-perfect Brahman?\n", "Swamiji: Yes, he did so many times. And he said this to all of us. One day\nwhile was staying at the Cossipore garden, his body in imminent danger of\nfalling off for ever, by the side of his bed I was saying in my mind, \"Well,\nnow if you can declare that you are God, then only will I believe you are\nreally  God  Himself.\"  It  was  only  two days before he passed away.\nImmediately, he looked up towards me all on a sudden and said, \"He who was\nRama, He who was Krishna, verily is He now Ramakrishna in this body. And\nthat not merely from the standpoint of your Vedanta!\"[2] At this I was\nstruck dumb. Even we haven't had yet the perfect faith, after hearing it\nagain and again from the holy lips of our Lord himself \u2014 our minds still get\ndisturbed now and then with doubt and despair \u2014 and so, what shall we speak\nof others being slow to believe? It is indeed a very difficult matter to be\nable to declare and believe a man with a body like ours to be God Himself.\nWe may just go to the length of declaring him to be a \"perfected one\", or a\n\"knower of Brahman\". Well, it matters nothing, whatever you may call him or\nthink of him, a saint, or a knower of Brahman, or anything. But take it from\nme,  never  did  come  to  this  earth such an all-perfect man as Shri\nRamakrishna! In the utter darkness of the world, this great man is like the\nshining pillar of illumination in this age! And by his light alone will man\nnow cross the ocean of Sams\u00e2ra!\n", "Disciple: To me it seems, sir, that true faith comes only after actually\nseeing or hearing something. Mathur[3] Babu, I have heard, actually saw so\nmany things about Shri Ramakrishna, and thus he had that wonderful faith in\nhim.\n", "Swamiji: He who believes not, believes not even after seeing, and thinks\nthat it is all hallucination, or dream and so on. The great transfiguration\nof Krishna \u2014 the Vishvarupa (form universal) \u2014 was seen alike by Duryodhana\nand by Arjuna. But only Arjuna believed, while Duryodhana took it to be\nmagic! Unless He makes us understand, nothing can be stated or understood.\nSomebody comes to the fullest faith even without seeing or hearing, while\nsomebody else remains plunged in doubt even after witnessing with his own\neyes various extraordinary powers for twelve years! The secret of it all is\nHis grace! But then one must persevere, so that the grace may be received.\n", "Disciple: Is there, sir, any law of graces\n", "Swamiji: Yes and no.\n", "Disciple: How is that\u00a0?\n", "Swamiji: Those who are pure always in body, mind, and speech, who have\nstrong devotion, who discriminate between the real and the unreal, who\npersevere in meditation and contemplation \u2014 upon them alone the grace of the\nLord descends. The Lord, however, is beyond all natural laws \u2014 is not under\nany rules and regulations, or just as Shri Ramakrishna used to say, He has\nthe child's nature \u2014 and that's why we find some failing to get any response\neven after calling on Him for millions of births, while some one else whom\nwe  regard  as  a  sinful or penitent man or a disbeliever, would have\nIllumination in a flash! \u2014 On the latter the Lord perhaps lavishes His grace\nquite unsolicited! Shri\nRamakrishna used to say sometimes, \"Do rely on Him; be like the dry leaf at\nthe mercy of the wind\"; and again he would say, \"The wind of His grace is\nalways blowing, what you need to do is to unfurl your sail.\"\n", "Disciple: But, sir, this is a most tremendous statement. No reasoning, I\nsee, can stand here.\n", "Swamiji: Ah, all reasoning and arguing is within the limit of the realm of\nMaya; it lies within the categories of space, time, and causation. But He is\nbeyond these categories. We speak of His law, still He is beyond all law. He\ncreates, or becomes, all that we speak of as laws of nature, and yet He is\noutside of them all. He on whom His grace descends, in a moment goes beyond\nall law. For this reason there is no condition in grace. It is as His play\nor  sport. But then it is just His pleasure, His play, to get\nsomebody through the practice of spiritual discipline and somebody else\nwithout it.\n", "Disciple: Sir, I can't understand this.\n", "Swamiji: And you needn't. Only get your mind to cling to Him as far as you\ncan. For then only the great magic of this world will break of itself. But\nthen, you must persevere. You must take off your mind from lust and lucre,\nmust discriminate always between the real and the unreal \u2014 must settle down\ninto the mood of bodilessness with the brooding thought that you are not\nthis  body,  and  must  always  have  the realisation that you are the\nall-pervading  Atman.

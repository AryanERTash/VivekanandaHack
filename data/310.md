["\n", "LXXV\n", "\n", "\nMy dear Doctor Shashi (Bhushan Ghosh),\n", "\nYour letter and the two bottles containing the medicines were duly received.\nI  have  begun  from  last evening a trial of your medicines. Hope the\ncombination will have a better effect than the one alone.\n", "\nI began to take a lot of exercise on horseback, both morning and evening.\nSince that I am very much better indeed. I was so much better the first week\nof my gymnastics that I have scarcely felt so well since I was a boy and\nused to have kusti (wrestling) exercises. I really began to feel that it was\na pleasure to have a body. Every movement made me conscious of strength \u2014\nevery movement of the muscles was pleasurable. That exhilarating feeling has\nsubsided somewhat, yet I feel very strong. In a trial of strength I could\nmake both G. G. and Niranjan go down before me in a minute. In Darjeeling I\nalways felt that I was not the same man. Here I feel that I have no disease\nwhatsoever, but there is one marked change. I never in my life could sleep\nas soon as I got into bed. I must toss for at least two hours. Only from\nMadras to Darjeeling (during the first month) I would sleep as soon as my\nhead  touched  the pillow. That ready disposition to sleep is gone now\nentirely, and my old tossing habit and feeling hot after the evening meal\nhave come back. I do not feel any heat after the day meal. There being an\norchard here, I began to take more fruit than usual as soon as I came. But\nthe only fruit to be got here now is the apricot. I am trying to get more\nvarieties from Naini T\u00e2l. There has not been any thirst even though the days\nare fearfully hot. . . . On the whole my own feeling is one of revival of\ngreat strength and cheerfulness, and a feeling of exuberant health, only I\nam afraid I am getting fat on a too much milk diet. Don't you listen to what\nYogen writes. He is a hypochondriac himself and wants to make everybody so.\nI ate one-sixteenth of a barphi (sweetmeat) in Lucknow, and that according\nto Yogen was what put me out of sorts in Almora! Yogen is expected here in a\nfew days. I am going to take him in hand. By the by, I am very susceptible\nto malarious influences. The first week's indisposition at Almora might have\nbeen caused to a certain extent by my passage through the Terai. Anyhow I\nfeel  very,  very  strong now. You ought to see me, Doctor, when I sit\nmeditating  in  front  of the beautiful snow-peaks and repeat from the\nUpanishads:\n", "\u2014 He has neither disease, nor decay, nor death; for, verily, he has obtained\na body full of the fire of Yoga.\"\n", "\nI am very glad to learn of the success of the meetings of the Ramakrishna\nMission at Calcutta. All blessings attend those that help in the great work.\n. . .\n", "\nWith all love,\n", "Yours in the Lord,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

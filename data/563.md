["\n", "CII\n", "63 ST. GEORGE'S ROAD, LONDON S.W.,\n", "6th July, 1896.\n", "DEAR FRANKINCENSE, (Mr. Francis H. Leggett whom Swamiji addressed thus.)\n", ". . . Things are going on with me very well on this side of the Atlantic.\n", "The Sunday lectures were quite successful; so were the classes. The season\nhas ended and I too am thoroughly exhausted. I am going to make a tour in\nSwitzerland with Miss M\u00fcller. The Galsworthys have been very very kind. Joe\n(Miss Josephine MacLeod, also referred to as Joe Joe.) brought them round\nsplendidly. I simply admire Joe in her tact and quiet way. She is a feminine\nstatesman or woman. She can wield a kingdom. I have seldom seen such strong\nyet good common sense in a human being. I will return next autumn and take\nup the work in America.\n", "The night before last I was at a party at Mrs. Martin's, about whom you must\nalready know a good deal from Joe.\n", "Well, the work is growing silently yet surely in England. Almost every other\nman or woman came to me and talked about the work. This British Empire with\nall  its  drawbacks  is the greatest machine that ever existed for the\ndissemination  of  ideas. I mean to put my ideas in the centre of this\nmachine, and they will spread all over the world. Of course, all great work\nis slow, and the difficulties are too many, especially as we Hindus are the\nconquered race. Yet, that is the very reason why it is bound to work, for\nspiritual ideals have always come from the downtrodden. Jews overwhelmed the\nRoman Empire with their spiritual ideals. You will be pleased to know that I\nam  also  learning my lessons every day in patience and, above all, in\nsympathy. I think I am beginning to see the Divine, even inside the high and\nmighty Anglo-Indians. I think I am slowly approaching to that state when I\nshould be able to love the very \"Devil\" himself, if there were any.\n", "At twenty years of age I was the most unsympathetic, uncompromising fanatic;\nI would not walk on the footpath on the theatre side of the streets in\nCalcutta. At thirty-three, I can live in the same house with prostitutes and\nnever would think of saying a word of reproach to them. Is it degenerate? Or\nis it that I am broadening out into the Universal Love which is the Lord\nHimself? Again I have heard that if one does not sea the evil round him he\ncannot do good work \u2014 he lapses into a sort of fatalism. I do not see that.\nOn the other hand, my power of work is immensely increasing and becoming\nimmensely effective. Some days I get into a sort of ecstasy. I feel that I\nmust bless every one, everything, love and embrace everything, and I do see\nthat evil is a delusion. I am in one of these moods now, dear Francis, and\nam actually shedding tears of joy at the thought of you and Mrs. Leggett's\nlove and kindness to me. I bless the day I was born. I have had so much of\nkindness and love here, and that Love Infinite that brought me into being\nhas guarded every one of my actions, good or bad, (don't be frightened), for\nwhat am I, what was I ever, but a tool in His hands, for whose service I\nhave  given up everything, my beloved ones, my joys, my life? He is my\nplayful darling, I am His playfellow. There is neither rhyme nor reason in\nthe universe! What reason binds Him? He the playful one is playing these\ntears and laughters over all parts of the play! Great fun, great fun, as Joe\nsays.\n", "It is a funny world, and the funniest chap you ever saw is He \u2014 the Beloved\nInfinite! Fun, is it not? Brotherhood or playmatehood \u2014 a school of romping\nchildren let out to play in this playground of the world! Isn't it? Whom to\npraise, whom to blame, it is all His play. They want explanations, but how\ncan you explain Him? He is brainless, nor has He any reason. He is fooling\nus with little brains and reason, but this time He won't find me napping.\n", "I have learnt a thing or two: Beyond, beyond reason and learning and talking\nis the feeling, the \"Love\", the \"Beloved\". Ay, sak\u00e9, fill up the cup and we\nwill be mad.\n", "Yours ever in madness,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

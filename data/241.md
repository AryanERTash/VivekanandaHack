["\n", "VI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", " C/O George W. Hale Esq.,\n541 Dearborn Avenue, Chicago, \n28th December, 1893.\n", "\n Dear Haripada,[1] \n", "\nIt is very strange that news of my Chicago lectures has appeared in the\nIndian papers; for whatever I do, I try my best to avoid publicity. Many\nthings strike me here. It may be fairly said that there is no poverty in\nthis country. I have never seen women elsewhere as cultured and educated as\nthey are here. Well-educated men there are in our country, but you will\nscarcely find anywhere women like those here. It is indeed true, that \"the\nGoddess Herself lives in the houses of virtuous men as Lakshmi\".  I have\nseen thousands of women here whose hearts are as pure and stainless as snow.\nOh,  how free they are! It is they who control social and civic duties\nSchools and colleges are full of women, and in our country women cannot be\nsafely allowed to walk in the streets! Their kindness to me is immeasurable.\nSince I came here, I have been welcomed by them to their houses. They are\nproviding me with food, arranging for my lectures, taking me to market, and\ndoing everything for my comfort and convenience. I shall never be able to\nrepay in the least the deep debt of gratitude I owe to them.\n", "\nDo you know who is the real \"Shakti-worshipper\"? It is he who knows that God\nis the omnipresent force in the universe and sees in women the manifestation\nof that Force. Many men here look upon their women in this light. Manu,\nagain, has said that gods bless those families where women are happy and\nwell treated. Here men treat their women as well as can be desired, and\nhence they are so prosperous, so learned, so free, and so energetic. But why\nis it that we are slavish, miserable, and dead? The answer is obvious.\n", "\nAnd how pure and chaste are they here! Few women are married before twenty\nor twenty-five, and they are as free as the birds in the air. They go to\nmarket, school, and college, earn money, and do all kinds of work. Those who\nare well-to-do devote themselves to doing good to the poor. And what are we\ndoing? We are very regular in marrying our girls at eleven years of age lest\nthey  should  become  corrupt  and immoral. What does our Manu enjoin?\n\"Daughters should be supported and educated with as much care and attention\nas the sons.\" As sons should be married after observing Brahmacharya up to\nthe thirtieth year, so daughters also must observe Brahmacharya and be\neducated by their parents. But what are we actually doing? Can you better\nthe condition of your women? Then there will be hope for your well-being.\nOtherwise you will remain as backward as you are now.\n", "\nIf anybody is born of a low caste in our country, he is gone for ever, there\nis no hope for him. Why? What a tyranny it is! There are possibilities,\nopportunities, and hope for every individual in this country. Today he is\npoor, tomorrow he may become rich and learned and respected. Here everyone\nis anxious to help the poor. In India there is a howling cry that we are\nvery poor, but how many charitable associations are there for the well-being\nof the poor? How many people really weep for the sorrows and sufferings of\nthe millions of poor in India? Are we men? What are we doing for their\nlivelihood, for their improvement? We do not touch them, we avoid their\ncompany! Are we men? Those thousands of Br\u00e2hmanas \u2014 what are they doing for\nthe low, downtrodden masses of India? \"Don't touch\", \"Don't touch\", is the\nonly  phrase that plays upon their lips! How mean and degraded has our\neternal religion become at their hands! Wherein does our religion lie now?\nIn \"Don't-touchism\" alone, and nowhere else!\n", "\nI came to this country not to satisfy my curiosity, nor for name or fame,\nbut to see if I could find any means for the support of the poor in India.\nIf God helps me, you will know gradually what those means are.\n", "\nAs regards spirituality, the Americans are far inferior to us, but their\nsociety is far superior to ours. We will teach them our spirituality and\nassimilate what is best in their society.\n", "\n", "Yours,\nVivekananda.\n"]

["\n", "CLXI\n", "\n", "6 PLACE DES ETATS UNIS, PARIS,\n", "25th Aug., 1900.\n", "DEAR NIVEDITA,\n", "Your letter reached me just now. Many thanks for the kind expressions.\n", "I gave a chance to Mrs. Bull to draw her money out of the Math; and as she\ndid not say anything about it, and the trust deeds were waiting here to be\nexecuted, I got them executed duly at the British Consulate; and they are on\ntheir way to India now.\n", "Now I am free, as I have kept no power or authority or position for me in\nthe work. I also have resigned the presidentship of the Ramakrishna Mission.\n", "The Math etc., belong now to the immediate disciples of Ramakrishna except\nmyself.  The presidentship is now Brahmananda's \u2014 next it will fall on\nPremananda etc., etc., in turn.\n", "I  am  so  glad  a whole load is off me, now I am happy. I have served\nRamakrishna through mistakes and success for 20 years now. I retire for good\nand devote the rest of my life to myself.\n", "I no longer represent anybody, nor am I responsible to anybody. As to my\nfriends, I had a morbid sense of obligation. I have thought well and find I\nowe nothing to anybody; if anything, I have given my best energies, unto\ndeath  almost,  and  received  only  hectoring and mischief-making and\nbotheration. I am done with everyone here and in India.\n", "Your letter indicates that I am jealous of your new friends. You must know\nonce for all, I am born without jealousy, without avarice, without the\ndesire to rule \u2014 whatever other vices I am born with.\n", "I never directed you before; now, after I am nobody in the work, I have no\ndirection whatever. I only know this much: So long as you serve \"Mother\"\nwith a whole heart, She will be your guide.\n", "I never had any jealousy about what friends you made. I never criticised my\nbrethren for mixing up in anything. Only I do believe the Western people\nhave the peculiarity of trying to force upon others whatever seems good to\nthem, forgetting that what is good for you may not be good for others. As\nsuch, I am afraid you might try to force upon others whatever turn your mind\nmight take in contact with new friends. That was the only reason I sometimes\ntried to stop any particular influence, and nothing else.\n", "You are free, have your own choice, your own work. ...\n", "Friends or foes, they are all instruments in Her hands to help us work out\nour own Karma, through pleasure or pain. As such \"Mother\" bless them all.\n", "With all love and blessings,\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

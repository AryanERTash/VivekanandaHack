## Original Dataset Text
["\n", "CXXIX\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "ALMORA,\n", "20th June, 1897.\n", "DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Glad to learn that you are better in health than before. Well, it is seldom\nthat Brother Yogen reports the bare truths, so do not at all be anxious to\nhear them. I am all right now, with plenty of muscular strength, and no\nthirst. ... The liver, too, acts well. I am not certain as to what effects\nShashi (Babu)'s medicine had. So I have stopped using it. I am having plenty\nof mangoes. I am getting exceptionally adept in riding, and do not feel the\nleast pain or exhaustion even after a run of twenty or thirty miles at a\nstretch. Milk I have altogether stopped for fear of corpulence.\n", "Yesterday  I  came to Almora, and shall not go any more to the garden.\nHenceforth I am to have three meals a day in the English fashion, as Miss\nM\u00fcller's guest. . . .\n", "Shuddhananda writes to say that they are going on with Ruddock's Practice of\nMedicine or something of that sort. What nonsense do you mean by having such\nthings  taught in the class? A set of common apparatus for physics and\nanother for chemistry, an ordinary telescope and a microscope \u2014 all these\ncan  be  had  for Rupees 150 to 200. Shashi Babu may give a lecture on\npractical chemistry once a week, and Hariprasanna on physics etc. And buy\nall the good scientific books that you can have in Bengali, and have them\nread.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

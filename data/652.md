["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "TUESDAY, July 2, 1895. (The Divine Mother.)\n", "Sh\u00e2ktas worship the Universal Energy as Mother, the sweetest name they know;\nfor the mother is the highest ideal of womanhood in India. When God is\nworshipped as \"Mother\", as Love, the Hindus call it the \"right-handed\" way,\nand it leads to spirituality but never to material prosperity. When God is\nworshipped on His terrible side, that is, in the \"left-handed\" way, it leads\nusually  to great material prosperity, but rarely to spirituality; and\neventually it leads to degeneration and the obliteration of the race that\npractices it.\n", "Mother is the first manifestation of power and is considered a higher idea\nthan father. With the name of Mother comes the idea of Shakti, Divine Energy\nand Omnipotence, just as the baby believes its mother to be all-powerful,\nable to do anything. The Divine Mother is the Kundalini (\"coiled up\" power)\nsleeping  in  us; without worshipping Her we can never know ourselves.\nAll-merciful, all-powerful, omnipresent are attributes of Divine Mother. She\nis the sum total of the energy in the universe. Every manifestation of power\nin the universe is \"Mother\". She is life, She is intelligence, She is Love.\nShe is in the universe yet separate from it. She is a person and can be seen\nand known (as Shri Ramakrishna saw and knew Her). Established in the idea of\nMother, we can do anything. She quickly answers prayer.\n", "She can show; Herself to us in any form at any moment. Divine Mother can\nhave form (Rupa) and name (N\u00e2ma) or name without form; and as we worship Her\nin these various aspects we can rise to pure Being, having neither form nor\nname.\n", "The sum total of all the cells in an organism is one person; so each soul is\nlike one cell and the sum of them is God, and beyond that is the Absolute.\nThe sea calm is the Absolute; the same sea in waves is Divine Mother. She is\ntime,  space,  and  causation.  God is Mother and has two natures, the\nconditioned and the unconditioned. As the former, She is God, nature, and\nsoul  (man).  As the latter, She is unknown and unknowable. Out of the\nUnconditioned came the trinity \u2014 God, nature, and soul, the triangle of\nexistence. This is the Vishisht\u00e2dvaitist idea.\n", "A  bit of Mother, a drop, was Krishna, another was Buddha, another was\nChrist. The worship of even one spark of Mother in our earthly mother leads\nto greatness. Worship Her if you want love and wisdom.\n"]

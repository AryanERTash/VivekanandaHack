["\n", "XII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "(From the Diary of a Disciple)\n", "(The disciple is Sharatchandra Chakravarty, who published his records in a\nBengali book, Swami-Shishya-Samv\u00e2da, in two parts. The present series of\n\"Conversations and Dialogues\" is a revised translation from this book. Five\ndialogues of this series have already appeared in the Complete Works,Volume 5)\n", "\n[Place: Balaram Babu's residence, Calcutta. Year: 1898.]\n", "\nSwamiji  had  been  staying during the last two days at Balaram Babu's\nresidence at Baghbazar. He was taking a short stroll on the roof of the\nhouse, and the disciple with four or five others was in attendance. While\nwalking to and fro, Swamiji took up the story of Guru Govind Singh and with\nhis great eloquence touched upon the various points in his life \u2014 how the\nrevival  of the Sikh sect was brought about by his great renunciation,\nausterities,  fortitude,  and  life-consecrating  labours \u2014 how by his\ninitiation he re-Hinduised Mohammedan converts and took them back into the\nSikh  community  \u2014  and how on the banks of the Narmada he brought his\nwonderful life to a close. Speaking of the great power that used to be\ninfused in those days into the initiates of Guru Govind, Swamiji recited a\npopular Doh\u00e2 (couplet) of the Sikhs:\n", "\n[xii_sharat_chakravarty_01.jpg]\n", "The meaning is: \"When Guru Govind gives the Name, i.e. the initiation, a\nsingle man becomes strong enough to triumph over a lakh and a quarter of his\nfoes.\"  Each  disciple, deriving from his inspiration a real spiritual\ndevotion, had his soul filled with such wonderful heroism! While holding\nforth  thus  on  the glories of religion, Swamiji's eyes dilating with\nenthusiasm seemed to be emitting fire, and his hearers, dumb-stricken and\nlooking at his face, kept watching the wonderful sight.\n", "After a while the disciple said: \"Sir, it was very remarkable that Guru\nGovind  could  unite both Hindus and Mussulmans within the fold of his\nreligion and lead them both towards the same end. In Indian history, no\nother example of this can be found.\"\n", "Swamiji: Men can never be united unless there is a bond of common interest.\nYou can never unite people merely by getting up meetings, societies, and\nlectures if their interests be not one and the same. Guru Govind made it\nunderstood everywhere that the men of his age, be they Hindus or Mussulmans,\nwere living under a regime of profound injustice and oppression. He did not\ncreate any common interest, he only pointed it out to the masses. And so\nboth  Hindus and Mussulmans followed him. He was a great worshipper of\nShakti. Yet, in Indian history, such an example is indeed very rare.\n", "Finding then that it was getting late into the night, Swamiji came down with\nothers into the parlour on the first floor, where the following conversation\non the subject of miracles took place.\n", "Swamiji said, \"It is possible to acquire miraculous powers by some little\ndegree of mental concentration\", and turning to the disciple he asked,\n\"Well, should you like to learn thought-reading? I can teach that to you in\nfour or five days.\"\n", "Disciple: Of what avail will it be to me, sir?\n", "Swamiji: Why, you will be able to know others' minds.\n", "Disciple: Will that help my attainment of the knowledge of Brahman?\n", "Swamiji: Not a bit.\n", "Disciple: Then I have no need to learn that science. But, sir, I would very\nmuch like to hear about what you have yourself seen of the manifestation of\nsuch psychic powers.\n", "Swamiji: Once when travelling in the Himalayas I had to take up my abode for\na night in a village of the hill-people. Hearing the beating of drums in the\nvillage some time after nightfall, I came to know upon inquiring of my host\nthat one of the villagers had been possessed by a Devat\u00e2 or good spirit. To\nmeet his importunate wishes and to satisfy my own curiosity, we went out to\nsee what the matter really was. Reaching the spot, I found a great concourse\nof people. A tall man with long, bushy hair was pointed out to me, and I was\ntold that person had got the Devata on him. I noticed an axe being heated in\nfire close by the man; and after a while, I found the red-hot thing being\nseized and applied to parts of his body and also to his hair! But wonder of\nwonders, no part of his body or hair thus branded with the red-hot axe was\nfound to be burnt, and there was no expression of any pain in his face! I\nstood mute with surprise. The headman of the village, meanwhile, came up to\nme and said, \"Mah\u00e2r\u00e2j, please exorcise this man out of your mercy.\" I felt\nmyself  in a nice fix, but moved to do something, I had to go near the\npossessed man. Once there, I felt a strong impulse to examine the axe rather\nclosely, but the instant I touched it, I burnt my fingers, although the\nthing had been cooled down to blackness. The smarting made me restless and\nall my theories about the axe phenomenon were spirited away from my mind!\nHowever, smarting with the burn, I placed my hand on the head of the man and\nrepeated for a short while the Japa. It was a matter of surprise to find\nthat the man came round in ten or twelve minutes. Then oh, the gushing\nreverence the villagers showed to me! I was taken to be some wonderful man!\nBut, all the same, I couldn't make any head or tail of the whole business.\nSo without a word one way or the other, I returned with my host to his hut.\nIt was about midnight, and I went to bed. But what with the smarting burn in\nthe hand and the impenetrable puzzle of the whole affair, I couldn't have\nany sleep that night. Thinking of the burning axe failing to harm living\nhuman flesh, it occurred again and again to my mind, \"There are more things\nin heaven and earth, Horatio, than are dreamt of in your philosophy.\"\n", "Disciple: But, could you later on ever explain the mystery, sir?\n", "Swamiji: No. The event came back to me in passing just now, and so I related\nit to you.\n", "He then resumed: But Shri Ramakrishna used to disparage these supernatural\npowers; his teaching was that one cannot attain to the supreme truth if the\nmind is diverted to the manifestation of these powers. The layman mind,\nhowever, is so weak that, not to speak of householders, even ninety per cent\nof the S\u00e2dhus happen to be votaries of these powers. In the West, men are\nlost in wonderment if they come across such miracles. It is only because\nShri Ramakrishna has mercifully made us understand the evil of these powers\nas being hindrances to real spirituality that we are able to take them at\ntheir proper value. Haven't you noticed how for that reason the children of\nShri Ramakrishna pay no heed to them?\n", "Swami Yogananda said to Swamiji at this moment, \"Well, why don't you narrate\nto  our  B\u00e2ng\u00e2l (Lit. A man from East Bengal, i.e. the disciple.) that\nincident of yours in Madras when you met the famous ghost-tamer?\"\n", "At the earnest entreaty of the disciple Swamiji was persuaded to give the\nfollowing account of his experience:\n", "Once  while  I  was  putting up at Manmatha Babu's (Babu Manmatha Nath\nBhattacharya, M.A., late Accountant General, Madras.) place, I dreamt one\nnight that my mother had died. My mind became much distracted. Not to speak\nof corresponding with anybody at home, I used to send no letters in those\ndays even to our Math. The dream being disclosed to Manmatha, he sent a wire\nto Calcutta to ascertain facts about the matter. For the dream had made my\nmind uneasy on the one hand, and on the other, our Madras friends, with all\narrangements ready, were insisting on my departing for America immediately,\nand I felt rather unwilling to leave before getting any news of my mother.\nSo Manmatha who discerned this state of my mind suggested our repairing to a\nman living some way off from town, who having acquired mystic powers over\nspirits could tell fortunes and read the past and the future of a man's\nlife. So at Manmatha's request and to get rid of my mental suspense, I\nagreed to go to this man. Covering the distance partly by railway and partly\non foot, we four of us \u2014 Manmatha, Alasinga, myself, and another \u2014 managed\nto reach the place, and what met our eyes there was a man with a ghoulish,\nhaggard, soot-black appearance, sitting close to a cremation ground. His\nattendants used some jargon of South Indian dialect to explain to us that\nthis was the man with perfect power over the ghosts. At first the man took\nabsolutely no notice of us; and then, when we were about to retire from the\nplace, he made a request for us to wait. Our Alasinga was acting as the\ninterpreter, and he explained the requests to us. Next, the man commenced\ndrawing  some figures with a pencil, and presently I found him getting\nperfectly still in mental concentration. Then he began to give out my name,\nmy genealogy, the history of my long line of forefathers and said that Shri\nRamakrishna was keeping close to me all through my wanderings, intimating\nalso to me good news about my mother. He also foretold that I would have to\ngo very soon to far-off lands for preaching religion. Getting good news thus\nabout my mother, we all travelled back to town, and after arrival received\nby wire from Calcutta the assurance of mother's doing well.\n", "Turning to Swami Yogananda, Swamiji remarked, \"Everything that the man had\nforetold  came  to be fulfilled to the letter, call it some fortuitous\nconcurrence or anything you will.\"\n", "Swami Yogananda said in reply, \"It was because you would not believe all\nthis before that this experience was necessary for you.\"\n", "Swamiji: Well, I am not a fool to believe anything and everything without\ndirect proof. And coming into this realm of Mah\u00e2m\u00e2ya, oh, the many magic\nmysteries I have come across alongside this bigger magic conjuration of a\nuniverse! Maya, it is all Maya! Goodness! What rubbish we have been talking\nso  long this day! By thinking constantly of ghosts, men become ghosts\nthemselves, while whoever repeats day and night, knowingly or unknowingly,\n\"I am the eternal, pure, free, self-illumined Atman\", verily becomes the\nknower of Brahman.\n", "Saying this, Swamiji affectionately turned to the disciple and said, \"Don't\nallow all that worthless nonsense to occupy your mind. Always discriminate\nbetween the real and the unreal, and devote yourself heart and soul to the\nattempt to realise the Atman. There is nothing higher than this knowledge of\nthe  Atman;  all  else  is  Maya,  mere jugglery. The Atman is the one\nunchangeable Truth. This I have come to understand, and that is why I try to\nbring it home to you all. \" [xii_sharat_chakravarty_02.jpg] \u2014 \"One Brahman\nthere  is  without a second\", \"There is nothing manifold in existence\" (Brihad\u00e2ranyaka, IV. iv. 19)\n", "All this conversation continued up to eleven o'clock at highs. After that,\nhis meal being finished, Swamiji retired for rest. The disciple bowed down\nat  his  feet  to bid him good-bye. Swamiji asked, \"Are you not coming\ntomorrow?\"\n", "Disciple: Yes, sir, I am coming, to be sure. The mind longs so much to meet\nyou at least once before the day is out.\n", "Swamiji: So good night now, it is getting very late.\n"]

["\n", "WORK IS WORSHIP\n", "\nThe highest man cannot work, for there is no binding element, no attachment,\nno ignorance in him. A ship is said to have passed over a mountain of magnet\nore, and all the bolts and bars were drawn out, and it went to pieces. It is\nin ignorance that struggle remains, because we are all really atheists. Real\ntheists cannot work. We are atheists more or less. We do not see God or\nbelieve in Him. He is G-O-D to us, and nothing more. There are moments when\nwe think He is near, but then we fall down again. When you see Him, who\nstruggles for whom? Help the Lord! There is a proverb in our language,\n\"Shall we teach the Architect of the universe how to build?\" So those are\nthe highest of mankind who do not work. The next time you see these silly\nphrases about the world and how we must all help God and do this or that for\nHim, remember this. Do not think such thoughts; they are too selfish. All\nthe work you do is subjective, is done for your own benefit. God has not\nfallen into a ditch for you and me to help Him out by building a hospital or\nsomething of that sort. He allows you to work. He allows you to exercise\nyour muscles in this great gymnasium, not in order to help Him but that you\nmay help yourself. Do you think even an ant will die for want of your help?\nMost arrant blasphemy! The world does not need you at all. The world goes on\nyou are like a drop in the ocean. A leaf does not move, the wind does not\nblow without Him. Blessed are we that we are given the privilege of working\nfor Him, not of helping Him. Cut out this word \"help\" from your mind. You\ncannot help; it is blaspheming. You are here yourself at His pleasure. Do\nyou mean to say, you help Him? You worship. When you give a morsel of food\nto the dog, you worship the dog as God. God is in that dog. He is the dog.\nHe is all and in all. We are allowed to worship Him. Stand in that reverent\nattitude to the whole universe, and then will come perfect non-attachment.\nThis should be your duty. This is the proper attitude of work. This is the\nsecret taught by Karma-Yoga.\n", "\n"]

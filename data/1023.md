["\n", "CLXXVI\n", "To Miss Josephine MacLeod\n", "ALAMEDA, CALIFORNIA,\n", "20th April, 1900.\n", "MY DEAR JOE,\n", "Received your note today. I wrote you one yesterday but directed it to\nEngland thinking you will be there.\n", "I have given your message to Mrs. Betts. I am so sorry this little quarrel\ncame with A__. I got also his letter you sent. He is correct so far as he\nsays, \"Swami wrote me 'Mr. Leggett is not interested in Vedanta and will not\nhelp any more. You stand on your own feet.'\" It was as you and Mrs. Leggett\ndesired me to write him from Los Angeles about New York \u2014 in reply to his\nasking me what to do for funds.\n", "Well, things will take their own shape, but it seems in Mrs. Bull's and your\nmind there is some idea that I ought to do something. But in the first place\nI do not know anything about the difficulties. None of you write me anything\nabout what that is for, and I am no thought-reader. You simply wrote me a\ngeneral  idea  that A__ wanted to keep things in his hands. What can I\nunderstand  from  it?  What  are  the difficulties? Regarding what the\ndifferences are about, I am as much in the dark as about the exact date of\nthe Day of Destruction! And yet Mrs. Bull's and your letters show quite an\namount of vexation! These things get complicated sometimes, in spite of\nourselves. Let them take their shape.\n", "I have executed and sent the will to Mr. Leggett as desired by Mrs. Bull.\n", "I am going on, sometimes well and at other times ill. I cannot say, on my\nconscience, that I have been the least benefited by Mrs. Milton. She has\nbeen good to me, I am very thankful. My love to her. Hope she will benefit\nothers.\n", "For writing to Mrs. Bull this fact, I got a four page sermon, as to how I\nought to be grateful and thankful, etc., etc. All that is, sure, the outcome\nof this A__ business! Sturdy and Mrs. Johnson got disturbed by Margot, and\nthey fell upon me. Now A__ disturbs Mrs. Bull and, of course, I have to bear\nthe brunt of it. Such is life!\n", "You and Mrs. Leggett wanted me to write him to be free and independent and\nthat Mr. Leggett was not going to help them. I wrote it \u2014 now what can I do?\nIf John or Jack does not obey you, am I to be hanged for it? What do I know\nabout this Vedanta Society? Did I start it? Had I any hand in it? Then\nagain, nobody condescends to write me anything about what the affair is!\nWell, this world is a great fun.\n", "I am glad Mrs. Leggett is recovering fast. I pray every moment for her\ncomplete recovery. I start for Chicago on Monday. A kind lady has given me a\npass up to New York to be used within three months. The Mother will take\ncare of me. She is not going to strand me now after guarding me all my life.\n", "Ever yours gratefully,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "HINDUISM AND SHRI RAMAKRISHNA\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\nBy the word \"Shastras\" the Vedas without beginning or end are meant. In\nmatters of religious duty the Vedas are the only capable authority.\n", "The Puranas and other religious scriptures are all denoted by the word\n\"Smriti\". And their authority goes so far as they follow the Vedas and do\nnot contradict them.\n", "Truth is of two kinds: (1) that which is cognisable by the five ordinary\nsenses of man, and by reasonings based thereon; (2) that which is cognisable\nby the subtle, supersensuous power of Yoga.\n", "Knowledge acquired by the first means is called science; and knowledge\nacquired by the second is called the Vedas.\n", "The whole body of supersensuous truths, having no beginning or end, and\ncalled by the name of the Vedas, is ever-existent. The Creator Himself is\ncreating, preserving, and destroying the universe with the help of these\ntruths.\n", "The person in whom this supersensuous power is manifested is called a Rishi,\nand the supersensuous truths which he realises by this power are called the\nVedas.\n", "This Rishihood, this power of supersensuous perception of the Vedas, is real\nreligion. And so long as this does not develop in the life of an initiate,\nso long is religion a mere empty word to him, and it is to be understood\nthat he has not taken yet the first step in religion.\n", "The authority of the Vedas extends to all ages, climes and persons; that is\nto say, their application is not confined to any particular place, time, and\npersons.\n", "The Vedas are the only exponent of the universal religion.\n", "Although  the supersensuous vision of truths is to be met with in some\nmeasure in our Puranas and Itihasas and in the religious scriptures of other\nraces, still the fourfold scripture known among the Aryan race as the Vedas\nbeing the first, the most complete, and the most undistorted collection of\nspiritual truths, deserve to occupy the highest place among all scriptures,\ncommand the respect of all nations of the earth, and furnish the rationale\nof all their respective scriptures.\n", "With regard to the whole Vedic collection of truths discovered by the Aryan\nrace, this also has to be understood that those portions alone which do not\nrefer to purely secular matters and which do not merely record tradition or\nhistory, or merely provide incentives to duty, form the Vedas in the real\nsense.\n", "The Vedas are divided into two portions, the Jn\u00e2na-k\u00e2nda (knowledge-portion)\nand the Karma-k\u00e2nda (ritual-portion). The ceremonies and the fruits of the\nKarma-kanda  are  confined within the limits of the world of M\u00e2y\u00e2, and\ntherefore  they  have  been undergoing and will undergo transformation\naccording to the law of change which operates through time, space, and\npersonality.\n", "Social laws and customs likewise, being based on this Karma-kanda, have been\nchanging and will continue to change hereafter. Minor social usages also\nwill be recognised and accepted when they are compatible with the spirit of\nthe true scriptures and the conduct and example of holy sages. But blind\nallegiance  only  to usages such as are repugnant to the spirit of the\nShastras and the conduct of holy sages has been one of the main causes of\nthe downfall of the Aryan race.\n", "It is the Jnana-kanda or the Vedanta only that has for all time commanded\nrecognition for leading men across Maya and bestowing salvation on them\nthrough the practice of Yoga, Bhakti, Jnana, or selfless work; and as its\nvalidity and authority remain unaffected by any limitations of time, place\nor persons, it is the only exponent of the universal and eternal religion\nfor all mankind.\n", "The Samhitas of Manu and other sages, following the lines laid down in the\nKarma-kanda, have mainly ordained rules of conduct conducive to social\nwelfare,  according to the exigencies of time, place, and persons. The\nPuranas etc. have taken up the truths imbedded in the Vedanta and have\nexplained them in detail in the course of describing the exalted life and\ndeeds of Avataras and others. They have each emphasised, besides, some out\nof the infinite aspects of the Divine Lord to teach men about them.\n", "But when by the process of time, fallen from the true ideals and rules of\nconduct and devoid of the spirit of renunciation, addicted only to blind\nusages, and degraded in intellect, the descendants of the Aryans failed to\nappreciate  even  the spirit of these Puranas etc. which taught men of\nordinary intelligence the abstruse truths of the Vedanta in concrete form\nand  diffuse  language and appeared antagonistic to one another on the\nsurface, because of each inculcating with special emphasis only particular\naspects of the spiritual ideal \u2014\n", "And when, as a consequence, they reduced India, the fair land of religion,\nto a scene of almost infernal confusion by breaking up piecemeal the one\nEternal Religion of the Vedas (San\u00e2tana Dharma), the grand synthesis of all\nthe aspects of the spiritual ideal, into conflicting sects and by seeking to\nsacrifice one another in the flames of sectarian hatred and intolerance \u2014\n", "Then it was that Shri Bhagavan Ramakrishna incarnated himself in India, to\ndemonstrate what the true religion of the Aryan race is; to show where\namidst all its many divisions and offshoots, scattered over the land in the\ncourse of its immemorial history, lies the true unity of the Hindu religion,\nwhich by its overwhelming number of sects discordant to superficial view,\nquarrelling constantly with each other and abounding in customs divergent in\nevery way, has constituted itself a misleading enigma for our countrymen and\nthe butt of contempt for foreigners; and above all, to hold up before men,\nfor their lasting welfare, as a living embodiment of the Sanatana Dharma,\nhis  own wonderful life into which he infused the universal spirit and\ncharacter of this Dharma, so long cast into oblivion by the process of time.\n", "In order to show how the Vedic truths \u2014 eternally existent as the instrument\nwith the Creator in His work of creation, preservation, and dissolution \u2014\nreveal themselves spontaneously in the minds of the Rishis purified from all\nimpressions  of  worldly attachment, and because such verification and\nconfirmation of the scriptural truths will help the revival, reinstatement,\nand spread of religion \u2014 the Lord, though the very embodiment of the Vedas,\nin this His new incarnation has thoroughly discarded all external forms of\nlearning.\n", "That the Lord incarnates again and again in human form for the protection of\nthe Vedas or the true religion, and of Brahminhood or the ministry of that\nreligion \u2014 is a doctrine well established in the Puranas etc.\n", "The waters of a river falling in a cataract acquire greater velocity, the\nrising wave after a hollow swells higher; so after every spell of decline,\nthe Aryan society recovering from all the evils by the merciful dispensation\nof  Providence  has risen the more glorious and powerful \u2014 such is the\ntestimony of history.\n", "After rising from every fall, our revived society is expressing more and\nmore its innate eternal perfection, and so also the omnipresent Lord in each\nsuccessive incarnation is manifesting Himself more and more.\n", "Again and again has our country fallen into a swoon, as it were, and again\nand again has India's Lord, by the manifestation of Himself, revivified her.\n", "But greater than the present deep dismal night, now almost over, no pall of\ndarkness had ever before enveloped this holy land of ours. And compared with\nthe depth of this fall, all previous falls appear like little hoof-marks.\n", "Therefore, before the effulgence of this new awakening' the glory of all\npast revivals in her history will pale like stars before the rising sun; and\ncompared with this mighty manifestation of renewed strength, all the many\npast epochs of such restoration will be as child's play.\n", "The various constituent ideals of the Religion Eternal, during its present\nstate of decline, have been lying scattered here and there for want of\ncompetent men to realise them \u2014 some being preserved partially among small\nsects and some completely lost.\n", "But strong in the strength of this new spiritual renaissance, men, after\nreorganising these scattered and disconnected spiritual ideals, will be able\nto comprehend and practice them in their own lives and also to recover from\noblivion  those that are lost. And as the sure pledge of this glorious\nfuture, the all-merciful Lord has manifested in the present age, as stated\nabove, an incarnation which in point of completeness in revelation, its\nsynthetic harmonising of all ideals, and its promoting of every sphere of\nspiritual culture, surpasses the manifestations of all past ages.\n", "So at the very dawn of this momentous epoch, the reconciliation of all\naspects and ideals of religious thought and worship is being proclaimed;\nthis boundless, all embracing idea had been lying inherent, but so long\nconcealed, in the Religion Eternal and its scriptures, and now rediscovered,\nit is being declared to humanity in a trumpet voice.\n", "This epochal new dispensation is the harbinger of great good to the whole\nworld, specially to India; and the inspirer of this dispensation, Shri\nBhagavan Ramakrishna, is the reformed and remodelled manifestation of all\nthe past great epoch-makers in religion. O man, have faith in this, and lay\nto heart.\n", "The dead never return; the past night does not reappear; a spent-up tidal\nwave does not rise anew; neither does man inhabit the same body over again.\nSo from the worship of the dead past, O man, we invite you to the worship of\nthe living present; from the regretful brooding over bygones, we invite you\nto the activities of the present; from the waste of energy in retracing lost\nand demolished pathways, we call you back to broad new-laid highways lying\nvery near. He that is wise, let him understand.\n", "Of that power, which at the very first impulse has roused distant echoes\nfrom  all  the  four  quarters of the globe, conceive in your mind the\nmanifestation  in  its  fullness;  and discarding all idle misgivings,\nweaknesses, and the jealousies characteristic of enslaved peoples, come and\nhelp in the turning of this mighty wheel of new dispensation!\n", "With the conviction firmly rooted in your heart that you are the servants of\nthe Lord, His children, helpers in the fulfilment of His purpose, enter the\narena of work.\n"]

["\n", "WHAT HAVE I LEARNT?\n(Delivered at Dacca, 30th March, 1901)\n", "\nAt Dacca Swamiji delivered two lectures in English. The first was on \"What\nhave I learnt?\" and the second one was \"The Religion we are born in\".\nThe following is translated from a report in Bengali by a disciple, and it\ncontains the substance of the first lecture:\n", "\nFirst of all, I must express my pleasure at the opportunity afforded me of\ncoming to Eastern Bengal to acquire an intimate knowledge of this part of\nthe country, which I hitherto lacked in spite of my wanderings through many\ncivilised countries of the West, as well as my gratification at the sight of\nmajestic rivers, wide fertile plains, and picturesque villages in this, my\nown country of Bengal, which I had not the good fortune of seeing for myself\nbefore. I did not know that there was everywhere in my country of Bengal \u2014\non land and water \u2014 so much beauty and charm. But this much has been my gain\nthat after seeing the various countries of the world I can now much more\nappreciate the beauties of my own land.\n", "\nIn the same way also, in search of religion, I had travelled among various\nsects \u2014 sects which had taken up the ideals of foreign nations as their own,\nand  I  had begged at the door of others, not knowing then that in the\nreligion of my country, in our national religion, there was so much beauty\nand grandeur. It is now many years since I found Hinduism to be the most\nperfectly satisfying religion in the world. Hence I feel sad at heart when I\nsee existing among my own countrymen, professing a peerless faith, such a\nwidespread indifference to our religion \u2014 though I am very well aware of the\nunfavourable materialistic conditions in which they pass their lives \u2014 owing\nto the diffusion of European modes of thought in this, our great motherland.\n", "\nThere are among us at the present day certain reformers who want to reform\nour religion or rather turn it topsyturvy with a view to the regeneration of\nthe Hindu nation. There are, no doubt, some thoughtful people among them,\nbut there are also many who follow others blindly and act most foolishly,\nnot  knowing  what  they  are  about. This class of reformers are very\nenthusiastic in introducing foreign ideas into our religion. They have taken\nhold of the word \"idolatry\", and aver that Hinduism is not true, because it\nis idolatrous. They never seek to find out what this so-called \"idolatry\"\nis, whether it is good or bad; only taking their cue from others, they are\nbold enough to shout down Hinduism as untrue. There is another class of men\namong us who are intent upon giving some slippery scientific explanations\nfor any and every Hindu custom, rite, etc., and who are always talking of\nelectricity, magnetism, air vibration, and all that sort of thing. Who knows\nbut they will perhaps some day define God Himself as nothing but a mass of\nelectric vibrations! However, Mother bless them all! She it is who is having\nHer work done in various ways through multifarious natures and tendencies.\n", "\nIn contradistinction to these, there is that ancient class who say, \"I do\nnot know, I do not care to know or understand all these your hair-splitting\nratiocinations; I want God, I want the Atman, I want to go to that Beyond,\nwhere there is no universe, where there is no pleasure or pain, where dwells\nthe Bliss Supreme\"; who say, \"I believe in salvation by bathing in the holy\nGang\u00e2 with faith\"; who say, \"whomsoever you may worship with singleness of\nfaith and devotion as the one God of the universe, in whatsoever form as\nShiva, R\u00e2ma, Vishnu, etc., you will get Moksha\"; to that sturdy ancient\nclass I am proud to belong.\n", "\nThen there is a sect who advise us to follow God and the world together.\nThey are not sincere, they do not express what they feel in their hearts.\nWhat is the teaching of the Great Ones? \u2014 \"Where there is Rama, there is no\nKama; where there is Kama, there Rama is not. Night and day can never exist\ntogether.\" The voice of the ancient sages proclaim to us, \"If you desire to\nattain God, you will have to renounce K\u00e2ma-K\u00e2nchana (lust and possession).\nThe Sams\u00e2ra is unreal, hollow, void of substance. Unless you give it up, you\ncan never reach God, try however you may. If you cannot do that, own that\nyou are weak, but by no means lower the Ideal. Do not cover the corrupting\ncorpse with leaves of gold!\" So according to them, if you want to gain\nspirituality, to attain God, the first thing that you have to do is to give\nup this playing \"hide-and-seek with your ideas\", this dishonesty, this\n\"theft within the chamber of thought\".\n", "\nWhat  have I learnt? What have I learnt from this ancient sect? Though  our  means  of realisation vary according to the\ndifference in sects and individuals \u2014 though different individuals can lay\nclaim  to their special rights and means to gain knowledge, which vary\naccording  to their different stations in life \u2014 yet it can be said in\ngeneral  without  fear  of contradiction that without this Mumukshut\u00e2,\nrealisation of God is impossible. Even if you\nhave a burning thirst for God, or have gained the Guru, unless you have\nalong with it the Abhyasa, unless you practice what you have been taught,\nyou cannot get realisation. When all these are firmly established in you,\nthen you will reach the Goal.\n", "\nTherefore, I say unto you, as Hindus, as descendants of the glorious \u00c2ryans,\ndo not forget the great ideal of our religion, that great ideal of the\nHindus, which is, to go beyond this Samsara \u2014 not only to renounce the\nworld, but to give up heaven too; ay, not only to give up evil, but to give\nup good too; and thus to go beyond all, beyond this phenomenal existence,\nand  ultimately  realise  the  Sat-Chit-\u00c2nanda  Brahman \u2014 the Absolute\nExistence-Knowledge-Bliss, which is Brahman.\n"]

Turiyananda and I have beautiful lodgings here.\nSaradananda's brother is with Miss Noble and starts Monday next.\n", "I have recovered quite a bit by the voyage. It was brought about by the\nexercise on the dumb-bells and monsoon storms tumbling the steamer about the\nwaves.  Queer, isn't it? Hope it will remain. Where is our Mother, the\nWorshipful Brahmini cow of India? She is with you in New York, I think.\n", "Sturdy is away, Mrs. Johnson and everybody. Margo is rather worried at that.\nShe cannot come to U.S. till next month. Already I have come to love the\nsea. The fish Avat\u00e2ra is on me, I am afraid \u2014 good deal of him in me, I am\nsure, a Bengali.\nHow is Alberta, . . . the old folks and the rest of them? I had a beautiful\nletter from dear Mrs. Brer Rabbit; she could not meet us in London; she\nstarted before we arrived.\n", "It is nice and warm here; rather too much they say. I have become for the\npresent a Shunyav\u00e2di, a believer in nothingness, or void. No plans, no\nafterthought, no attempt, for anything, laissez faire to the fullest. Well,\nJoe, Margo would always take your side on board the steamer, whenever I\ncriticised you or the Divine cow. Poor child, she knows so little! The\nupshot  of the whole is, Joe, that there cannot be any work in London,\nbecause you are not here. You seem to be my fate! Grind on, old lady; it is\nKarma and none can avoid. Say, I look several years younger by this voyage.\nOnly when the heart gives a lurch, I feel my age. What is this osteopathy,\nanyway? Will they cut off a rib or two to cure me? Not I, no manufacturing\nof . . . from my ribs, sure. Whatever it be, it will be hard work for him to\nfind my bones. My bones are destined to make corals in the Ganga. Now I am\ngoing to study French if you give me a lesson every day; but no grammar\nbusiness \u2014 only I will read and you explain in English. Kindly give my love\nto Abhedananda, and ask him to get ready for Turiyananda. I will leave with\nhim. Write soon.\n", "With all love etc.,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "LXXIV\n", "\n", "U.S.A.\n", "(End of?) 1895.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Just now I got your letter and was glad to go through it. No matter whether\nthere is any work done in India or not, the real work lies here. I do not\nwant anybody to come over now. On my return to India I shall train a few\nmen, and after that there will be no danger for them in the West. Yes, it\nwas of Gunanidhi that I wrote. Give my special love and blessings to Hari\nSingh and others. Never take part in quarrels and disputes. Who on earth\npossesses the power to put the Raja of Khetri down? \u2014 The Divine Mother is\nat his elbow! I have received Kali's letter too. It will be very good indeed\nif you can start a centre in Kashmir. Wherever you can, open a centre....\nNow I have laid the foundations firm here and in England, and nobody has the\npower to shake them. New York is in a commotion this year. Next year will\ncome the turn of London. Even big giants will give way, who counts your\npigmies! Gird up your loins and set yourselves to work! We must throw the\nworld into convulsions with our triumphal shouts. This is but the beginning,\nmy boy. Do you think there are men in our country, it is a Golgotha! There\nis some chance if you can impart education to the masses. Is there a greater\nstrength than that of Knowledge? Can you give them education? Name me the\ncountry where rich men ever helped anybody! In all countries it is the\nmiddle classes that do all great works. How long will it take to raise the\nmoney? Where are the men? Are there any in our country? Our countrymen are\nboys, and even must treat them as such.... There are some few religious and\nphilosophical books left \u2014 the remnants of the mansion that has been burnt\ndown; take them with you, quick and come over to this country. ...\n", "Never fear! The Divine Mother is helping me! This year such work is going to\nbe turned out that you w ill be struck dumb to hear of it!\n", "What fear! Whom to fear! Steel your hearts and set yourselves to work!\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Sarada is talking of bringing out a Bengali magazine. Help it with all\nyour might. It is not a bad idea. You must not throw cold water on anybody's\nproject. Give up criticism altogether. Help all as long as you find they are\ndoing all right, and in cases where they seem to be going wrong, show them\ntheir mistakes gently. It is criticising each other that is at the root of\nall mischief. That is the chief factor in breaking down organizations. ...\n"]

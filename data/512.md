["\n", "LI\n", "\n", "U.S.A.,\n", "30th November, 1894.\n", "DEAR AND BELOVED, (Dr. Nanjunda Rao.)\n", "Your beautiful letter just came to hand. I am so glad that you have come to\nknow Shri Ramakrishna. I am very glad at the strength of your Vair\u00e2gya. It\nis the one primary necessity in reaching God. I had always great hopes for\nMadras, and still I have the firm belief that from Madras will come the\nspiritual wave that will deluge India. I can only say Godspeed to your good\nintentions; but here, my son, are the difficulties. In the first place, no\nman ought to take a hasty step. In the second place, you must have some\nrespect for the feelings of your mother and wife. True, you may say that we,\nthe disciples of Ramakrishna, had not always shown great deference to the\nopinions of our parents. I know, and know for sure, that great things are\ndone only by great sacrifices. I know for certain that India requires the\nsacrifice of her highest and best, and I sincerely hope that it will be your\ngood fortune to be one of them.\n", "Throughout the history of the world you find great men make great sacrifices\nand the mass of mankind enjoy the benefit. If you want to give up everything\nfor your own salvation, it is nothing. Do you want to forgo even your own\nsalvation for the good of the world? You are God, think of that. My advice\nto you is to live the life of a Brahmacharin, i.e. giving up all sexual\nenjoyments for a certain time live in the house of your father; this is the\n\"Kutichaka\" stage. Try to bring your wife to consent to your great sacrifice\nfor the good of the world. And if you have burning faith and all-conquering\nlove and almighty purity, I do not doubt that you will shortly succeed. Give\nyourself  body and soul to the work of spreading the teachings of Shri\nRamakrishna, for work (Karma) is the first stage. Study Sanskrit diligently\nas well as practice devotion. For you are to be a great teacher of mankind,\nand  my Guru Maharaja used to say, \"A penknife is sufficient to commit\nsuicide with, but to kill others one requires guns and swords.\" And in the\nfullness of time it will be given unto you when to go forth out of the world\nand preach His sacred name. Your determination is holy and good. Godspeed to\nyou, but do not take any hasty step. First purify yourself by work and\ndevotion India has suffered long, the Religion Eternal has suffered long.\nBut the Lord is merciful. Once more He has come to help His children, once\nmore the opportunity is given to rise to fallen India. India can only rise\nby sitting at the feet of Shri Ramakrishna. His life and his teachings are\nto be spread far and wide, are to be made to penetrate every pore of Hindu\nsociety. Who will do it? Who are to take up the flag of Ramakrishna and\nmarch  for  the  salvation  of  the world? Who are to stem the tide of\ndegeneration at the sacrifice of name and fame, wealth and enjoyment \u2014 nay\nof every hope of this or other worlds? A few young men have jumped in the\nbreach, have sacrificed themselves. They are a few; we want a few thousands\nof such as they, and they will come. I am glad that our Lord has put it in\nyour mind to be one of them Glory unto him on whom falls the Lord's choice.\nYour determination is good, your hopes are high, your aim is the noblest in\nthe world \u2014 to bring millions sunk in darkness to the light of the Lord.\n", "But, my son, here are the drawbacks. Nothing shall be done in haste. Purity,\npatience, and perseverance are the three essentials to success and, above\nall, love. All time is yours, there is no indecent haste. Everything will\ncome right if you are pure and sincere. We want hundreds like you bursting\nupon society and bringing new life and vigour of the Spirit wherever they\ngo. Godspeed to you.\n", "Yours with all blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

I have handed them over to Mrs. Bull who has\ntaken charge of reporting the different parts of the accounts to different\ndonors. Never mind what I have said in previous harsh letters. You know the heart\nalways, whatever the lips say. All blessings on you. For the last year or so\nI  have not been in my senses at all. I do not know why. I had to pass\nthrough this hell \u2014 and I have. I am much better \u2014 well, in fact. Lord help\nyou all. I am going to the Himalayas soon to retire for ever. My work is\ndone.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Mrs. Bull sends her love.\n", "V.\n"]

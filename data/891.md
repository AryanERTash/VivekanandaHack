I told him to get a few cylinders\n\u2014 I talk in them through somebody's phonograph and send them to Joe \u2014 to\nwhich he replied that he could buy one, because \"I always do what Joe asks\nme to do.\" I am glad there is so much of hidden poetry in his nature.\n", "I am going today to live with the Guernseys as the doctor wants to watch me\nand  cure me. . . . Dr. Guernsey burst\nout laughing and declared he would have paid that man for coming just then,\nfor he was then sure of his diagnosis of my case. Is not he broad?\n", "I expect to see \"the sacred cow\" today in town. I will be in New York a few\ndays more. Helmer wants me to take three treatments a week for four weeks,\nthen two a week for four more, and I will be all right. In case I go to\nBoston, he recommends me to a very good ostad (expert) there whom he would\nadvise on the matter.\n", "I said a few kind words to Landsberg and went upstairs to Mother Guernsey to\nsave poor Landsberg from embarrassment.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

I am very well and happy and\nsame as ever. Waves must come before a rise. So with me. I am very glad you\nare going to pray. Why don't you get up a Methodist camp-meeting? I am the Advaitist; our goal is knowledge\n\u2014 no feelings, no love, as all that belongs to matter and superstition and\nbondage. I am only existence and knowledge.\n", "Greenacre will give you good rest. I am sure. I wish you all joy there.\nDon't for a moment worry on my account. \"Mother\" looks after me. She is\nbringing me fast out of the hell of emotionalism, and bringing me into the\nlight of pure reason. With everlasting wishes for your happiness,\n", "Ever your brother,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Margot starts on the 26th. I may follow in a week or two. Nobody has any\npower over me, for I am the spirit. I have no ambition; it is all Mother's\nwork; I have no part.\n", "V.\n", "I could not digest your letter as the dyspepsia was rather bad last few\ndays.\n", "V.\n", "Non-attachment has always been there. It has come in a minute. Very soon I\nstand where no sentiment, no feeling, can touch me.\n", "V.\n"]

Mother knows what is best. Each one of us is in the hands of Karma\n\u2014 it works out itself, and no nay. There is only one element in life which\nis worth having at any cost \u2014 and it is love. Love immense and infinite,\nbroad as the sky and deep as the ocean. This is the one great gain in life.\nBlessed is he who gets it.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

OLE BULL\n", "RIVERVIEW, 168 BRATTLE STREET\n", "CAMBRIDGE, MASS.\n", "[Postmarked: Oct. 10, 1894, 4:30 a.m.]\n", "DEAR MOTHER,\n", "Received two letters from you and a large number from India but none from\nKhetri.\n", "I am sorry the sisters have got bad colds and more sorry for your getting\nworried over it. Nothing can make a Christian worry. I hope Narasimha will\nbe a good boy this time forth. Sister Mary is coming to Boston \u2014 good. I am\ngoing  off from here tomorrow to Baltimore. I had enough to pay all my\nexpenses here; and since I am living with Mrs. Bull, there is no expense.\nShe is a rich and highly cultured lady. She has given me $500 for my work or\nanything  I like. As I am not going west very soon, I will have a bank\naccount here in Boston. From Philadelphia I go to Washington, and then I\nwill run between New York and Boston. So I do not think I will be able to\nsee you, except perhaps Sister Mary. I want so very much that Mary will see\nMrs. Bull and others of my friends here. I have the fat of the land as\nusual, and my dinner is cooking very well both here and in India. Do not\nmake it public, Mother \u2014 that is between you and me and the babies \u2014 and do\nnot worry yourself about anything. All things come to him that waits. I am\ngoing to send the greater part of the money I have got to India and then\nmoney will come faster. I have always found that the faster I spend, the\nfaster it comes. Nature abhors a vacuum. I am in very good spirits, only you\nmust not stop keeping me informed about yourself, Babies and Father Pope\nfrom time to time.\n", "Perhaps you remember the two letters that came from Mysore \u2014 I want one of\nthose envelopes with the Mysore King's seal on the outside to be sent to\nMiss Phillips, 19 West 38th Street, New York.\n", "I cannot go to New York now nor to Chicago, although I had a number of\ninvitations and offers from both the places. I must see now the capital and\nthe other cities. I am in His Hands. If Miss Mary be in Boston, sometime I\nmay hope to see her.\n", "I am glad that Narasimha was never fast \u2014 hope he will never be.\n", "From India they always write me to come, come, come. They do not know the\nsecret. I am acting more from here than I will ever do from there.\n", "Kindly send my letters to this address and they will reach me safe wherever\nI be. This will be one of my homes when I am in Boston.\n", "Lord bless you all, dear Mother.\n", "Yours ever affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

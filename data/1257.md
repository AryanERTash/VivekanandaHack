The\nstars brought you a tremendous blow. (Sister Christine\u2019s mother had passed\naway.) Blessed be the name of the Lord. After all, it is only \"Thy will be\ndone\" \u2014 our only refuge. I will not insult you by offering you consolation\n\u2014 you know it all already. Only this line to remind you of one who is in\nentire sympathy with you and who knows that all your plans must be good in\njoy or sorrow, as you are dedicated to the eternal Mother. Well, the Mother\nphenomenal has merged in the Mother absolute, eternal. Thy will be done.\n", "By this time you must have made a decision, or, rather, the \"Mother\" has\nshown you the way, surely. I rest content.\n", "The soldier of the Queen has gone abroad to fight for Her cause, leaving all\nhe loves to Her care. The soldier is to look to his duty. The Queen of the\nUniverse knows the rest.\n", "With all love as usual,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

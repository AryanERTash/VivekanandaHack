I was driven out\nof a private temple by the owners for eating with Europeans. I wish I were\nmalleable enough to be moulded into whatever one desired, but unfortunately\nI never saw a man who could satisfy everyone. Nor can anyone who has to go\nto different places possibly satisfy all.\n", "When I first came to America, they ill-treated me if I had not trousers on.\nNext I was forced to wear cuffs and collars, else they would not touch me\netc., etc. They thought me awfully funny if I did not eat what they offered\netc., etc. . . .\n", "In India the moment I landed they made me shave my head and wear \"Kaupin\"\n(loin cloth), with the result that I got diabetes etc. Saradananda never\ngave up his underwear \u2014 this saved his life, with just a touch of rheumatism\nand much comment from our people.\n", "Of course, it is my Karma, and I am glad that it is so. I am glad it is so \u2014 as it\nproves my theory of \"every wave having its corresponding dip\" on my own\nperson.\n", "As for me, I stick to my nature and principle \u2014 once a friend, always a\nfriend \u2014 also the true Indian principle of looking subjectively for the\ncause of the objective.\n", "I am sure that the fault is mine, and mine only, for every wave of dislike\nand hatred that I get.

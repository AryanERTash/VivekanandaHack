You must\nhave got my letters by this time, and [I] hope this will give you a taste of\nhow sometimes silence is gold.\n", "I have beautiful letters from everywhere this morning and am quite happy. I will write more in my next; just now these few lines \u2014 and a hundred\npardons I beg for their scantiness. Yet silence tells more sometimes than\nall the speech in the world.\n", "With all love and blessings,\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "V\n", "IVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "C\n", "(Translated from Bengali.)\n", "To Swami Ramakrishnananda\n", "ALMORA,\n", "29th July, 1897.\n", "DEAR SHASHI,\n", "I got information that your work there is going on very well. Get a thorough\nmastery of the three Bh\u00e2shyas (commentaries), and also study well European\nphilosophy and allied subjects \u2014 see to it without fail. To fight with\nothers one requires sword and shield \u2014 this fact should never be forgotten.\nI hope Sukul has now reached there and is attending on you all right. If\nSadananda does not like to stay there, send him to Calcutta. Don't forget to\nsend  to the Math every week a report of the work including income and\nexpenditure and other information.\n", "Alasinga's sister's husband borrowed four hundred rupees from Badridas here,\npromising  to  send it back as soon as he reached Madras; inquire from\nAlasinga and tell him to send it quickly. For I am leaving this place the\nday after tomorrow \u2014 whether for Mussoorie Hills or somewhere else I shall\ndecide later.\n", "Yesterday I delivered a lecture in the circle of the local English people,\nand all were highly pleased with it. But I was very much pleased with the\nlecture in Hindi that I delivered the previous day \u2014 I did not know before\nthat I could be oratorical in Hindi.\n", "Are there any new boys joining the Math? If so, then carry on the work in\nthe same manner as it is being done in Calcutta. At present don't use up\nyour wisdom too much, lest it should become completely exhausted \u2014 you can\ndo that later on.\n", "Pay particular attention to your health, but too much coddling of the body\nwill, on the contrary, also spoil the health. If there is not the strength\nof knowledge, nobody would care twopence for your ringing of the bell \u2014 this\nis certain; and knowing this for certain equip yourself accordingly. My\nheart's love and blessings to you and to Goodwin and others.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

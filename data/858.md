["\n", "XI\n", "To Shri Alasinga Perumal\n", "C/o Babu Madhusudan Chattopadhyaya\nSuperintending Engineer\n", "KHARTABAD, HYDERABAD,\n", "11th February, 1893.\n", "DEAR ALASINGA,\n", "Your friend, the young graduate, came to receive me at the station, so also\na Bengali gentleman. At present I am living with the Bengali gentleman;\ntomorrow I go to live with your young friend for a few days, and then I see\nthe different sights here, and in a few days you may expect me at Madras.\nFor  I  am  very sorry to tell you that I cannot go back at present to\nRajputana. It is so very dreadfully hot here already. I do not know how hot\nit would be at Rajputana, and I cannot bear heat at all. So the next thing,\nI would do, would be to go back to Bangalore and then to Ootacamund to pass\nthe summer there. My brain boils in heat.\n", "So all my plans have been dashed to the ground. That is why I wanted to\nhurry off from Madras early. In that case I would have months left in my\nhands to seek out for somebody amongst our northern princes to send me over\nto America. But alas, it is now too late. First, I cannot wander about in\nthis heat \u2014 I would die. Secondly, my fast friends in Rajputana would keep\nme bound down to their sides if they get hold of me and would not let me go\nover to Europe. So my plan was to get hold of some new person without my\nfriends' knowledge. But this delay at Madras has dashed all my hopes to the\nground, and with a deep sigh I give it up, and the Lord's will be done!\nHowever, you may be almost sure that I shall see you in a few days for a day\nor two in Madras and then go to Bangalore and thence to Ootacamund to see\n\"if\" the M\u2014Maharaja sends me up. \"If\" \u2014 because you see I cannot be sure of\nany promise of a Dakshini (southern) Raja. They are not Rajputs. A Rajput\nwould rather die than break his promise. However, man learns as he lives,\nand experience is the greatest teacher in the world.\n", "\"Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven, for Thine is the glory and\nthe kingdom for ever and ever.\" My compliments to you all.\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "SACHCHIDANANDA.\n", "(Swamiji used to call himself such in those days.)\n"]

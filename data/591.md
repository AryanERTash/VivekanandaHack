["\n", "CXXX\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "\n", "ALMORA,\n", "10th July, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Today I send back the proofs of the objects of our Association that you sent\nme, corrected. The rules and regulations portion (which the members of our\nAssociation had read) is full of mistakes. Correct it very carefully and\nreprint it, or people will laugh.\n", ". . . The kind of work that is going on at Berhampore is exceedingly nice.\nIt is those works that will triumph \u2014 can doctrines and dogmas touch the\nheart? Work, work \u2014 live the life; what do doctrines and opinions count?\nPhilosophy and Yoga and penance \u2014 the worship-room \u2014 your sunned rice or\nvegetable offerings \u2014 all these constitute the religion of one man or one\ncountry; doing good to others is the one great, universal religion. Men and\nwomen, young and old, down to the Pariah, nay, the very animal \u2014 all can\ngrasp this religion. Can a merely negative religion be of any avail? The\nstone is never unchaste, the cow never tells a lie, nor do trees commit\ntheft or robbery, but what does it matter? Granted that you do not steal,\nnor tell a lie, nor lead an unchaste life, but meditate four hours a day and\nreligiously ring the bell for twice as many hours \u2014 yet, what matters it\nafter all? That work, little as it is, that you have done, has brought\nBerhampore to your feet for ever \u2014 now people will do whatever you wish them\nto. Now you will no longer have to argue to the people that \"Ramakrishna is\nGod.\" Without it what will mere lectures do? \u2014 Do fine words butter any\nparsnips? If you could do like that in ten districts, all the ten would\nbecome yours to have and hold. Therefore, like the intelligent boy that you\nare, lay your greatest stress, for the present, on that work department, and\ntry heart and soul to augment the utility of that alone. Organise a number\nof  boys to go from door to door, let them fetch, in the manner of the\nAlakhi\u00e2 S\u00e2dhus, whatever they can get \u2014 money, or worn out clothes, or rice\nand eatables, or anything. Then distribute them. That is work, work indeed.\nAfter that people will have faith, and will then do what they are told.\n", "Whatever is left over after defraying the expenses of the Calcutta meeting,\nremit for famine relief, or help with it the countless poor that live in the\nslums of Calcutta; let Memorial Halls and things of that kind go to the\ndogs. The Lord will do what He thinks best. I am at present in excellent\nhealth. . . .\n", "Why are you not collecting materials? \u2014 I shall go down and start the paper\nmyself. Kindness and love can buy you the whole world; lectures and books\nand philosophy all stand lower than these.\n", "Please write to Shashi to open a work department like this for the service\nof the poor.\n", ". . . Curtail the expenses of worship to a rupee or two per mensem. The\nchildren of the Lord are dying of starvation. . . . Worship with water and\nTulasi leaves alone, and let the allowance for His Bhoga (food offerings) be\nspent in offering food to the Living God who dwells in the persons of the\npoor \u2014 then will His grace descend on everything. Yogen felt unwell here; so\ntoday he started for Calcutta. I shall again go to Dewaldhar tomorrow.\nPlease accept my love and tender it to all.\n", "Affly. yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

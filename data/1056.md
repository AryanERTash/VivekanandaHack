["\n", "XII\n", "To Mrs. G. W. Hale\n", "DETROIT,\n", "10 March 1894.\n", "DEAR MOTHER,\n", "Reached Detroit safely yesterday evening.[6]* The two younger daughters were\nwaiting for me with a carriage. So everything was all right. I hope the\nlecture will be a success, as one of the girls said the tickets are selling\nlike hot cakes. Here I found a letter from Mr. Palmer awaiting me with a\nrequest that I should come over to his house and be his guest.\n", "Could not go last night. He will come in the course of the day to take me\nover.  As  I  am  going  over  to  Mr. Palmer's, I have not opened the\nawfully-packed bag. The very idea of repacking seems to me to be hopeless.\nSo I could not shave this morning. However, I hope to shave during the\ncourse of the day. I am thinking of going over to Boston and New York just\nnow, as the Michigan cities I can come and take over in summer; but the\nfashionables of New York and Boston will fly off. Lord will show the way.\n", "Mrs. Bagley and all the family are heartily glad at my return and people are\nagain coming in to see me.\n", "The photographer here has sent me some of the pictures he made. They are\npositively villainous \u2014 Mrs. Bagley does not like them at all. The real fact\nis that between the two photos my face has become so fat and heavy \u2014 what\ncan the poor photographers do?\n", "Kindly send over four copies of photographs. Not yet made any arrangement\nwith Holden. (A lecture agent at Detroit.) Everything promises to be very\nnice. \"Ssenator Ppalmer\"[7]* is a very nice gentleman and very kind to me.\nHe has got a French chef \u2014 Lord bless his stomach! I am trying to starve and\nthe whole world is against me!! He used to give the best dinners in all\nWashington! Hopeless! I am resigned!\n", "I will write more from Mr. Palmer's house.\n", "If the Himalayas become the inkpot, the ocean ink, if the heavenly eternal\nDevadaroo[8]* becomes the pen, and if the sky itself becomes paper, still I\nwould not be able to write a drop of the debt of gratitude I owe to you and\nyours. Kindly convey my love to the four full notes and the four half notes\nof the Hale gamut.[9]*\n", "May the blessings of the Lord be upon you and yours ever and ever.\n", "\nEver yours in grateful affection,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "XXXIX\n", "To Mr. Francis Leggett\n", "NEW YORK,\n", "10th April, 1895.\n", "DEAR FRIEND,\n", "It is impossible to express my gratitude for your kindly inviting me to your\ncountry  seat  [Ridgely].  I  am involved in a mistake now and find it\nimpossible for me to come tomorrow. Tomorrow I have a class at Miss Andrews'\nof 40 W. 9th Street. As I was given to understand by Miss MacLeod that that\nclass could be postponed, I was only too glad at the prospect of joining the\ncompany tomorrow. But I find that Miss MacLeod was mistaken and Miss Andrews\ncame to tell me that she could not by any means stop the class tomorrow or\neven give notice to the members, who are about 50 or 60 in number.\n", "In view of this I sincerely regret my inability and hope that Miss MacLeod\nand Mrs. Sturges will understand that it is an unavoidable circumstance, and\nnot the will, that stands in the way of my taking advantage of your kind\ninvitation.\n", "I shall only be too glad to come day after tomorrow, or any other day this\nweek, as it suits you.\n", "Ever sincerely yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "LXI\n", "\n", "\nDear Dr. Nanjunda Rao,\n", "\nI received your note this morning. As I am sailing for England tomorrow, I\ncan only write a few hearty lines. I have every sympathy with your proposed\nmagazine for boys, and will do my best to help it on. You ought to make it\nindependent, following the same lines as the Brahmav\u00e2din, only making the\nstyle and matter much more popular. As for example, there is a great chance,\nmuch more than you ever dream of, for those wonderful stories scattered all\nover the Sanskrit literature, to be re-written and made popular. That should\nbe the one great feature of your journal. I will write stories, as many as I\ncan, when time permits. Avoid all attempts to make the journal scholarly \u2014\nthe Brahmavadin stands for that \u2014 and it will slowly make its way all over\nthe world, I am sure. Use the simplest language possible, and you will\nsucceed. The main feature should be the teaching of principles through\nstories. Don't make it metaphysical at all. As to the business part, keep it\nwholly in your hands. \"Too many cooks spoil the broth.\" In India the one\nthing we lack is the power of combination, organisation, the first secret of\nwhich is obedience.\n", "\nI have also promised to help starting a magazine in Bengali in Calcutta.\nOnly the first year I used to charge for my lectures The last two years, my\nwork was entirely free of all charges. As such, I have almost no money to\nsend you or the Calcutta people. But I will get people to help you with\nfunds very soon. Go on bravely. Do not expect success in a day or a year.\nAlways hold on to the highest. Be steady. Avoid jealousy and selfishness. Be\nobedient and eternally faithful to the cause of truth, humanity, and your\ncountry, and you will move the world. Remember it is the person, the life,\nwhich is the secret of power \u2014 nothing else. Keep this letter and read the\nlast lines whenever you feel worried or jealous. Jealousy is the bane of all\nslaves. It is the bane of our nation. Avoid that always. All blessings attend\nyou and all success.\n", "\n", "Yours affectionately,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

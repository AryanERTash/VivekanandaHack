["\n", "LII\n", "To Miss Josephine MacLeod\n", "C/O E. T. STURDY, ESQ.,\n", "HIGH VIEW, CAVERSHAM,\n", "READING, ENGLAND,\n", "September, 1895.\n", "DEAR JOE JOE,\n", "A  thousand pardons for not promptly writing to you. I arrived safe in\nLondon, found my friend, and am all right in his home. It is beautiful. His\nwife is surely an angel, and his life is full of India. He has been years\nthere \u2014 mixing with the Sannyasins, eating their food, etc., etc.; so you\nsee I am very happy. I found already several retired Generals from India;\nthey were very civil and polite to me. That wonderful knowledge of the\nAmericans that identify every black man with the negro is entirely absent\nhere, and nobody even stares at me in the street.\n", "I am very much more at home here than anywhere out of India. The English\npeople know us, we know them. The standard of education and civilisation is\nvery high here \u2014 that makes a great change, so does the education of many\ngenerations.\n", "Have the Turtle-doves returned? The Lord bless them and theirs for ever and\never. How are the babies \u2014 Alberta and Holister? Give them my oceans of love\nand know it yourself.\n", "My  friend  being a Sanskrit scholar, we are busy working on the great\ncommentaries of Shankara etc. Nothing but philosophy and religion here, Joe\nJoe. I am going to try to get up classes in October in London.\n", "Ever affectionately with love and blessings,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

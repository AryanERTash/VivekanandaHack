["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "FRIDAY, July 19, 1895.\n", "So long as I say \"you\", I have the right to speak of God protecting us. When\nI see another, I must take all the consequences and put in the third, the\nideal, which stands between us; that is the apex of the triangle. The vapour\nbecomes snow, then water, then Ganga; but when it is vapour, there is no\nGanga,  and when it is water, we think of no vapour in it. The idea of\ncreation  or  change is inseparably connected with will. So long as we\nperceive this world in motion, we have to conceive will behind it. Physics\nproves the utter delusion of the senses; nothing really is as ever see,\nhear, feel, smell, taste it. Certain vibrations producing certain results\naffect our senses; we know only relative truth.\n", "The Sanskrit word for truth is \"isness\" (Sat). From our present standpoint,\nthis world appears to us as will and consciousness. Personal God is as much\nan entity for Himself as we are for ourselves, and no more. God can also be\nseen as a form, just as we are seen. As men, we must have a God; as God, we\nneed none. This is why Shri Ramakrishna constantly saw the Divine Mother\never present with him, more real than any other thing around him; but in\nSam\u00e2dhi all went but the Self. Personal God comes nearer and nearer until He\nmelts away, and there is no more Personal God and no more \"I\", all is merged\nin Self.\n", "Consciousness  is  a  bondage.  The  argument  from design claims that\nintelligence precedes form; but if intelligence is the cause of anything, it\nitself is in its turn an effect. It is Maya. God creates us, and we create\nGod, and this is Maya. The circle is unbroken; mind creates body, and body\ncreates mind; the egg brings the chicken, the chicken the egg; the tree the\nseed, the seed the tree. The world is neither entirely differentiated nor\nyet entirely homogeneous. Man is free and must rise above both sides. Both\nare right in their place; but to reach truth, \"isness\", we must transcend\nall  that we now know of existence, will, consciousness, doing, going,\nknowing.  There  is no real individuality of the Jiva (separate soul);\neventually it, as a compound, will go to pieces. Only that which is beyond\nfurther analysis is \"simple\", and that alone is truth, freedom, immortality,\nbliss. All struggles for the preservation of this illusive individuality are\nreally  vices.  All  struggles to lose this individuality are virtues.\nEverything in the universe is trying to break down this individuality,\neither  consciously  or  unconsciously. All morality is based upon the\ndestruction of separateness or false individuality, because that is the\ncause  of all sin. Morality exists first; later, religion codifies it.\nCustoms come first, and then mythology follows to explain them. While things\nare happening, they come by a higher law than reasoning; that arises later\nin the attempt to understand them. Reasoning is not the motive power, it is\n\"chewing the cud\" afterwards. Reason is the historian of the actions of the\nhuman beings.\n", "*            *            *\n", "Buddha was a great Vedantist (for Buddhism was really only an offshoot of\nVedanta), and Shankara is often called a \"hidden Buddhist\". Buddha made the\nanalysis, Shankara made the synthesis out of it. Buddha never bowed down to\nanything \u2014 neither Veda, nor caste, nor priest, nor custom. He fearlessly\nreasoned so far as reason could take him. Such a fearless search for truth\nand such love for every living thing the world has never seen. Buddha was\nthe Washington of the religious world; he conquered a throne only to give it\nto the world, as Washington did to the American people. He sought nothing\nfor himself.\n"]

["\n", "XCV\n", "To Sister Nivedita\n", "ALMORA,\n", "20th June, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR MISS NOBLE,\n", ". . . Let me tell you plainly. Every word you write I value, and every\nletter  is welcome a hundred times. Write whenever you have a mind and\nopportunity,  and  whatever  you  like,  knowing  that nothing will be\nmisinterpreted, nothing unappreciated. I have not had any news of the work\nfor so long. Can you tell me anything? I do not expect any help from India,\nin spite of all the jubilating over me. They are so poor!\n", "But I have started work in the fashion in which I myself was trained \u2014 that\nis to say, under the trees, and keeping body and soul together anyhow. The\nplan has also changed a little. I have sent some of my boys to work in the\nfamine districts. It has acted like a miracle. I find, as I always thought,\nthat it is through the heart, and that alone, that the world can be reached.\nThe present plan is, therefore, to train up numbers of young men (from the\nhighest classes, not the lowest. For the latter I shall have to wait a\nlittle), and the first attack will be made by sending a number of them over\na district. When these sappers and miners of religion have cleared the way,\nthere will then be time enough to put in theory and philosophy.\n", "A number of boys are already in training, but the recent earthquake has\ndestroyed the poor shelter we had to work in, which was only rented, anyway.\nNever mind. The work must be done without shelter and under difficulties. .\n. . As yet it is shaven heads, rags, and casual meals. This must change,\nhowever, and will, for are we not working for it, head and heart? . . .\n", "It is true in one way that the people here have so little to give up \u2014 yet\nrenunciation  is  in our blood. One of my boys in training has been an\nexecutive engineer, in charge of a district. That means a very big position\nhere. He gave it up like straw! . . .\n", "With all love,\n", "Yours in the Truth,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

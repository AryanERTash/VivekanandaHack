Mr.\nSevier speaks well of you in his letter. I am very pleased at this.\n", "Write to me in minute detail who all are there, and what each one is doing.\nWhy don't you write letters to your mother? What is this? Devotion to the\nmother is the root of all welfare. How is your brother getting on with his\nstudies at Calcutta? The Sannyasin-names of those there escape my memory \u2014\nhow to address each? Give my love to all conjointly. I got the news that\nKhagen has now fully recovered. This is happy news. Write to me whether the\nSeviers are attending to your comforts and other details. I am glad to know\nthat Dinu's health is all right. The boy Kali has a tendency to become fat;\nbut this will all surely go away by constantly climbing up and down the\nhills there. Tell Swarup that I am very much pleased with his conducting of\nthe paper. He is doing splendid work. Give to all others also my love and\nblessings. Tell everybody that my health is now all right. From here I shall\ngo to England and from there to India very shortly.\n", "With all blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

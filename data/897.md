["\n", "L\n", "To Mr. E. T. Sturdy\n", "19, WEST 38TH STREET,\n", "NEW YORK,\n", "9th August, 1895.\n", "DEAR FRIEND,\n", ". . . It is only just that I should try to give you a little of my views. I\nfully believe that there are periodic ferments of religion in human society,\nand that such a period is now sweeping over the educated world. While each\nferment, moreover, appears broken into various little bubbles, these are all\neventually similar, showing the cause or causes behind them to be the same.\nThat religious ferment which at present is every day gaining a greater hold\nover  thinking  men,  has  this  characteristic  that  all  the little\nthought-whirlpools into which it has broken itself declare one single aim\n\u2014  a vision and a search after the Unity of Being. On planes physical,\nethical, and spiritual, an ever-broadening generalisation \u2014 leading up to a\nconcept  of  Unity Eternal \u2014 is in the air; and this being so, all the\nmovements of the time may be taken to represent, knowingly or unknowingly,\nthe noblest philosophy of the unity man ever had \u2014 the Advaita Vedanta.\n", "Again, it has always been observed that as a result of the struggles of the\nvarious fragments of thought in a given epoch, one bubble survives. The rest\nonly arise to melt into it and form a single great wave, which sweeps over\nsociety with irresistible force.\n", "In  India, America, and England (the countries I happen to know about)\nhundreds of these are struggling at the present moment. In India, dualistic\nformulae are already on the wane, the Advaita alone holds the field in\nforce. In America, many movements are struggling for the mastery. All these\nrepresent Advaita thought more or less, and that series, which is spreading\nmost  rapidly,  approaches nearer to it than any of the others. Now if\nanything  was  ever clear to me, it is that one of these must survive,\nswallowing up all the rest, to be the power of the future. Which is it to\nbe?\n", "Referring to history, we see that only that fragment which is fit will\nsurvive, and what makes fit to survive but character? Advaita will be the\nfuture religion of thinking humanity. No doubt of that. And of all the\nsects, they alone shall gain the day who are able to show most character in\ntheir lives, no matter how far they may be.\n", "Let me tell you a little personal experience. When my Master left the body,\nwe were a dozen penniless and unknown young men. Against us were a hundred\npowerful  organisations,  struggling  hard  to  nip us in the bud. But\nRamakrishna  had given us one great gift, the desire, and the lifelong\nstruggle not to talk alone, but to live the life. And today all India knows\nand reverences the Master, and the truths he taught are spreading like wild\nfire. Ten years ago I could not get a hundred persons together to celebrate\nhis birthday anniversary. Last year there were fifty thousand.\n", "Neither  numbers  nor powers nor wealth nor learning nor eloquence nor\nanything  else will prevail, but purity, living the life, in one word,\nanubhuti, realisation. Let there be a dozen such lion-souls in each country,\nlions who have broken their own bonds, who have touched the Infinite, whose\nwhole soul is gone to Brahman, who care neither for wealth nor power nor\nfame, and these will be enough to shake the world.\n", "Here  lies the secret. Says Patanjali, the father of Yoga, \"When a man\nrejects all the superhuman powers, then he attains to the cloud of virtue.\"\nHe sees God. He becomes God and helps others to become the same. This is all\nI have to preach. Doctrines have been expounded enough. There are books by\nthe million. Oh, for an ounce of practice!\n", "As to societies and organisations, these will come of themselves. Can there\nbe jealousy where there is nothing to be jealous of? The names of those who\nwill wish to injure us will be legion. But is not that the surest sign of\nour having the truth? The more I have been opposed, the more my energy has\nalways found expression. I have been driven and worshipped by princes. I\nhave been slandered by priests and laymen alike. But what of it? Bless them\nall! They are my very Self, and have they not helped me by acting as a\nspring-board from which my energy could take higher and higher flights?\n", ". . . I have discovered one great secret \u2014 I have nothing to fear from\ntalkers of religion. And the great ones who realise \u2014 they become enemies to\nnone! Let talkers talk! They know no better! Let them have their fill of\nname and fame and money and woman. Hold we on to realisation, to being\nBrahman, to becoming Brahman. Let us hold on to truth unto death, and from\nlife to life. Let us not pay the least attention to what others say, and if,\nafter a lifetime's effort, one soul, only one, can break the fetters of the\nworld and be free, we have done our work. Hari Om!\n", ". . . One word more. Doubtless I do love India. But every day my sight grows\nclearer. What is India, or England, or America to us? We are the servants of\nthat God who by the ignorant is called MAN. He who pours water at the root,\ndoes he not water the whole tree?\n", "There is but one basis of well-being, social, political or spiritual \u2014 to\nknow that I and my brother are one. This is true for all countries and all\npeople.  And Westerners, let me say, will realise it more quickly than\nOrientals, who have almost exhausted themselves in formulating the idea and\nproducing a few cases of individual realisation.\n", "Let us work without desire for name or fame or rule over others. Let us be\nfree from the triple bonds of lust, greed of gain, and anger. And this truth\nis with us!\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

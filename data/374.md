["\n", "SADHANAS OR PREPARATIONS FOR HIGHER LIFE\n", "\nIf atavism gains, you go down; if evolution gains, you go on. Therefore, we\nmust not allow atavism to take place. Here, in my own body, is the first\nwork  of  the  study.  We  are too busy trying to mend the ways of our\nneighbours, that is the difficulty. We must begin with our own bodies. The\nheart,  the  liver,  etc.,  are  all  atavistic;  bring them back into\nconsciousness, control them, so that they will obey your commands and act up\nto your wishes. There was a time when we had control of the liver; we could\nshake the whole skin, as can the cow. I have seen many people bring the\ncontrol back by sheer hard practice. Once an impress is made, it is there.\nBring back all the submerged activities \u2014 the vast ocean of action. This is\nthe first part of the great study, and it is absolutely necessary for our\nsocial well-being. On the other hand, only the consciousness need not be\nstudied all the time.\n", "\nThen there is the other part of study, not so necessary in our social life,\nwhich tends to liberation. Its direct action is to free the soul, to take\nthe torch into the gloom, to clean out what is behind, to shake it up or\neven defy it, and to make us march onward piercing the gloom. That is the\ngoal \u2014 the superconscious. Then when that state is reached, this very man\nbecomes divine, becomes free. And to the mind thus trained to transcend all,\ngradually this universe will begin to give up its secrets; the book of\nnature will be read chapter after chapter, till the goal is attained, and we\npass from this valley of life and death to that One, where death and life do\nnot exist, and we know the Real and become the Real.\n", "\nThe first thing necessary is a quiet and peaceable life. If I have to go\nabout the world the whole day to make a living, it is hard for me to attain\nto anything very high in this life. Perhaps in another life I shall be born\nunder more propitious circumstances. But if I am earnest enough, these very\ncircumstances will change even in this birth. Was there anything you did not\nget  which you really wanted? It could not be. For it is the want that\ncreates the body. It is the light that has bored the holes, as it were, in\nyour head, called the eyes. If the light had not existed, you would have had\nno  eyes. It is sound that had made the ears. The object of perception\nexisted first, before you made the organ. In a few hundred thousand years or\nearlier, we may have other organs to perceive electricity and other things.\nThere is no desire for a peaceful mind. Desire will not come unless there is\nsomething outside to fulfil it. The outside something just bores a hole in\nthe body, as it were, and tries to get into the mind. So, when the desire\nwill arise to have a peaceful, quiet life, that shall come where everything\nshall be propitious for the development of the mind \u2014 you may take that as\nmy experience. It may come after thousands of lives, but it must come. Hold\non to that, the desire. You cannot have the strong desire if its object was\nnot outside for you already. Of course you must understand, there is a\ndifference between desire and desire. The master said, \"My child, if you\ndesire after God, God shall come to you.\" The disciple did not understand\nhis master fully. One day both went to bathe in a river, and the master\nsaid, \"Plunge in\", and the boy did so. In a moment the master was upon him,\nholding him down. He would not let the boy come up. When the boy struggled\nand was exhausted, he let him go. \"Yes, my child, how did you feel there;\"\n\"Oh, the desire for a breath of air!\" \"Do you have that kind of desire for\nGod?\" \"No, sir.\" \"Have that kind of desire for God and you shall have God.\"\n", "\nThat, without which we cannot live, must come to us. If it did not come to\nus, life could not go on.\n", "\nIf  you  want  to  be  a  Yogi, you must be free and place yourself in\ncircumstances where you are alone and free from all anxiety. He who desires\nfor a comfortable and nice life and at the same time wants to realise the\nSelf is like the fool who, wanting to cross the river, caught hold of a\ncrocodile mistaking it for a log of wood. \"Seek ye first the Kingdom of God and\nHis righteousness, and all these things shall be added unto you.\" Unto him\neverything who does not care for anything. Fortune is like a flirt; she\ncares not for him who wants her, but she is at the feet of him who does not\ncare for her. Money comes and showers itself upon one who does not care for\nit; so does fame come in abundance until it is a trouble and a burden. They\nalways come to the Master. The slave never gets anything. The Master is he\nwho can live in spite of them, whose life does not depend upon the little,\nfoolish things of the world. Live for an ideal, and that one ideal alone.\nLet it be so great, so strong, that there may be nothing else left in the\nmind; no place for anything else, no time for anything else.\n", "\nHow some people give all their energies, time, brain, body, and everything,\nto become rich! They have no time for breakfast! Early in the morning they\nare out and at work! They die in the attempt \u2014 ninety per cent of them \u2014 and\nthe rest when they make money, cannot enjoy it. That is grand! I do not say\nit is bad to try to be rich. It is marvellous, wonderful. Why, what does it\nshow? It shows that one can have the same amount of energy and struggle for\nfreedom as one has for money. We know we have to give up money and all other\nthings when we die, and yet, see the amount of energy we can put forth for\nthem. But we, the same human beings, should we not put forth a thousandfold\nmore  strength and energy to acquire that which never fades, but which\nremains to us for ever? For this is the one great friend, our own good\ndeeds, our own spiritual excellence, that follows us beyond the grave.\nEverything else is left behind here with the body.\n", "\nThat is the one great first step \u2014 the real desire for the ideal. Everything\ncomes easy after that. That the Indian mind found out; there, in India, men\ngo to any length to find truth. But here, in the West, the difficulty is\nthat everything is made so easy. It is not truth, but development, that is\nthe great aim. The struggle is the great lesson. Mind you, the great benefit\nin this life is struggle. It is through that we pass. If there is any road\nto Heaven, it is through Hell. Through Hell to Heaven is always the way.\nWhen the soul has wrestled with circumstance and has met death, a thousand\ntimes death on the way, but nothing daunted has struggled forward again and\nagain and yet again \u2014 then the soul comes out as a giant and laughs at the\nideal he has been struggling for, because he finds how much greater is he\nthan the ideal. I am the end, my own Self, and nothing else, for what is\nthere to compare to me own Self? Can a bag of gold be the ideal of my Soul?\nCertainly not! My Soul is the highest ideal that I can have. Realising my\nown real nature is the one goal of my life.\n", "\nThere is nothing that is absolutely evil. The devil has a place here as well\nas God, else he would not be here. Just as I told you, it is through Hell\nthat we pass to Heaven. Our mistakes have places here. Go on! Do not look\nback if you think you have done something that is not right. Now, do you\nbelieve you could be what you are today, had you not made those mistakes\nbefore? Bless your mistakes, then. They have been angels unawares. Blessed\nbe torture! Blessed be happiness! Do not care what be your lot. Hold on to\nthe ideal. March on! Do not look back upon little mistakes and things. In\nthis battlefield of ours, the dust of mistakes must be raised. Those who are\nso thin-skinned that they cannot bear the dust, let them get out of the\nranks.\n", "\nSo, then, this tremendous determination to struggle a hundredfold more\ndetermination than that which you put forth to gain anything which belongs\nto this life, is the first great preparation.\n", "\nAnd then along with it, there must be meditation Meditation is the one\nthing.  Meditate!  The greatest thing is meditation. It is the nearest\napproach to spiritual life \u2014 the mind meditating. It is the one moment in\nour  daily life that we are not at all material \u2014 the Soul thinking of\nItself, free from all matter \u2014 this marvellous touch of the Soul!\n", "\nThe body is our enemy, and yet is our friend. Which of you can bear the\nsight of misery? And which of you cannot do so when you see it only as a\npainting? Because it is unreal, we do not identify ourselves with it, eve\nknow it is only a painting; it cannot bless us, it cannot hurt us. The most\nterrible misery painted upon a price of canvas, we may even enjoy; we praise\nthe technique of the artist, we wonder at his marvellous genius, even though\nthe  scene  he  paints  is  most  horrible.  That  is the secret; that\nnon-attachment. Be the Witness.\n", "\nNo breathing, no physical training of Yoga, nothing is of any use until you\nreach to the idea, \"I am the Witness.\" Say, when the tyrant hand is on your\nneck, \"I am the Witness! I am the Witness!\" Say, \"I am the Spirit! Nothing\nexternal can touch me.\" When evil thoughts arise, repeat that, give that\nsledge-hammer blow on their heads, \"I am the Spirit! I am the Witness, the\nEver-Blessed! I have no reason to do, no reason to suffer, I have finished\nwith  everything,  I am the Witness. I am in my picture gallery \u2014 this\nuniverse is my museum, I am looking at these successive paintings. They are\nall beautiful. Whether good or evil. I see the marvellous skill, but it is\nall one. Infinite flames of the Great Painter!\" Really speaking, there is\nnaught \u2014 neither volition, nor desire. He is all. He \u2014 She \u2014 the Mother, is\nplaying, and we are like dolls, Her helpers in this play. Here, She puts one\nnow in the garb of a beggar, another moment in the garb of a king, the next\nmoment in the garb of a saint, and again in the garb of a devil. We are\nputting on different garbs to help the Mother Spirit in Her play.\n", "\nWhen the baby is at play, she will not come even if called by her mother.\nBut when she finishes her play, she will rush to her mother, and will have\nno  play.  So there come moments in our life, when we feel our play is\nfinished, and we want to rush to the Mother. Then all our toil here will be\nof no value; men, women, and children \u2014 wealth, name, and fame, joys and\nglories of life \u2014 punishments and successes \u2014 will be no more, and the whole\nlife will seem like a show. We shall see only the infinite rhythm going on,\nendless and purposeless, going we do not know where. Only this much shall we\nsay; our play is done.\n", "\n"]

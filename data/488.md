["\n", "XXVII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "\n", "GHAZIPUR,\n", "March, 1890.\n", "BELOVED AKHANDANANDA,\n", "Very glad to receive your letter yesterday. I am at present staying with the\nwonderful Yogi and devotee of this place, called Pavhariji. He never comes\nout of his room and holds conversations with people from behind the door.\nInside the room there is a pit in which he lives. It is rumoured that he\nremains in a state of Samadhi for months together. His fortitude is most\nwonderful. Our Bengal is the land of Bhakti and of Jnana, where Yoga is\nscarcely so much as talked of even. What little there is, is but the queer\nbreathing exercises of the Hatha-Yoga \u2014 which is nothing but a kind of\ngymnastics. Therefore I am staying with this wonderful Raja-Yogi \u2014 and he\nhas given me some hopes, too. There is a beautiful bungalow in a small\ngarden belonging to a gentleman here; I mean to stay there. The garden is\nquite close to Babaji's cottage. A brother of the Babaji stays there to look\nafter the comforts of the Sadhus, and I shall have my Bhiksh\u00e2 at his place.\nHence, with a view to seeing to the end of this fun, I give up for the\npresent my plan of going to the hills. For the last two months I have had an\nattack of lumbago in the waist, which also makes it impossible to climb the\nhills now. Therefore let me wait and see what Babaji will give me.\n", "My motto is to learn whatever good things I may come across anywhere. This\nleads many friends to think that it will take away from my devotion to the\nGuru. These ideas I count as those of lunatics and bigots. For all Gurus are\none and are fragments and radiations of God, the Universal Guru.\n", "If you come to Ghazipur, you have but to inquire at Satish Babu's or Gagan\nBabu's at Gorabazar, and you know my whereabouts. Or, Pavhari Baba is so\nwell-known a person here that everyone will inform you about his Ashrama at\nthe very mention of his name, and you have only to go there and inquire\nabout the Paramahamsa, and they will tell you of me. Near Moghul Sarai there\nis a station named Dildarnagar, where you have to change to a short branch\nrailway and get down at Tarighat, opposite Ghazipur; then you have to cross\nthe Ganga to reach Ghazipur.\n", "For the present, I stay at Ghazipur for some days, and wait and see what the\nBabaji does. If you come, we shall stay together at the said bungalow for\nsome time, and then start for the hills, or for any other place we may\ndecide upon. Don't, please, write to anyone at Baranagore that I am staying\nat Ghazipur.\n", "With blessings and best wishes,\n", "Ever yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

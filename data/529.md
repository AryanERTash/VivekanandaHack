["\n", "LXVIII\n", "\n54 WEST 33rd STREET, NEW YORK,\nJune, 1895.\n", "DEAR MRS. BULL,\n", "I have just arrived home. The trip did me good, and I enjoyed the country\nand the hills, and especially Mr. Leggett's country-house in New York State.\nPoor  Landsberg  has gone from this house. Neither has he left one his\naddress. May the Lord bless Landsberg wherever he goes! He is one of the few\nsincere souls I have had the privilege in this life to come across.\n", "All is for good. All conjunctions are for subsequent disjunction. I hope I\nshall be perfectly able to work alone. The less help from men, the more from\nthe Lord! Just now I received a letter from an Englishman in London who had\nlived in India in the Himalayas with two of my brethren. He asks me to come\nto London.\n", "Yours,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

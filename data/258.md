["\n", "XXIII\n", "\n", "\nDear Kidi,\n", "\n. . . As to the wonderful stories published about Shri Ramakrishna, I advise\nyou to keep clear of them and the fools who write them. They are true, but\nthe fools will make a mess of the whole thing, I am sure. He had a whole\nworld of knowledge to teach, why insist upon unnecessary things as miracles\nreally are! They do not prove anything. Matter does not prove Spirit. What\nconnection is there between the existence of God, Soul, or immortality, and\nthe working of miracles? . . . Preach Shri Ramakrishna. Pass the Cup that\nhas satisfied your thirst. . . . Preach Bhakti. Do not disturb your head\nwith metaphysical nonsense, and do not disturb others by your bigotry. . .\n.\n", "\n", "Yours ever with blessings,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

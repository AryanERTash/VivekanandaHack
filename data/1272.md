["\n", "THE WOMEN OF INDIA\n", "(New Discoveries, Vol. 2, pp. 411-26.)\n", "The following lecture was delivered at Cambridge, December 17, 1894, and\nrecorded by Miss Frances Willard\u2019s stenographer.\n", "Swami Vivekananda faced bigotry in America on several issues of Indian\nculture \u2014 one was the Indian woman.

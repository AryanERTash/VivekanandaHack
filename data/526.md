BULL,\n", "...I had a newspaper from India with a publication in it of Dr. Barrows'\nshort reply to the thanks sent over from India. Miss Thursby will send it to\nyou. Yesterday I received another letter from India from the President of\nMadras meeting to thank the Americans and to send me an Address.... The first one will be on Monday next, on the\nScience of Religion. The next, on the Rationale of Yoga.... Has Miss Hamlin\nsent you the book on the financial condition of India? I wish your brother\nwill read it and then find out for himself what the English rule in India\nmeans.\n", "Ever gratefully your son,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

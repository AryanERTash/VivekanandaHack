You are correct that I have many other\nthoughts to think besides Indian people, but they have all to go to the\nbackground before the all-absorbing mission \u2014 my Master's work.\n", "I would that this sacrifice were pleasant. Whenever I get a letter from Mrs.\nSevier from her Himalayan home, I feel like flying off the Himalayas. I am\nreally sick of this platform work and eternal trudging and seeing new faces\nand lecturing.\n", "You need not bother about getting up classes in Chicago. I am getting money\nin Frisco and will soon make enough for my passage home.\n", "How are you and the sisters?

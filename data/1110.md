As they do not\nwant it, it seems I ought not to disturb them with my letters. But you\nkindly convey them my love and eternal, undying blessings. So to you, Mother\nand Father Pope. I will pen a longer epistle in a few days. We will see each\nother next spring in Chicago, Mother, if we all live.\n", "Ever gratefully your Son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

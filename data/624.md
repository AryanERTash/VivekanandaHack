["\n", "CLXIII\n", "6 PLACE DES ETATS UNIS, PARIS,\n", "3rd Sept., 1900.\n", "DEAR MOTHER, (Mrs. Francis Leggett.)\n", "We had a congress of cranks here in this house.\n", "The representatives came from various countries, from India in the south, to\nScotland in the north, with England and America buttressing the sides.\n", "We were having great difficulty in electing the president, for though Dr.\nJames  (Professor William James) was there, he was more mindful of the\nblisters raised on him by Mrs. Melton (probably a magnetic healer) than\nsolution of world problems.\n", "I  proposed  Joe (Josephine MacLeod), but she refused on the ground of\nnon-arrival of her new gown \u2014 and went to a corner to watch the scene, from\na coign of vantage.\n", "Mrs. (Ole) Bull was ready, but Margot (Sister Nivedita) objected to this\nmeeting being reduced to a comparative philosophy class.\n", "When we were thus in a fix \u2014 up sprung a short, square, almost round figure\nfrom the corner, and without any ceremony declared that all difficulties\nwill be solved, not only of electing a president but of life itself, if we\nall took to worshipping the Sun God and Moon God. He delivered his speech in\nfive  minutes;  but it took his disciple, who was present, fully three\nquarters of an hour to translate. In the meanwhile, the master began to draw\nthe rugs in your parlour up in a heap, with the intention, as he said, of\ngiving us an ocular demonstration of the power of \"Fire God\", then and\nthere.\n", "At this juncture Joe interposed and insisted that she did not want a fire\nsacrifice in her parlour; whereupon the Indian saint looked daggers at Joe,\nentirely disgusted at the behaviour of one he confidently believed to be a\nperfect convert to fire worship.\n", "Then Dr. James snatched a minute from nursing his blisters and declared that\nhe would have something very interesting to speak upon Fire God and his\nbrethren, if he were not entirely occupied with the evolution of Meltonian\nblisters.  Moreover  his  great  Master,  Herbert  Spencer, not having\ninvestigated the subject before him, he would stick to golden silence.\n", "\"Chutney is the thing\", said a voice near the door. We all looked back and\nsaw Margot. \"It is Chutney,\" she said, \"Chutney and Kali, that will remove\nall difficulties of Life, and make it easy for us to swallow all evils, and\nrelish  what  is good.\" But she stopped all of a sudden and vehemently\nasserted  that she was not going to speak any further, as she has been\nobstructed by a certain male animal in the audience in her speech. She was\nsure one man in the audience had his head turned towards the window and was\nnot paying the attention proper to a lady, and though as to herself she\nbelieved in the equality of the sexes, yet she wanted to know the reason of\nthat  disgusting man's want of due respect for women. Then one and all\ndeclared that they had been giving her the most undivided attention, and all\nabove the equal right, her due, but to no purpose. Margot would have nothing\nto do with that horrible crowd and sat down.\n", "Then Mrs. Bull of Boston took the floor and began to explain how all the\ndifficulties of the world were from not understanding the true relation\nbetween the sexes. She said, \"The only panacea was a right understanding of\nthe proper persons, and then to find liberty in love and freedom in liberty\nand  motherhood, brotherhood, fatherhood, Godhood, love in freedom and\nfreedom in love, in the right holding up of the true ideal in sex.\"\n", "To this the Scotch delegate vehemently objected and said that as the hunter\nchased the goatherd, the goatherd the shepherd, the shepherd the peasant,\nand the peasant drove the fisher into the sea, now we wanted to fish out of\nthe deep the fisher and let him fall upon the peasant, the peasant upon the\nshepherd, and so on; and the web of life will be completed and we will be\nall happy. He was not allowed to continue his driving businesss long. In a\nsecond everyone was on his feet, and we could only hear a confusion of\nvoices \u2014 \"Sun God and Moon God\", \"Chutney and Kali,\" \"Freedom holdings up\nright understanding, sex, motherhood\", \"Never, the fisherman must go back to\nthe shore\", etc. Whereupon Joe declared that she was yearning to be the\nhunter for the time and chase them all out of the house if they did not stop\ntheir nonsense.\n", "Then was peace and calm restored, and I hasten to write you about it.\n", "Yours affly.,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

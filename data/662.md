["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "SATURDAY, July 13th, 1895.\n", "Everything we know is a compound, and all sense-knowledge comes through\nanalysis. To think that mind is a simple, single, or independent is dualism.\nPhilosophy is not got by studying books; the more you read books, the more\nmuddled becomes the mind. The idea of unthinking philosophers was that the\nmind was a simple, and this led them to believe in free-will. Psychology,\nthe  analysis  of the mind, shows the mind to be a compound, and every\ncompound must be held together by some outside force; so the will is bound\nby the combination of outside forces. Man cannot even will to eat unless he\nis hungry. Will is subject to desire. But we are free; everyone feels it.\n", "The agnostic says this idea is a delusion. Then, how do you prove the world?\nIts only proof is that we all see it and feel it; so just as much we all\nfeel freedom. If universal consensus affirms this world, then it must be\naccepted as affirming freedom; but freedom is not of the will as it is. The\nconstitutional belief of man in freedom is the basis of all reasoning.\nFreedom is of the will as it was before it became bound. The very idea of\nfree-will shows every moment man's struggle against bondage. The free can be\nonly one, the Unconditioned, the Infinite, the Unlimited. Freedom in man is\nnow a memory, an attempt towards freedom.\n", "Everything in the universe is struggling to complete a circle, to return to\nits  source,  to return to its only real Source, Atman. The search for\nhappiness is a struggle to find the balance, to restore the equilibrium.\nMorality is the struggle of the bound will to get free and is the proof that\nwe have come from perfection. . . .\n", "The idea of duty is the midday sun of misery scorching the very soul. \"O\nking, drink this one drop of nectar and be happy.\" (\"I am not the doer\",\nthis is the nectar.)\n", "Let there be action without reaction; action is pleasant, all misery is\nreaction. The child puts its hand in the flame, that is pleasure; but when\nits system reacts, then comes the pain of burning. When we can stop that\nreaction, then we have nothing to fear. Control the brain and do not let it\nread the record; be the witness and do not react, only thus can you be\nhappy.  The  happiest moments we ever know are when we entirely forget\nourselves. Work of your own free will, not from duty. We have no duty. This\nworld is just a gymnasium in which we play; our life is an eternal holiday.\n", "The whole secret of existence is to have no fear. Never fear what will\nbecome of you, depend on no one. Only the moment you reject all help are you\nfree. The full sponge can absorb no more.\n", "*    *    *\n", "Even fighting in self-defence is wrong, though it is higher than fighting in\naggression. There is no \"righteous\" indignation, because indignation comes\nfrom not recognising sameness in all things.\n"]

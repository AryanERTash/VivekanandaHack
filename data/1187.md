["\n", "CXLIII\n", "To Mrs. Ole Bull\n", "THE LYMES, WOODSIDE\n", "WIMBLEDON, ENGLAND\n", "6 August 1899\n", "MY DEAR MOTHER,\n", "Your letter directed to Sturdy at hand. I am very thankful for your kind\nwords. As for me, I don't know what I am to do next or anything to do at\nall. On board the steamer I was all right, but since landing [I am] feeling\nquite bad again. As to mental worry, there has been enough of late. The aunt\nwhom  you saw had a deep-laid plan to cheat me, and she and her people\ncontrived to sell me a house for 6,000 Rs., or \u00a3400, and I bought [it] for\nmy mother in good faith. Then they would not give me possession, hoping that\nI would not go to court for the shame of taking forcible possession as a\nSannyasin.\n", "I do not think I have spent even one rupee from what you and others gave me\nfor the work. Cap. Sevier gave me 8,000 Rs. with the express desire of\nhelping my mother. This money, it seems, has [also] gone to the dogs. Beyond\nthis,  nothing  has been spent on my family or even on my own personal\nexpenses \u2014 my food etc. being paid for by the Khetri Raja, and more than\nhalf of that went to the Math every month. Only, if Brahmananda spends some\nin the lawsuit [against the aunt], as I must not be robbed that way \u2014 if he\ndoes, I will make it good anyway, if I live to do it.\n", "The money which I got in Europe and America by lecturing alone, I spent just\nas I like; but every cent I got for the work has been accounted for and is\nin  the  Math,  and  the  whole thing ought to be clear as daylight if\nBrahmananda never cheated me. I don't believe he will ever cheat me. I got a\nletter at Aden from Saradananda that they were preparing an account. I have\nnot received any yet.\n", "I have no plans yet, nor care to make any. Neither do I wish to work. Let\nthe Mother find other workers. I have my burden enough already.\n", "Ever your devoted son,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

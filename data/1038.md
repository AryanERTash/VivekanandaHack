The session of the Congress of\nHistory of Religions is over. It was not a big affair; some twenty scholars\nchattered a lot on the origin of the Sh\u00e2lagr\u00e2ma and the origin of Jehovah,\nand similar topics. I also said something on the occasion.\n", "My body and mind are broken down; I need rest badly. In addition, there is\nnot a single person on whom I can depend; on the other hand so long as I\nlive, all will become very selfish depending upon me for everything. . . .\nDealing with people entails constant mental uneasiness. . . . I have cut\nmyself off by a will. Now I am writing to say that nobody will have sole\npower. All will be done in accordance with the view of the majority. . . .\nIf a trust-deed on similar lines can be executed, then I am free. . . .\nWhat you are doing is also Guru Maharaj's work. Continue to do it. Now I\nhave done my part. Don't write to me any more about those things; do not\neven  mention the subject. I have no opinions whatever to give on that\nsubject. . . .\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Convey my love to all.\n"]

["\n", "XIII\n", "541 DEARBORN AVENUE, CHICAGO,\n19th November, 1893.\n", "DEAR MRS. WOODS,\n", "\nExcuse my delay in answering your letter. I do not know when I will be able\nto see you again. I am starting tomorrow for Madison and Minneapolis.\n", "The  English  gentleman you speak of is Dr. Momerie of London. He is a\nwell-known worker amongst the poor of London and is a very sweet man. You\nperhaps  do  not  know  that the English church was the only religious\ndenomination in the world who did not send to us a representative, and Dr.\nMomerie came to the Parliament in spite of the Archbishop of Canterbury's\ndenouncing of the Parliament of Religions.\n", "My love for you, my kind friend, and your noble son is all the same whether\nI write pretty often or not.\n", "Can you express my books and the cover-all to the care of Mr. Hale? I am in\nneed of them. The express will be paid here.\n", "The blessings of the Lord on you and yours.\n", "Ever your friend,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. If you have the occasion to write to Miss Sanborn and others of our\nfriends in the east, kindly give them my deepest respects.\n", "Yours truly,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

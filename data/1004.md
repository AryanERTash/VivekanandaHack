["\n", "CLVII\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "1251 PINE STREET,\n", "SAN FRANCISCO,\n", "2nd March, 1900.\n", "DEAR MARY,\n", "Very kind of you to write to invite me to Chicago. I wish I could be there\nthis minute. But I am busy making money; only I do not make much. Well, I\nhave to make enough to pay my passage home at any rate. Here is a new field,\nwhere I find ready listeners by hundreds, prepared beforehand by my books.\n", "Of course money making is slow and tedious. If I could make a few hundreds,\nI would be only too glad. By this time you must have received my previous\nnote. I am coming eastward in a month or six weeks, I hope.\n", "How are you all? Give Mother my heartfelt love. I wish I had her strength,\nshe is a true Christian. My health is much better, but the old strength is\nnot there yet. I hope it will come some day, but then, one had to work so\nhard to do the least little thing. I wish I had rest and peace for a few\ndays at least, which I am sure I can get with the sisters at Chicago. Well,\nMother knows best, as I say always. She knows best. The last two years have\nbeen specially bad. I have been living in mental hell. It is partially\nlifted now, and I hope for better days, better states. All blessings on you\nand the sisters and Mother. Mary, you have been always the sweetest notes in\nmy jarring and clashing life. Then you had the great good Karma to start\nwithout oppressive surroundings. I never know a moment's peaceful life. It\nhas always been high pressure, mentally. Lord bless you.\n", "Ever your loving brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

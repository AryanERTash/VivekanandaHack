["\n", "CLII\n", "To Mrs. Ole Bull\n", "921 W. 21ST STREET,\n", "LOS ANGELES,\n", "27th December, 1899.\n", "BELOVED DHIRA MATA,\n", "An eventful and happy New Year to you and many such returns!\n", "I am much better in health \u2014 able enough to work once more. I have started\nwork already and have sent to Saradananda some money \u2014 Rs. 1,300 already \u2014\nas expenses for the law suit. I shall send more, if they need it. I had a\nvery bad dream this morning and had not any news of Saradananda for three\nweeks. Poor boys! How hard I am on them at times. Well, they know, in spite\nof all that, I am their best friend.\n", "Mr. Leggett has got a little over \u00a3500 I had with Sturdy on account of\nRaja-Yoga and the Maharaja of Khetri. I have now about a thousand dollars\nwith Mr. Leggett. If I die, kindly send that money to my mother. I wired to\nthe boys three weeks ago that I was perfectly cured. If I don't get any\nworse, this much health as I have now will do well enough. Do not worry at\nall on my account; I am up and working with a will.\n", "I am sorry I could not write any more of the stories. I have written some\nother things and mean to write something almost every day.\n", "I am very much more peaceful and find that the only way to keep my peace is\nto teach others. Work is my only safety valve.\n", "I only want some clear business head to take care of the details as I push\nonwards and work on. I am afraid it will be a long time to find such in\nIndia, and if there are any, they ought to be educated by somebody from the\nWest.\n", "Again, I can only work when thrown completely on my own feet. I am at my\nbest when I am alone. Mother seems to arrange so. Joe believes great things\nare brewing \u2014 in Mother's cup; hope it is so.\n", "Joe and Margot have developed into actual prophets, it seems. I can only\nsay, every blow I had in this life, every pang, will only become joyful\nsacrifice if Mother becomes propitious to India once more.\n", "Miss Greenstidel writes a beautiful letter to me, about you most of it. She\nthinks a lot about Turiyananda too. Give Turiyananda my love. I am sure he\nwill work well. He has the pluck and stamina.\n", "I  am  going soon to work in California; when I leave I shall send for\nTuriyananda and make him work on the Pacific coast. I am sure here is a\ngreat field. The Raja-Yoga book seems to be very well known here. Miss\nGreenstidel had found great peace under your roof and is very happy. I am so\nglad it is so. May things go a little better with her every day. She has a\ngood business head and practical sense.\n", "Joe has unearthed a magnetic healing woman. We are both under her treatment.\nJoe  thinks  she is pulling me up splendidly. On her has been worked a\nmiracle, she claims. Whether it is magnetic healing, California ozone, or\nthe end of the present spell of bad Karma, I am improving. It is a great\nthing to be able to walk three miles, even after a heavy dinner.\n", "All  love and blessings to Olea. My love to Dr. Janes and other Boston\nfriends.\n", "Ever your son,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "CVI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "LAKE LUCERNE, SWITZERLAND,\n", "23rd August, 1896.\n", "MY DEAR SHASHI,\n", "Today I received a letter from Ramdayal Babu, in which he writes that many\npublic women attend the Ramakrishna anniversary festival at Dakshineswar,\nwhich makes many less inclined to go there. Moreover, in his opinion one day\nshould be appointed for men and another for women. My decision on the point\nis this:\n", "1. If public women are not allowed to go to such a great place of pilgrimage\nas Dakshineswar, where else shall they go to? It is for the sinful that the\nLord manifests Himself specially, not so much for the virtuous.\n", "2. Let distinctions of sex, caste, wealth, learning, and the whole host of\nthem, which are so many gateways to hell, be confined to the world alone. If\nsuch distinctions persist in holy places of pilgrimage, where then lies the\ndifference between them and hell itself?\n", "3. Ours is a gigantic City of Jagann\u00e2tha, where those who have sinned and\nthose who have not, the saintly and the vicious, men and women and children\nirrespective of age, all have equal right. That for one day at least in the\nyear thousand of men and women get rid of the sense of sin and ideas of\ndistinction and sing and hear the name of the Lord, is in itself a supreme\ngood.\n", "4. If even in a place of pilgrimage people's tendency to evil be not curbed\nfor one day, the fault lies with you, not them. Create such a huge tidal\nwave of spirituality that whatever people come near will be swept away.\n", "5. Those who, even in a chapel, would think this is a public woman, that man\nis of a low caste, a third is poor, and yet another belongs to the masses\n\u2014 the less be the number of such people (that is, whom you call gentlemen)\nthe better. Will they who look to the caste, sex, or profession of Bhaktas\nappreciate our Lord? I pray to the Lord that hundreds of public women may\ncome  and  bow  their heads at His feet; it does not matter if not one\ngentleman comes. Come public women, come drunkards, come thieves and all \u2014\nHis Gate is open to all. \"It is easier for a camel to pass through the eye\nof a needle than for a rich man to enter the Kingdom of God.\" Never let such\ncruel, demoniacal ideas have a place in your mind.\n", "6. But then some social vigilance is needed. How are we to do that? A few\nmen (old men, preferably) should take charge as the warders for the day.\nThey will make circuits round the scene of the festival, and in case they\nfind any man or woman showing impropriety of speech or conduct, they will at\nonce expel them from the garden. But so long as they behave like good men\nand women, they are Bhaktas and are to be respected \u2014 be they men or women,\nhonest citizens or unchaste.\n", "I am at present travelling in Switzerland, and shall soon go to Germany, to\nsee Professor Deussen. I shall return to England from there about the 23rd\nor 24th September, and the next winter will find me back in my country.\n", "My love to you and all.\n", "Yours etc.,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "XCVI\n", "\n", "\nMy Dear Mother,[1] \n", "\nMany thanks for your very encouraging words. I needed them very much just\nnow. The gloom has not lifted with the advent of the new century, it is\nvisibly thickening. I went to see Mrs. Sevier at Mayavati. On my way I\nlearnt  of  the  sudden death of the Raja of Khetri. It appears he was\nrestoring some old architectural monument at Agra, at his own expense, and\nwas up some tower on inspection. Part of the tower came down, and he was\ninstantly killed.\n", "\nThe three cheques have arrived. They will reach my cousin when next I see\nher.\n", "\nJoe is here, but I have not seen her yet.\n", "\nThe moment I touch Bengal, especially the Math, the asthmatic fits return!\nThe moment I leave, I recover!\n", "\nI am going to take my mother on pilgrimage next week. It may take months to\nmake the complete round of pilgrimages. This is the one great wish of a\nHindu widow. I have brought only misery to my people all my life. I am\ntrying at least to fulfil this one wish of hers.\n", "\nI am so glad to learn all that about Margot; everybody here is eager to\nwelcome her back.\n", "\nI hope Dr. Bose has completely recovered by this time.\n", "\nI had a beautiful letter also from Mrs. Hammond. She is a great soul.\n", "\nHowever, I am very calm and self-possessed this time and find everything\nbetter than I ever expected.\n", "With all love,\n", "Ever your son,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

I have not done much work, but my heart is growing stronger\nevery day, physically and mentally. Some days I feel I can bear everything\nand suffer everything. There was nothing of note inside the bundle of papers\nsent by Miss M\u00fcller. I did not write her, not knowing her address. Then\nagain, I am afraid.\n", "I can always work better alone, and am physically and mentally best when\nentirely alone! I scarcely had a day's illness during my eight years of lone\nlife away from my brethren. Now I am again getting up, being alone. Strange,\nbut  that  is  what  Mother  wants me to be. \"Wandering alone like the\nrhinoceros\",  as Joe likes it. I think the conferences are ended. Poor\nTuriyananda suffered so much and never let me know; he is so strong and\ngood.  Poor Niranjan, I learn from Mrs. Sevier, is so seriously ill in\nCalcutta that I don't know whether he has passed away or not. Well, good and\nevil both love company; queer, they come in strings. I had a letter from my\ncousin  telling  me  her daughter (the adopted little child) was dead.\nSuffering seems to be the lot of India! Good. I am getting rather callous,\nrather stilted, of late. Good. Mother knows. I am so ashamed of myself \u2014 of\nthis display of weakness for the last two years! Glad it is ended.\n", "Ever your loving son,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

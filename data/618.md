["\n", "CLVII\n", "\n", "1719 TURK STREET,\n", "SAN FRANCISCO,\n", "28th March, 1900.\n", "MY DEAR MARGOT, (Margaret E. Noble or Sister Nivedita)\n", "I am so glad at your good fortune. Things have got to come round if we are\nsteady. I am sure you will get all the money you require here or in England.\n", "I am working hard; and the harder I work, the better I feel. This ill health\nhas  done  me  a  great  good,  sure.  I  am really understanding what\nnon-attachment means. And I hope very soon to be perfectly non-attached.\n", "We put all our energies to concentrate and get attached to one thing; but\nthe other part, though equally difficult, we seldom pay any attention to \u2014\nthe faculty of detaching ourselves at a moment's notice from anything.\n", "Both attachment and detachment perfectly developed make a man great and\nhappy.\n", "I am so glad at Mrs. Leggett's gift of $1,000. She is working up, wait. She\nhas a great part to play in Ramakrishna's work, whether she knows it or not.\n", "I enjoyed your accounts of Prof. Geddes, and Joe has a funny account of a\nclairvoyant. Things are just now beginning to turn. . . .\n", "This letter, I think, Will reach you at Chicago. . . .\n", "I had a nice letter from Max Gysic, the young Swiss who is a great friend of\nMiss Souter. Miss Souter also sends her love, and they ask to know the time\nwhen I come over to England. Many people are inquiring, they say.\n", "Things have got to come round \u2014 the seed must die underground to come up as\nthe tree. The last two years were the underground rotting. I never had a\nstruggle in the jaws of death, but it meant a tremendous upheaval of the\nwhole life. One such brought me to Ramakrishna, another sent me to the U.S.,\nthis  has  been the greatest of all. It is gone \u2014 I am so calm that it\nastonishes me sometimes!! I work every day morning and evening, eat anything\nany hour \u2014 and go to bed at 12 p.m. in the night \u2014 but such fine sleep!! I\nnever had such power of sleeping before!\n", "Yours with all love and blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

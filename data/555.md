["\n", "XCIV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "\n", "BOSTON,\n", "2nd March, 1896.\n", "DEAR SARADA,\n", "Your letter informed me of everything; but I note that you do not so much as\nrefer to the cable I sent about the celebration. The dictionary that Shashi\nsent a few months ago has not arrived so far. ... I am going to England\nsoon. Sharat need not come now at all; for I am myself going to England. I\ndo not want people who take such a long time to make up their minds. I did\nnot invite him for a European tour, and I do not have the money either. So\nask him not to come, and none else need.\n", "On perusal of your letter on Tibet, I came to lose all regard for your\ncommon sense. In the first place, it is nonsense to say that Notovitch's\nbook  is genuine. Did you see any original copy, or bring it to India?\nSecondly, you say you saw in the Kailas Math the portrait of Jesus and the\nSamaritan Woman. How do you know that it was Jesus' portrait, and not that\nof a man in the street? Even taking it for granted, how do you know that it\nwas not put up in the said Math by someone who was a Christian? And your\nopinions on the Tibetans too are unsound; you did not certainly see the\nheart of Tibet, but only a fringe of the trade route. In places like those\nonly the dregs of a nation are to be met. If on seeing the Chinabazar and\nBarabazar quarters of Calcutta, anybody called every Bengali a liar, would\nthat be correct?\n", "Consult Shashi properly when writing any article. ... What you need is only\nobedience. ...\n", "Yours affectionately\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

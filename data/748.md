["\n", "III\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "GHAZIPUR,\n", "30th January, 1890.\n", "REVERED SIR (Shri Balaram Bose),\n", "I am now stopping with Satish Babu at Ghazipur. Of the few places I have\nrecently visited, this is the healthiest. The water of Baidyanath is very\nbad \u2014 it leads to indigestion. Allahabad is very congested. The few days I\npassed at Varanasi, I suffered from fever day and night \u2014 the place is so\nmalarious! Ghazipur has a very salubrious climate \u2014 specially the quarter I\nam living in. I have visited Pavhari Baba's house \u2014 there are high walls all\nround, and it is fashioned like an English bungalow. There is a garden\ninside and big rooms and chimneys, etc. He allows nobody to enter. If he is\nso inclined, he comes up to the door and speaks from inside \u2014 that is all.\nOne day I went and waited and waited in the cold and had to return. I shall\ngo to Varanasi on Sunday next. If the meeting with the Babaji takes place in\nthe meantime, all right, otherwise I bid him good-bye. About Pramada Babu's\nplace  I shall write definitely from Varanasi. If Kali Bhattacharya is\ndetermined to come, let him do so after I leave for Varanasi on Sunday, but\nhe should rather not. After a few days' stay at Varanasi, I shall start for\nHrishikesh. Pramada Babu may accompany me. Please accept all of you my\ncordial greetings \u2014 and blessing to Fakir, Ram, Krishnamayi, etc.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. In my opinion, it will do you much good if you come and stay for some\ntime at Ghazipur. Here Satish will be able to secure a bungalow for you, and\nthere is a gentleman, Gagan Chandra Ray by name, who is the head of the\nOpium Office and is exceedingly courteous, philanthropic, and social \u2014 they\nwill arrange for everything. The house-rent is fifteen to twenty rupees;\nrice is dear, and milk sells at sixteen to twenty seers a rupee; all other\nthings are very cheap. Besides, under the care of these gentlemen, there is\nno chance of any difficulty. But it is slightly expensive \u2014 it will cost\nover forty to fifty rupees. Varanasi is horribly malarious. I have never\nlived in Pramada Babu's garden. He likes to have me always in his company.\nThe garden is indeed very beautiful, richly laid out, spacious, and open.\nThis time when I go, I shall live there and report to you.\n"]

["\n", "XL\n", "To Mr. E. T. Sturdy\n", "54 W. 33RD STREET,\n", "NEW YORK,\n", "24th April, 1895.\n", "DEAR FRIEND,\n", "I am perfectly aware that although some truth underlies the mass of mystical\nthought which has burst upon the Western world of late, it is for the most\npart full of motives, unworthy, or insane. For this reason, I have never had\nanything to do with these phases of religion, either in India or elsewhere,\nand mystics as a class are not very favourable to me. . . .\n", "I quite agree with you that only the Advaita philosophy can save mankind,\nwhether in East or West, from \"devil worship\" and kindred superstitions,\ngiving tone and strength to the very nature of man. India herself requires\nthis, quite as much or even more than the West. Yet it is hard uphill work,\nfor we have first to create a taste, then teach, and lastly proceed to build\nup the whole fabric.\n", "Perfect sincerity, holiness, gigantic intellect, and an all-conquering will.\nLet only a handful of men work with these, and the whole world will be\nrevolutionised. I did a good deal of platform work in this country last\nyear, and received plenty of applause, but found that I was only working for\nmyself. It is the patient upbuilding of character, the intense struggle to\nrealise the truth, which alone will tell in the future of humanity. So this\nyear I am hoping to work along this line \u2014 training up to practical Advaita\nrealisation a small band of men and women. I do not know how far I shall\nsucceed. The West is the field for work if a man wants to benefit humanity,\nrather than his own particular sect or country. I agree perfectly as to your\nidea of a magazine. But I have no business capacity at all to do these\nthings. I can teach and preach, and sometimes write. But I have intense\nfaith in Truth. The Lord will send help and hands to work with me. Only let\nme be perfectly pure, perfectly sincere, and perfectly unselfish.\n", "\"Truth alone triumphs, not untruth; through truth alone stretches the way to\nthe Lord\" (Atharva-Veda). He who gives up the little self for the world will\nfind the whole universe his. . . . I am very uncertain about coming to\nEngland. I know no one there, and here I am doing some work. The Lord will\nguide, in His own time.\n"]

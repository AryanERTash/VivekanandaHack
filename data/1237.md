Of course, it was only her love and\nanxiety to see you \u2014 that was all; but poor Yum has been much too serious\nfor once, and hence all these letters. However, I am glad it should happen,\nas I learnt so much about your work from Mr. Dutt, who can't be accused of a\nrelative's blind love.\n", "I have written to Mrs. Bull already about this matter. I am now at last in\nDacca and had some lectures here. I depart for Chandranath tomorrow, near\nChittagong, the farthest eastern extremity of Bengal. My Mother, aunt,\ncousin, another cousin's widow, and nine boys are with me. They all send you\nlove.\n", "I had just now a few lines from Mrs. Bull, also a letter from Mr. Sturdy. As\nit would be almost impossible for me to write for some days now, I ask you\nto thank Mrs. Bull for me for her letter, and tell her kindly that I have\njust now a long letter from Miss [Christina] Greenstidel of Detroit. She\nmentions  a  beautiful  letter from Mrs. Bull. Sturdy writes about the\npublication of any further edition of Raja-Yoga by Longmans. I leave that\nconsideration with Mrs. Bull. She may talk over the matter with Sturdy and\ndo what she thinks proper.\n", "Please give Sturdy my best love, and tell him I am on the march and will\ntake time to reply to his letter; in the meanwhile the business will be\nlooked after by Mrs. Bull.\n", "With everlasting love and blessings,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

["\n", "CXXVIII\n", "Maharaja Ajit Singh, the Raja of Khetri\n", "MATH BELUR\n", "22 November 1898\n", "YOUR HIGHNESS \u2014\n", "Many thanks for your kind note and the Nimbarka Bhashya \u2014 reached through\nJaga Mohan Lalji.\n", "I approach your Highness today on a most important business of mine, knowing\nwell that I have not the least shame in opening my mind to you, and that I\nconsider you as my only friend in this life. If the following appeals to\nyou, good; if not, pardon my foolishness as a friend should.\n", "As you know already, I have been ailing since my return. In Calcutta your\nHighness  assured me of your friendship and help for me personally and\n[advised me] not to be worried about this incurable malady. This disease has\nbeen caused by nervous excitement; and no amount of change can do me good,\nunless the worry and anxiety and excitement are taken off me.\n", "After trying these two years a different climate, I am getting worse every\nday  and now almost at death's door. I appeal to your Highness's work,\ngenerosity  and friendship. I have one great sin rankling always in my\nbreast, and that is [in order] to do a service to the world, I have sadly\nneglected my mother. Again, since my second brother has gone away, she has\nbecome awfully worn-out with grief. Now my last desire is to make Sev\u00e2 [give\nservice] and serve my mother, for some years at least. I want to live with\nmy mother and get my younger brother married to prevent extinction of the\nfamily. This will certainly smoothen my last days as well as those of my\nmother. She lives now in a hovel. I want to build a little, decent home for\nher and make some provision for the youngest, as there is very little hope\nof his being a good earning man. Is it too much for a royal descendent of\nRamchandra to do for one he loves and calls his friend? I do not know whom\nelse to appeal to. The money I got from Europe was for the \"work\", and every\npenny almost has been given over to that work. Nor can I beg of others for\nhelp for my own self. About my own family affairs \u2014 I have exposed myself to\nyour Highness, and none else shall know of it. I am tired, heartsick and\ndying. Do, I pray, this last great work of kindness to me, befitting your\ngreat and generous nature and [as] a crest to the numerous kindnesses you\nhave shown me. And as your Highness will make my last days smooth and easy,\nmay He whom I have tried to serve all my life ever shower His choicest\nblessings on you and yours.\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n", "P.S. This is strictly private. Will you please drop a wire to me whether you\nwill do it or not?\n", "Ever yours,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

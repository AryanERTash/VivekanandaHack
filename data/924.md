["\n", "LXXVII\n", "To the Hale sisters\n", "LONDON,\n", "7th July, 1896.\n", "DEAR BABIES,\n", "The work here progressed wonderfully. I had one monk here from India. I have\nsent  him to the U.S.A. and sent for another from India. The season is\nclosed; the classes, therefore, and the Sunday lectures are to be closed on\nthe 16th next. And on the 19th I go for a month or so for quiet and rest in\nthe Swiss Mountains to return next autumn to London and begin again. The\nwork here has been very satisfactory. By rousing interest here I really do\nmore for India than in India. Mother wrote to me that if you could rent your\nflat, she would be glad to take you with her to see Egypt. I am going with\nthree English friends to the Swiss Hills. Later on, towards the end of\nwinter, I expect to go to India with some English friends who are going to\nlive in my monastery there, which, by the by, is in the air yet. It is\nstruggling to materialise somewhere in the Himalayas.\n", "Where are You? Now the summer is in full swing, even London is getting very\nhot. Kindly give my best love to Mrs. Adams, Mrs. Conger, and all the rest\nof my friends in Chicago.\n", "Your affectionate brother,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

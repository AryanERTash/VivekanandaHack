["\n", "LVI[6]*\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "\n(Summer?) 1894.\n", "DEAR AND BELOVED, (The brother-disciples at Alambazar monastery.)\n", "Your letter gives me all the news over there. I am grieved to hear of the\nbereavement Balaram Babu's wife has sustained. Such is the Lord's will. This\nis a place for action, not enjoyment, and everyone will go home when his\ntask is done \u2014 some earlier, and some later, that is all. Fakir has gone \u2014\nwell, such is the will of the Lord!\n", "It is a welcome news that Shri Ramakrishna's festival has come off with\ngreat \u00e9clat; the more his name is spread, the better it is. But there is one\nthing to know: Great sages come with special messages for the world, and not\nfor name; but their followers throw their teachings overboard and fight over\ntheir names \u2014 this is verily the history of the world. I do not take into\nany consideration whether people accept his name or not, but I am ready to\nlay down my life to help his teachings, his life, and his message spread all\nover the world. What I am most afraid of is the worship-room. It is not bad\nin itself, but there is a tendency in some to make this all in all and set\nup that old-fashioned nonsense over again \u2014 this is what makes me nervous. I\nknow why they busy themselves with those old, effete ceremonials. Their\nspirit craves for work, but having no outlet they waste their energy in\nringing bells and all that.\n", "I am giving you a new idea. If you can work it out, then I shall know you\nare men and will be of service. . . . Make an organised plan. A few cameras,\nsome maps, globes, and some chemicals, etc., are needed. The next thing you\nwant is a big hut. Then you must get together a number of poor, indigent\nfolk. Having done all this, show them pictures to teach them astronomy,\ngeography, etc., and preach Shri Ramakrishna to them. Try to have their eyes\nopened as to what has taken place or is taking place in different countries,\nwhat  this  world is like and, so forth. You have got lots of poor and\nignorant  folk  there. Go to their cottages, from door to door, in the\nevening, at noon, any time and open their eyes. Books etc., won't do \u2014 give\nthem oral teaching. Then slowly extend your centres. Can you do all this? Or\nonly bell-ringing?\n", "I have heard everything about Brother Tarak from Madras. They are highly\npleased with him. Dear Brother Tarak, if you go to Madras and live there for\nsome time, a lot of work will be done. But before you go, start this work\nthere first. Can't the lady devotees convert some widows; into disciples?\nAnd can't you put a bit of learning into their heads? And can't you then\nsend them out to preach Sari Ramakrishna from door to door, and impart\neducation along with it? . . .\n", "Come!  Apply  yourselves  heart  and soul to it. The day of gossip and\nceremonials is gone, my boy, you must work now. Now, let me see how far a\nBengali's religion will go. Niranjan writes that Latu (Adbhutananda) wants\nsome warm clothing. The people here import winter clothing from Europe and\nIndia. You will get a woollen wrap in Calcutta at one-fourth of the price at\nwhich I might buy it here. . . . I don't know when I shall go to Europe,\neverything is uncertain with me \u2014 I am getting on somehow in this country,\nthat is all.\n", "This is a very funny country. It is now summer; this morning it was as hot\nas April in Bengal, but now it is as cold as February at Allahabad! So much\nfluctuation  within  four  hours!  The  hotels  of this country beggar\ndescription. For instance there is a hotel in New York where a room can be\nhired for up to Rs. 5,000 a day, excluding boarding charges. Not even in\nEurope is there a country like this in point of luxury. It is indeed the\nrichest country in the world, where money is drained off like water. I\nseldom live in hotels, but am mostly the guest of big people here. To them I\nam a widely known man. The whole country knows me now; so wherever I go they\nreceive me with open arms into their homes. Mr. Hale's home is my centre in\nChicago.  I call his wife mother, and his daughters call me brother. I\nscarcely find a family so highly pure and kind. Or why should God shower His\nblessings on them in such abundance, my brother? Oh, how wonderfully kind\nthey are! If they chance to learn that a poor man is in a strait at such and\nsuch a place, there they will go ladies and gentlemen, to give him food and\nclothing and find him some job! And what do we do!\n", "In summer they leave their homes to go to foreign lands, or to the seaside.\nI, too, shall go somewhere, but have not yet fixed a place. In other points,\nthey are just as you see Englishmen. They have got books and things of that\nsort, but very dear. You can have five times those things In Calcutta for\nthe same price. In other words, these people will not let foreign goods be\nimported into the country. They set a heavy tax on them, and as a result,\nthe market goes up enormously. Besides, they are not much in the way of\nmanufacturing clothing etc. They construct tools and machinery, and grow\nwheat, rice, cotton, etc., which are fairly cheap.\n", "By the bye, nowadays we have plenty of Hils\u00e2 fish here. Eat your fill, but\neverything digests. There are many kinds of fruits; plantain, lemon, guava,\napple, almond, raisin, and grape are in abundance; besides many other fruits\ncome  from California. There are plenty of pineapples but there are no\nmangoes or lichis, or things of that sort.\n", "There is a kind of spinach, which, when cooked, tastes just like our Not\u00e9 of\nBengal, and another class, which they call asparagus, tastes exactly like\nthe tender Dengo herb, but you can't have our Charchari made of it here.\nThere is no Kal\u00e2i or any other pulse; they do not even know of them. There\nis  rice,  and  bread, and numerous varieties of fish and meat, of all\ndescriptions. Their menu is like that of the French. There is your milk,\nrarely curd, but plenty of whey. Cream is an article of everyday use. In tea\nand coffee and everything there is that cream \u2014 not the hardened crust of\nboiled milk, mind you \u2014 and there is your butter, too, and ice-water \u2014 no\nmatter whether it is summer or winter, day or night, whether you have got a\nbad cold or fever \u2014 you have ice-water in abundance. These are scientific\npeople and laugh when they are told that ice-water aggravates cold. The more\nyou take, the better. And there is plenty of ice-cream, of all sorts of\nshapes. I have seen the Niagara Falls seven or eight times, the Lord be\npraised! Very grand no doubt, but not quite as you have heard them spoken\nof. One day, in winter, we had the aurora borealis.\n", ". . . Only childish prattle! I have not much time to listen to that sort of\nthing in this life; it will be time enough to see if I can do that in the\nnext. Yogen has completely rallied by this time, I hope? The vagabond spirit\nof Sarada (Trigun\u00e2tita) is not yet at an end, I see. What is wanted is a\npower of organisation \u2014 do you understand me? Have any of you got that much\nbrain in your head? If you do, let your mind work. Brother Tarak, Sharat,\nand Hari will be able to do it.  \u2014 has got very little originality, but is a\nvery good workman and persevering \u2014 which is an essential necessity, and\nShashi  (Ramakrishnananda)  is executive to a degree. ... We want some\ndisciples \u2014 fiery young men \u2014 do you see? \u2014 intelligent and brave, who dare\nto go to the jaws of Death, and are ready to swim the ocean across. Do you\nfollow me? We want hundreds like that, both men and women. Try your utmost\nfor that end alone. Make converts right and left, and put them into our\npurity-drilling machine.\n", ". . . What made you communicate to the Indian Mirror that Paramahamsa Deva\nused to call Narendra such and such, and all sorts of nonsense? \u2014 As if he\nhad  nothing else to do but that! Only thought-reading and nonsensical\nmystery-mongering! . . . It is excellent that Sanyal is visiting you often.\nDo you write letters to Gupta? Convey to him my love, and take kind care of\nhim. Everything will come right by degrees. I don't find much time to write\nheaps  of  letters. As for lectures and so forth, I don't prepare them\nbeforehand. Only one I wrote out, which you have printed. The rest I deliver\noff-hand, whatever comes to my lips \u2014 Gurudeva backs me up. I have nothing\nto do with pen and paper. Once at Detroit I held forth for three hours at a\nstretch. Sometimes I myself wonder at my own achievement \u2014 to think that\nthere was such stuff in this pate! They ask me here to write a book. Well, I\nthink I must do something that way, this time. But that's the botheration;\nwho will take the trouble of putting things in black and white and all that!\n. . . We must electrify society, electrify the world. Idle gossip and barren\nceremonials won't do. Ceremonials are meant for householders, your work is\nthe distribution and propagation of thought-currents. If you can do that,\nthen it is all right. . . .\n", "Let character be formed and then I shall be in your midst. Do you see? We\nwant two thousand Sannyasins, nay ten, or even twenty thousand \u2014 men and\nwomen, both. What are our matrons doing? We want converts at any risk. Go\nand  tell  them,  and  try yourselves, heart and soul. Not householder\ndisciples, mind you, we want Sannyasins. Let each one of you have a hundred\nheads tonsured \u2014 young educated men, not fools. Then you are heroes. We must\nmake a sensation. Give up your passive attitude, gird your loins and stand\nup. Let me see you make some electric circuits between Calcutta and Madras.\nStart centres at places, go on always making converts. Convert everyone into\nthe monastic order whoever seeks for it, irrespective of sex, and then I\nshall be in your midst. A huge spiritual tidal wave is coming \u2014 he who is\nlow shall become noble, and he who is ignorant shall become the teacher of\ngreat scholars \u2014 through HIS grace. \" [056_dear_and_beloved_01.jpg] \u2014 Arise!\nAwake! and stop not till the goal is reached.\" Life is ever expanding,\ncontraction is death. The self-seeking man who is looking after his personal\ncomforts and leading a lazy life \u2014 there is no room for him even in hell. He\nalone is a child of Shri Ramakrishna who is moved to pity for all creatures\nand  exerts  himself  for  them even at the risk of incurring personal\ndamnation,   [056_dear_and_beloved_02.jpg] \u2014 others are vulgar people.\nWhoever, at this great spiritual juncture, will stand up with a courageous\nheart and go on spreading from door to door, from village to village, his\nmessage, is alone my brother, and a son of his. This is the test, he who is\nRamakrishna's   child   does   not   seek   his   personal   good.   \"\nthe point of death.\" Those that care for their personal comforts and seek a\nlazy life, who are ready to sacrifice all before their personal whims, are\nnone  of us; let them pack off, while yet there is time. Propagate his\ncharacter, his teaching, his religion. This is the only spiritual practice,\nthe only worship, this verily is the means, and this the goal. Arise! Arise!\nA tidal wave is coming! Onward! Men and women, down to the Chand\u00e2la (Pariah)\n\u2014 all are pure in his eyes. Onward! Onward! There is no time to care for\nname, or fame, or Mukti, or Bhakti! We shall look to these some other time.\nNow in this life let us infinitely spread his lofty character, his sublime\nlife, his infinite soul. This is the only work \u2014 there is nothing else to\ndo. Wherever his name will reach, the veriest worm will attain divinity,\nnay, is actually attaining it; you have got eyes, and don't you see it? Is\nit   a   child's   play?  Is  it  silly  prattle?  Is  it  foolery?  \"\nback. I cannot write any more. \u2014 Onward! I only tell you this, that whoever\nreads this letter will imbibe my spirit! Have faith! Onward! Great Lord! . .\n. I feel as if somebody is moving my hand to write in this way. Onward!\nGreat Lord! Everyone will be swept away! Take care, he is coming! Whoever\nwill be ready to serve him \u2014 no, not him but his children \u2014 the poor and the\ndowntrodden, the sinful and the afflicted, down to the very worm \u2014 who will\nbe ready to serve these, in them he will manifest himself. Through their\ntongue the Goddess of Learning Herself will speak, and the Divine Mother \u2014\nthe Embodiment of all Power \u2014 will enthrone Herself in their hearts. Those\nthat are atheists, unbelievers, worthless, and foppish, why do they call\nthemselves as belonging to his fold. . . .\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. . . . The term organisation means division of labour. Each does his own\npart, and all the parts taken together express an ideal of harmony. . . .\n"]

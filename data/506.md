["XLV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\nSalutations to Bhagavan Shri Ramakrishna!\n", "1894.\n", "DEAR BROTHERS, (Brother-disciples of Swamiji.)\n", "Before  this  I  wrote to you a letter which for want of time was very\nincomplete. Rakhal (Brahmananda) and Hari (Turiyananda) wrote in a letter\nfrom Lucknow that Hindu newspapers were praising me, and that they were very\nglad that twenty thousand people had partaken of food at Shri Ramakrishna's\nanniversary. I could do much more work but for the Brahmos and missionaries\nwho have been opposing me unceasingly, and the Hindus of India too did\nnothing for me. I mean, if the Hindus of Calcutta or Madras had held a\nmeeting and passed a resolution recognising me as their representative, and\nthanking the American people for receiving me with kindness, things would\nhave progressed appreciably. But it is over a year, and nothing done. Of\ncourse I never relied on the Bengalis, but the Madrasis couldn't do anything\neither. ...\n", "There is no hope for our nation. Not one original idea crosses anyone's\nbrains, all fighting over the same old, threadbare rug\u2014that Ramakrishna\nParamahamsa was such and such\u2014and cock-and-bull stories\u2014stories having\nneither head nor tail. My God! Won't you do something to show that you are\nin any way removed from the common run of men!\u2014Only indulging in madness!\n... Today you have your bell, tomorrow you add a horn, and follow suit with\na chowry the day after; or you introduce a cot today, and tomorrow you have\nits legs silver-mounted, and people help themselves to a rice-porridge, and\nyou spin out two thousand cock-and-bull stories\u2014in short, nothing but\nexternal ceremonials. This is called in English imbecility. Those into whose\nheads nothing but that sort of silliness enters are called imbecile. Those\nwhose heads have a tendency to be troubled day and night over such questions\nas whether the bell should ring on the right or on the left, whether the\nsandal-paste mark should be put on the head or anywhere else, whether the\nlight should be waved twice or four times\u2014simply deserve the name of\nwretches, and it is owing to that sort of notion that we are the outcasts of\nFortune, kicked and spurned at, while the people of the West are masters of\nthe whole world. ... There is an ocean of difference between idleness and\nrenunciation.\n", "If you want any good to come, just throw your ceremonials overboard and\nworship the Living God, the Man-God\u2014every being that wears a human form\u2014God in His universal as well as individual aspect. The universal aspect of\nGod means this world, and worshipping it means serving it\u2014this indeed is\nwork, not indulging in ceremonials. Neither is it work to cogitate as to\nwhether the rice-plate should be placed in front of the God for ten minutes\nor for half an hour\u2014that is called lunacy. Millions of rupees have been\nspent only that the templedoors at Varanasi or Vrindaban may play at opening\nand shutting all day long! Now the Lord is having His toilet, now He is\ntaking His meals, now He is busy on something else we know not what. ... And\nall  this, while the Living God is dying for want of food, for want of\neducation! The banias of Bombay are erecting hospitals for bugs\u2014while they\nwould  do  nothing for men even if they die! You have not the brain to\nunderstand this simple thing\u2014that it is a plague with our country, and\nlunatic asylums are rife all over. ... Let some of you spread like fire, and\npreach this worship of the universal aspect of the Godhead\u2014a thing that\nwas never undertaken before in our country. No quarrelling with people, we\nmust be friends with all. ...\n", "Spread ideas\u2014go from village to village, from door to door\u2014then only\nthere  will be real work. Otherwise, lying complacently on the bed and\nringing the bell now and then is a sort of disease, pure and simple. ... Be\nindependent, learn to form independent judgments.\u2014That such and such a\nchapter of such and such a Tantra has prescribed a standard length for the\nhandle of a bell,\u2014what matters it to me? Through the Lord's will, out of\nyour lips shall come millions of Vedas and Tantras and Pur\u00e2nas. ... If now\nyou  can  show this in practice, if you can make three or four hundred\nthousand disciples in India within a year, then only I may have some hope.\n...\n", "By the bye, you know the boy who had his head shaven and went with Brother\nTarak from Bombay to Rameswaram? He calls himself a disciple of Ramakrishna\nParamahamsa! Let Brother Tarak initiate him. ... He had never even met Shri\nRamakrishna in his life, and yet a disciple!\u2014What impudence! Without an\nunbroken chain of discipleship\u2014Guruparampara\u2014nothing can be done. Is it\na  child's  play?  To have no connection whatsoever and call oneself a\ndisciple! The idiot! If that boy refuses to go on in the right way, turn him\nout. Nothing, I say, can be done without the chain of discipleship, that is,\nthe power that is transmitted from the Guru to the disciple, and from him to\nhis disciple, and so on. Here he comes and proclaims himself a disciple of\nRamakrishna\u2014is it tomfoolery? Jagamohan told me of somebody calling\nhimself a brother-disciple of mine. I have now a suspicion that it is that\nboy. To pose as a brother-disciple! He feels humiliated to call himself a\ndisciple, I dare say, and would fain turn a Guru straightway! Turn him out\nif he does not follow the established procedure.\n", "Talking  of  the  restlessness  of  Tulasi  (Nirmalananda)  and Subodh\n(Subodhananda) it all means that they have got no work to do. ... Go from\nvillage to village, do good to humanity and to the world at large. Go to\nhell yourself to buy salvation for others. There is no Mukti on earth to\ncall my own. Whenever you think of yourself, you are bound to feel restless.\nWhat  business  have  you to do with peace, my boy? You have renounced\neverything. Come! Now is the turn for you to banish the desire for peace,\nand that for Mukti too! Don't worry in the least; heaven or hell, or Bhakti\nor Mukti\u2014don't care for anything, but go, my boy, and spread the name of\nthe Lord from door to door! It is only by doing good to others that one\nattains to one's own good, and it is by leading others to Bhakti and Mukti\nthat one attains them oneself. Take that up, forget-your own self for it,\nbe mad over the idea. As Shri Ramakrishna used to love you, as I love you,\ncome, love the world like that. Bring all together. Where is Gunanidhi? You\nmust have him with you. My infinite love to him. Where is Gupta (Sadananda)?\nLet him join if he likes. Call him in my name. Remember these few points:\n", "1. We are Sannyasins, who have given up everything\u2014Bhakti, and Mukti, and\nenjoyment, and all.\n", "2. To do the highest good to the world, everyone down to the lowest\u2014this\nis our vow. Welcome Mukti or hell, whichever comes of it.\n", "3. Ramakrishna Paramahamsa came for the good of the world. Call him a man,\nor God, or an Incarnation, just as you please. Accept him each in your own\nlight.\n", "4. He who will bow before him will be converted into purest gold that very\nmoment. Go with this message from door to door, if you can, my boy, and all\nyour disquietude will be at an end. Never fear\u2014where's the room for fear?\u2014Caring for nothing whatsoever is a part of your life. You have so long\nspread his name and your character all around, well and good. Now spread\nthem in an organised way. The Lord is with you. Take heart!\n", "Whether  I  live  or die, whether I go back to India or not, you go on\nspreading love, love that knows no bounds. Put Gupta too to this task. But\nremember one needs weapons to overcome others. \"\u0938\u0928\u094d\u0928\u093f\u092e\u093f\u0924\u094d\u0924\u0946 \u0935\u0930\u0902 \u0924\u094d\u092f\u093e\u0917\u094a \u0935\u093f\u0928\u093e\u0936\u0946 \u0935\u093f\u0928\u093e\u0936\u0946 \u0928\u093f\u092f\u0924\u0946 \u0938\u0924\u093f\u2014When death is so certain, it is better to die for a good cause.\"\n", "Yours affly.,\nVivekananda.\n", "PS. Remember my previous letter\u2014we want both men and women. There is no\ndistinction of sex in the soul. It won't do merely to call Shri Ramakrishna\nan Incarnation, you must manifest power. Where are Gour-M\u00e2, Yogin-M\u00e2, and\nGolap-M\u00e2? Tell them to spread these ideas. We want thousands of men and\nthousands of women who will spread like wild fire from the Himalayas to Cape\nComorin, from the North Pole to the South Pole\u2014all over the world. It is\nno use indulging in child's play\u2014neither is there time for it. Let those\nwho have come for child's play be off now, while there is time, or they will\nsurely come to grief. We want an organisation. Off with laziness. Spread!\nSpread! Run like fire to all places. Do not depend upon me. Whether I live\nor die, go on spreading, yourselves.\n"]

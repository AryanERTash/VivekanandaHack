Now for the\nworld that gave me this body \u2014 the country that gave me the ideas, the\nhumanity which allows me to be one of them!\n", "\nThe older I grow, the more I see behind the idea of the Hindus that man is\nthe greatest of all beings. So say the Mohammedans too. The angels were\nasked by Allah to bow down to Adam. Iblis did not, and therefore he became\nSatan. This earth is higher than all heavens; this is the greatest school in\nthe universe; and the Mars or Jupiter people cannot be higher than we,\nbecause they cannot communicate with us. The only so-called higher beings\nare the departed, and these are nothing but men who have taken another body.\nThis is finer, it is true, but still a man-body, with hands and feet, and so\non. So they also are men, so are the Devas, the angels. But man alone\nbecomes God; and they all have to become men again in order to become God. .\n. .\n", "\n", "Yours etc.,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

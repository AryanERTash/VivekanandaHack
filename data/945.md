["\n", "XCVIII\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "DEULDHAR, ALMORA,\n", "13th July, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Going to Almora from here I made special efforts for Yogen. But he left for\nthe plains as soon as he had recovered a little. From Subhala valley he will\nwrite to me of his safe arrival there. As it is impossible to procure a\nDandi (a carrying chair) or any other conveyance, Latu could not go. Achyut\nand myself have again come back to this place. Today my health is a little\nbad owing to this riding on horseback at breakneck speed in the sun. I took\nShashi Babu's medicine for two weeks \u2014 I find no special benefit. . . . The\npain in the liver is gone, and owing to plenty of exercise my hands and legs\nhave  become muscular, but the abdomen is distending very much. I feel\nsuffocated while getting up or sitting down. Perhaps this is due to the\ntaking of milk. Ask Shashi if I can give up milk. Previously I suffered from\ntwo attacks of sunstroke. From that time, my eyes become red if I expose\nmyself to the sun, and the health continues to be bad for two or three days\nat a stretch.\n", "I was very pleased to get all the news from the Math, and I also heard that\nthe famine relief work is going on well. Please let me know if any money has\nbeen received from the office of the Brahmavadin for famine relief. Some\nmoney will be sent soon from here also. There is famine in many other places\nas well, so it is not necessary to stay so long in one place. Tell them to\nmove to other localities and write to each man to go to a separate place.\nAll such work is real work. If the field is made ready in this way, the\nseeds of spiritual knowledge can be sown. Remember this always \u2014 that the\nonly answer to those conservative fanatics who abuse us is such work. I have\nno  objection  to  getting the thing printed as Shashi and Sarada have\nsuggested.\n", "You yourselves come to a decision as to what the name of the Math should be.\n. . . The money will come within seven weeks; but I have no further news\nabout the land. In this matter it seems to me that it will be good if we can\nget the garden of Kristo Gopal in Cossipore. (Where Shri Ramakrishna passed\nhis last days.) What do you say? In future great works will be accomplished.\nIf you agree with me, don't let this matter out to anybody either within the\nMath or outside, but quietly make inquiries. The work is spoiled if plans\nare not kept secret. If it can be bought with fifteen or sixteen thousand,\nthen buy at once \u2014 of course, only if you think it good. If something more\nis demanded, make some advance payment and wait for those seven weeks. My\nview is that for the present it is better to buy it. Everything else will\ncome by and by. All our associations centre round that garden. In reality\nthat is our first Math. Let the thing be done very privately.\n", "A work can be judged by its results only, just as one can infer the nature\nof previous mental tendencies by their resultant in present behaviour. . . .\n", "Undoubtedly the price of the land of the garden at Cossipore has increased;\nbut our purse has, on the other hand, dwindled. Do something or other, but\ndo it quickly. All work is spoilt by dilatoriness. This garden also has to\nbe acquired \u2014 if not today, tomorrow \u2014 however big the Math on the banks of\nthe Ganga may be. It will be still better if you can broach the subject\nthrough a proxy. If they hear that we are willing to buy, they will bid\nhigh. Do the work very confidentially. Be fearless; Shri Ramakrishna is our\nhelper, what fear? Give my love to all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. (on the cover): . . . Make special efforts for Cossipore. . . . Give up\nthe land at Belur. Should the poor (The famine-stricken people for whom the\nMahabodhi Society agreed to pay, on condition that the work would be done in\nits name.) die of starvation while you people at the top are indulging in\ncontroversy regarding to whom the credit should go? If \"Mahabodhi\" takes all\nthe credit, let it. Let the poor be benefited. That the work is going on\nwell is good news. Work on with greater energy. I am beginning to send\narticles. The saccharine and lime have reached.\n", "V.\n"]

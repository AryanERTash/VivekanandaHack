["\n", "LXII\n", "\n54 W. 33rd ST., NEW YORK,\n11th April, 1895.\n", "DEAR MRS. BULL,\n", ". . . I am going away to the country tomorrow to see Mr. Leggett for a few\ndays. A little fresh air will do me good, I hope.\n", "I have given up the project of removing from this house just now, as it will\nbe too expensive, and moreover it is not advisable to change just now. I am\nworking it up slowly.\n", ". . . I send you herewith the letter from H. H. the Maharaja of Khetri; also\nenclose the slip on Gurjan oil for leprosy. Miss Hamlin has been helping me\na  good deal. I am very grateful to her. She is very kind and, I hope,\nsincere. She wants me to be introduced to the \"right kind of people\". This\nis the second edition of the \"Hold yourself steady\" business, I am afraid.\nThe only \"right sort of people\" are those whom the Lord sends \u2014 that is what\nI understand in my life's experience. They alone can and will help me. As\nfor the rest, Lord help them in a mass and save me from them.\n", "Every one of my friends thought it would end in nothing, this my getting up\nquarters all by myself, and that no ladies would ever come here. Miss Hamlin\nespecially thought that \"she\" or \"her right sort of people\" were way up from\nsuch things as to go and listen to a man who lives by himself in a poor\nlodging. But the \"right kind\" came for all that, day and night, and she too.\nLord! how hard it is for man to believe in Thee and Thy mercies! Shiva!\nShiva! Where is the right kind and where is the bad, mother? It is all He!\nIn the tiger and in the lamb, in the saint and sinner all He! In Him I have\ntaken my refuge, body, soul, and Atman. Will He leave me now after carrying\nme in His arms all my life? Not a drop will be in the ocean, not a twig in\nthe deepest forest, not a crumb in the house of the god of wealth, if the\nLord is not merciful. Streams will be in the desert and the beggar will have\nplenty, if He wills it. He seeth the sparrow's fall. Are these but words,\nmother, or literal, actual life?\n", "Truce to this \"right sort of presentation\". Thou art my right, Thou my\nwrong, my Shiva. Lord, since a child I have taken refuge in Thee. Thou wilt\nbe with me in the tropics or at the poles, on the tops of mountains or in\nthe depth of oceans. My stay \u2014 my guide in life \u2014 my refuge \u2014 my friend \u2014 my\nteacher \u2014 my God \u2014 my real Self, Thou wilt never leave me, never. I know it\nfor sure. Sometimes I become weak, being alone and struggling against odds,\nmy  God;  and  I think of human help. Save Thou me for ever from these\nweaknesses, and may I never, never seek for help from any being but Thee. If\na  man puts his trust in another good man, he is never betrayed, never\nforsaken. Wilt Thou forsake me, Father of all good, Thou who knowest that\nall my life I am Thy servant and Thine alone? Wilt Thou give me over to be\nplayed upon by others, or dragged down by evil? He will never leave me, I am\nsure, mother.\n", "Your ever obedient son,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

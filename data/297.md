["\n", "LXII\n", "\n", "\nDear Dr. Nanjunda Rao,\n\n", "\nThe numbers of Prabuddha Bharata have been received and distributed too to\nthe class. It is very satisfactory. It will have a great sale, no doubt, in\nIndia. In America I may get also a number of subscribers. I have already\narranged for advertising it in America and Goodyear has done it already. But\nhere in England the progress will be slower indeed. The great drawback here\nis \u2014 they all want to start papers of their own; and it is right that it\nshould be so, seeing that, after all, no foreigner will ever write the\nEnglish language as well as the native Englishman, end the ideas, when put\nin good English, will spread farther than in Hindu English. Then again it is\nmuch more difficult to write a story in a foreign language than an essay. I\nam trying my best to get you subscribers here. But you must not depend on\nany foreign help. Nations, like individuals, must help themselves. This is\nreal patriotism. If a nation cannot do that, its time has not yet come. It\nmust wait. It is from Madras that the new light must spread all over India.\nWith this end you must stork. One point I will remark however. The cover is\nsimply barbarous. It is awful and hideous. If it is possible, change it.\nMake it symbolical and simple, without human figures at all. The banyan tree\ndoes not mean awakening, nor does the hill, nor the saint, nor the European\ncouple. The lotus is a symbol of regeneration.\n", "\nWe  are  awfully behindhand in art especially in that of painting. For\ninstance, make a small scene of spring re-awakening in a forest, showing how\nthe leaves and buds are coming again. Slowly go on, there are hundreds of\nideas  to be put forward. You see the symbol I made for the Raja-Yoga,\nprinted by Longman Green and Co. You can get it at Bombay. It consists of my\nlectures on Raja-Yoga in New York.\n", "\nI am going to Switzerland next Sunday, and shall return to London in the\nautumn, and take up the work again. . . . I want rest very badly, you know.\n", "\n", "Yours with all blessings etc.,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

["\n", "CIII\n", "(Translated from Bengali.)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "C/O RISHIBAR MUKHOPADHYAYA,\n", "CHIEF JUSTICE,\n", "SRINAGAR, KASHMIR,\n", "13th September, 1897.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "Now Kashmir. The excellent accounts you heard of this place are all true.\nThere is no place so beautiful as this; and the people also are fair and\ngood-looking, though their eyes are not beautiful. But I have also never\nseen elsewhere villages and towns so horribly dirty. In Srinagar I am now\nputting up at the house of Rishibar Babu. He is very hospitable and kind.\nSend all my letters to his address. In a few days I shall go out somewhere\nelse on excursions; but while returning, I shall come by way of Srinagar,\nand so shall get the letters also. I have read the letter that you sent\nregarding Gangadhar. Write to him that there are many orphans in Central\nIndia and in Gorakhpur. From there the Punjabis are getting many children.\nYou must persuade Mahendra Babu and get up an agitation about this matter,\nso that the people of Calcutta are induced to take up the charge of these\norphans \u2014 such a movement is very desirable. Especially a memorial should be\nsent to the Government requesting it to see that orphans taken over by the\nmissionaries are returned to the Hindus. Tell Gangadhar to come over; and on\nbehalf of the Ramakrishna Society a tearing campaign should be made. Gird up\nyour  loins, and go to every house to carry on the campaign. Hold mass\nmeetings etc. Whether you succeed or not, start a furious agitation. Get all\nthe facts from the important Bengali friends at Gorakhpur by writing to\nthem,  and  let  there  be  a countrywide agitation over this. Let the\nRamakrishna Society be fully established. The secret of the whole thing is\nto  agitate  and agitate without respite. I am much pleased to see the\norderliness of Sarada's work. Gangadhar and Sarada should not rest satisfied\nuntil they have succeeded in creating a centre in every place they visit.\n", "Just now I received a letter from Gangadhar. It is good news that he is\ndetermined to start a centre in that district. Write to him saying that his\nfriend, the Magistrate, has sent an excellent reply to my letter. As soon as\nwe come down to the plains from Kashmir, I shall send back Latu, Niranjan,\nDinu, and Khoka. For there is no suitable work for them here any more; also\nwithin three to four weeks send Shuddhananda, Sushil, and one other to me.\nSend them to the house of Mr. Shyamacharan Mukhopadhyaya, Medical Hall,\nCantonment, Ambala. From there I shall go to Lahore. They should have each\ntwo thick gerua-coloured jerseys, and two blankets for bedding. I shall buy\nthem woollen chaddars, and other woollen necessities in Lahore. If the\ntranslation of R\u00e2ja-Yoga has been completed, get it published bearing all\nthe cost. . . . Where the language is obscure, make it very simple and\nclear, and let Tulsi make a Hindi translation of it if he can. If these\nbooks are published, they will help the Math very greatly.\n", "I hope your health is now quite all right. Since reaching Dharamsala I have\nbeen all right. I like the cold places; there the body keeps well. I have a\ndesire either to visit a few places in Kashmir and then choose an excellent\nsite and live a quiet life there, or to go on floating on the water. I shall\ndo what the doctor advises. The Raja is not here now. His brother, the one\njust next to him in age, is the Commander-in-Chief. Efforts are being made\nto arrange a lecture under his chairmanship. I shall write all about this\nafterwards. If the meeting for the lecture is held in a day or two, I shall\nstay  back, otherwise I go out again on my travels. Sevier is still at\nMurree. His health is very bad \u2014 going about in the jolting tongas and\njutkas. The Bengali gentlemen of Murree are very good and courteous. Give my\nrespects to G. C. Ghosh, Atul, Master Mahashay, and others, and keep up the\nspirits of everybody. What is the news about the house which Yogen suggested\nwe should buy? In October I shall go down from here and shall deliver a few\nlectures in the Punjab. After that I may go via Sind to Cutch, Bhuj, and\nKathiawar \u2014 even down to Poona if circumstances are favourable; otherwise I\ngo  to  Rajputana via Baroda. From Rajputana I go to the North-Western\nProvince, (In those days this was made up of Uttar Pradesh and part of the\nPunjab.) then Nepal, and finally Calcutta \u2014 this is my present programme.\nEverything, however, is in God's hands. My love and greetings to all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

Joe [Miss Josephine MacLeod] is\ncoming to India shortly \u2014 at Christmas time with some Japanese friends. I am\nexpected to meet her in Madras.\n", "I am going off to the N.W.P. [North-Western Provinces] etc. soon, as Bengal\nis malarious \u2014 now that the rains are over.\n", "Mrs. Bull has been a mother to us all, and any time and service spent for\nher is as nothing to what she has been doing for us all. Remain with her as\nlong as she wants you \u2014 the work can wait well; \"Mother\" sees to her work.\nWe needn't be anxious.\n", "By the by, Miss [Henrietta] M\u00fcller is here in Calcutta. She wrote a letter\nto Akhandananda, with whom she has been in regular correspondence \u2014 care of\nthe Math. So I sent some flowers and fruits and a letter of welcome to her\nhotel. I have not had a reply yet.\n", "Mrs. [Charlotte] Sevier, I expect, has already started. Swarupananda had his\nheart  weakened  by  the  constant uphill and downhill. He is here and\nimproving.\n", "Things are going on well with us, slowly but surely. The boys of late have\nbeen very active, and it is work only that tells and nothing else.\n", "Yours with all love and blessings,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n"]

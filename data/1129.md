["\n", "LXXXV\n", "To Mrs. Ole Bull\n", "228 W. 39\n", "NEW YORK\n", "the 6th of Feb. '96\n", "DEAR MRS. BULL \u2014\n", "I received your last duly, but owing to many things I have given up the idea\nof taking rest next month. I go to Detroit the first week of March and then,\ntowards the middle or last week, come to Boston. I have not much faith in\nworking such things as the Procopeia [Club] etc.\u2014 because these mixed-up\nconglomerations of all isms and ities \u2014 mostly fads \u2014 disturb the steadiness\nof  the  mind, and life becomes a mass of frivolities. I am very glad,\nhowever, to get an opportunity to talk to the graduates of Harvard. This\ndoes not mean that I am not coming to Procopeia. I will come but it will be\nonly  for  your  sake.  There is one if, however \u2014 and that is if I am\nphysically able. My health has nearly broken down. I have not slept even one\nnight soundly in New York since I came; and this year there is incessant\nwork, both with the pen and the mouth. The accumulated work and worry of\nyears is on me now, I am afraid. Then a big struggle awaits me in England. I\nwish to go to the bottom of the sea and have a good, long sleep.\n", "To Detroit I must go, dead or alive, as I have disappointed them several\ntimes last year. There were big money offers from near Chicago. I have\nrejected them as I do not any longer believe in paid lectures and their\nutility in any country. If after Detroit I feel the body able to drag itself\non to Boston, I will come, else I will remain in Detroit or some other quiet\nplace and rest to recuperate for the coming work in England. So far I have\ntried to work conscientiously \u2014 let the fruits belong to the Lord. If they\nwere good they will sprout up sooner or later; if bad, the sooner they die\nthe better. I am quite satisfied with my task in life. I have been much more\nactive than a Sannyasin ought to be. Now I will disappear from society\naltogether. The touch of the world is degenerating me, I am sure, so it is\ntime to be off. Work has no more value beyond purifying the heart. My heart\nis pure enough; why shall I bother my head about doing good to others? \"If\nyou have known the Atman as the one, only existence and nothing else exists,\ndesiring what? \u2014 for whose desire you trouble yourself?\"[6]* This universe\nis a dream, pure and simple. Why bother myself about a dream? The very\natmosphere of the world is poison to the Yogi, but I am waking up. My old\niron heart is coming back \u2014 all attachments of relatives, friends, disciples\nare vanishing fast. \"Neither through wealth nor through progeny, but by\ngiving up everything as chaff is that immortality attained\"[7]* \u2014 the Vedas.\nI am so tired of talking too; I want to close my lips and sit in silence for\nyears. All talk is nonsense.\n", "Yours faithfully,\n", "VIVEKANANDA\n"]

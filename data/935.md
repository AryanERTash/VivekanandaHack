["\n", "LXXXVIII\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "DAMPFER, \"PRINZ-REGENT LEOPOLD\"\n", "3rd January, 1897.\n", "DEAR MARY,\n", "I received your letter forwarded from London in Rome. It was very very kind\nof you to write such a beautiful letter, and I enjoyed every bit of it. I do\nnot know anything about the evolution of the orchestra in Europe. We are\nnearing Port Said after four days of frightfully bad sailing from Naples.\nThe ship is rolling as hard as she can, and you must pardon my scrawls under\nsuch circumstances.\n", "From Suez begins Asia. Once more Asia. What am I? Asiatic, European, or\nAmerican? I feel a curious medley of personalities in me. You didn't write\nanything about Dharmapala, his goings and doings. I am much more interested\nin him than in Gandhi.\n", "I land in a few days at Colombo and mean to \"do\" Ceylon a bit. There was a\ntime when Ceylon had more than 20 million inhabitants and a huge capital of\nwhich the ruins cover nearly a hundred square miles!\n", "The Ceylonese are not Dravidians but pure Aryans. It was colonised from\nBengal about 800 B.C., and they have kept a very clear history of their\ncountry from that time. It was the greatest trade centre of the ancient\nworld, and Anuradhapuram was the London of the ancients.\n", "I enjoyed Rome more than anything in the West, and after seeing Pompeii I\nhave lost all regard for the so-called \"Modern Civilisation\". With the\nexception of steam and electricity they had everything else and infinitely\nmore art conceptions and executions than the Moderns.\n", "Please tell Miss Locke that I was mistaken when I told her that sculpturing\nof the human figure was not developed in India as among the Greeks. I am\nreading in Fergusson and other authorities that in Orissa or Jagannath,\nwhich I did not visit, there are among the ruins human figures which for\nbeauty and anatomical skill would compare with any production of the Greeks.\nThere is a colossal figure of Death, a huge female skeleton covered with a\nshrivelled skin \u2014 the awful fidelity to anatomical details are frightening\nand disgusting. Says my author, one of the female figures in the niche is\nexactly like the Venus de Medici and so on. But you must remember that\neverything almost has been destroyed by the iconoclastic Mohammedan, yet the\nremnants are more than all European debris put together! I have travelled\neight years and not seen many of the masterpieces.\n", "Tell sister Locke also that there is a ruined temple in a forest in India\nwhich and the Parthenon of Greece Fergusson considers as the climax of\narchitectural art \u2014 each of its type \u2014 the one of conception, the other of\nconception and detail. The later Mogul buildings etc., the Indo-Saracenic\narchitecture, does not compare a bit with the best types of the ancients. .\n. .\n", "With all my love,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. Just by chance saw Mother Church and Father Pope at Florence. You know\nof it already.\n", "V.\n"]

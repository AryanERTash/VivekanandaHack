["\n", "LXIV\n", "To Mr. E. T. Sturdy\n", "228 WEST 39TH STREET,\n", "NEW YORK,\n", "16th December, 1895.\n", "BLESSED AND BELOVED,\n", "All your letters reached by one mail today. Miss M\u00fcller also writes me one.\nShe has read in the Indian Mirror that Swami Krishnananda is coming over to\nEngland. If that is so, he is the strongest man that I can get.\n", "The classes I had here were six in the week, besides a question class. The\ngeneral attendance varies between 70 to 120. Besides every Sunday I have a\npublic lecture. The last month my lectures were in a small hall holding\nabout 600. But 900 will come as a rule, 300 standing, and about 300 going\noff, not finding room. This week therefore I have a bigger hall, with a\ncapacity of holding 1200 people.\n", "There is no admission charged in these lectures, but a collection covers the\nrent. The newspapers have taken me up this week, and altogether I have\nstirred up New York considerably this year. If I could have remained here\nthis summer and organised a summer place, the work would be going on sure\nfoundations here. But as I intended to come over in May to England, I shall\nhave to leave it unfinished. If, however, Krishnananda comes to England, and\nyou find him strong and able, and if you find the work in London will not be\nhurt by my absence this summer, I would rather be here this summer.\n", "Again, I am afraid my health is breaking down under constant work. I want\nsome rest. We are so unused to these Western methods, especially the keeping\nto time. I will leave you to decide all these. The Brahmav\u00e2din is going on\nhere very satisfactorily. I have begun to write articles on Bhakti; also\nsend  them a monthly account of the work. Miss M\u00fcller wants to come to\nAmerica.  I do not know whether she will or not. Some friends here are\npublishing my Sunday lectures. I have sent you a few copies of the first\none. I shall send you next mail a few of the next two lectures, and if you\nlike them I shall ask them to send you a number. Can you manage to get a few\nhundred copies sold in England? That will encourage them in publishing the\nsubsequent ones.\n", "Next month I go to Detroit, then to Boston, and Harvard University. Then I\nshall have a rest, and then I come to England, unless you think that things\ngo on without me and with Krishnananda.\n", "Ever yours with love and blessings,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

It is\nneither gas nor anything else.\n", "K\u00e2li worship is not a necessary step in any religion. The Upanishads teach\nus all there is of religion. Kali worship is my special fad; you never heard\nme preach it, or read of my preaching it in India. I only preach what is\ngood for universal humanity. If there is any curious method which applies\nentirely to me, I keep it a secret and there it ends. I must not explain to\nyou what Kali worship is, as I never taught it to anybody.\n", "You are entirely mistaken if you think the Boses are rejected by the Hindu\npeople. The English rulers want to push him into a corner. They don't of\ncourse like that sort of development in the Indian race. They make it hot\nfor him, that is why he seeks to go elsewhere.\n", "By the \"anglicised\" are meant people who by their manners and conduct show\nthat they are ashamed of us poor, old type Hindus. I am not ashamed of my\nrace or my birth or nationality. That such people are not liked by the\nHindus, I cannot wonder.\n", "Ceremonials and symbols etc. have no place in our religion which is the\ndoctrine  of  the  Upanishads,  pure and simple. Many people think the\nceremonial etc. help them in realising religion. I have no objection.\n", "Religion is that which does not depend upon books or teachers or prophets or\nsaviours, and that which does not make us dependent in this or in any other\nlives upon others. In this sense Advaitism of the Upanishads is the only\nreligion.  But saviours, books, prophets, ceremonials, etc. have their\nplaces. They may help many as Kali worship helps me in my secular work. They\nare welcome.\n", "The Guru, however, is a different idea. It is the relation between the\ntransmitter and the receiver of force \u2014 psychic power and knowledge. Each\nnation is a type, physically and mentally. Each is constantly receiving\nideas from others only to work them out into its type, that is, along the\nnational line. The time has not come for the destruction of types. All\neducation from any source is compatible with the ideals in every country;\nonly they must be nationalised, i.e. fall in line with the rest of the type\nmanifestation.\n", "Renunciation is always the ideal of every race; only other races do not know\nwhat they are made to do by nature unconsciously. Through the ages one\npurpose runs sure. And that will be finished with the destruction of this\nearth and the sun! And worlds are always in progress indeed! And nobody as\nyet developed enough in any one of the infinite worlds to communicate with\nus! Bosh! They are born, show the same phenomena, and die the same death!\nIncreasing purpose! Babies! Live in the land of dreams, you babies!\n", "Well, now about me. You must persuade Harriet to give me a few dollars every\nmonth, and I will have some other friends do the same. If I succeed, I fly\noff to India. I am dead tired of the platform work for a living. It does not\nplease me any more. I retire and do some writing if I can do some scholarly\nwork.\n", "I am coming soon to Chicago, hope to be there in a few days. Say, would not\nMrs. Adams be able to get up a class for me to pay my passage back?\n", "Of course I shall try different places. So much of optimism has come to me,\nMary, that I should fly off to the Himalayas if I had wings.\n", "I have worked for this world, Mary, all my life, and it does not give me a\npiece of bread without taking a pound of flesh.\n", "If  I  can  get a piece of bread a day, I retire entirely; but this is\nimpossible  \u2014 this is the increasing purpose that is unfolding all the\ndevilish inwardness, as I am getting older!\n", "Ever yours in the Lord,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. If ever a man found the vanity of things, I have it now. This is the\nworld, hideous, beastly corpse. Who thinks of helping it is a fool! But we\nhave to work out our slavery by doing good or evil; I have worked it out, I\nhope. May the Lord take me to the other shore! Amen! I have given up all\nthoughts about India or any land. I am now selfish, want to save myself!\n", "\"He who revealed unto Brahm\u00e2 (the first of the gods) the Vedas, who is\nmanifest in every heart, unto Him I take refuge, hoping deliverance from\nbondage.\"\n", "V.\n"]

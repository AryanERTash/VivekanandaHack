["\n", "CXXXI\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "To Swami Brahmananda\n", "SRINAGAR,\n", "1st August, 1898.\n", "MY DEAR RAKHAL,\n", "You are always under a delusion, and it does not leave you because of the\nstrong influence, good or bad, of other brains. It is this: whenever I write\nto you about accounts, you feel that I have no confidence in you. . . . My\ngreat anxiety is this: the work has somehow been started, but it should go\non and progress even when we are not here; such thoughts worry me day and\nnight. Any amount of theoretical knowledge one may have; but unless one does\nthe thing actually, nothing is learnt. I refer repeatedly to election,\naccounts, and discussion so that everybody may be prepared to shoulder the\nwork. If one man dies, another \u2014 why another only, ten if necessary \u2014 should\nbe ready to take it up. Secondly, if a man's interest in a thing is not\nroused, he will not work whole-heartedly; all should be made to understand\nthat everyone has a share in the work and property, and a voice in the\nmanagement. This should be done while there is yet time. Give a responsible\nposition to everyone alternately, but keep a watchful eye so that you can\ncontrol when necessary; thus only can men be trained for the work. Set up\nsuch a machine as will go on automatically, no matter who dies or lives. We\nIndians  suffer  from  a  great defect, viz we cannot make a permanent\norganisation \u2014 and the reason is that we never like to share power with\nothers and never think of what will come after we are gone.\n", "I have already written everything regarding the plague. Mrs. Bull and Miss\nM\u00fcller and others are of opinion that it is not desirable to spend money\nuselessly  when hospitals have been started in every Ward. We lend our\nservices as nurses and the like. Those that pay the piper must command the\ntune.\n", "The Maharaja of Kashmir has agreed to give us a plot of land. I have also\nvisited the site. Now the matter will be finalised in a few days, if the\nLord wills. Right now, before leaving, I hope to build a small house here. I\nshall leave it in the charge of Justice Mukherjee when departing. Why not\ncome here with somebody else and spend the winter? Your health will improve,\nand a need, too, will be fulfilled. The money I have set apart for the press\nwill be sufficient for the purpose, but all will be as you decide. This time\nI shall surely get some money from N.W.P., Rajputana, and other places.\nWell, give as directed . . . money to a few persons. I am borrowing this\namount from the Math and will pay it back to you with interest.\n", "My health is all right in a way. It is good news that the building work has\nbegun. My love to all.\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

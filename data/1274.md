["\n", "BHAKTI-YOGA\n", "(New Discoveries, Vol. 3, pp. 543-54.)\n", "[A bhakti-yoga class delivered in New York, Monday morning, January 20,\n1896, and recorded by Mr. Josiah J. One idea more of\nthe preparatory Bhakti, and then we will go on to the Par\u00e2, the Supreme.\nThis idea is what is called Nishth\u00e2, devotion to one idea.\n", "We know that all these ideas of worship are right and all good, and we have\nseen that the worship of God, and God alone, is Bhakti. The worship of any\nother being will not be Bhakti, but God can be worshipped in various forms\nand through various ideas. And we have seen that all these ideas are right\nand  good,  but the difficulty is here: If we just stop with this last\nconclusion, we find that in the end we have frittered away our energies and\ndone nothing.\n", "It is a great tendency among liberal people to become a jack-of-all-trades\nand master of none \u2014 to nibble a little here and there and, in the long run,\nfind they have nothing. Hanuman lived very long and was a great Yogi.\n", "During his lifetime, Rama came again as Krishna; and Hanuman, being a great\nYogi, knew that the same God had come back again as Krishna. We must not worship the others at all; we\nmust not hate or criticize them, but respect them.\n", "The elephant has two teeth coming out from his mouth. Stick to your own ideal of worship. When you worship, worship that\nideal of God which is your own Ishta, your own Chosen Ideal. If you do not,\nyou will have nothing.

["\n", "IV\n", "(Translated from Bengali)\n", "Salutation to Bhagavan Ramakrishna!\n", "\nC/O Satish Mukherji,\n", "GORABAZAR, GHAZIPUR.\n", "14th February, 1890.\n", "REVERED SIR (Shri Balaram Bose),\n", "I am in receipt of your letter of contrition. I am not leaving this place\nsoon \u2014 it is impossible to avoid the Babaji's request. You have expressed\nremorse at not having reaped any appreciable results by serving the Sadhus.\nIt is true, and yet not true; it is true if you look towards ideal bliss;\nbut if you look behind to the place from which you started, you will find\nthat before you were an animal, now you are a man, and will be a god or God\nHimself in future. Moreover, that sort of regret and dissatisfaction is very\ngood; it is the prelude to improvement. Without this none can rise. He who\nputs on a turban and immediately sees the Lord, progresses thus far and no\nfarther.  You are blessed indeed to have that constant dissatisfaction\npreying upon your mind \u2014 rest assured that there is no danger for you. . . .\nYou are a keenly intelligent man, and know full well that patience is the\nbest means of success. In this respect I have no doubt that we light-headed\nboys have much to learn from you. . . . You are a considerate man, and I\nneed not add anything. Man has two ears but one mouth. You specially are\ngiven to plain-speaking and are chary of making large promises \u2014 things that\nsometimes make me cross with you, but upon reflection I find that it is you\nwho have acted with discretion. \"Slow but sure.\" \"What is lost in power is\ngained in speed.\" However, in this world everything depends upon one's\nwords. To get an insight behind the words (specially, with your economical\nspirit masking all) is not given to all, and one must associate long with a\nman to be able to understand him. . . . Religion is not in sects, nor in\nmaking a fuss \u2014 why do you forget these teachings of our revered Master?\nPlease help as far as it lies in you, but to judge what came of it, whether\nit was turned to good or evil account, is perhaps beyond our jurisdiction. .\n. . Considering the great shock which Girish Babu has received, it will give\nhim immense peace to serve Mother at this moment. He is a very keen-witted\nperson. And our beloved Master had perfect confidence in you, used to dine\nnowhere else except at your place, and, I have heard, Mother too has the\nfullest  confidence in you. In view of these, you will please bear and\nforbear all shortcomings of us fickle boys, treating them as if they were\ndone by your own boy. This is all I have got to say. Please let me know by\nreturn of post when the Anniversary is to take place. A pain in the loins is\ngiving  me much trouble. In a few days the place will look exceedingly\nbeautiful, with miles and miles of rose-banks all in flower. Satish says he\nwill then send some fresh roses and cuttings for the Festival. . . . May the\nLord ordain that your son becomes a man, and never a coward!\n", "Yours affectionately,\n", "VIVEKANANDA.\n", "PS. If Mother has come, please convey to her my countless salutations, and\nask her to bless me that I may have unflinching perseverance. Or, if that be\nimpossible in this body, may it fall off soon!\n"]

["\n", "CXLV\n", "\n", "BAIDYANATH, DEOGHAR,\n", "29th Dec., 1898.\n", "MY DEAR DHIRA MATA, (Mrs. Ole Bull)\n", "You know already my inability to accompany you. I cannot gather strength\nenough to accompany you. The cold in the lungs continues, and that is just\nwhat makes me unfit for travel. On the whole I hope to improve here.\n", "I find my cousin has been all these years cultivating her mind with a will,\nand she knows all that the Bengali literature can give her, and that is a\ngood deal, especially of metaphysics. She has already learnt to sign her\nname in English and the Roman alphabet. It is now real brain work to teach\nher, and therefore I have desisted. I am trying simply to idle away my time\nand force myself to take rest.\n", "Ere this I had only love for you, but recent development proves that you are\nappointed by the Mother to watch over my life; hence, faith has been added\nto love! As regards me and my work, I hold henceforth that you are inspired,\nand I will gladly shake off all responsibilities from my shoulder and abide\nby what the Mother ordains through you.\n", "Hoping soon to join you in Europe or America, I remain,\n", "Ever your loving son,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

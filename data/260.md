["\n", "XXV\n", "\n", "\n541 Dearborn Avenue,\n\nChicago, 1894.\n", "\nDear Alasinga,\n", "\nYour letter just to hand. . . . I was mistaken in asking you to publish the\nscraps I sent you. It was one of my awful mistakes. It shows a moment's\nweakness. Money can be raised in this country by lecturing for two or three\nyears.  But  I  have tried a little, and although there is much public\nappreciation of my work, it is thoroughly uncongenial and demoralising to\nme. . . .\n", "\nI have read what you say about the Indian papers and their criticisms, which\nare natural. Jealousy is the central vice of every enslaved race. And it is\njealousy and want of combination which cause and perpetuate slavery. You\ncannot feel the truth of this remark until you come out of India. The secret\nof Westerners' success is this power of combination, the basis of which is\nmutual trust and appreciation. The weaker and more cowardly a nation is, so\nmuch the more is this sin visible. . . . But, my son, you ought not to\nexpect anything from a slavish race. The case is almost desperate no doubt,\nbut let me put the case before you all. Can you put life into this dead mass\n\u2014 dead to almost all moral aspiration, dead to all future possibilities \u2014\nand always ready to spring upon those that would try to do good to them? Can\nyou take the position of a physician who tries to pour medicine down the\nthroat of a kicking and refractory child? . . . An American or a European\nalways supports his countrymen in a foreign country. . . . Let me remind you\nagain, \"Thou hast the right to work but not to the fruits thereof.\" Stand\nfirm  like  a  rock.  Truth  always triumphs. Let the children of Shri\nRamakrishna be true to themselves and everything will be all right. We may\nnot live to see the outcome, but as sure as we live, it will come sooner or\nlater. What India wants is a new electric fire to stir up a fresh vigour in\nthe national veins. This was ever, and always will be, slow work. Be content\nto work, and, above all, be true to yourself. Be pure, staunch, and sincere\nto the very backbone, and everything will be all right. If you have marked\nanything in the disciples of Shri Ramakrishna, it is this \u2014 they are sincere\nto the backbone. My task will be done, and I shall be quite content to die,\nif I can bring up and launch one hundred such men over India. He, the Lord,\nknows best. Let ignorant men talk nonsense. We neither seek aid nor avoid it\n\u2014 we are the servants of the Most High. The petty attempts of small men\nshould be beneath our notice. Onward! Upon ages of struggle a character is\nbuilt. Be not discouraged. One word of truth can never be lost; for ages it\nmay be hidden under rubbish, but it will show itself sooner or later. Truth\nis indestructible, virtue is indestructible, purity is indestructible. Give\nme a genuine man; I do not want masses of converts. My son, hold fast! Do\nnot care for anybody to help you. Is not the Lord infinitely greater than\nall human help? Be holy \u2014 trust in the Lord, depend on Him always, and you\nare on the right track; nothing can prevail against you. . . .\n", "\nLet us pray, \"Lead, Kindly Light\" \u2014 a beam will come through the dark, and a\nhand will be stretched forth to lead us. I always pray for you: you must\npray for me. Let each one of us pray day and night for the downtrodden\nmillions in India who are held fast by poverty, priestcraft, and tyranny \u2014\npray day and night for them. I care more to preach religion to them than to\nthe high and the rich. I am no metaphysician, no philosopher, nay, no saint.\nBut  I am poor, I love the poor. I see what they call the poor of this\ncountry,  and  how  many  there are who feel for them! What an immense\ndifference in India! Who feels there for the two hundred millions of men and\nwomen sunken for ever in poverty and ignorance? Where is the way out? Who\nfeels for them? They cannot find light or education. Who will bring the\nlight to them \u2014 who will travel from door to door bringing education to\nthem? Let these people be your God \u2014 think of them, work for them, pray for\nthem incessantly \u2014 the Lord will show you the way. Him I call a Mah\u00e2tman\n(great soul) whose heart bleeds for the poor, otherwise he is a Dur\u00e2tman\n(wicked soul). Let us unite our wills in continued prayer for their good. We\nmay die unknown, unpitied, unbewailed, without accomplishing anything \u2014 but\nnot one thought will be lost. It will take effect, sooner or later. My heart\nis too full to express my feeling; you know it, you can imagine it. So long\nas the millions live in hunger and ignorance, I hold every man a traitor\nwho, having been educated at their expense, pays not the least heed to them!\nI call those men who strut about in their finery, having got all their money\nby grinding the poor, wretches, so long as they do not do anything for those\ntwo hundred millions who are now no better than hungry savages! We are poor,\nmy brothers, we are nobodies, but such have been always the instruments of\nthe Most High. The Lord bless you all.\n", "\n", "With all love,\nVivekananda.\n", "\n"]

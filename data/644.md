["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "MONDAY, June 24, 1895. (The reading today was from the Bhakti-Sutras by\nN\u00e2rada.)\n", "\"Extreme love to God is Bhakti, and this love is the real immortality,\ngetting which a man becomes perfectly satisfied, sorrows for no loss, and is\nnever jealous; knowing which man becomes mad.\"\n", "My Master used to say, \"This world is a huge lunatic asylum where all men\nare mad, some after money, some after women, some after name or fame, and a\nfew after God. I prefer to be mad after God. God is the philosophers' stone\nthat turns us to gold in an instant; the form remains, but the nature is\nchanged \u2014 the human form remains, but no more can we hurt or sin.\"\n", "\"Thinking of God, some weep, some sing, some laugh, some dance, some say\nwonderful things, but all speak of nothing but God.\"\n", "Prophets preach, but the Incarnations like Jesus, Buddha, Ramakrishna, can\ngive religion; one glance, one touch is enough. That is the power of the\nHoly Ghost, the \"laying on of hands\"; the power was actually transmitted to\nthe disciples by the Master \u2014 the \"chain of Guru-power\". That, the real\nbaptism, has been handed down for untold ages.\n", "\"Bhakti cannot be used to fulfil any desires, itself being the check to all\ndesires.\" Narada gives these as the signs of love: \"When all thoughts, all\nwords, and all deeds are given up unto the Lord, and the least forgetfulness\nof God makes one intensely miserable, then love has begun.\"\n", "\"This  is  the  highest  form of love because therein is no desire for\nreciprocity, which desire is in all human love.\"\n", "\"A man who has gone beyond social and scriptural usage, he is a Sanny\u00e2sin.\nWhen the whole soul goes to God, when we take refuge only in God, then we\nknow that we are about to get this love.\"\n", "Obey the scriptures until you are strong enough to do without them; then go\nbeyond them. Books are not an end-all. Verification is the only proof of\nreligious truth. Each must verify for himself; and no teacher who says, \"I\nhave seen, but you cannot\", is to be trusted, only that one who says, \"You\ncan see too\". All scriptures, all truths are Vedas in all times, in all\ncountries; because these truths are to be seen, and any one may discover\nthem.\n", "\"When the sun of Love begins to break on the horizon, we want to give up all\nour actions unto God; and when we forget Him for a moment, it grieves us\ngreatly.\"\n", "Let nothing stand between God and your love for Him. Love Him, love Him,\nlove Him; and let the world say what it will. Love is of three sorts \u2014 one\ndemands, but gives nothing; the second is exchange; and the third is love\nwithout thought of return \u2014 love like that of the moth for the light.\n", "\"Love is higher than work, than Yoga, than knowledge.\"\n", "Work is merely a schooling for the doer; it can do no good to others. We\nmust work out our own problem; the prophets only show us how to work. \"What\nyou think, you become\", so if you throw your burden on Jesus, you will have\nto think of Him and thus become like Him \u2014 you love Him.\n", "\"Extreme love and highest knowledge are one.\"\n", "But theorising about God will not do; we must love and work. Give up the\nworld and all worldly things, especially while the \"plant\" is tender. Day\nand night think of God and think of nothing else as far as possible. The\ndaily necessary thoughts can all be thought through God. Eat to Him, drink\nto Him, sleep to Him, see Him in all. Talk of God to others; this is most\nbeneficial.\n", "Get the mercy of God and of His greatest children: these are the two chief\nways to God. The company of these children of light is very hard to get;\nfive minutes in their company will change a whole life; and if you really\nwant it enough, one will come to you. The presence of those who love God\nmakes a place holy, \"such is the glory of the children of the Lord\". They\nare He; and when they speak, their words are scriptures. The place where\nthey have been becomes filled with their vibrations, and those going there\nfeel them and have a tendency to become holy also.\n", "\"To such lovers there is no distinction of caste, learning, beauty, birth,\nwealth, or occupation; because all are His.\"\n", "Give  up  all evil company, especially at the beginning. Avoid worldly\ncompany, that will distract your mind. Give up all \"me and mine\". To him who\nhas nothing in the universe the Lord comes. Cut the bondage of all worldly\naffections; go beyond laziness and all care as to what becomes of you. Never\nturn back to see the result of what you have done. Give all to the Lord and\ngo on and think not of it. The whole soul pours in a continuous current to\nGod; there is no time to seek money, or name, or fame, no time to think of\nanything but God; then will come into our hearts that infinite, wonderful\nbliss of Love. All desires are but beads of glass. Love of God increases\nevery moment and is ever new, to be known only by feeling it. Love is the\neasiest  of  all,  it  waits  for  no logic, it is natural. We need no\ndemonstration, no proof. Reasoning is limiting something by our own minds.\nWe throw a net and catch something, and then say that we have demonstrated\nit; but never, never can we catch God in a net.\n", "Love should be unrelated. Even when we love wrongly, it is of the true love,\nof the true bliss; the power is the same, use it as we may. Its very nature\nis peace and bliss. The murderer when he kisses his baby forgets for an\ninstant all but love. Give up all self, all egotisms get out of anger,\nlust, give all to God. \"I am not, but Thou art; the old man is all gone,\nonly Thou remainest.\" \"I am Thou.\" Blame none; if evil comes, know the Lord\nis playing with you and be exceeding glad.\n", "Love is beyond time and space, it is absolute.\n"]

["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "TUESDAY, July 23, 1895. (Bhagavad-Gita, Karma-Yoga)\n", "To attain liberation through work, join yourself to work but without desire,\nlooking for no result. Such work leads to knowledge, which in turn brings\nemancipation. To give up work before you know, leads to misery. Work done\nfor the Self gives no bondage. Neither desire pleasure nor fear pain from\nwork.  It  is  the  mind and body that work, not I. Tell yourself this\nunceasingly and realise it. Try not to know that you work.\n", "Do all as a sacrifice or offering to the Lord. Be in the world, but not of\nit, like the lotus leaf whose roots are in the mud but which remains always\npure. Let your love go to all, whatever they do to you. A blind man cannot\nsee colour, so how can we see evil unless it is in us? We compare what we\nsee  outside  with  what  we  find in ourselves and pronounce judgment\naccordingly. If we are pure, we cannot see impurity. It may exist, but not\nfor  us.  See  only  God  in every man, woman and child; see it by the\nantarjyotis, \"inner light\", and seeing that, we can see naught else. Do not\nwant this world, because what you desire you get. Seek the Lord and the Lord\nonly. The more power there is, the more bondage, the more fear. How much\nmore afraid and miserable are we than the ant! Get out of it all and come to\nthe Lord. Seek the science of the maker and not that of the made.\n", "\"I am the doer and the deed.\" \"He who can stem the tide of lust and anger is\na great Yogi.\"\n", "\"Only by practice and non-attachment can we conquer mind.\" . . .\n", "Our Hindu ancestors sat down and thought on God and morality, and so have we\nbrains to use for the same ends; but in the rush of trying to get gain, we\nare likely to lose them again.\n", "*            *            *\n", "The body has in itself a certain power of curing itself and many things can\nrouse  this  curative power into action, such as mental conditions, or\nmedicine,  or  exercise,  etc. As long as we are disturbed by physical\nconditions, so long we need the help of physical agencies. Not until we have\ngot rid of bondage to the nerves, can we disregard them.\n", "There is the unconscious mind, but it is below consciousness, which is just\none part of the human organism. Philosophy is guess-work about the mind.\nReligion  is  based upon sense contact, upon seeing, the only basis of\nknowledge. What comes in contact with the superconscious mind is fact. \u00c2ptas\nare those who have \"sensed\" religion. The proof is that if you follow their\nmethod, you too will see. Each science requires its own particular method\nand instruments. An astronomer cannot show you the rings of Saturn by the\naid of all the pots and pans in the kitchen. He needs a telescope. So, to\nsee the great facts of religion, the methods of those who have already seen\nmust be followed. The greater the science the more varied the means of\nstudying it. Before we came into the world, God provided the means to get\nout;  so all we have to do is to find the means. But do not fight over\nmethods. Look only for realisation and choose the best method you can find\nto suit you. Eat the mangoes and let the rest quarrel over the basket. See\nChrist, then you will be a Christian. All else is talk; the less talking the\nbetter.\n", "The message makes the messenger. The Lord makes the temple; not vice versa.\n", "Learn until \"the glory of the Lord shines through your face\", as it shone\nthrough the face of Shvetaketu.\n", "Guess against guess makes fight; but talk of what you have been, and no\nhuman  heart  can  resist  it.  Paul was converted against his will by\nrealisation.\n", "TUESDAY AFTERNOON. (After dinner there was a short conversation in the\ncourse of which the Swami said:)\n", "Delusion creates delusion. Delusion creates itself and destroys itself, such\nis  Maya. All knowledge (so-called), being based on Maya, is a vicious\ncircle, and in time that very knowledge destroys itself. \"Let go the rope\",\ndelusion cannot touch the Atman. When we lay hold of the rope \u2014 identify\nourselves with Maya \u2014 she has power over us. Let go of it, be the Witness\nonly, then you can admire the picture of the universe undisturbed.\n"]

["\n", "(RECORDED BY MISS S. E. WALDO, A DISCIPLE)\n", "SUNDAY, June 30, 1895.\n", "To  try  to  think without a phantasm is to try to make the impossible\npossible. We cannot think \"mammalia\" without a concrete example. So with the\nidea of God.\n", "The great abstraction of ideas in the world is what we call God.\n", "Each thought has two parts \u2014 the thinking and the word; and we must have\nboth. Neither idealists nor materialists are right; we must take both idea\nand expression.\n", "All knowledge is of the reflected, as we can only see our face in a mirror.\nNo one will ever know his own Self or God; but we are that own Self, we are\nGod.\n", "In Nirvana you are when you are not. Buddha said, \"You are best, you are\nreal, when you are not\" \u2014 when the little self is gone.\n", "The Light Divine within is obscured in most people. It is like a lamp in a\ncask of iron, no gleam of light can shine through. Gradually, by purity and\nunselfishness we can make the obscuring medium less and less dense, until at\nlast it becomes as transparent as glass. Shri Ramakrishna was like the iron\ncask transformed into a glass cask through which can be seen the inner light\nas it is. We are all on the way to become the cask of glass and even higher\nand higher reflections. As long as there is a \"cask\" at all, we must think\nthrough material means. No impatient one can ever succeed.\n", "*    *    *\n", "Great saints are the object-lessons of the Principle. But the disciples make\nthe saint the Principle, and then they forget the Principle in the person.\n", "The result of Buddha's constant inveighing against a personal God was the\nintroduction of idols into India. In the Vedas they knew them not, because\nthey saw God everywhere, but the reaction against the loss of God as Creator\nand Friend was to make idols, and Buddha became an idol \u2014 so too with Jesus.\nThe range of idols is from wood and stone to Jesus and Buddha, but we must\nhave idols.\n", "*    *    *\n", "Violent attempts at reform always end by retarding reform. Do not say, \"You\nare bad\"; say only, \"You are good, but be better.\"\n", "Priests are an evil in every country, because they denounce and criticise,\npulling at one string to mend it until two or three others are out of place.\nLove never denounces, only ambition does that. There is no such thing as\n\"righteous\" anger or justifiable killing.\n", "If you do not allow one to become a lion, he will become a fox. Women are a\npower, only now it is more for evil because man oppresses woman; she is the\nfox, but when she is not longer oppressed, she will become the lion.\n", "Ordinarily speaking, spiritual aspiration ought to be balanced through the\nintellect; otherwise it may degenerate into mere sentimentality. . . .\n", "All theists agree that behind the changeable there is an Unchangeable,\nthough they vary in their conception of the Ultimate. Buddha denied this in\ntoto. \"There is no Brahman, no Atman, no soul,\" he said.\n", "As a character Buddha was the greatest the world has ever seen; next to him\nChrist. But the teachings of Krishna as taught by the Gita are the grandest\nthe world has ever known. He who wrote that wonderful poem was one of those\nrare souls whose lives sent a wave of regeneration through the world. The\nhuman race will never again see such a brain as his who wrote the Gita.\n", "*    *    *\n", "There is only one Power, whether manifesting as evil or good. God and the\ndevil are the same river with the water flowing in opposite directions.\n"]

["\n", "CLIII\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "C/O MRS. BLODGETT,\n", "921, WEST 21ST ST.,\n", "LOS ANGELES,\n", "27th December, 1899.\n", "MY DEAR MARY,\n", "Merry Christmas and Happy New Year and many, many glorious returns of such\nfor your birthday. All these wishes, prayers, greetings in one breath. I am\ncured, you will be glad to know. It was only indigestion and no heart or\nkidney affection, quoth the healers; nothing more. And I am walking three\nmiles a day \u2014 after a heavy dinner.\n", "Say \u2014 the person healing me insisted on my smoking! So I am having my pipe\nnicely and am all the better for it. In plain English the nervousness etc.\nwas all due to dyspepsia and nothing more.\n", ". . . I am at work too; working, working, not hard; but I don't care, and I\nwant to make money this time. Tell this to Margot, especially the pipe\nbusiness. You know who is healing me? No physician, no Christian Science\nhealer, but a magnetic healing woman who skins me every time she treats me.\nWonders \u2014 she performs operations by rubbing \u2014 internal operations too, her\npatients tell me.\n", "It is getting late in the night. I have to give up writing separate letters\nto Margot, Harriet, Isabelle, and Mother Church. Wish is half the work. They\nall know how I love them dearly, passionately; so you become the medium for\nmy spirit for the time, and carry them my New Year's messages.\n", "It is exactly like Northern Indian winter here, only some days a little\nwarmer; the roses are here and the beautiful palms. Barley is in the fields,\nroses and many other flowers round about the cottage where I live. Mrs.\nBlodgett, my host, is a Chicago lady \u2014 fat, old, and extremely witty. She\nheard me in Chicago and is very motherly.\n", "I am so sorry, the English have caught a Tartar in South Africa. A soldier\non duty outside a camp bawled out that he had caught a Tartar. \"Bring him\nin\", was the order from inside the tent. \"He will not come\", replied the\nsentry. \"Then you come yourself\", rang the order again. \"He will not let me\ncome either\". Hence the phrase \"to catch a Tartar\". Don't you catch any.\n", "I am happy just now and hope to remain so for all the rest of my life. Just\nnow I am Christian Science \u2014 no evil, and \"love is a drawing card\".\n", "I shall be very happy if I can make a lot of money. I am making some. Tell\nMargot, I am going to make a lot of money and go home by way of Japan,\nHonolulu, China, and Java. This is a nice place to make money quick in; and\nSan Francisco is better, I hear. Has she made any?\n", "You  could  not  get  the millionaire. Why don't you start for half or\none-fourth million? Something is better than nothing. We want money; he may\ngo into Lake Michigan, we have not the least objection. We had a bit of an\nearthquake here the other day. I hope it has gone to Chicago and raised\nIsabelle's mud-puddle up. It is getting late. I am yawning, so here I quit.\n", "Good-bye; all blessings, all love,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]

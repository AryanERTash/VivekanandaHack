["\n", "XVIII\n", "To Miss Mary Hale\n", "DETROIT,\n", "18th March, 1894.\n", "DEAR SISTER MARY,\n", "My heartfelt thanks for your kindly sending me the letter from Calcutta. It\nwas from my brethren at Calcutta, and it is written on the occasion of a\nprivate invitation to celebrate the birthday of my Master about whom you\nhave heard so much from me \u2014 so I send it over to you. The letter says that\nMazoomdar has gone back to Calcutta and is preaching that Vivekananda is\ncommitting every sin under the sun in America. . . . This is your America's\nwonderful  spiritual  man!  It is not their fault; until one is really\nspiritual, that is, until one has got a real insight into the nature of\none's own soul and has got a glimpse of the world of the soul, one cannot\ndistinguish chaff from seed, tall talk from depth, and so on. I am sorry for\npoor Mazoomdar that he should stoop so low! Lord bless the old boy!\n", "The address inside the letter is in English and is my old, old name as\nwritten by a companion of my childhood who has also taken orders. It is a\nvery poetic name. That written in the letter is an abbreviation, the full\nname being Narendra meaning the \"Chief of men\" (\"nara\" means \"man\", and\n\"indra\" stands for \"ruler\", \"chief\") \u2014 very ludicrous, isn't it? But such\nare the names in our country; we cannot help, but I am glad I have given\nthat up.\n", "I am all right. Hoping it is same with you.\n", "I remain your brother,\n", "\nVIVEKANANDA.\n"]
